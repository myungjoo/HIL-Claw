## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a record and its decision, or a requirement, is named with it; it then says what that source says and adds nothing. Every other sentence is **this record's own** — among them that the five values reach an operation as one further thing beside the request and the hw_profile, that the point reaches an operation in the request and in no other way, that a report is returned as a byte string and a condition as one of two strings, the spelling of those two strings, that an answer of neither form is never made into a result, and every weighing. No sentence is the owner's.

### 1. Scope and terms

- This record is about **a check** — "one call that reaches ⑥" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 1) — of `correctness.check` about one attempt of a run that ADR-0021 and ADR-0038 decision 6 cover and whose stated op_signature is covered (cited for "covered": ADR-0041 decision 5), **carried out by an operation that runs on the host of the tool function or by an operation of the mock**. It fixes no form for an operation whose target is another machine (decision 6).
- **The form is one at both execution points.** "the judging is the same code at both points" (cited: ADR-0038 decision 4), and the tool function's part is "the same code for every backend" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2); a form that differed by point would give that one code two things to judge.
- Of the form this record fixes **four points and nothing else**: what an operation is handed for the classes (i) and (ii) (decision 2); how the execution point reaches it (decision 3); how it returns (A) and (B) (decision 4); and what holds for an answer of neither form (decision 5). Decision 6 says what is thereby not decided.
- The terms of ADR-0016 (backend, operation, target, script, the moments ① to ⑦), ADR-0017 (draw, report, completed, broken), ADR-0021 (the cut, a check, the classes (i) to (iii) and (A), (B)), ADR-0038 (execution point, the local configuration), ADR-0040 (`point`) and ADR-0041 (the dimensions of a check, covered) are used unchanged. **The check parameters**: decision 2. **An answer** of an operation: what a call of it returned or raised.
- **What "the level of the contract" means below.** This record fixes which values cross the cut, what each is, and of which kind of value it is. What is "the code task's" (cited for the phrase: ADR-0016 decision 2; ADR-0021 decision 2) is named with each decision.

### 2. What an operation is handed: the check parameters

- An operation is called with "the request, and the hw_profile of the call" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2). For this tool it is handed, beyond them, the seed of the call, `draws` and the dimensions `m`, `k`, `n` (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2, classes (i) and (ii)).
- **An operation of this tool is called with three things: the request, the hw_profile of the call, and the check parameters. The check parameters are one value that holds exactly these five values and nothing else:**

| Value | What it is | Kind of value and bounds | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| the seed | the seed of the call: drawn by the tool function "once for each check, after ④ and after the answer of decision 4 [of ADR-0021], and before the operation is called" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3) | an integer of "the whole range of seeds the harness accepts" (cited, same decision) | ADR-0021 decision 2, class (i) |
| `draws` | the number of draws of the check | an integer of at least 1 (cited: ADR-0017 decision 9) | ADR-0021 decision 2, class (ii) |
| `m`, `k`, `n` | the dimensions of the check: the `shape.m`, `shape.k` and `shape.n` of the op_signature the run states (cited: ADR-0041 decision 2) | three integers, each of at least 1 (cited: ADR-0041 decision 5, for a covered op_signature) | ADR-0021 decision 2, class (ii); ADR-0041 decision 2 |

- **They are the values the tool function judges with.** The tool function "regenerates from" the seed it handed over (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2, the table), judges the form of the report "for the dimensions and `draws` of the call" (cited, same decision) and "hands the same three numbers to the operation" (cited: ADR-0041 decision 2). No value of the five is derived, rounded or replaced on its way across the cut.
- **The upper bounds are observed, not adopted.** The harness of this source commit refuses a seed outside 0 ≤ seed < 2^64 and a `draws` of 2^64 or more (`program_arguments`, `verification_arrays.SEED_LIMIT`); both follow from its generator, whose algorithm "is the code task's and is identified by the source commit" (cited: ADR-0017 decision 3). This record fixes no upper bound of its own: the range of a seed is "the whole range of seeds the harness accepts" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3), whatever that harness is.
- **No value, source or default of `draws` is fixed.** The form hands a number of draws; where it comes from stays with the slice of `draws` (OD-16 sub-question ⑤), as ADR-0042 decision 5 left it for the member `draws` of a result.
- **Cited, not re-decided**: "An operation is never handed the tolerance, a reference or an expected output" (ADR-0021 decision 2). The check parameters hold none of them.
- **Three forms were weighed.**
  - **(a) One further thing that holds exactly the five.** The frame's two things stay as they are and this tool adds one. Whoever writes an operation or a script sees in one place what this contract adds to the call, and a record that adds to what crosses the cut has one table to amend.
  - **(b) Five further things**, one for each value. Each later addition changes the number of things every operation and every script of this tool is called with.
  - **(c) The five as members of the object handed as the request.** "The seed is not a member of a request" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3). An object that held the seed beside the members of the request would be no request, and an operation or a script could not tell what the caller stated from what the tool function added.
- **The choice is (a).**
- **What is fixed and what is the code task's.** Fixed: that there are three things; that the third holds exactly the five; what each of the five is and its kind and bounds. The code task's: the Python type of the check parameters, by which name or position code reaches each of the five, whether the third thing is passed by position or by keyword, and the name of the parameter. The words `seed`, `draws`, `m`, `k` and `n` are those of ADR-0017 decision 9 and are used here to say which value is meant; this record does not make them names of code.
- **Class (iii) is not in the check parameters, and the choice of one group is provisional for that reason.** "For (iii) this record says only that the operation's side needs it; whether the tool function hands it over or the backend obtains it otherwise, and whether an operation is handed the data root …, is the second record's" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2). This record fixes nothing of it — no path, no built file, no data root, no flag, no time limit, no environment (decision 6). **How a later record adds**: the record that fixes class (iii) says, for each thing the tool function then hands over, whether it is a further value of the check parameters or a further thing beside them, and amends the table above. It need not reopen the five values or the first two things. It may find that one group is the wrong cut — for example because what class (iii) needs is of another kind than five integers — and then revisits the choice of (a).
- **What an operation needs and does not have from this form.** An operation finds the condition "the time limit" (decision 4), and no record says where the time limit of a process comes from (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2, "Where `draws`, the dimensions, the flags and the time limits come from is not decided"; OD-14). The form of this record is therefore not all a backend with a process needs; decision 6 and Consequences say what follows.

### 3. The execution point

- A request of this tool carries the member `point`, with the values `"sim"` and `"board"` (cited: ADR-0040 decision 4), and an operation is called with the request (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2). **The point therefore reaches every operation of this tool as a member of the request, without anything this record adds.**
- **This record leaves it at that: the point reaches an operation in the request and in no other way.** The check parameters do not hold it, and the request is not handed over without it.
- **What an operation may make depend on the point**: where and how the kernel is carried out — at which of the targets of its backend, by which means. This is what a point is: "one of the two places of the round loop of FR-1 at which an attempt is run and its correctness is checked" (cited: ADR-0038 decision 1), and "a target whose two points are two machines would need it" (cited: ADR-0038 decision 4). This says what the form allows and chooses no target: "Which of several targets of one hw_profile a call goes to" is "not decided here (#5 B-2, OD-10)" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2).
- **What does not depend on the point.**
  - The form of what an operation returns: decision 4 holds at both points (decision 1).
  - The judging: an operation has none — "An operation computes no reference, compares nothing and gives no verdict" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2) — and the tool function's is "the same code at both points" (cited: ADR-0038 decision 4).
- **No outcome of the check at one point depends on state a backend kept in the front process from the check at the other**: "No outcome depends on state a backend kept in the front process from an earlier call" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2 (c)), and each check has a seed of its own (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3; ADR-0038 decision 5). What the operation's side keeps at its target is not restricted by this (decision 6).
- **Three choices were weighed.**
  - **(a) Leave it in the request.** Nothing is added, and the one statement of the point is the caller's. Against it stands that "the judging is the same code at both points" (cited: ADR-0038 decision 4): an operation that sees the point can answer differently at each. It can do so only within the form of decision 4, and what it returns is judged by the tool function on inputs regenerated from the seed, at either point alike.
  - **(b) Hand the point separately**, as a sixth value of the check parameters. It would be a second statement of one thing, with a rule needed for what an operation does when the two differ.
  - **(c) Keep the point from an operation**: hand over the request without the member. The frame hands an operation the request (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2); a request with one member taken out would be a special case of that sentence for one tool, and a backend whose two points are two machines could not be written under it (cited: ADR-0038 decision 4).
- **The choice is (a).** It closes the item "whether an operation is handed the point" of ADR-0042 Consequences: an operation is handed the point as a member of the request, and is handed it in no other way.
- **Not said**: what an operation of `cpu_standin` does at either point (the mechanism of a backend; "In the local configuration nothing at the target differs between the two checks" — cited: ADR-0038 decision 4); and what an operation may make depend on any other member of the request, which belongs to class (iii) and to how a backend is reached (decision 6).

### 4. What an operation returns: a report, or one of two conditions

- An operation returns "exactly one of: (A) **the report** — what the process wrote on its report channel, unchanged, when the process ended by itself with exit status 0 within its time limit; (B) **that there is no such report, and which of two conditions failed** — the time limit, or a non-zero status or a signal (told apart no further than ADR-0017 decision 7 does); a process that was ended for its time limit is reported as the time limit. An operation does not judge the form or the content of a report" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2).
- **(A) is returned as a byte string: the bytes the process wrote on its report channel, in their order, none added, removed or changed.** The channel and the content of a report are those of ADR-0017 decision 7 (cited: "the standard output the process was started with"; "the bytes they have in memory"), whose premise is that "both sides run on one machine" (cited, same decision) — the scope of decision 1.
  - **Every byte string is (A).** An operation does not judge the form (cited above), so a report that is short, long, malformed or empty is returned as it is, and the tool function finds its form (cited: ADR-0021 decision 5, "found by the tool function").
- **(B) is returned as one of two strings: `"time_limit"` when the process did not end within its time limit, and `"exit_status"` when it ended with a non-zero status or by a signal.** There are exactly two (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2, "which of two conditions"); a process that was ended for its time limit is `"time_limit"` (cited, same decision). The value is compared as it is, and nothing is normalised.
- **An answer of the form (B) holds the condition and nothing else**, and one of the form (A) the report and nothing else: no exit status, no signal, no time that passed, no command line, no standard error, and with (B) no byte of what the process wrote on its report channel. **This says what the form holds under this record and closes nothing.** "Whether anything else the process wrote — its standard error — crosses the cut is not decided" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2), and that question stays where ADR-0021 decision 3 put it (OD-16 sub-question ⑤), as "What is kept of a broken run and what follows it" stays with OD-10 and OD-14 (cited: ADR-0017 decision 7). A record that answers either may add to what an operation returns, and then amends the table of decision 7.
- **The two strings are those of the member `condition` of a result** (ADR-0042 decision 3, its first two values), taken on purpose. **Five forms of (B) were weighed.**
  - **(a) The two strings of ADR-0042 decision 3.** The two conditions an operation finds are the first two of the four of ADR-0021 decision 5, and ADR-0042 spelt them after the words of that decision. With one spelling, the tool function puts the value an operation returned into `condition` as it is, and a reader of the two records needs no table between them. ADR-0042 decision 8 says of its four values that "They are not the spelling of what an operation returns … that slice is the backends'": it kept the result slice from deciding this one. This record is that slice and takes two of the four by its own choice. **From here on the two spellings are tied**: a record that changes one says what becomes of the other.
  - **(b) The members `TIMEOUT` and `EXIT_STATUS` of the harness's `Broken`** (an observation of the code, which binds nothing). That enumeration has four members, two of which an operation never finds, and every backend and every script would import a module of the harness to answer.
  - **(c) An exception for (B).** A broken run is a result (cited: ADR-0021 decision 5), an outcome the contract expects, and what an operation raises is either a tool failure, which "says that no outcome was established" (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4), or a defect (decision 5). (B) is neither.
  - **(d) A pair in the manner of `local_run.RunResult`** — a status, whether the limit was reached, and what was captured (an observation of the code). The tool function would then decide what "non-zero" is, and the status, and what a failed process wrote, would cross the cut: more than "which of two conditions failed", and a part of what is "not decided".
  - **(e) Two values whose spelling is left to the code task.** "The default script and the rule by which it derives a report are the second record's" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2); the record that writes that script would have to wait for a code task or name the two values itself.
- **The choice is (a).**
- **How the two forms are told apart: by the kind of value.** A byte string is a report, whatever its bytes are: the bytes `time_limit` returned as a byte string are a report of the wrong form. One of the two strings is a condition.
- **What is fixed and what is the code task's.** Fixed: that (A) is a byte string with exactly the bytes of the report, and that (B) is one of the two strings above. The code task's: which Python types count as a byte string (observation: `judge_report` takes exactly `bytes`) and as a string, and how the tool function tests for the two forms.
- **The names collide with no other.** Checked on 2026-10-12 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`. This record fixes no new name. `"time_limit"` and `"exit_status"` occur as values in `hilclaw/check_result.py` (`CONDITIONS`), in the docstring of `hilclaw/check_judging.py` and in the tests of those two modules, and in ADR-0042 and the two passages that render it — each time as the two values of `condition` this record takes. The words `time_limit` and `exit_status` are also parts of the names of three test functions (in `tests/harness/test_timing.py`, `tests/harness/test_verification.py` and `tests/test_merge_guard.py`), which are no values. Neither is a reserved kind, a kind of a record, a `step` value or a member name. The term "check parameters" occurs nowhere else.

### 5. An answer of neither form

- **What is meant.** An operation that returns a value that is neither a byte string nor one of the two strings of decision 4 — nothing at all, a number, the string `"report_form"` or `"pass"`, a pair — or that raises something that is no tool failure. **Not meant**: a tool failure an operation raises (decision 6), and the death of the process while the operation runs, after which the step is open (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (d); ADR-0011 decision 3).
- **What the landed records carry.**
  - "A backend answers exactly the contract of the tool", and "it may use fewer of the outcomes a contract names, never others" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2 (b)). An answer of neither form is therefore a backend that does not answer the contract.
  - "**A backend that does not answer the contract** — another member, another kind, a reserved kind for another situation, kept state — is not detected by the frame" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 6).
  - The reserved kinds `tool_crashed` and `bad_result` mean that "The tool function raised something that is no tool failure, or returned no result object" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2). The sentence is about a tool function; it does not say what a tool function does with what an operation returned or raised.
  - The `failure` of an end line "holds the `kind` and `message` of the tool failure when the step ended without an outcome" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3). The sentence says what the line holds for a tool failure; it does not say which answers of an operation are one.
- **Decided here: an answer of neither form is never made into a result.** No pass, no fail and no broken result follows from it — in particular not a broken result with `"report_form"`.
  - Why: each value of `condition` names a condition of a completed run that the run failed (cited: ADR-0042 decision 3; ADR-0017 decision 7), and "the condition is reported so that whoever acts on it can look" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 5). An answer of neither form shows a defect of the backend and establishes nothing about the run of the kernel's process. ADR-0021 decision 5 refused to report a property of the attempt as a failure of the tool — "it would have a caller read a property of the attempt out of a failure of the tool" (cited) —; a result made from such an answer would be the reverse, a failure of the tool reported as a property of the attempt.
- **The tool function establishes which of the two forms an answer has before it uses it.** It judges nothing but a byte string, and without judging it makes a broken result from nothing but one of the two strings. This is no check the frame lacked: the tool function of this tool must tell (A) from (B) to do its part at all, and whatever is neither is what is left over. **It establishes the form, not the truth**: an answer of the right form from a backend that did not do what the contract says is not recognised (decision 8).
- **What the call then answers is not decided.** The fixed inputs do not carry it. No landed sentence gives this tool a tool-failure kind for the case, and this record gives it none, new or existing. Whether the identity is then ended, by an end line with a `failure`, or stays open — and "Until a recovery rule exists (OD-10, the retry part of OD-17), an open step is a point the run does not pass by itself" (cited: ADR-0011 Consequences, as ADR-0040 decision 2 quotes it) — is a question of recovery (OD-10, the retry part of OD-17). It is registered as **OD-18 sub-question ⑮** (Boundaries).

### 6. What is not decided by this

- **Class (iii)**: how an operation comes to the verification executable of the attempt, whether it is handed the data root, flags, the time limit of the process, the environment (ADR-0021 decision 2; the first clause of OD-18 sub-question ⑩; OD-23; OD-14; the isolation means).
- **A target on another machine**: "how a report is represented when the target is another machine, whose `float` bytes need not be those of the host of the tool function …, is not decided (#5 B-2)" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2). The record of such a backend says how its operation returns a report, and may amend decision 4 for it.
- **The tool failures of a backend**: "Which tool failure a backend may raise when it cannot carry out the experiment at all … is not listed (OD-10, #5 B-2)" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 5), and neither is what a call answers when no verification executable can be had (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2). What the tool function does with a tool failure an operation raises belongs to the slice that names those kinds.
- **What follows for a script of the mock, by citation only.** "a script of this tool is asked with what this contract hands an operation, not with the request and the hw_profile alone" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2; the condition under which that was written holds — ADR-0038 decision 6), and a script is a function "to what the operation does" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (c)). So a script of this tool is asked with the called tool, the request, the hw_profile and the check parameters, and of the cases ADR-0021 decision 2 lists for it, the four reports are byte strings and the two cases without a report are the two strings of decision 4. **The default script, the rule by which it derives a report, and how the operation of the mock for this tool is written are the next slice's.**
- **The spend.** The form holds no amount. The `spend` of an end line stays as ADR-0040 decision 6 left it.
- **What the operation's side keeps after a check** stays as ADR-0021 decision 6 left it: "a backend or a target that keeps the seed, a log of the process or its standard error after the check is not stopped by anything here" (cited).

### 7. What crosses the cut

| Direction | What | Kind of value | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| to the operation | the request | the request of the call, the member `point` in it | ADR-0016 decision 2; ADR-0040 decisions 4 and 5; decision 3 |
| to the operation | the hw_profile of the call | the record of the run | ADR-0016 decision 2 |
| to the operation | the check parameters: the seed, `draws`, `m`, `k`, `n` | one value that holds exactly five integers, with the bounds of decision 2 | decision 2 (the classes: ADR-0021 decision 2) |
| from the operation | (A) the report | a byte string | decision 4 (the class: ADR-0021 decision 2) |
| from the operation | (B) the condition | the string `"time_limit"` or `"exit_status"` | decision 4 (the class: ADR-0021 decision 2) |

- **Under this record nothing else crosses in either direction as a value handed or returned.** Only a record adds a row, and the open questions whose answer could do so are named in decisions 2, 4 and 6.
- A tool failure an operation raises is no row: this record names none (decision 6).

### Walk-through

The local configuration and the names of ADR-0040 to ADR-0042: the run `r1`, the attempt `round` 3, `candidate` 2, `attempt` 1, and "the request" `{"run_id": "r1", "round": 3, "candidate": 2, "attempt": 1, "execution": 1, "point": "sim", …}`. `runs/r1/instance.json` states a covered op_signature and a tolerance. In every row but the last the operation is called with the request, the hw_profile of the run and the check parameters. **No value of `draws` is proposed and no seed is shown.**

| The check | The operation returns | The tool function |
|---|---|---|
| The request; the kernel is right | A byte string: the report | Judges it with the five values it handed over and the tolerance: a result that reports pass |
| The same request with `"point": "board"` (another identity, another seed); the kernel is wrong on those inputs | A byte string: the report. The operation found `"board"` in the request it was handed | Judges it in the same way: a result that reports fail |
| The request with `"candidate": 3`; the process ends with status 0 and wrote half a report | A byte string: what the process wrote, unchanged | Finds the form: a broken result with `"report_form"` |
| The request with `"candidate": 4`; the process does not end within its time limit | The string `"time_limit"`, and nothing the process wrote | A broken result with `"time_limit"` |
| The request with `"candidate": 5`; the process is ended by a signal | The string `"exit_status"` | A broken result with `"exit_status"` |
| Another run, whose hw_profile names the mock; either point | What its script returned; the script was asked with the tool, the request, the hw_profile and the check parameters | The same as in the rows above, by what was returned |
| The request with `"candidate": 6`; a defective operation returns the string `"pass"` | An answer of neither form | Makes no result from it. What the call answers: sub-question ⑮ |
| The request while the file states no tolerance | Not called (ADR-0021 decision 4) | Answers `tolerance_not_stated`; no seed was drawn and there are no check parameters |

### 8. Limits

- **A backend that returns outputs the kernel did not produce** is not detected: "A backend holds the seed and can compute the product itself; the cut keeps a defective backend from passing an attempt by accident, not a dishonest one" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 6). The check parameters are what gives it the seed.
- **A wrong answer of the right form is taken as it is.** An operation that returns `"exit_status"` for a process that ended with status 0, or a report it cut short, leads to a broken result about an attempt that may be sound. Decision 5 recognises a form and nothing else.
- **What crosses the cut is large and held at once**: "4·m·n bytes per draw, 16 MB for the largest output of the first instances …, held at once for all draws" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 6). A byte string is returned whole; this record adds no streaming and no size limit.
- **The form carries no version.** It is an interface between two parts of the package of one source commit: a tool function and the backends of the table that was built with it (cited for the table: ADR-0016 decision 2). ADR-0017 decision 7 says the like of the harness's own form — "an interface between two parts of the harness of one source commit …; it carries no version and **is not a file or a field of the data plane**" (cited for that form only).
- **Nothing in an answer says which backend gave it**, the mock included (cited: ADR-0016 decision 6, "it is told apart by its hw_profile only").
- **An operation is handed the seed**: "A backend is handed the seed and captures what the process wrote", and "The kernel's process holds the seed during the check" (both cited: ADR-0021 decision 6).
- The limits of ADR-0016 decision 6, ADR-0021 decision 6, ADR-0038 decision 8 and ADR-0042 decision 9 stand unchanged.

## Consequences

- **What the next slices of the second record must still add** — the list of ADR-0042 Consequences, without the item that record struck off itself (the members of the result): every other member of the request, the input schema and the description; where `draws` comes from; where the kernel of the attempt, the flags and the time limits come from, and with them what class (iii) adds to the form of this record; the tool-failure kinds a backend may raise; the spend beyond ADR-0040 decision 6; the operation and the default script of the mock; ~~whether an operation is handed the point~~ (this record). Of OD-18 sub-question ⑩ the first three clauses, the kernel language and another accelerator; and sub-question ⑮.
- **Code** (observations; **this record binds no code**). *Possible after this record*: the part of the tool function that turns an answer of an operation into a result — `hilclaw.check_judging.from_report` for a byte string, `hilclaw.check_result.broken` for one of the two strings — as far as decision 5 goes; what that code raises for an answer of neither form is the code task's, and what the call then answers is sub-question ⑮. *Possible after the next slice*: the operation of the mock for this tool, which is called with a third thing; `hilclaw/mock_backend.py` and its tests pin the call of an operation at two arguments and that of a script at three today, so that task changes those assertions or uses another module. A tool function that hands an operation values its own judging would refuse has called the operation for nothing; when it checks them is the code task's. **Not yet possible**: an operation of `cpu_standin` (class (iii)), the tool function of `correctness.check` and its registration.
- **Not edited and now less exact** (follow-ups for the integrator; the list holds what this record found): ADR-0021 decision 2 ("The spelling of what is handed and returned is the second record's and the code task's" — a record now fixes a part of it) and its Boundaries where they leave the form to the second record; ADR-0038 decision 4 ("**Not decided**: whether an operation of this tool is handed the point") and ADR-0040 decision 4 ("Whether an operation is handed the point is not decided"); the item "whether an operation is handed the point" in the Consequences of ADR-0040, ADR-0041 and ADR-0042 and in the Boundaries of ADR-0040 and ADR-0041, and "whether an operation is handed the point; the form between the operation and the tool function" in the Boundaries of ADR-0042; ADR-0042 decision 8 ("They are not the spelling of what an operation returns" — two of the four now are, by decision 4); ADR-0016 decision 5 (c) and the script row of its Walk-through, as ADR-0021 decision 2 already names them (for this tool a script is asked with a fourth thing). In `hilclaw/mock_backend.py` the two docstring sentences ADR-0021 Consequences names stay less exact for this tool. No sentence of UsageCases became false.
- **A-13 reading**: document mode; no code, dependency, settings file or schema file changes. The record continues the inner structure of layer 2 — the line between a tool function and a backend of one source commit — as ADR-0016 and ADR-0021 did. **This record adds no member to a request or to a result and gives no value of the form a place in a file of the data plane**: the check parameters and an answer of an operation are values the tool function hands over and receives within one call. What of them reaches a file does so by ADR-0042 alone, as members of a result: the dimensions as `shape`, the number of draws as `draws`, one of the two strings of decision 4 as `condition`, and what the tool function makes of an answer — by judging a report, or from a condition — as `outcome`, `counts` and the other two values of `condition`. What the operation's side keeps is not restricted here (decision 6). No member of `STATE.json`, `instance.json`, a spec, `artifacts.json`, a result file or a line of `events.ndjson` is added, removed or changed, and no value of `error.kind` is added or given to a situation. **This is therefore read as no "데이터 plane 스키마 변경".**

## Boundaries (not decided here)

- **Class (iii) and OD-18 sub-question ⑩, first clause**: how an operation comes to the verification executable of the attempt and who builds it (OD-23; the isolation means, HQ-0010); the data root; flags; the time limit of the process and the hierarchy of time limits (OD-14); the environment; what a call answers when no executable can be had.
- **A target on another machine**: how a backend is reached and how a report travels and is represented (**#5 B-2 — owner-only**); the simulator (#5 B-3).
- **The tool failures of a backend**: which kinds a backend may raise and what the tool function does with one (OD-10; #5 B-2); any value of `error.kind`.
- **The mock**: its operation of this tool, the default script and the rule by which a script derives a report; fault injection in a live session (OD-18 sub-question ⑧).
- **The operation of `cpu_standin`** and which functions of `hilclaw/harness/` it calls.
- **`draws` and what is recorded after a check**: the value, the source and any default of `draws`; whether a seed or a standard error is recorded, returned or crosses the cut (OD-16 sub-question ⑤); what is kept of a broken run (OD-10, OD-14).
- **The rest of the request**: every other request member, the input schema, the description. **The spend** beyond ADR-0040 decision 6.
- **What follows a result**: what anybody does after a fail or a broken result; recovery, retry and a further `execution` (FR-1, OD-10, the rest of OD-17, OD-19); OD-18 sub-questions ⑫ and ⑭.
- **Other tools**: the forms between the tool function and an operation of `sim.run`, `board.run` and `profiler.collect`; hooks and gates (the rest of OD-18; OD-3).
- **Owner-only (DRIVER §9)**: the #5 A and B items; OD-3; OD-25; C-6.
- **Sub-question this record registers** (under OD-18): ⑮ For `correctness.check` — what a call answers, and what the log then holds (an end line with a `failure`, and of which kind, or no end line, so that the identity stays open), when the answer of the operation has neither form of ADR-0043 decision 4: a returned value that is neither a report nor one of the two conditions, or something raised that is no tool failure (with the tool-failure kinds of a backend — sub-question ⑩; OD-10; the retry part of OD-17).
- Any change to ADR-0001 to ADR-0042, to UsageCases, to `hilclaw/`, to tests, to CI, to `pyproject.toml`, to any settings file, or to the `HIL-Claw-data` repository; any new external dependency; any new reserved `kind`.
