## Decision

A sentence marked **owner** quotes O1 or O4 of ADR-0061, as a reason only. A sentence is **cited** when a requirement, or a record with its decision, is named with it; it then says what that source says and adds nothing. Every other sentence is **this record's own** — among them every member name, type and value, when a member is present, that a result holds the intervals, the resolution, the figure, the dimensions, `n_max`, the sample form and the `bound` and holds no summary, that the condition values take the spellings of `correctness.check` where the meaning is the same, that the member list is closed, and every weighing. No decision is the owner's.

### 1. Scope and terms

- This record is about **a result of a call of `sim.run` or `board.run` about one attempt of a run that ADR-0061 decision 1 covers** (cited): what the tool function returns for a call that reached the operation and whose judging ended in a completed measurement or in broken — each a result (cited: ADR-0061 decision 7) — and what the end line of that call holds as `result` (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3, "`result` is the result object of the call itself").
- **One member list for both tools.** "nothing but the execution point distinguishes the two operations" (**owner** O1, as ADR-0061 decision 1 quotes it), and every point of ADR-0061 and ADR-0062 is stated once for both (cited). Every member below has the same name, type, values and presence rule in a result of either tool; nothing in a result says which tool returned it, since the caller called it and the `step` of the end line says it (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3).
- **A tool failure is not a result and gets no member here.** Its form is the `kind` and `message` of ADR-0010 decision 4 (cited). A repeated call that the lookup answers from an end line that holds a result returns that result (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3).
- Of a result this record fixes **seven points and nothing else**: which of converged, not converged and broken (decision 2); the condition of broken (decision 3); the measurement (decision 4); what the run was made with (decision 5); the memory figure (decision 6); the verdict and *no ceiling stated* (decision 7); what a result never holds (decision 8). Decision 9 gives the member list and the sense in which it is closed; decision 10 says what is thereby not decided.
- The terms of ADR-0009 (report, completed, broken, interval, resolution, sample form), ADR-0007 (measurement, sample, converged, not converged), ADR-0052 (the figure; *within*, *over*, *not judged*), ADR-0061 (the read of ④, the cut, *no ceiling stated*) and ADR-0062 are used unchanged. A *name* and an *integer* (0 to 2^53 − 1) are those of ADR-0001.
- **Form rules, taken from ADR-0042 decision 1 for the same reasons.** A result is one JSON object, "(strict JSON, no non-finite number)" (cited: ADR-0010 decision 2). No member holds `null`: a member that does not apply is left out. A string value is compared as it is.

### 2. Which of converged, not converged and broken: the member `outcome`

- A result lets a caller tell "which of converged, not converged and broken" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 7).
- **The member is `outcome`, a string with exactly three values: `"converged"`, `"not_converged"` and `"broken"`. It is present in every result.**
- What each value says is cited: a completed run reports `stop converged` or `stop not_converged` and is completed only if the replay agrees (cited: ADR-0009 decision 8); a measurement that reaches `n_max` without CV < `cv_max` "is **usable and marked \"not converged\"**" (cited: ADR-0007 decision 4); every other run "is broken and yields no measurement" (cited: ADR-0009 decision 8). The two first values are the words of the report's last line; whether such a measurement may stand as a best or a baseline "is not decided here" (cited: ADR-0007 decision 4) and is OD-13 sub-question ②.
- **Three forms were weighed**, as ADR-0042 decision 2 weighed them: (a) one member with three values; (b) two members in the manner of `Judgement`, a `state` and a `broken`, one of them absent — a reader that asks only whether `state` is `"not_converged"` would take a broken result for a converged one, and the pair has states without a meaning; (c) a boolean `completed` beside a state — the form (b) again. **The choice is (a).**
- **The name.** Not `state`: in ADR-0009 decision 8 a state is what a completed run reports, so a member of that name with the value `"broken"` would give the word a third value. **`outcome` is the name ADR-0042 decision 2 gave the member of `correctness.check` that says which class of result a result is**; one name for that question across the HW tools' contracts lets a reader find it the same way. The values differ by tool because the classes differ; `"broken"` has in both the meaning "the run was not completed, and no verdict (there) or measurement (here) was established". A reader knows which tool's result it holds from the call or from the `step` of the end line (decision 1).

### 3. The condition of a broken result: the member `condition`

- For broken, a result lets a caller tell which condition failed first, in the order of ADR-0061 decision 7 (cited): the build past its time limit, the compiler's non-zero status, the run past its time limit, a non-zero status or a signal — "these four found by the operation" — then a report not of the form, an interval of 0, a replay that disagrees, found by the tool function.
- **The member is `condition`, a string with exactly seven values, in that order:**

| Value | The condition that failed first | Found by (cited: ADR-0061 decisions 3 and 7) |
|---|---|---|
| `"build_time_limit"` | The timing build did not end within its time limit | the operation |
| `"build_exit_status"` | The compiler ended with a non-zero status — a compile or a link failure (a timing build that ended not ok is a result: **owner** O4, as ADR-0061 decision 7 quotes it) | the operation |
| `"time_limit"` | The timing run did not end within its time limit | the operation |
| `"exit_status"` | The timing run ended with a non-zero status or a signal, told apart no further | the operation |
| `"report_form"` | The captured standard output is not exactly of the form of ADR-0009 decision 8 | the tool function |
| `"zero_interval"` | The report holds "an interval of 0" (cited: ADR-0009 decision 8) | the tool function |
| `"replay"` | The replay of the stopping rule disagrees with the reported state — "a disagreement" (cited: ADR-0009 decisions 7 and 8) | the tool function |

- **It is present exactly when `outcome` is `"broken"`, and left out otherwise**, for the reason ADR-0042 decision 3 gave: a fill value for a completed run would name no condition.
- **The first five values take the spellings of the values of `condition` of `correctness.check` that have the same meaning** (ADR-0042 decision 3; ADR-0060 decision 4) — this record's own choice. *For*: in both contracts each says the same thing of the same kind of process — a build of one target by the backend, a harness process with a time limit, a captured report not of the form its record fixes — and a reader that acts on a condition (OD-10, OD-14) then meets one word for one thing; no value of one tool means something else in the other. *Against*: a reader might take a value for a statement about the other tool's run; the `step` of the end line says whose run it is (decision 1). The two build values were given by the owner for the check (HQ-0020 point 1, as ADR-0060 decision 4 quotes it); O4 takes from that answer the principle only, so their use here is this record's, not the owner's. **The last two values are this record's own** and name the condition whose test failed, as `check_value` does there; the enumeration `Broken` of the harness spells them `ZERO_INTERVAL` and `REPLAY` (observation — neither spelling binds the other).
- **A broken result carries no measurement**: a broken run "yields no measurement, however many intervals it reported" (cited: ADR-0009 decision 8), and "A run that is not completed yields no figure" (cited: ADR-0052 decision 4). It holds no `resolution_ns`, no `intervals_ns` and no `memory_bytes`.
- The member holds the condition and nothing of the run: not the exit status, not the signal, not a time, no byte of the report or of the compiler's log (decision 8). It does not say whose defect it was: each condition "can be caused by the kernel" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 7), and a defect of the harness or the backend is not told apart.
- The seven values are values of a member of a result, **not the spelling of what an operation returns**; that form is ADR-0061 Consequences item (4) (decision 10).

### 4. The measurement of a completed run: `resolution_ns` and `intervals_ns`, and no summary

- Cited: "The Python side returns, for a completed run, the intervals in order, the resolution and the state" (ADR-0009 decision 8); "Every sample of a measurement is kept, in the order taken" and "a scalar alone is never the record" (ADR-0007 decision 3).
- **`intervals_ns`: an array of JSON integers of at least 1 — the lengths of the timed intervals in nanoseconds, exactly as the report gave them, one entry per repeat, in the order taken. `resolution_ns`: a JSON integer of at least 0 — the reported clock resolution in nanoseconds. Both are present exactly when `outcome` is `"converged"` or `"not_converged"`.**
- **No upper bound is added, and these are not *integers* of ADR-0001**: the report's form gives its integers no upper bound (ADR-0009 decision 8 gives none; `hilclaw/harness/timing.py` admits any decimal digits the interpreter converts — observation), and a result holds what the report gave. A bound of 2^53 − 1 would leave a completed run whose report holds a larger value with no answer under this table, and giving one would be a new rule of what a completed run is or a new kind, which this record does not make. A JSON integer is strict JSON (ADR-0010 decision 2); how a code task writes a value that its interpreter cannot convert is the code task's, as `hilclaw/check_result.py` refuses one today (observation).
- What follows from the cited definitions and is no further rule: every interval of a completed run is at least 1, since an interval of 0 makes the run broken (ADR-0009 decision 8); the array has at least `n_min` and at most `n_max` entries, and exactly `n_max` when `outcome` is `"not_converged"` (ADR-0007 decision 4; ADR-0009 decision 7); and the replay of the stopping rule over the array with the `sample_form` and `n_max` of decision 5 and the constants `n_min` and `cv_max` of the instance's source commit (Limit 8) gives the `outcome`. No threshold on `resolution_ns` is decided (ADR-0009 decision 8 decides none).
- **Why the whole sequence.** ADR-0007 decision 3 asks that the result files that hold a measurement "be able to hold the whole ordered sequence and the mark of decision 4", and ADR-0006 decision 7 makes it a property of the line of an expensive result that it carry the result "completely enough that the result file can be written from the line". This record reads the two together: **a result that held less than the ordered sequence and the mark would leave nothing from which a result file that meets ADR-0007 decision 3 could be written after the call** — the samples exist in the process of the tool function only, and "an outcome lost with its process can therefore not be obtained again" (cited: ADR-0062 decision 2). It names no member of a result file (decision 10). **Which of these results are expensive in the sense of ADR-0006 decision 7 is not decided here** — that decision names the board measurement and leaves the rest open (cited) —; the sequence is held by a result of either tool because ADR-0007 decision 3 asks it of every result file that holds a measurement and one member list answers both tools (decision 1).
- **A third sentence on result files, weighed.** ADR-0009 Consequences: a record of a measurement "should be able to hold what the source commit does not determine — the parameters of decision 9 the caller chose and the reported resolution. **No field is named**; whether a seed may be recorded after a run goes with sub-question ⑦" (cited). Of those parameters, a result holds the dimensions, `n_max` and the sample form (decision 5) and the resolution (above); the seed it does not hold, which that sentence itself leaves to OD-13 sub-question ⑦ (decision 8); and the time limits, the flags, the compiler and the environment are now the backend's own and not handed by the tool function (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3; their source and values are OD-18 sub-question ⑲ (라)), so the tool function has no value of them to put into a result. Whether a result file needs them, and from where, is for the record of that file; if it needs them from the line, it revisits this record (decision 10). No member of a result file is named.
- **Intervals, not samples.** The samples are the interval lengths or their reciprocals by the sample form (cited: ADR-0009 decision 7). The result holds the reported integers, which are exact, and the sample form (decision 5), from which the samples follow; a reciprocal would be a non-integer number whose spelling needs a rule.
- **No summary.** The trimmed mean of ADR-0007 decision 5 is not a member. *For leaving it out*: "The summary of decision 5 and the verdict of decision 7 are derived values" and "A judgement can then be redone later with another rule" (cited: ADR-0007 decision 3); it is computed from the intervals with constants of the source (cited: ADR-0007 decision 8), so a stored summary would be a second statement of the samples fixed at one source commit; and it is a non-integer number. *Against*: every reader that wants the one number must compute it. **Which component computes a summary, compares measurements or shows a number to anybody is not decided here** (OD-13 sub-questions ① and ②, OD-15, OD-29; PLAN P2-1). `hilclaw/harness/timing.py` "summarises nothing" (observation).

### 5. What the run was made with: `shape`, `n_max` and `sample_form`, and not the point

Each was weighed on its own, in the manner of ADR-0042 decision 5.

- **The dimensions: in. The member is `shape`, an object with exactly the three members `m`, `k` and `n`, each an *integer* of at least 1 — the `shape.m`, `shape.k` and `shape.n` the tool function took from the read of ④ and handed to the operation** (cited for the source: ADR-0061 decision 3). Its form is that of the member `shape` of a result of `correctness.check` (ADR-0042 decision 5) and of the stated op_signature record, so the three can be compared. Present in every result. *Why*: "two reads of the file of one run can find two op_signatures" (cited: ADR-0031 decision 7, as ADR-0041 decision 9 cites it), neither line of a step names the shape (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3), and a timing of one shape says nothing about another.
- **`n_max`: in. An *integer* of at least 5 (the form of the member: ADR-0023 decision 3) — the bound of the repeats the tool function read in ④ and handed to the operation** (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3; ADR-0023 decisions 2 and 5). Present in every result. *Why*: "a file changed after the start is read as it is at that read ..., so two measurements of one run can be taken under two bounds" (cited: ADR-0023 decision 6); a not converged result is one that reached `n_max` (cited: ADR-0007 decision 4), which a reader cannot check without it; and the replay of decision 4 needs it.
- **The sample form: in. The member is `sample_form`, a string: one of the names of ADR-0009 decision 7, `"interval"` or `"reciprocal"` — the form the tool function derived in ④ and handed to the operation** (cited: ADR-0061 decisions 3 and 4). Present in every result; **under ADR-0061 decision 4 every result today holds `"interval"`**. *Why*: the state of a run is the stopping rule applied to the samples of that form (cited: ADR-0009 decision 7), the form is derived at each call from what the file then states (cited: ADR-0061 decision 4) and "Whoever can write `instance.json` chooses the objective and the `bound`" (cited: ADR-0061 Limit 8, after ADR-0053 L2); with the form in the result, the replay can be redone from the line and the constants of the instance's source commit (Limit 8). *Against*: one value today. This adds no value of the form and no objective type.
- **In a broken result the three are present as well**: they say with what the run was to be made, and their presence then depends on nothing.
- **Not in a result** (this record's own, for the reasons of ADR-0042 decision 5): none of the five request members of ADR-0062 decision 4, which the caller stated and the envelope numbers, `step` and `execution` of the end line say (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3); not the point, which the tool called says (cited: ADR-0061 decision 5) and the `step` follows from (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3); not `n_min` or `cv_max`, which are constants of the harness source, to which "A result is traced ... through the source commit hash of the instance" (cited: ADR-0007 decision 8); not the hw_profile or the backend; not the seed (decision 8); not a flag, the compiler, a time limit or the environment, which are the backend's own (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3; their values are ⑲ (라)); not the output path or a build directory; not the source commit.

### 6. The memory figure: the member `memory_bytes`

- Cited: the figure is the maximum resident set size of the process of one timing run, in bytes (ADR-0052 decisions 3 and 4); "A run that is not completed yields no figure"; "A reported value that is not a positive integer yields no figure" (ADR-0052 decision 4); the operation returns a number of bytes or none, and "A figure returned with (A) is used only if the tool function finds the run completed" (ADR-0061 decision 3).
- **The member is `memory_bytes`, a JSON integer of at least 1** (no upper bound is added, as ADR-0052 decision 4 gives none; the reasoning of decision 4 on the intervals). **It is present exactly when `outcome` is `"converged"` or `"not_converged"` and the run yielded a figure; it is absent from every broken result and from a completed result whose run yielded none** (a platform without a unit rule, a value that is no positive integer — cited: ADR-0052 decisions 4 and 5).
- **It is present whether or not a ceiling is stated.** *For*: NFR-2; "Whether the figure of a run is kept in a result file is part of sub-question ⑩" (cited: ADR-0052 decision 4), and by the reasoning of decision 4 a figure not in the result could not be written to a result file from the line later; it costs no run (cited: ADR-0052 decision 4, "no run of its own"); and where a ceiling is stated it is the number the verdict is about. *Against*: a kernel can set its own figure by what it allocates, which adds one more path from the kernel's process into the log beside the intervals it can already shape (Limits).
- **How its absence is said**: by the absence of the member. Where a ceiling is stated, `ceiling` then says `"not_judged"` (decision 7); where none is, the result does not say why a completed run yielded no figure, as ADR-0052 decision 5 groups those cases as "it yielded no figure".
- The name carries its unit, as `resolution_ns` and `intervals_ns` do; `TimingResult.memory_bytes` is spelled the same (observation — neither binds the other).

### 7. The verdict and *no ceiling stated*: the member `ceiling`, and `bound`

- Cited: where a ceiling is stated, a result says "which of *within*, *over* and *not judged*, exactly one", and "where none is stated, that none is" (ADR-0061 decision 7); *no ceiling stated* is "an outcome distinct from *not judged*" (ADR-0061 decision 6); "**\"Not judged\" is never \"within\"**" and no part "may ... leave the three outcomes as two" (ADR-0052 decision 5); "No part of the runtime may treat a ceiling that was not judged as met" (ADR-0050 decision 5).
- **The member is `ceiling`, a string with exactly four values. It is present in every result.**
  - `"within"`, `"over"`, `"not_judged"`: the verdict of ADR-0052 decision 5 for this run and the `bound` read in ④ (cited: ADR-0061 decisions 3 and 6) — *within* when the run yielded a figure and the figure ≤ `bound`, *over* when it yielded a figure and the figure > `bound`, *not judged* when it yielded none (cited: ADR-0052 decision 5). A broken run is *not judged* (cited: ADR-0061 decision 7, which reads a broken build the same way).
  - `"not_stated"`: the `objective.constraints` the call read holds no entry of type `memory_ceiling` — under ADR-0061 decision 4 a covered member is then one with an empty `constraints`, which states "no constraint" (cited: ADR-0053 decision 5). No verdict was computed (cited: ADR-0061 decision 6).
- What follows from the cited definitions and is no further rule: `"within"` and `"over"` occur only with `memory_bytes`; with `"not_judged"` there is no `memory_bytes`; with `"not_stated"` `memory_bytes` may or may not be present.
- **Four forms were weighed.** (a) One member with four values. (b) A boolean that says whether a ceiling is stated beside a verdict of three values — states without a meaning (no ceiling and a verdict; a ceiling and none) need a rule. (c) A verdict of three values, left out when no ceiling is stated — ADR-0061 decision 6 leaves the form open and (c) maps nothing to *within*, but the absence of a member would then be an outcome: a reader could not tell a result that states no ceiling from one whose tool function failed to write the member, which a closed list (decision 9) can otherwise catch, and a reader that tests only for *over* would pass both. (d) The presence of `bound` as the sign that a ceiling is stated — the form (c) with another member. **The choice is (a)**: every result says one of the four in one place and by a value, and no two members can disagree.
- **The name.** Not `verdict`: ADR-0052 decision 5 calls the three outcomes a verdict, and `"not_stated"` is none — the reason ADR-0042 decision 2 gave against `verdict` for broken. Not `memory_ceiling`: that is the `type` value of a constraint entry (cited: ADR-0050 decision 4), and one spelling would then be a value there and a member here. `ceiling` says what the member is about; its values are the words of ADR-0052 and ADR-0061 as *names*.
- **The `bound` read: in. The member is `bound`, an *integer* — the `bound` of the `memory_ceiling` entry the tool function read in ④, in bytes** (cited for the read: ADR-0061 decision 6; for the form: ADR-0050 decision 4). **It is present exactly when `ceiling` is not `"not_stated"`**, so also in a *not judged* result. *Why*: "Whoever can write `instance.json` chooses the objective and the `bound`" (cited: ADR-0061 Limit 8, after ADR-0053 L2, "Whoever can write the file changes what the run states"), a stored result "says nothing ... about an objective or a `bound` the file states later" (cited: ADR-0062 Limit 2), and a verdict stored without its bound could not be checked against the figure from the line; the manner is that of `tolerance` in ADR-0042 decision 5. *The cut*: "the `bound` then never crosses the cut" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3) is about the cut between the tool function and the operation (the term of ADR-0021, used unchanged by ADR-0061 decision 1); a result goes from the tool function to the caller and crosses no cut, so that sentence is untouched and no operation is handed the `bound`. *ADR-0052 decision 7 (c)*: the property is that the verdict's `bound` is "taken from what the run itself has recorded of its instance and not from a value the caller of that call chooses" (cited); a `bound` in a result is a value returned to a caller, not one a caller hands, and a later call still reads its `bound` from the file in ④ and from nothing kept (cited: ADR-0061 decision 6; ADR-0062 decision 3), so the echo gives a caller no way to choose a `bound`.
- **What every result carries today**: `"ceiling": "not_stated"` and no `bound`, since "A file whose `objective.constraints` holds an entry ... is invalid today" (cited: ADR-0053 decision 4) and a valid file without the member is the tool failure `objective_not_stated` (cited: ADR-0061 decision 4). The other three values and `bound` describe results after a later admission of `memory_ceiling`, which this record does not make.

### 8. What a result never holds

- Cited and not re-decided: "anything the kernel's process wrote but the report it is parsed from — the standard error ...; the command line, which holds the seed ...; an input value ...; and, until OD-13 sub-question ⑦ is answered, the seed or anything a caller could use to pick the seed of a later run" (ADR-0061 decision 7). "The compiler's log is not returned under this record" (same decision; whether it is kept is ⑲ (다)). The point is said by the tool called (cited: ADR-0061 decision 5) and is not a member (decision 5).
- **Added by this record** (as ADR-0042 decision 6 did for the check): of what the runner and the build captured for a broken run, a result holds nothing but the condition — no exit status, no signal, no elapsed time, no byte of the report; no path; no value of a setting; and no text member (no `message`, `detail` or `note`) into which a tool function could put what the bullet above keeps out.
- **This record's reading of the cited sentence**: it binds what the tool function puts into a result. Every member here is computed by the tool function from the report, the figure, the file and the condition. A kernel shares its process with the driver, which has the seed on its command line (cited: ADR-0009 decisions 5 and 10), and can shape its intervals and its memory use, and with them `outcome`, `condition`, `intervals_ns` and `memory_bytes`. That a kernel can thereby put a value into these members is this record's own reading; what the cited records name is the forgery of the timing (ADR-0009 decision 10) and that a kernel "can evade the figure" (ADR-0052 L4). The path is not closed here and belongs to OD-16 (Limits). If the answer to OD-13 sub-question ⑦ makes the seed of a finished run something nothing may reach the log, that answer reaches these members as it reaches `counts` in ADR-0042 decision 4.

- **In which sense the list is closed**, as ADR-0042 decision 7: a tool function that follows the records up to this one returns exactly these members; **only a record adds a member**, and a record that does so amends this table. - **What a caller may rely on**: the table and the presence rules. Not that every result it is given was made under this table: a repeated call returns what the end line holds (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3).
- **No version member**, for the reasons of ADR-0042 decision 7: "The convention carries **no version member**" (cited: ADR-0010 decision 7), and the version of a line is not raised "by a change in the `data` of a kind" (cited: ADR-0006 decision 8); how a reader treats a result of another list is OD-8 sub-question ⑧ and OD-11.

### 11. Limits

1. **A record, not code; nothing was built or run.**
2. **A backend's report and figure are believed** (cited: ADR-0061 Limit 7; ADR-0016 decision 6). The members show what the operation returned and what the tool function judged from it.
3. **A result says nothing about another `execution` of the attempt or the other point** (cited: ADR-0062 Limit 2, "A stored outcome says nothing about another run of the same kernel"; for which of several counts, ADR-0062 Limit 1 and OD-18 sub-question ⑳; ADR-0052 decision 6), nor about an objective or a `bound` the file states later (cited: ADR-0062 Limit 2).
4. **The result-file half of OD-13 sub-question ⑩ is open**: nothing yet writes `sim.json` or `board.json`, and which of their members carry the figure and the verdict is PLAN P1-8's (Status).
5. **A result taken in an imprecise timing environment carries no mark**: the owner decision of 2026-10-15, item 3, and OD-13 sub-question ⑪ are untouched (cited: ADR-0061 Limit 3); such a result has the same members as any other.
6. **A kernel can set what a result reports about it** (this record's own reading): its intervals, which it can forge (cited: ADR-0009 decision 10), its figure, which it can at least evade (cited: ADR-0052 L4, "can evade the figure") and, by what it allocates, move, and so the `outcome`, the `condition` and the `ceiling`. A result is therefore a path by which a kernel can put a value it holds — the seed among them (decision 8) — into the log. Nothing here closes it (OD-16).
7. **A result does not show which backend answered**: as for the check, an outcome at the mock is told apart by the hw_profile only (the same reasoning: ADR-0038 decision 8), and the hw_profile is in the file of the run, not in the result.
8. **A result does not name the source commit**, so the `n_min`, `cv_max` and replay it was judged with are known through the instance's commit only (cited: ADR-0007 decision 8); a repeated call returns a result stored by an earlier commit unchanged (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3).
9. **Nothing checks a stored result against this table**: whoever can append a line can store a result of any content (cited for the absence of access control: ADR-0010 decision 8).
10. **Under a container** the intervals and the figure may not be those of ADR-0009 and ADR-0052 (cited: ADR-0061 Limit 2; OD-14 sub-question ① (마)); the members do not change with it.
11. The limits of ADR-0061 decision 8 and ADR-0062 decision 7 stand unchanged.

So (c) is not met, check 4 of ADR-0050 still refuses every constraint entry, and `memory_ceiling` stays not admitted; this record admits nothing.

- **A-13 reading** (in the manner of ADR-0042's, re-judged for these tools): document mode; no code, dependency, settings file or schema file changes. A result of either tool is stored in one place: as the member `result` of the `data` of a `step_ended` line of `runs/<run_id>/events.ndjson` (cited: ADR-0062 decision 2; ADR-0011 decision 3), and returned by the call and again by a repeated call answered from that line. ADR-0011 decision 3 fixed that member — "`result` is the result object of the call itself" — and its Boundaries left "the request and result members of any tool" undecided; this record fills that place for two tools. **No member of `STATE.json`, `instance.json`, a spec, `artifacts.json` or a result file of an attempt, and no member of the envelope or of the `data` of a line, is added, removed or changed** in type, form or meaning: the `data` of a `step_ended` line holds `step`, `execution`, `spend` and exactly one of `result` and `failure`, as before, and the version of a line is not raised "by a change in the `data` of a kind" (cited: ADR-0006 decision 8), which here does not even change. **The two requirements on result files were weighed and are not overridden**: ADR-0007 decision 3 is "a requirement on the result files" and names no field (cited); ADR-0006 decision 7 is a property of the line, which this record makes the line able to meet for the measurement (decision 4) without saying what a result file holds or who writes it. `sim.json` and `board.json` are other files and are not defined here; the result-file half of OD-13 sub-question ⑩ stays open (Status). **This is therefore read as no "데이터 plane 스키마 변경"**: the members inside `result` are the interface between layer 1 and layer 2 — what a tool function returns (ADR-0010 decision 2) — and no schema of a data-plane file existed for them or is changed by them. **The opposite precedent, weighed.** [ADR-0060](ADR-0060-od18-correctness-check-build-answers-and-settings.md) Context calls a fifth value of `condition` of `correctness.check` "a data-plane schema change (CLAUDE.md A-13)", and the driver put it to the owner as HQ-0020, whose question said "This grows ADR-0042's closed four-value list, stored in `events.ndjson`"; ADR-0060's A-13 reading rests on the owner's approval of those values. This record reads the two cases apart: **there, a closed member list that a landed record had fixed was changed; here, the place ADR-0011 decision 3 made and its Boundaries left empty is filled for these tools for the first time**, as ADR-0042 filled it for the check. (That no result of either tool can exist in any log yet, since neither tool can be registered, is no part of the distinction: it was then true of `correctness.check` as well.) **Owner** (O4, given in answer to that same HQ-0020, point 3): "the members of their results stay with their records." This record reads O4 as leaving the members to these records, consistent with its reading; it does not read O4 as the owner's approval of this A-13 reading. **Once this table has landed, a change of it** — a member, a value of `outcome`, `condition`, `sample_form` or `ceiling`, a presence rule — **is a change of a closed list stored in `events.ndjson`, and is read as ADR-0060 read it.** If the owner reads A-13 otherwise — that the first definition of a closed list stored in the log is already a schema change, or that members from which a later result file is to be written are part of that file's schema — that is an objection to this whole record.
