# ADR-0099: The form of a moment in the files of the data plane — every member that records a moment takes the form of ADR-0006 decision 3, `gc_at` included; `STATE.json` and `instance.json` hold none and get none (sub-question ⑨ of OD-8)

## Context

- **The open point.** "⑨ 다른 파일의 시각 표기 — ADR-0006은 `time`의 표기를 이 파일에 한해 정했다. `artifacts.json`의 `gc_at`(ADR-0005가 미정으로 둠)과 `STATE.json`·`instance.json`의 시각이 같은 표기를 쓸지는 그 스키마들(PLAN P1-8)과 GC 설계에서 정한다" (cited: OpenDecisionProposals OD-8).
- **The one form there is.** `time` is a "string of exactly 20 characters `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ`: a date that exists in the Gregorian calendar with a year from `0001` to `9999`, `hh` 00-23, `mm` 00-59, `ss` 00-59 (no leap second), in UTC"; "The form is fixed **for this file only**; whether other files of the data plane (the `gc_at` of ADR-0005, the state and instance schemas) take the same form is not decided here" (cited: ADR-0006 decision 3). Rejected there: "`time` as a number (epoch seconds), with fractions, or with a zone offset — A number is not readable in a diff or an audit; fractions and offsets give several written forms of one instant, and a precise duration is a measurement that belongs in `data`" (cited: ADR-0006 Alternatives).
- **`gc_at`.** "*string*: the time at which a GC removed the blob from the store. Absent until then"; "Its time form is not fixed here: the MVP has no GC tool, so no MVP tool writes the member, and the first time stamp form of the data plane belongs to the schemas of PLAN P1-8 or to the GC design. A version 1 validator checks that a present `gc_at` is a *string* and nothing more" (cited: ADR-0005 decision 3). Fixing the form then was rejected because "It would set the time stamp form of the data plane ahead of the event and state schemas (PLAN P1-8) for a member that no MVP tool writes" (cited: ADR-0005 Alternatives). ADR-0005 Boundaries lists "the time form of `gc_at` within a *string*" under the GC.
- **The other files.** "The times a file of a run holds today are moments, not amounts, and none of them is a start of the instance"; "ADR-0012 defined no time member of `STATE.json`" (cited: ADR-0033 Context). No record of `instance.json` defines a time member (ADR-0018 and the later instance records; ADR-0033 Context). The time part of a `run_id` "is the reading of a clock at the moment of the build and is **no record of when the run started**" (cited: ADR-0027 decision 3).

## Decision

Terms: a **file of the data plane** is a file of the `HIL-Claw-data` tree (ADR-0004) whose content an accepted record defines. A **moment member** is a member of such a file, at any depth of its JSON content, the `data` of a line of `events.ndjson` included, whose value states a point in calendar time, whether read from a clock or given by a user. An **amount** is a length of time (a duration, a budget, a time limit).

### 1. What the code holds today (observation, binds nothing)

- `hilclaw/events.py` checks `time` with `_TIME`, the pattern `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ`, and `_is_time`, which accepts a match only if it is an existing date and time; `append` writes the reading of `datetime.now(UTC)` with its offset removed, as `isoformat(timespec="seconds") + "Z"`, and validates the line before writing. It checks and writes exactly the form of ADR-0006 decision 3.
- `hilclaw/artifacts.py` checks a present `gc_at` as `_STRING`, 1 to 255 printable ASCII characters — "A ``gc_at`` is checked to be a string of ADR-0001 and nothing more" (cited: `validate`) — and "never writes ``gc_at`` (the MVP has no GC tool)" (cited: its module docstring); a `gc_at` that a file holds is written back unchanged.
- A search of `hilclaw/` for clock readings (`datetime`, `time.time`, `monotonic`, `strftime`, `isoformat`) finds, besides `events.py`: `run_id.py`, which reads the UTC clock for the time part of an id (decision 5); `clang_build.py` and `local_run.py`, which read a monotonic clock for a time limit and a `duration_s` (amounts); and `driver_state.py`, which reads the times of `PROCESS/STATE.json`, a file of the development driver and not of the data plane. **No other module of `hilclaw/` writes a moment into a file of the data plane.**

### 2. One form for the data plane

- **Every moment member of every file of the data plane takes the form of ADR-0006 decision 3**: a string of exactly 20 characters `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ`, an existing Gregorian date with a year from `0001` to `9999`, `hh` 00-23, `mm` 00-59, `ss` 00-59 (no leap second), in UTC. A value is of the form exactly when `time` would accept it.
- This holds for the moment members that exist — `time` and `gc_at` — and for every moment member a later record defines, in any file: `STATE.json`, `instance.json`, a result file, a key record file, the `data` of an event kind. A later record that needs another form for a member (a finer resolution, a date alone) does not choose it for that member: it revisits this record.
- ADR-0006 is not changed: its form stays what it is, and its sentence "for this file only" stays true of what ADR-0006 decided. This record extends the form; it does not move it.
- **Why one form** (this record's own): the history of the data repository is the audit record (NFR-2), and it is read across files — "언제 지웠나" (#5 A-2) is answered by a `gc_at` that an auditor sets beside the `time` of the run's lines. With one form, one moment has one written form in every file, two values of the form compare as strings in the order of their moments (fixed width, UTC, the most significant part first), and one check serves every file. ADR-0006's reasons against a number, fractions and an offset are not reasons of a log: they hold for any file read in a diff or an audit.

### 3. `gc_at`

- **`gc_at` takes the form of decision 2.** Its type stays the *string* of ADR-0001 (20 printable ASCII characters lie within it); its meaning stays ADR-0005's, "the time at which a GC removed the blob from the store"; the allowed changes of ADR-0005 decision 7 stay as they are. The version is not raised (ADR-0005 decision 9, quoted in Status).
- **What stays with the GC design** (ADR-0005 Boundaries): everything the GC does — the tool, when it runs, which reading of the clock in its work becomes the value, what happens to a `gc_at` when the content is stored again. Only how the value is written leaves it.
- **Why now** (this record's own): ADR-0005's reason against fixing the form was that it would come "ahead of the event and state schemas"; the event schema has since fixed one (ADR-0006), and the state and instance schemas hold no moment (decision 4), so the form is no longer ahead of anything. The other half of that reason — no MVP tool writes the member — still holds and is why the consequence is a narrower check and no writer (Consequences). Left to the GC design, the form would be chosen again for one member against a rule that every other moment of the data plane follows, and a version 1 validator would go on accepting any *string* for it.

### 4. `STATE.json` and `instance.json`

- **Neither file holds a moment member today** (ADR-0012; ADR-0018 and the later instance records; ADR-0033 Context), and **this record adds none and reserves no name**.
- When a later record defines a moment member of either file — a start time of an instance, for example, which no record defines (ADR-0027 decision 3, ADR-0033) — the member takes the form of decision 2, and whether it raises the version is that file's own rule (ADR-0012 decision 2, ADR-0018 decision 2).

### 5. What is not a moment member

- **The time part of a `run_id`** is part of a name, not a moment member: "An id is a name … no fact of a run is read from its id" (cited: ADR-0027 decision 3). Its form `YYYYMMDDthhmmssz` is not changed. The two forms differ by design: the id part is the basic notation of ISO 8601 in lower case, the letters lower case "because the pattern has no upper-case letter" (cited: ADR-0027 decision 2, citing ADR-0004 decision 8), and nothing parses it.
- **Amounts are not moments** and are not covered: the wall-clock budget of ADR-0033 (an amount, for which ADR-0033 rejected a duration notation), a time limit, a `duration_s`, a measured time of a kernel.
- Also not covered: text that is no member defined by a record (the content of a blob, a compiler log file), the times git keeps for a commit, and files outside the data plane (`PROCESS/STATE.json` of the development driver, the source repository).

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| (a) ADR-0006's form for every moment member of the data plane, `gc_at` included | **Adopted** (decisions 2 and 3). Its cost: one code task that narrows the check of `gc_at` for a member no tool writes (Consequences). |
| (b) The same rule, but `gc_at` left to the GC design | The one member that exists outside the log would be the one exception to the rule; ADR-0005's reason for leaving it no longer holds (decision 3), and the GC design loses nothing by the form being fixed. A version 1 validator would accept any *string* until a GC is built, which the owner put after the MVP (#5 A-2). |
| (c) A form chosen per file when its first moment member is defined | Each record would choose again; two files of one run could write one moment two ways, and the comparison across files that the audit needs (decision 2) would need a conversion per pair of files. `STATE.json` and `instance.json` would each start with a choice, not a rule. |
| (d) Another form for files that are not logs: fractions of a second, a zone offset, or a number (epoch seconds) | ADR-0006's reasons hold for any audited file: a number is not readable in a diff, fractions and offsets give one instant several written forms. No moment outside the log needs a finer resolution today; a precise duration is an amount (decision 5). |
| (e) Leave ⑨ open until a member exists | The only existing member outside the log, `gc_at`, would stay without a form, and each later record would decide its own (c) unrecorded. The answer needs no GC, no clock and no new member. |
| (f) The basic form of the `run_id` (`YYYYMMDDthhmmssz`) for the other files | It was chosen for a name inside a path (ADR-0027 decision 2), not for a value; the extended form is the one the log already holds, and a second form beside it would bring back (c)'s conversion. |

## Consequences

- **`hilclaw/events.py` stays right**: it checks and writes exactly the form (decision 1).
- **One code follow-up task**: narrow the check of a present `gc_at` in `hilclaw/artifacts.py` `validate` to the form of decision 2 (the check `time` gets in `hilclaw/events.py`), with tests. The module still never writes `gc_at`. Of `tests/test_artifacts.py`, the existing tests that hold a `gc_at` not of the form are edited: `EXAMPLE` (used by `test_validate_accepts_the_example_of_adr_0005` and `test_the_writer_keeps_the_layout_of_the_adr_example`; its `gc_at` is ADR-0005's placeholder `TIME-FORM-NOT-DECIDED-HERE`, which is replaced by a value of the form, the layout of ADR-0005's Example kept), the `gc_at` cases of `test_validate_accepts`, the `SYNTHETIC-TIME` values of `test_a_file_in_another_valid_form_is_read_and_normalised_by_the_next_write`, `test_set_retention_class_on_a_record_with_gc_at` and `test_add_record_requires_the_blob_also_for_a_recorded_hash`. Beside them, `BAD_GC_AT` (used by `test_validate_rejects`) gains values that are strings of ADR-0001 but not of the form, ADR-0005's placeholder among them. No other test is edited. Nothing is written here. Until that task lands, `hilclaw/artifacts.py` accepts a `gc_at` this record does not allow; since no tool writes one (decision 1), nothing written by a tool differs.
- **A-13 reading**: document mode; no code, dependency, settings or schema file changes. The narrowing of the follow-up is the change this record decides under §9, within the reservation of ADR-0005 decision 9, with no version raise.
- **Not edited and now less exact** (each leaves the form open or names ⑨ as open): ADR-0005 decision 3 ("Its time form is not fixed here"; "checks that a present `gc_at` is a *string* and nothing more"), its Alternatives row on a fixed form, its Boundaries ("the time form of `gc_at` within a *string*" under the GC) and its Example (`TIME-FORM-NOT-DECIDED-HERE` is not of the form); ADR-0006 decision 3 ("is not decided here") and its Boundaries; [ADR-0012](ADR-0012-od8-runtime-state-file.md) and [ADR-0018](ADR-0018-od8-instance-record-hw-profile.md) Boundaries; ADR-0027 decision 3 ("sub-question ⑨, untouched") and Boundaries; ADR-0033 Context, decision 3, Limits, Alternatives and Boundaries; the Boundaries of [ADR-0023](ADR-0023-od8-instance-record-n-max.md), [ADR-0028](ADR-0028-od8-instance-record-candidates-per-round.md), [ADR-0031](ADR-0031-od8-instance-record-op-signature.md) and [ADR-0032](ADR-0032-od8-instance-record-round-budget.md) ("any time member (OD-8 sub-question ⑨)"); [ADR-0097](ADR-0097-od8-event-payload-change.md) Status and Boundaries and ADR-0098 Status and Boundaries. ## Boundaries (not decided here)

- **The GC** (C-3, #5 A-2; owner decisions stand as they are): the tool, when it runs, the grace period, what "run end" means for it, a `gc_at` after the content is stored again, a hash recorded with several classes, and which reading of the clock in its work becomes `gc_at`.
- Retention classes and their policy (OD-20, OD-29, OD-23); data governance (OD-25, owner-only).
- **Any new moment member of any file**, a start time of an instance included (ADR-0027 decision 3 and ADR-0033 stay as they are; sub-question ㉚), and the names such members take.
- **Clocks and their correctness**: which clock a writer reads, a clock that is set back or differs between machines (ADR-0006 decision 4 already says `time` may decrease), and the clock of the wall-clock budget (sub-question ㉚ (가)).
- Sub-questions ⑥ and ⑦ (the vocabulary of `type`; candidate and attempt in a blob record) and ㉟ of OD-8.
- Reading files of older versions and migration (OD-11).
- Code, tests, a JSON Schema file, CI, any settings file, and any change to the `HIL-Claw-data` repository.
