## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a record and its decision, or a requirement, is named with it; it then says what that source says and adds nothing. Every other sentence is **this record's own** — among them the reading of S1 to S10 for the timing build, the eight kinds as kinds of this contract, what the tool function does with a raised tool failure, `operation_failure_invalid` as a kind of this contract, the stored kinds, the message rule, and every weighing.

### 1. Scope and terms

- **A call of `sim.run` or `board.run` about one attempt of a run of ADR-0061 decision 1** — a run whose hw_profile names `cpu_standin` in the local configuration, or names the mock (cited) — **whose operation, called at ⑥, raised a tool failure**, or met one of the situations of decision 2. Both tools follow one contract (cited: ADR-0061 decision 1), and every point is stated once for both. The isolated environment is not read (decision 8).
- **Not in scope**, as the landed records have it: an answer of neither form and a raised error that is no tool failure (`operation_answer_invalid`, cited: ADR-0065 decisions 6 and 7); a raised request to end and the death of the process, after which the identity is open (cited: ADR-0065 decisions 6 and 7); S7 and S8 and the four conditions of (B) (cited: ADR-0065 decision 4; ADR-0063 decision 3); the write of a handed log and `build_log_unwritable` (cited: ADR-0108 decision 7), which decision 4 only places beside this record's rule.
- **A backend kind** (the term of ADR-0047 decision 1, taken for these tools): a tool-failure kind that a record of the run tools' contract names as one a backend may raise. **A kind outside the contract**: every other kind a raised tool failure has.
- It fixes **six points and nothing else**: the situations (decision 2), their kinds (decision 3), what the tool function does with a raised tool failure (decision 4), which kinds a backend may raise (decision 5), the messages (decision 6), and the moments (decision 7). Decision 8 says what of (나) stays open.

### 3. Their kinds: the eight backend kinds of `correctness.check`, defined again by the run tools' contract

- **S1 to S6, S9 and S10 are tool failures of the backend kinds `no_kernel_source`, `kernel_source_unreadable`, `build_directory_unusable`, `settings_refused`, `build_input_missing`, `compiler_unavailable`, `build_output_missing` and `executable_unstartable`, in that order of the rows.** Each is a kind of the run tools' contract with the spelling and the meaning of the kind of `correctness.check` for the same row (ADR-0056 decision 6; ADR-0060 decision 4), in the manner ADR-0064 decision 4 took `seed_not_drawn`, ADR-0067 decisions 2 and 3 the two op_signature kinds and ADR-0108 decision 7 `build_log_unwritable`: "no record forbids two contracts from defining the same value with the same meaning; a value is reserved only by a record of the frame" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 4). They are **the first backend kinds of the run tools' contract**.
- **What each kind means for these tools.** The situations of ADR-0056 decision 5 and ADR-0060 decision 4 hold with the readings of decision 2. **The sentence a caller may rely on for each of the eight is this record's own**: *in the call of this identity the operation met the situation of the row while it tried to have or start the timing executable; no result was made, and the identity is ended.* For `build_input_missing` the counterpart of "the verification program is missing" is "the driver program is missing"; for `build_output_missing` and `executable_unstartable` the executable is the timing executable; for `settings_refused` the setting is one of the timing build or the timing run. A kind says which row, not which tool: the `step` of the end line says whose call it was (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3).
- **Whether a timing run was started**, as the rows are read in decision 2 (this record's reading, not part of the sentences): under every kind but `settings_refused` no process of a timing run was started; under `settings_refused` one was started and killed in one case of S4, the run's time limit the platform cannot wait for (cited for the moment: ADR-0060 decision 4; observation: the `OverflowError` of `local_run.run`), and in every other case none was. **This record does not sort the kinds for a comparison**, so ADR-0140 decision 3 ("Every `failure` refuses, whatever its kind") stands and is not revisited; which kinds lie before any timed run and could be ignored there stays open under ⑲ (나) (decision 8).
- **Four answers were weighed.**
  - **(a) The eight kinds, defined again with their spelling and meaning.** One word for one situation of the same reader of ADR-0055 and the same two functions (`clang_build.build`, `local_run.run`) at two contracts; a caller that knows the check knows these.
  - **(b) Kinds of this contract with other spellings** (for example `timing_build_input_missing`): no landed record says anything different of the eight situations at the two contracts, and two names for one situation would have a caller learn which tool answered before reading the kind (the reasoning of ADR-0064 decision 4, cited for the manner).
  - **(c) One general kind** (for example `no_timing_executable`): it is the "one general kind" ADR-0047 decision 5 declined and ADR-0060 decision 4 weighed (cited); a caller could not tell eight rows apart by the kind: ADR-0056 decision 5 "keeps the six apart by what failed and when" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4, of S3 to S6, S9 and S10), and ADR-0056 decision 6 split S1 from S2 because "What the reader cannot see is a failure, not an answer" (cited there from ADR-0055 R8).
  - **(d) Kinds the frame reserves**: the situations now belong to three tools of two contracts, so the test of ADR-0015 decision 2 as ADR-0048 decision 5 applied it — "the situation belongs to one tool" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 4, which found that it no longer carries the choice for the seed draw) — no longer carries it here either; what remains is that `profiler.collect` has no contract, so a reserved kind would be a rule of the frame for a tool whose contract does not exist (the reasoning of ADR-0041 decision 4, as ADR-0064 decision 4 applies it). **This part of the choice is provisional**, and a record of the frame that reserves these situations takes other names or amends ADR-0056, ADR-0060 and this record (cited for the cost: ADR-0016 decision 3).
- **The choice is (a).**
- **The names**, checked on 2026-10-26 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`: the eight occur as the backend kinds of `correctness.check` (`hilclaw/check_failure.py`, five of them also in `hilclaw/check_build.py`, the check's tests, `compiler_unavailable` also as test data of `checks.read`, `verdicts.write` and the spec feedback, and the records that name them), and `no_kernel_source` also inside the names of test functions of the harness, of the kernel-source reader and of `hilclaw/standin_copies.py`; they are taken **on purpose**. None equals a reserved kind (`hilclaw.tools.RESERVED_KINDS`), another kind of the run tools' contract (`objective_not_stated`, `objective_not_covered`, `seed_not_drawn`, `operation_answer_invalid`, `op_signature_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_covered`, `n_max_not_stated`, `build_log_unwritable`), a `step` value ([StepValues](../StepValues.md)), a value of `condition` (the seven of ADR-0063 decision 3) or a member name.

### 4. What the tool function does with a tool failure an operation raises

- **After an operation raised a tool failure, the tool function ends the step without an outcome: it appends the end line of the identity, and that line holds a `failure`. This holds whatever the kind of the raised tool failure is.** The identity is then ended. It is ADR-0047 decision 3 taken for these tools, for its reason, which holds here: both tools are journaled and the start line is complete before ⑥ (cited: ADR-0062 decisions 2 and 6), and after a raised tool failure the tool function knows that the call of the operation is over and that no outcome exists — "the step is over without an outcome" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4, as ADR-0047 decision 3 quotes it). The cost is that of ADR-0047 decision 3 and ADR-0065 decision 7: the identity is used up, and a further timing run of that attempt at that point is a further `execution`, "a new measurement" (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3).
- **Where the operation handed over a log before it raised** (it can, for example in S9, in S10 and in S4 after the build; cited for when a log exists: ADR-0108 decision 2), **ADR-0108 decision 7 comes first** (cited): the tool function writes the file; if the write fails, "the step ends with a `failure` of the kind `build_log_unwritable`, in place of whatever the operation returned or raised". **The rules below apply only where no log was handed over, or its write succeeded.** This takes in the case ADR-0108 Consequences asked the record of ⑲ (나) to take in.
- **Where the raised kind is a backend kind, the `failure` holds that kind and the message that was raised.** "A backend kind raised for a situation it does not mean is taken as it is" (cited: ADR-0047 Limits).
- **Where the raised kind is a kind outside the contract, the `failure` holds `operation_failure_invalid`** — a kind of the run tools' contract with the spelling and the meaning of ADR-0047's, shared in the manner of ADR-0064 decision 4 and ADR-0065 decision 7. **The kinds outside the contract** are: the reserved kinds; every kind of the run tools' contract that is no backend kind — the six of ADR-0065 decision 8 and ADR-0067 decision 6, `operation_answer_invalid`, `build_log_unwritable` (which "Only the tool function raises" — cited: ADR-0108 decision 7) and `operation_failure_invalid` itself; and every other *name*. **So no end line of either tool holds one of the six kinds**, as ADR-0065 decision 8 and ADR-0067 decision 6 bind (cited), and none holds a reserved kind for a situation it does not mean (cited for the rule: ADR-0015 decision 2, "no tool function or step raises one for another situation").
- **The sentence a caller may rely on for `operation_failure_invalid`**: *in the call of this identity the operation was called and its call is over; it raised a tool failure whose kind the contract, as the source of that call has it, does not let a backend raise. No result was made. The answer is what a complete end line of the identity holds: the identity is ended.* It says nothing about the attempt or about why.
- **Four answers for a kind outside the contract were weighed.** (a) `operation_failure_invalid`, defined again with its spelling and meaning. (b) Another spelling — the reasoning of ADR-0064 decision 4 (cited), as in decision 3 (b). (c) `operation_answer_invalid` — its sentence says that what the operation raised "was no tool failure" (cited: ADR-0065 decision 7), which is false of the line, and ADR-0065 decision 6 does not mean the case. (d) The raised kind as it is — a reserved kind or one of the six would stand on an end line for another situation, against ADR-0015 decision 2 and ADR-0065 decision 8, `build_log_unwritable` would claim a write that was not tried, and any other name would put a kind no record of this contract gives a meaning into the log for good (cited: ADR-0006 decision 5). A kind of the frame was weighed as in decision 3 (d) and falls the same way. **The choice is (a).**
- **Only the tool function raises `operation_failure_invalid`**, and only as the `failure` of an end line; an operation that raises it raises a kind outside the contract and is answered by this rule, with this kind. **The tool function raises none of the eight backend kinds itself.**
- **`tool_crashed` stays.** ADR-0019 decision 4 keeps it for "such an exception out of the step, which leaves the step open" (cited), read as ADR-0065 decision 7 read it: the tool function does not let a raised tool failure of an operation come out of the step. The sentence stands for every exception that does come out, a defect in the tool function's own part of ⑥ included.
- **The stored kinds** (the term of ADR-0101 decision 2, cited). With this record the eight backend kinds and `operation_failure_invalid` become stored kinds of `sim_run_timing` and `board_run_timing`, their meanings fixed by its acceptance and never changed (cited: ADR-0101 decision 3). With `operation_answer_invalid` (ADR-0065 decision 7) and `build_log_unwritable` (ADR-0108 decision 7) these values have eleven, the same eleven names as the check's (ADR-0101 decision 2). Every other kind of the run tools' contract is "a kind answered without an end line" (cited: ADR-0101 decision 2). No `step` value changes.
- **The name check** for `operation_failure_invalid` (2026-10-26, the three trees): it occurs as the kind of `correctness.check` (`hilclaw/check_failure.py`, `hilclaw/check_operation.py`, their tests and test data of `verdicts.write`) and in passages that cite it, and is taken on purpose; it equals no reserved kind, no other kind of the run tools' contract, no `step` value, no value of `condition` and no member name.

### 5. Which kinds a backend may raise; the mock

- **Any backend may raise the eight kinds of decision 3, a script of the mock included**; no other kind is a backend kind of this contract. The list is the records', "the tool function of a source commit has the list of that commit" (cited: ADR-0047 decision 5).
- **For the mock.** A script must be able to express "each tool-failure kind the contract lets a backend raise" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (d)), so a script of a test can raise each of the eight; **this answers the sentence of ADR-0066 decision 5** that leaves them unnamed. A script is asked with the log keeper and may hand a log over (cited: ADR-0108 decision 8), so a script of a test can also hand a log and then raise. **The default script still raises no tool failure and hands no log** (cited: ADR-0066 decision 5, "the default script raises none"; ADR-0108 decision 8); nothing here changes what it answers.
- **What follows for a test of the tool function**: a script that raises a backend kind gives the end line with that kind and message; one that raises any other kind gives the end line with `operation_failure_invalid`; one that handed a log whose write fails gives `build_log_unwritable` whatever it raised (cited: ADR-0108 decision 7); when the append of the end line fails, the call is `end_line_failed` (cited: ADR-0026 decision 3).

### 6. The messages

- **The message of a backend kind** follows ADR-0056 decision 6 and ADR-0060 decision 5, taken here: it may say which case of its row it was (for S4 which setting; for S5 a kernel file or the driver program; for S10 not found, not executable or refused) and of which kind the error was, as the code names kinds of values. **It shows no path, no name of an entry, no byte of a file, no text of an error, no value of a setting, nothing of the compiler's log, and nothing from which the seed could be recovered** — the operation is handed the seed (cited: ADR-0064 decision 7), "The seed travels as a command-line argument" (cited: ADR-0009 decision 5), and the seed is kept nowhere by the tool layer (cited: ADR-0064 decision 5); the text of an error of a timing run can show a value it was handed (observation: `TimingArgumentError` shows the value it refuses). This is a rule for the code of an operation that raises; "a caller tells the cases apart by the kind and never by the message" (cited: ADR-0026 decision 6), and a message that breaks it is in the log for good and is not detected (cited for the manner: ADR-0060 decision 5).
- **The message of `operation_failure_invalid`** follows ADR-0047 decision 4, taken here: it says that the operation raised a tool failure of a kind the contract does not let a backend raise; it may say which class the kind was of, and name it where it is a reserved kind or a kind of this contract (the name is then one of a list the records fix); it carries nothing of the raised message and does not show any other raised kind. The wording is the code's.

### 9. Limits

- **The list of decision 2 is read against the host code**, not against a container (decision 8), and not tried: what a real compiler, a real timing run or a container produces was not observed.
- **A backend kind raised for a situation it does not mean is taken as it is**, and so is its message (cited: ADR-0047 Limits; ADR-0016 decision 6, "is not detected by the frame").
- **The kind says nothing about whose defect it was.** S5, S9 and S10 can follow from the attempt (decision 2), S4 from the backend's settings and S6 from the machine; nothing in the answer tells a cause that would not occur again from a lasting one, and whoever wants the timing run made again needs a further `execution`, whose asking stays with OD-10 and the retry part of OD-17.
- **The list is that of the source that made the call** (cited: ADR-0047 Limits): a line written with `operation_failure_invalid` stays as it is when a later record names the raised kind, and "`operation_failure_invalid` on an earlier line does not say which list of backend kinds its source had" (cited: ADR-0101 Consequences).
- **A defect of the tool function is not told from the backend's by the answer** (cited for the manner: ADR-0047 Limits; ADR-0065 Limits).
- The limits of ADR-0047, ADR-0056, ADR-0060, ADR-0061, ADR-0065 and ADR-0108 stand unchanged.
