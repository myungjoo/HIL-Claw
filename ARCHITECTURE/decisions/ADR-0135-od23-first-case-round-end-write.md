## Context

- **The open point** (cited): "The write that ends a round sets `round` to the next round and `budget_spent` to the view of the log folded to a position the log has reached. Who folds, and how the result reaches the writer, is not decided here (OD-23)" (ADR-0012 decision 6); "Which function folds, and how the result reaches the Round-Manager as the only writer of `STATE.json`, is PLAN P1-8 and OD-23" (ADR-0011 decision 6); "a tool for the fold is not added here" (ADR-0124 Boundaries).
- **The rules the write must keep** (cited): *Replay* — the current view is the stored view folded over the tail, the complete lines after the position; *Bound* — what is interpreted grows with one round, not with the run; *Disagreement* — the log is authoritative, a fold from the first line "is always allowed and is never required", a position beyond the log is an error of the kind of a damaged line, and an end line whose identity is not open "is counted and reported" (ADR-0011 decision 6). A torn tail is no line and the reader skips it; a damaged complete line is an error the reader reports and does not repair (ADR-0006 decision 6). In the write that ends the run's last round `run_status` is `"over"`, and the run is over when `round` ≥ `round_budget` of the `instance.json` read then (ADR-0121 decisions 3, 4). The replacement writes exactly the content it is given (ADR-0013 decision 7).
- **The order around the write** (cited): in a round ≥ 1, `case.write` right before it (ADR-0133 decision 5), round 0 having no case (ADR-0133 decision 1); `run.commit` right after it (ADR-0127 decision 3). The session ends after it (ADR-0118 decision 2 (i)).
- **What exists** (code, as observation only): `hilclaw.run_journal.current` reads `STATE.json` before the log and returns the stored view folded over the tail, the fold's reports and the torn tail's length, and raises for a missing or invalid file, another version, a damaged line and a position beyond the log; `hilclaw.journal.fold` returns a `SpendView(totals, position, open_steps)`; `hilclaw.events.read` skips a torn tail and `remove_torn_tail` removes it; `hilclaw.run_state_file.replace(data_root, run_id, round, view, others)` writes by ADR-0013; `state.write` of `hilclaw/run_file_tools.py` writes exactly the `state` it is given; `run.commit` removes a torn tail before an owed commit. **Today the Round-Manager has only `state.write`**, so it would itself produce the totals, the position, the open steps and the next round.

## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a source is named with it. Every other sentence is **this record's own**.

### 1. Scope

- **The write that ends a round of a run of ADR-0120 decision 1**, given that the Round-Manager has reached it. **What ends a round**, and when a round's work is done (bring-up, baseline, the coder call, the measurement, the verdicts), is not decided here. **Every end of a run other than `round_budget`** stays open as ADR-0121 decision 4 lists them. The first `STATE.json` (ADR-0120 decision 2) and ADR-0121 decision 3's own `"over"` write, which end no round, keep `state.write`.

### 2. (a) Who folds, and how the result reaches the write: the tool `round.end`

- **The Round-Manager makes the write that ends its round only by calling `round.end` through the package's MCP front**, naming its run by `run_id` and the round by `round`. The tool folds, compares and replaces `STATE.json` below the front; **the model supplies no total, position, open step, next round or run status**. The Round-Manager makes this write by no other means, `state.write`, Bash and its own file tools included.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Name (MCP name) | `round.end` (`mcp__hilclaw__round_end`); no `_` in the name, so the mapping of ADR-0010 decision 4 stays reversible |
| Request | Exactly `run_id` and `round`, both required, with the meaning and form of ADR-0015 decision 4; `round` from 0 to 2^53 − 2, so that the next round is an *integer* of ADR-0001. Any other member, one missing, or `round` out of that range is `invalid_request` |
| Result | `{"round": k + 1, "over": true or false, "reported_lines": [...]}`: the round written, whether `run_status` `"over"` was written, and the line numbers (from 1, ascending, possibly none) of the lines the fold reports (ADR-0011 decision 6, *Disagreement*) |
| Checks, in order | ① the request (`invalid_request`, ADR-0015 decision 2); ② the data root (`no_data_root`); ③ the run's `STATE.json` read and parsed (`run_file_missing`, `run_file_invalid`, `run_file_unsupported`, `run_file_unreadable`), its `round` equal to the request's (`round_not_current`), and no top-level member beside `schema_version`, `round` and `budget_spent` (`state_member_extra`, `run_status` of any value included); ④ the tail of `events.ndjson` after the stored position, read and folded into the stored view (decision 3), with the `run_file_*` kinds as ADR-0019 decision 3's table maps the log's failures (`run_file_invalid` for a damaged complete line, a step line that breaks ADR-0011 or a position beyond the log, `run_file_unsupported` for a line of another version, `run_file_unreadable` for any other failing read); ⑤ the run's `instance.json` read and parsed (the four `run_file_*` kinds). At ③ to ⑤ a data root gone since ② is `run_file_missing`, as ADR-0019 decision 4 answers it; ⑥ the replacement of decision 4 by `run_state_file.replace` (`state_not_written` for a failing step of the file system, a data root gone since ② among them) |

- **Nothing is written before ⑥**, and ⑥ is the only write. Reading is the tool's step, as ADR-0133 decision 3 has it for `case.write`.
- **③ makes the call happen once per round.** The request names the round the Round-Manager is ending; after a call that wrote, `STATE.json` names the next round, so a second call for the same round — a repeat after a result that did not arrive included — is refused and ends no second round. It also holds the order of ADR-0133 decision 5 against a `case.write` placed after it: that call is then refused by its own ③ (cited: ADR-0133 decision 3).
- **Kinds.** Reused with their meanings: `invalid_request`, `no_data_root`; the four `run_file_*` kinds of ADR-0019 decision 3 for `STATE.json`, the log and `instance.json`, with the mapping of its table (a damaged line, a step line that breaks ADR-0011 and a position beyond the log are `run_file_invalid`) — **this record widens ADR-0019 decision 2's "only at the lookup or the selection" to these reads of `round.end`**, as ADR-0124 decision 3 and ADR-0133 decision 3 did; the property holds: such a call appended no line, selected no backend, ran no step and consumed nothing; `round_not_current` of `case.write` (the request's round is not `STATE.json`'s); `state_not_written` of `state.write` (cited: ADR-0124 decision 3). **New**: `state_member_extra`, a *name* of ADR-0001 decision 1, because no reused kind says that a valid file holds a member this write would have to drop or carry (decision 4). **Searched on 2026-10-20** in `hilclaw/`, `tests/`, `orchestration/` and `ARCHITECTURE/` for `round.end`, `round_end`, `mcp__hilclaw__round_end` and `state_member_extra`: none occurs as a name; `round_end` occurs only inside two longer test function names of `tests/test_run_commit.py`.
- **Not journaled**: it costs no limited resource, its outcome is the file (as ADR-0124 decision 3 reads `state.write`), and a journaled call would append its own start line to the log it folds, an open step in the view it writes. **Not an FR-10 tool**: it runs no experiment and selects no backend; a tool of the registry beside them, added to `default_registry` in the source and in no other way (cited: #5 A-12 point 3).
- **Left to the code slice**: the module, the description and input schema, the message texts, and the conversion of a total above `keys.MAX_INTEGER` (ADR-0012 decision 4 allows it).

### 3. (b) Which view is stored

- **The stored view folded over the tail** (cited: ADR-0011 decision 6, *Replay*), **to the end of the log's complete lines at the read of ④**: `position` is the stored position plus the number of complete lines after it. That is a position the log has reached (ADR-0012 decision 6). A line appended after the read is in the next round's tail and is counted there, so no amount is lost. Lines before the position are not interpreted (*Bound*).
- **A torn tail** is no line (cited: ADR-0006 decision 6): it is not folded, it is not counted in `position`, and `round.end` does not remove it. `run.commit`, called right after, removes it before the commit (cited: ADR-0127 decision 4), and so does the next append.
- **A damaged complete line after the position, a line of another version, a step line that breaks ADR-0011, or a position beyond the log**: the call fails (decision 2 ④), nothing is written and nothing is repaired; what was committed is recovered from the history (cited: ADR-0006 decision 6; ADR-0011 decision 6).
- **Open steps** at the end of a round are written as the fold lists them; the write is not refused for them. Their recovery and how an unknown amount weighs against a budget stay OD-10 and OD-19 (cited: ADR-0011 decision 7).
- **Reported lines** — an end line whose identity is not open (cited: ADR-0011 decision 6) and a repeated start line (observation: the `fold` docstring of `hilclaw/journal.py`) — are counted as the fold counts them; the write is made, and the Round-Manager reports a non-empty `reported_lines` in one line and goes on to `run.commit`.
- **The disagreement between the stored view and a fold from the first line** (OD-17 sub-question ③) is answered only as far as ADR-0011 decision 6 does: such a fold is never required at the entry of a turn, and `round.end` makes none and compares none. When and by whom it is made stays open.

### 4. (c) What the one write holds

- **Exactly** `schema_version` `1`, `round` *k* + 1 (cited: ADR-0012 decisions 2 and 3), `budget_spent` the view of decision 3, and — **when `instance.json` states `round_budget` and *k* + 1 ≥ `round_budget`** — `run_status` `"over"` (cited: ADR-0121 decisions 3 and 4). Nothing else.
- **The tool makes the comparison**, with the `round` it is writing and the `round_budget` it read at ⑤. This record reads ADR-0121 decision 4's "the Round-Manager compares in the write that ends a round" as met by the Round-Manager's tool; the comparison at the start of a turn (ADR-0121 decision 3) stays the Round-Manager's, as its definition has it today.
- **No `round_budget` stated** (a valid `instance.json` may lack it, cited: ADR-0032 decision 4): the write holds no `run_status` and `over` is false. An absent member does not say that the run is over (cited: ADR-0121 decision 2). What a run without a round budget does then is OD-8 ㉙ (나) and is not answered; the definition's entry today reports such a run and ends (observation).
- **A top-level member the record does not define** is never dropped or carried by this write: ③ refuses a `STATE.json` that holds one (`state_member_extra`), and nothing is written. The file stays valid, and the refusal is no `run_file_*` situation (cited: ADR-0019 decision 1, "a top-level member a reader does not know" is not one); it is a rule of this one writer, which must give `replace` a whole content (cited: ADR-0013 decision 7). **This record's reading** is that the refusal leaves OD-8 ⑫ open: it neither keeps nor drops, so no member loses a value or keeps a stale one, and a member's own record or OD-11 still chooses. The definition's entry already refuses ADR-0121's own write on the same ground (observation: `orchestration/round-manager.md`, entry step 6). `run_status` already present is refused as well — a run that is over is not written again (cited: ADR-0121 decision 3). A later record that defines a member written inside a round says what this write does with it.
- **No member or value is added to the data plane**: the content is a version-1 file of ADR-0012 with ADR-0121's member.

### 5. (d) What the Round-Manager does when the write fails

- **With any kind of decision 2, the Round-Manager reports the kind in one line and ends the session**; the call wrote nothing. Any other failure of the call is handled the same way. It does not call `round.end` again in this turn, does not write `STATE.json` another way and does not call `run.commit`.
- **With every kind but `round_not_current`**, the round is not ended: `STATE.json` names it still (cited: ADR-0012 decision 3). The next turn's entry calls `run.commit`, which finds nothing owed (cited: ADR-0127 decisions 4, 5), and then goes on with the round (cited: ADR-0118 decision 2 (ii)) — unless its entry steps end it first, as they do for a `run_status` that made ③ refuse (cited: ADR-0121 decisions 2, 3).
- **`round_not_current`** says that `STATE.json` no longer names the round this turn is ending — a repeated call after one that wrote, or another writer (rest of OD-17). A round end may then be owed, and the next turn's entry makes that commit (cited: ADR-0127 decision 5). A call that wrote and whose result did not reach the Round-Manager is the same case seen from the next turn: its entry commits, and `state.read` names the next round.
- **Why not go on, as after a refused `case.write`.** ADR-0133 decision 4 rejected ending the session on a refusal, because the round could still end without its case. Here there is nothing after the write but `run.commit`, which has nothing to commit, so ending the session is the only step left; what the next turn does in the round it goes on with stays OD-17's, as ADR-0133 decision 4 said.
- **Calling again within the turn** after a passing failure is a per-tool retry policy, OD-17's, and is not made here. **Whether the end of such a turn commits anything is HQ-0043** (cited: ADR-0127 decision 8) and is not answered.

### 6. (e) The place in the definition

- **In "The round steps" of `orchestration/round-manager.md`**: in a round ≥ 1 after `case.write`, in round 0 after the round's last other step (round 0 has no `case.write`, cited: ADR-0133 decision 1); then `run.commit` (cited: ADR-0133 decision 5; ADR-0127 decision 3). That order is cited, not decided again. The tool sentence of "Your run" adds `mcp__hilclaw__round_end` as the only means of this write. This record does not edit the definition.

### Walk-through

| Situation | What follows |
|---|---|
| End of round 0, `round_budget` 6 | `round.end` with `round` 0: `round` 1, the view folded over round 0's lines, no `run_status`; `{"round": 1, "over": false, ...}`; then `run.commit` |
| End of round 4, `round_budget` 5 (ADR-0121's worked example) | `round` 5 ≥ 5: `run_status` `"over"` in the same write; `over` true; `run.commit` commits it (cited: ADR-0127 decision 3) |

A later member written inside a round makes every round end refuse until its record revises ③, and a run whose `STATE.json` holds an unknown member does a round's work, the coder call included, before that refusal, on every turn; slice 2 may check the members at the entry instead. A transient failure costs a turn: the round's work is redone or gone on with by the next turn (OD-17). The reads of one call are not atomic and take no lock (cited: ADR-0004 decision 10; rest of OD-17): the window of a read and a write in one call that ADR-0124 Alternatives (a″) weighed. It is taken here because its other reason, ⑫, is met by ③, and because the alternative — the model reading and then writing in two calls — has the same window and adds the copying.
