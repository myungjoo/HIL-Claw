## Decision

A sentence marked **owner** quotes O1, as a reason only. A sentence is **cited** when a requirement or a record with its decision is named with it; it then says what that source says and adds nothing. Every other sentence is **this record's own** — among them the four things an operation is called with, the timing parameters and their six values, that the point is given by the operation called, that (A) is a pair and (B) one of four strings tied to `condition`, that the form is established before any judging, that an answer of neither form ends the step with `operation_answer_invalid` shared with `correctness.check`, which kinds no end line holds, and every weighing. Nothing decided here is the owner's.

### 1. Scope and terms

- This record is about **a call of `sim.run` or `board.run` about one attempt of a run of ADR-0061 decision 1 that reaches ⑥**, the call of the operation (cited for the order: ADR-0062 decision 6; ADR-0064 decision 7), **carried out by an operation that runs on the host of the tool function or by an operation of the mock**. It fixes no form for an operation whose target is another machine (decision 9).
- **One form for both tools and both points**: "nothing but the execution point distinguishes the two operations" (**owner**, O1), and the judging of a run is the tool function's, "the same for every backend" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3); a form that differed by tool or by point would give that one code two things to judge.
- It fixes **six points and nothing else**: what an operation is handed (decision 2); how the point reaches it (decision 3); what it returns (decision 4); the table of what crosses the cut (decision 5); what holds for an answer of neither form (decisions 6 and 7); which kinds no end line of these tools holds (decision 8). Decision 9 says what is not decided.
- The terms of ADR-0009 (report, timing run, completed, broken), ADR-0016 (backend, operation, script, the moments ① to ⑦), ADR-0021 (the cut), ADR-0043 (an answer of an operation), ADR-0045 (a returned value, a raised error, a raised request to end), ADR-0052 (the figure), ADR-0056 (the output path) and ADR-0061 (the read of ④) are used unchanged. **The timing parameters**: decision 2.

### 2. What an operation is handed: the timing parameters and the output path

- **An operation of either tool is called with four things: the request, the hw_profile of the call, the timing parameters and the output path.** The first two are the frame's (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2). **The timing parameters are one value that holds exactly these six values and nothing else:**

| Value | What it is | Kind of value and bounds | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `m`, `k`, `n` | the dimensions: the `shape.m`, `shape.k` and `shape.n` of the op_signature the run states, taken in the read of ④ (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3) | three integers, each of at least 1 (the form of `shape`: ADR-0063 decision 5) | ADR-0061 decision 3 |
| `n_max` | the run's bound of the repeats of one measurement, read in ④ (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3) | an integer of at least 5 (the form of the member: ADR-0023 decision 3, as ADR-0063 decision 5 cites it) | ADR-0061 decision 3 |
| the sample form | derived by the tool function in ④ from the run's `objective` (cited: ADR-0061 decision 4) | the string `"interval"` or `"reciprocal"` (the names of ADR-0009 decision 7); today always `"interval"` (cited: ADR-0063 decision 5) | ADR-0061 decisions 3 and 4 |
| the seed | the timing seed, drawn by the tool function between ④ and ⑤ (cited: ADR-0064 decisions 2 and 3) | an integer with 0 <= seed < 2**64, "the whole range of seeds the harness accepts for a timing run" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 2) | ADR-0064 decision 2 |

- **They are the values the tool function judges with.** It replays the stopping rule "with the sample form and `n_max` it handed over" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3), and the result repeats the dimensions, `n_max` and the sample form "handed to the operation" (cited: ADR-0063 decision 5). No value is derived, rounded or replaced on its way across the cut.
- **The output path** is "the path of the `output/` of the attempt the request names, formed by the tool function, which reads nothing there; the operation applies the reader" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3, taking ADR-0056 decision 3 (b)). It is a path and is handed over in ⑥ with the call; where in the call the tool function forms it stays as ADR-0062 decision 6 and ADR-0064 decision 3 left it.
- **Never handed** (cited unless marked): "An operation is never handed the `bound` or the `objective`"; `n_min` and `cv_max` "are not handed"; of the settings — flags, trailing flags, compiler, the time limits of the build and of the run, the environment — "the tool function hands none of them over" (all ADR-0061 decision 3); the data root ("An operation is not handed the data root" — ADR-0056 decision 3, whose reasons ADR-0061 decision 3 carries over); and, this record's own, the point (decision 3) and the name of the tool.
- **The weighing.**
  - **(a) Four things: the timing parameters as one value, the output path beside it.** It is the shape the check has — "four things: the request, the hw_profile of the call, the check parameters and the output path" (cited: ADR-0056 decision 3) — so a backend that offers both kinds of HW tool meets one shape. The six values are those the tool function judges or records with; the path is of another kind and is handed for the reader only, the reason ADR-0056 decision 3 gave for keeping it out of the check parameters (cited for the reasoning).
  - **(b) Seven further things, one for each value and the path.** Each later addition changes the number of things every operation and every script is called with (the reasoning of ADR-0043 decision 2 (b)).
  - **(c) The six as members of the object handed as the request.** "No request member carries it" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 2, of the seed) and "the request carries none" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 4, of the sample form); a request that held them would be no request, and an operation could not tell what the caller stated from what the tool function added.
  - **(d) The output path as a seventh value of the timing parameters.** One group would then hold the values the tool function judges with and one it never judges or reads.
- **The choice is (a).** Fixed: that there are four things; that the third holds exactly the six values; what each value and the fourth thing are, with their kinds and bounds. The code task's: the types of the language, by which name or position code reaches each value, whether the things are passed by position or by keyword. The words `m`, `k`, `n` and `n_max` are those of ADR-0009 decision 9, and "the seed" is its "Seed of the inputs"; they say which value is meant and are not made names of code.
- **No further upper bound is added at the cut.** The dimensions and `n_max` come from a valid file read in ④, so they lie within the forms cited in the table, which are *integers* of ADR-0001 (at most 2^53 − 1); the harness of this source commit gives them no bound of its own (observation: `driver_arguments`), and this record adds none.

### 3. How the execution point reaches an operation

- "The tool called says the point; no member of a request says it" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 5), and a backend has, "for each HW tool it offers ... one **operation**" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2); the two tools "stay two tools with two operations" (cited: ADR-0038 decision 2, as ADR-0061 decision 1 cites it). **The point therefore reaches an operation as the operation that is called: the operation of `sim.run` is called for the sim point, that of `board.run` for the board point. Nothing is handed for it.**
- **An operation takes its point from no member of the request.** "A member named `point` in a request of either tool states nothing" (cited: ADR-0062 decision 4); the request is handed to the operation as it is (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2), and this record adds that an operation does not read such a member.
- **This differs from the check**, where the point reaches an operation as the member `point` of the request (cited: ADR-0043 decision 3); there one tool has two points, here the two points are split across two tools.
- **What an operation may make depend on its point**: where and how the timing run is carried out. In the local configuration "the two carry out the same local run" (**owner**, O1, as ADR-0061 quotes it — Context, O1; decision 2), and which target a call goes to is "not decided here (#5 B-2, OD-10)" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2). The form of decision 4 and the judging do not depend on it (decision 1).
- **Three ways were weighed.** (a) The operation called says it — nothing added, one statement of the point. (b) The point or the tool name handed as a further value — a second statement of what the operation called says, with a rule needed when the two differ. (c) A request member — refused (cited: ADR-0061 decision 5). **The choice is (a).**
- **For the mock**, a script is asked with "the called tool, the request, the hw_profile" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (c)), so the tool, and with it the point, reaches a script. With what else a script of these tools is asked is the mock's slice (decision 9).

### 4. What an operation returns: a report with its figure, or one of four conditions

- Cited (ADR-0061 decision 3): an operation returns "exactly one of: (A) **the report** — what the timing run's process wrote on its report channel, unchanged, when it ended by itself with exit status 0 within its time limit — **with the figure of that process or that there is none**; (B) **that there is no report, and which condition failed first**, in this order: the build past its time limit, the compiler ending with a non-zero status, the run past its time limit, the run ending with a non-zero status or a signal." "The operation does not judge the form or the content of a report"; it "converts the usage and returns a number of bytes or none".
- **(A) is returned as a pair, in this order: the report, a byte string — the bytes the process wrote on its report channel, in their order, none added, removed or changed; and the figure, an integer of at least 1, or *none*.** *None* is the language's value for the absence of a value (observation: Python's `None`, as `TimingResult.memory_bytes` uses it). The channel and the content of a report are those of ADR-0009 decision 8 (cited). **Every byte string in the first place is a report**: a short, malformed or empty one is returned as it is, and the tool function finds its form (the manner of ADR-0043 decision 4, "Every byte string is (A)"). The figure has no upper bound (cited: ADR-0063 decision 6, after ADR-0052 decision 4).
- **(B) is returned as one of four strings: `"build_time_limit"`, `"build_exit_status"`, `"time_limit"`, `"exit_status"`**, for the four conditions in the order cited above. The value is compared as it is, and nothing is normalised.
- **An answer of the form (A) holds the report and the figure or none, and one of the form (B) the condition, and nothing else**: no exit status, no signal, no elapsed time, no command line, no standard error, no compiler's log, no path; with (B) no figure and no byte of a report (cited for (B): "With (B) no figure is returned" — ADR-0061 decision 3).
- **The four strings are the first four values of `condition` of ADR-0063 decision 3, in its order, taken on purpose — and, by ADR-0060 decision 4, the four strings of the form (B) of `correctness.check`.** From here on the spellings are tied: a record that changes one says what becomes of the other. With one spelling the tool function puts the value an operation returned into `condition` as it is, and a reader of either HW tool's contract meets one word for one condition.
- **How the forms are told apart: by the kind of value.** A pair is (A); one of the four strings is (B). A byte string that is not in a pair is no (A): the figure or none is part of (A) (cited above). A figure that is neither an integer of at least 1 nor none — 0, a negative or non-integer number, a string — makes the pair no (A): "A reported value that is not a positive integer yields no figure" (cited: ADR-0052 decision 4), and ADR-0061 decision 3 places that conversion with the operation, which returns "a number of bytes or none" (cited). Which types of the language count as a pair, a byte string, an integer and a string is the code task's (observation: the harness counts no `bool` as an `int`).
- **The form is established before anything is judged.** The tool function judges a report only from an answer of the form (A), and makes a broken result only from one of the four strings; "A figure returned with (A) is used only if the tool function finds the run completed" (cited: ADR-0061 decision 3), so a figure of the right kind with a report judged broken is dropped and is no fault.
- **Five forms of (A) were weighed.** (a) A pair. (b) A mapping with two named members — names would cross the cut beside the members of a result, and a missing or an extra member is a state that needs a rule. (c) The figure always an integer, 0 for none — a fill value that a defective conversion would also produce, which would then pass as "no figure" unseen. (d) The report alone when there is no figure, a pair when there is one — two spellings of one form. (e) The figure handed back by a second call — two answers of one call, with state between them. **The choice is (a).**
- **Four forms of (B) were weighed.** (a) The four strings, tied as above. (b) The members of the harness's `Broken` — it has no member for a build, and every backend and script would import the harness to answer. (c) An exception for (B) — a broken run and a failed build are results (cited: ADR-0061 decision 7), and what an operation raises is a tool failure or a defect (decision 6). (d) A status and a flag in the manner of `local_run.RunResult` — the status would cross the cut, more than "which condition failed first". **The choice is (a).**
- **The names collide with no other.** Checked on 2026-10-15 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`: the four strings occur as the values of `condition` of both HW tools' results and as the form (B) of `correctness.check`, each with the meaning taken here; the term "timing parameters" occurs nowhere else. No new name is fixed beyond that term.

### 6. An answer of neither form: never a result

- **What is meant.** An operation that returns a value that is neither (A) nor (B) of decision 4 — nothing, a number, a byte string that is not in a pair, a pair whose first value is no byte string or whose figure is of the wrong kind, a pair of another length, a string that is not one of the four (`"report_form"` and `"replay"` among them, which only the tool function finds — cited: ADR-0063 decision 3) — or that raises something that is no tool failure. **Not meant**: a tool failure an operation raises (decisions 8 and 9), and the death of the process while the operation runs, after which the step is open (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (d); ADR-0062 Walk-through).
- **The three ways into the case are those of ADR-0045 decision 1** (cited): a returned value; a raised error, which the language counts as an error of the program (observation: an `Exception`); a raised request to end (observation: a `BaseException` that is no `Exception`). Where the line between the last two runs in code is the code task's.
- **An answer of neither form is never made into a result**: no completed and no broken result follows from it — in particular not a broken result with `"report_form"`, and no figure is taken from it. Why: each value of `condition` names a condition of the build or the run that failed (cited: ADR-0063 decision 3), and such an answer shows a defect of the backend and establishes nothing about the attempt — the reasoning of ADR-0043 decision 5 (cited for the reasoning), which holds here as it held there.
- **It establishes the form, not the truth**: "A wrong answer of the right form is taken as it is" (cited: ADR-0043 decision 8; Limits).

### 7. What the log holds and what the call answers

- **After a returned value and after a raised error, the tool function ends the step without an outcome: it appends the end line of the identity with a `failure`, and the identity is ended. The call is a tool failure of the kind `operation_answer_invalid`, the one its end line holds.** A later call with the same identity is given "the same tool failure again" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3, as ADR-0045 decision 4 cites it), and "no backend is selected or called for it" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2).
- **Why ended, not open.** Both tools are journaled (cited: ADR-0062 decision 2) and the start line is complete before ⑥ (cited: ADR-0062 decision 6), so the four tests and the five candidates of ADR-0045 decision 3 fall the same way here: after such an answer the tool function knows that the call of the operation is over and that no outcome exists, which is what an ended identity says — "the step is over without an outcome" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4, as ADR-0045 decision 3 quotes it); an open one would record less, and nothing in the log would tell it from a death. **The cost** is that of ADR-0045 decision 3: the identity is used up, and a further timing run of that attempt at that point is a further `execution`, "a new measurement" (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3), whose asking stays with OD-10 and the retry part of OD-17.
- **The `spend` of that end line** is what ADR-0062 decision 5 says of every end line of these tools: the empty object, also on an end line with `failure`, as long as no record names an axis that is accounted through the log; that decision calls it "no rule of this record" (cited). Nothing is added.
- **The kind is the run tools' contract's own, with the spelling and the meaning of ADR-0045's, shared in the manner of ADR-0064 decision 4**: "no record forbids two contracts from defining the same value with the same meaning; a value is reserved only by a record of the frame" (cited). So `correctness.check` keeps its own `operation_answer_invalid` (ADR-0045 decision 4) and the run tools' contract defines one of its own, with one meaning in both tools of it (cited for the manner: ADR-0061 decision 4). **The sentence a caller may rely on**: *in the call of this identity the operation was called and its call is over; what it returned or raised had neither form the tool's contract lets an operation return, and was no tool failure. No result was made. The answer is what a complete end line of the identity holds: the identity is ended.* The forms are each contract's (ADR-0043 decision 4 as ADR-0060 decision 4 extends it; decision 4 here); the sentence says nothing about the attempt or about why.
- **Four answers were weighed.** (a) The kind of ADR-0045, with its spelling and meaning, defined again by this contract. (b) Another spelling (for example `run_answer_invalid`) — no landed record says anything different of the case at the two contracts, and two names for one situation would have a caller learn which tool answered before reading the kind (the reasoning of ADR-0064 decision 4). (c) A kind the frame reserves. ADR-0045 decision 4 chose a kind of one tool because "This situation is found by one tool's function against the form of one tool's contract" and "No other HW tool has a form" (cited); with this record two contracts have a form, so that test **no longer carries the choice**, as ADR-0064 decision 4 said of the test of ADR-0048 (cited for the manner). What remains is that a reserved kind would speak for `profiler.collect`, whose contract does not exist (the reasoning of ADR-0041 decision 4, as ADR-0064 decision 4 applies it); the choice is provisional in that respect, and a record of the frame that reserves the case takes another name or amends ADR-0045 and this record (cited for the cost: ADR-0016 decision 3). (d) `tool_crashed` or an open identity — the reasons of ADR-0045 decision 3 (cited). **The choice is (a), the shared kind.**
- **Only the tool function raises it**, and only as the `failure` of an end line. When the append of that end line fails, the call is `end_line_failed` (cited: ADR-0026 decision 3, as ADR-0045 decision 4 cites it).
- **A raised error does not come out of the step.** [ADR-0019](ADR-0019-od18-unusable-run-kind.md) decision 4 keeps `tool_crashed` the answer for "such an exception out of the step, which leaves the step open" (cited), and ADR-0045 decision 5 reads that sentence "as what holds for an exception that comes out of a step, not as a rule that a tool's contract may not end its own step" (cited). This record takes that reading for these tools: their tool function does not let a raised error of an operation come out of the step and ends the step as above. The sentence stands as written for every exception that does come out of the step, a defect in the tool function's own part of ⑥ included (Limits).
- **A raised request to end is not answered**: it passes through the tool function, nothing is appended, the identity stays open, and a later call is `step_open` (cited: ADR-0045 decision 5, taken here for its reasons; ADR-0015 decision 2).
- **The message** follows ADR-0045 decision 6 (cited): it says that the answer had neither form and which of the two ways it was, and may name the kind of the returned value or raised error; it carries nothing taken from the content of the answer — no byte of a report, no figure, no text or argument of an error. "a caller tells the cases apart by the kind and never by the message" (cited: ADR-0026 decision 6). Since a backend holds the seed (cited: "The operation is handed the seed among its inputs" — ADR-0064 decision 7) and the seed is kept nowhere by the tool layer after the run (cited: ADR-0064 decision 5), this record's own inference is that the rule keeps the seed out of the log on this path too.
- **The name** was checked on 2026-10-15 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`: it occurs as the kind of `correctness.check` only, and it equals no reserved kind, no other kind of the run tools' contract (`objective_not_stated`, `objective_not_covered`, `seed_not_drawn`), no `step` value and no member name.

### 8. Which kinds no end line of these tools holds — the answer to ADR-0064 decision 4

- **No end line of `sim.run` or `board.run` holds `seed_not_drawn`, `objective_not_stated` or `objective_not_covered` as its `failure` kind**, whatever an operation raises. For `seed_not_drawn` this answers the request of ADR-0064 decision 4 (cited), "so that the kind keeps telling that no line was appended"; for the two others it repeats ADR-0061 decision 4, "never as the `failure` of an end line" (cited).
- **What follows for the next slice.** "What the tool function does with an operation that raises a tool failure of this kind belongs to the slice that says which tool failures stand as the `failure` of an end line" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 4). That slice (ADR-0061 Consequences item (6); OD-18 sub-question ⑲ (나)) is bound by the bullet above: it may end such a step under another kind or decide otherwise, and it never stores one of the three kinds on an end line.
- **`operation_answer_invalid` is the first kind of the run tools' contract that stands on an end line.**

### 10. Limits

1. **A record, not code; nothing was built or run.**
2. **The operation's answer is believed** (cited: ADR-0061 Limit 7): a report of intervals not measured, or a figure not read, of the right form is taken as it is; and an operation that returns `"exit_status"` for a run that ended with status 0 leads to a broken result about an attempt that may be sound (the manner of ADR-0043 decision 8). Decisions 4 and 6 recognise a form and nothing else.
3. **A defect of the tool function is not told from a defect of the backend by the answer** (cited for the manner: ADR-0045 Limits); a defect in the tool function's own part of ⑥ that comes out of the step as an exception is `tool_crashed`, and the identity stays open (cited: ADR-0019 decision 4, as ADR-0045 Limits cites it); where the code draws the line between what an operation raised and what the tool function's own code raised is the code task's.
4. **An identity ended by such an answer stays ended**, also where the cause would not recur; a further timing run needs a further `execution`, which nobody may yet grant (decision 7).
5. **A raised request to end leaves the identity open** although the call of the operation is over, as a death does; the log does not tell the two apart (cited: ADR-0045 Limits).
6. **The form carries no version**: it is an interface between a tool function and the backends of one source commit (the manner of ADR-0043 Limits), not a file or a field of the data plane.
7. The limits of ADR-0061 decision 8, ADR-0062 decision 7, ADR-0063 decision 11 and ADR-0064 decision 8 stand unchanged.
