## Context

- **The open point.** "(가) Whether a change in the failure kinds a contract states for a journaled step — held as `failure` in an end line and raised again by the repeated call (ADR-0011 decision 3) — is told apart by the rule of decision 3, or otherwise. (나) Where the `step` values used so far are listed, if anywhere, so that a record can check that a value is unused (decision 3)" (cited: ADR-0100 Boundaries). ADR-0100 decision 4 counts "the failure kinds" among "What is not a change" of a result definition (cited).
- **The form of a failure.** `step_ended` holds "exactly one of `result` and `failure`"; "`failure` holds the `kind` and `message` of the tool failure when the step ended without an outcome"; a repeated call of an ended identity returns "the same tool failure again when the line holds `failure`" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3). "`error.kind` is a *name* … and `error.message` a string; this record fixes the form and **no list of kinds**" (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4). A resumed turn that finds a step ended with a `failure` takes it that "the step is over without an outcome and is not run again under this identity" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4).
- **Two sources of kinds.** Kinds reserved by the frame, with "one meaning in every tool", raised "for no other situation" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 2; ADR-0026 decision 3), of which "no tool function or step raises one for another situation" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2), and kinds each contract names for its own tool (ADR-0010 decision 4; for `correctness.check` for example ADR-0045 decision 4, ADR-0047 decisions 4 and 5, ADR-0056 decision 6, ADR-0060 decision 4).
- **What a caller relies on.** "What a kind lets a caller tell is all this record says of it" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 2; ADR-0026 decision 3); ADR-0047 decision 4 states "The sentence a caller may rely on" for its kind (cited); "A caller tells the cases apart by the kind and never by the message" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 6).
- **Why it matters.** The log is never rewritten "while the tool layer may be replaced at a round boundary (NFR-7 ①)" (cited: ADR-0006 decision 8), and runs are resumed (FR-6): a `failure` stored under one source can reach a caller of another.
- **Part (나).** ADR-0100 decision 3 asks of a changed result definition "a new `step` value that no accepted record and no tool has used" and says "A value is never reused" (cited). ADR-0040 and ADR-0062 checked their values by a search of the records and the trees (cited: ADR-0040 decision 3; ADR-0062 decision 3). ADR-0098 answered the same need for event kinds with one index, `ARCHITECTURE/EventKinds.md` (cited: ADR-0098 decision 3).

## Decision

### 1. What the code does today (observation, binds nothing)

- `hilclaw/journaled_step.py`: the end line holds a `failure` only when the step raised `StepFailed`; "Any other exception out of the step passes through unchanged, a plain `hilclaw.tools.ToolFailure` and a `BaseException` included, and no end line is appended: the step stays open" (markup simplified); for an ended identity `_answer` raises a `ToolFailure` of the `kind` and `message` on the line. The kinds of the lookup (`step_open`, the four `run_file_*`) and of a failed append (`start_line_failed`, `end_line_failed`) are raised by this module itself and never put into a line; "A step that states it [`step_open`] anyway is not detected" (cited: its module docstring).
- `hilclaw/journal.py` (`_checked`) checks a `failure` as an object with exactly `kind`, a *name*, and `message`, a `str`; it knows no list of kinds.
- `hilclaw/check_tool.py` raises `StepFailed` only with the kind and message `hilclaw/check_ending.py` gives: `operation_answer_invalid` (`hilclaw/check_operation.py`) or what `hilclaw/check_failure.ended_with` gives — one of the eight `BACKEND_KINDS` as raised, or `operation_failure_invalid` for every other raised kind, a reserved kind included. `build_log_unwritable` (ADR-0078 decision 7) occurs nowhere in `hilclaw/`.
- `hilclaw/tools.py` holds the reserved kinds in `RESERVED_KINDS` (thirteen `KIND_*` constants); `tool_crashed` and `bad_result` are raised by `call` on what a tool function raised or returned.
- No tool function of `sim.run` or `board.run` is built in `hilclaw/` (as ADR-0100 decision 1 observed at `ab71b86`; unchanged at `07247ff`). The four `step` values are bound once, in `hilclaw/check_identity.py` and `hilclaw/run_identity.py`. Nothing in `hilclaw/` lists `step` values across tools.

### 2. Terms

- **A stored kind of a `step` value** is a failure kind that the accepted records let the `failure` of an end line under that value hold. For `correctness_check_sim` and `correctness_check_board` these are, at `07247ff`: `operation_answer_invalid` (ADR-0045 decision 4), `operation_failure_invalid` (ADR-0047 decision 4), `build_log_unwritable` (ADR-0078 decision 7) and the eight backend kinds (ADR-0056 decision 6; ADR-0060 decision 4) — eleven. For `sim_run_timing` and `board_run_timing` the run tools' own `operation_answer_invalid`, of which "Only the tool function raises it, and only as the `failure` of an end line" (cited, markup simplified: ADR-0065 decision 7), is one; which others are is not stated here (Boundaries).
- **A kind answered without an end line** is every other failure kind: it is answered by a call before its start line, at an append, or out of a step that stays open, and no line holds it. Every reserved kind is one (decision 4). So is every kind of `correctness.check` that the records say is "never the `failure` of an end line" (cited: ADR-0047 decision 4, of `tolerance_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_covered`, `draws_not_stated`) or that a call answers having "appended no line" (cited: ADR-0048 decision 5, of `seed_not_drawn`).
- **The meaning of a kind** is what the record that names it states of it: the sentence a caller may rely on, where that record states one ("The sentence a caller may rely on": ADR-0045 decision 4, ADR-0047 decision 4, ADR-0056 decision 6, ADR-0078 decision 7; "What a kind lets a caller tell is all this record says of it": ADR-0019 decision 2, ADR-0026 decision 3); otherwise the situation it means, as the record names it — a kind becomes a backend kind when a record "names it with the situation it means" (cited: ADR-0047 decision 5), as ADR-0060 decision 4 names its six kinds by the rows S3 to S6, S9 and S10 of ADR-0056 decision 5. Of the eleven stored kinds of `correctness.check` today, `operation_answer_invalid`, `operation_failure_invalid`, `build_log_unwritable` (ADR-0078 decision 7), `no_kernel_source` and `kernel_source_unreadable` have a sentence, and the six of ADR-0060 have their situation; the run tools' `operation_answer_invalid` has "the spelling and the meaning of ADR-0045's" (cited: ADR-0065 decision 7). A further case that a later record places under a kind (decision 5) is not part of its meaning; the message is not either (ADR-0019 decision 6).

### 3. Part (가), kinds a contract names for its own tool: the meaning of a stored kind never changes, and a name is never reused

- **The meaning of a stored kind is fixed and never changes.** It is fixed when the record that names the kind is accepted, or at `07247ff` for the stored kinds that decision 2 names, the amendments the accepted records made included. A record that wants a caller to rely on another meaning **names a new kind**, under a *name* of ADR-0001 that no record of that tool's contract has used and that is no reserved kind (ADR-0016 decision 3), and states its meaning.
- **A stored kind stays a stored kind.** A kind the contract stops raising keeps its meaning and stays a kind that an end line under that value may hold, since lines that hold it may exist and are never removed (ADR-0006 decision 5). This holds also for a kind whose naming record is reverted. Its name is never given to another kind of that tool. Because the name stays used by the contract, a later record of the frame cannot reserve it ("as long as no tool's contract uses the name", cited: ADR-0016 decision 3).
- **How a stored `failure` is told apart**: by its `kind` alone. A `failure` holds a name that selects one meaning in any log and at any time; nothing inside `failure`, and no member of `data`, of the envelope or of `instance.json`, says which source wrote it, and the `step` value does not change.
- **Why the kind and not the `step` value** (this record's own; ADR-0100 decision 4 left the failure kinds outside the result definition, and this is why they are told apart differently). A result carries no name that says which definition it was made under — "A result carries no version member" (cited: ADR-0042 decision 7) — so ADR-0100 decision 3 had to put that name into `step`. A failure already carries one: its `kind` is a *name* the records give a meaning, and a caller tells the cases apart "by the kind" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 6). This is the shape of ADR-0097 decision 2 for event kinds: a new definition takes a name never used before, and a name is never reused.
- **ADR-0015 decision 4** is untouched: `step` stays the contract's, is no member of a request and is shared by no two tools; this rule asks for no new value.

### 4. Part (가), kinds reserved by the frame: excluded from what a log must tell apart, since no end line holds one

- **No end line holds a reserved kind** under the accepted records. `invalid_request` and `no_data_root` are answered before anything is read (ADR-0015 decisions 2 and 3); `step_open` by the lookup of an open identity (ADR-0011 decision 3; ADR-0026 decision 5); the four `run_file_*` kinds by a call that "appended no line" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 2); `no_backend` and `not_offered` at the selection, before the start line (ADR-0016 decisions 2, 3 and 4; ADR-0019 decision 4); `start_line_failed` and `end_line_failed` by a call whose append failed (ADR-0026 decision 3); `tool_crashed` for an exception that leaves the step open or comes from no step (ADR-0019 decision 4), and `bad_result` by `hilclaw/tools.py` on what a tool function returned (decision 1). A reserved kind an operation of `correctness.check` raises is stored as `operation_failure_invalid`, never as raised (cited: ADR-0047 decision 4). "no tool function or step raises one for another situation" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2).
- **So a change of a reserved kind gives no `step` value a new value and is not told apart in a log**: a call that answers a reserved kind answers it from the source that makes that call, and no later call is given it from a line. The same holds for every other kind answered without an end line (decision 2).
- **A record that would let an end line hold a reserved kind** (none does) makes it a stored kind of the values it names, and decision 3 then binds its meaning for them from that record's acceptance. This record does not say whether such a record is admissible; ADR-0015 decision 2 and ADR-0047 decision 4 stand against it today.
- **A line that holds a reserved kind against ADR-0015 decision 2** — a step of some tool that ends with one, which `hilclaw/journaled_step.py` does not detect (decision 1) — is a defect of that tool and is not covered by this rule; what a reader makes of such a line is OD-11's. For `correctness.check` it cannot arise, since `hilclaw/check_failure.py` maps every reserved kind to `operation_failure_invalid` (decision 1).

### 5. What counts as a change

| Change | For a stored kind (decision 3) |
|---|---|
| A kind added | No change of any existing kind; no earlier line holds it. The new kind's meaning is fixed with its record |
| A kind removed (no longer raised) | Not a removal: it stays a stored kind with its meaning |
| A kind renamed | A new kind with the meaning, beside the old one, which stays a stored kind; a caller of the later contract is given either from lines |
| A change of its meaning | A new kind under a name not used before; the old kind keeps its meaning |
| A further case placed under it, or a case taken from it | No change of the kind where its meaning covers every case placed under it: for a kind with a sentence, the sentence is true of the case; for a kind named by its situation, the case is one of that situation. For example ADR-0079 places an `OSError` from starting the compiler in S6, "The compiler is not found, or its process cannot be started" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4), the situation of `compiler_unavailable`; and after ADR-0060 named six backend kinds, cases once answered with `operation_failure_invalid` are answered otherwise, while its sentence speaks of "the contract, as the source of that call has it" (cited: ADR-0047 decision 4). A case taken from a kind leaves the earlier lines that hold it true. A case its meaning does not cover is a change of its meaning |
| A change of what its message shows | Not told apart: "nothing may depend on the wording" (cited: ADR-0047 decision 4); a rule for what a message may show binds the lines written after it, and earlier lines are not rewritten (ADR-0006 decision 5) |
| A situation moved between `result` and `failure` | A change of the result definition by ADR-0100 decision 4, so ADR-0100 decision 3 applies; this record adds nothing to it |

- **Why the meaning and not every detail, unlike ADR-0100 decision 4** (this record's own): ADR-0100 decision 4 refused to judge compatibility because "A caller relies on a closed list member by member" (cited: ADR-0100 decision 4, from ADR-0042 decision 7). For a kind the records say what a caller may tell from it — its sentence, "all this record says of it" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 2), or the situation it means (ADR-0047 decision 5) — and the record that places a case under a kind is the one that has to show that the meaning covers it, as ADR-0079 and its successors placed cases in the rows of ADR-0060. No reader judges a stored line.

### 6. What the repeated call finds across such a change

- **The repeated call returns the stored `failure` unchanged** and does not run the step (ADR-0011 decision 3; decision 1). Its `kind` has the one meaning it had when it was written (decision 3), so a caller of the later contract reads it rightly, also where the later contract no longer raises it or would answer the same situation with another kind.
- **NFR-7 ① and FR-6.** When the tool layer is replaced at a round boundary, or a run is resumed by a later source, a step ended with a `failure` under the earlier kinds stays ended: no new `step` value makes it run again, and nothing is appended. Whether a turn asks for a further `execution` after such a change, or what it makes of a `failure` the later source would not give, is not the tool layer's (OD-10, the retry part of OD-17, OD-11; Boundaries).

### 7. Part (나): the `step` values are listed in one index, `ARCHITECTURE/StepValues.md`

- **There is one list of `step` values, the file `ARCHITECTURE/StepValues.md`**, created with this record. It is an **index**: one row per used value, with four columns — the value, the tool, a link to the record that fixes it (with its decision), and the state.
- **The states**: `defined` — the fixing record is accepted; `not used` — an accepted later record, linked in the row, says the contract no longer calls a step under it (for example when ADR-0100 decision 3 gives a step a new value); `reverted` — the fixing record has been reverted. A row is never removed and its value never changes: "A value is never reused" (cited: ADR-0100 decision 3), and lines under any of them may exist.
- **What the index does not hold**: no result definition, no stored kind, no meaning; the fixing record is the only source, and wins when the two disagree; the index is then corrected by a document change, which fixes nothing.
- **Why an index** (this record's own): the word `step` is common in the records, the values belong to several records of several contracts, and a reverted record's values are found only by reading it (as ADR-0098 decision 3 argued for kinds). One short index is the means by which "a new `step` value that no accepted record and no tool has used" (ADR-0100 decision 3) can be checked.

### 8. How a value enters the index

- **A record that fixes a `step` value** chooses a *name* of ADR-0001 that is not in the index and is no placeholder of a record or a test (`example_step` of ADR-0011, ADR-0012 and ADR-0015, `other_step` and the like — ADR-0062 decision 3), checks as ADR-0040 and ADR-0062 did that the string is no other name in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`, and **adds its row** with the state `defined` **in the commit that lands the record**.
- A record that stops a value being used sets its row to `not used` and links itself; a task that reverts a fixing record sets the row to `reverted`.
- No row is added by code, by a test or by a run. **Nothing checks a line's `step` against the index**: `hilclaw/journal.py` checks the *name* form only (decision 1), and the guard is the records and their review, as for `ARCHITECTURE/EventKinds.md` (ADR-0098 decision 5).

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | What a step ended with a `failure` under the earlier kinds and called again later meets | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) ADR-0100 decision 3 extended to failure kinds: a new `step` value per change | No line: the step is run again under the new value | A failure already carries a name with a meaning (decision 3), so the step value adds nothing a caller needs, and every added kind would renew the values of a contract — ADR-0056 and ADR-0060 added eight backend kinds, and ADR-0079 and its successors placed situations under them. Each such change would make every step that ended with a `failure` run again after a replaced tool layer (NFR-7 ①) or a resumption (FR-6): a retry decided by a change of the tool layer, which is OD-10's and the retry part of OD-17's (ADR-0011 decision 4: "not run again under this identity"). For reserved kinds it would renew every journaled tool's values for kinds no line holds (decision 4) |
| (b) A member inside `failure` that marks its list | The stored `failure` with the marker | Against the form: "the `kind` and `message`" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3), checked as "exactly `kind` … and `message`" (decision 1), and "no member is added to the error object" (cited: ADR-0026 decision 4, of ADR-0010 decision 4). A data-plane schema change of a closed member set of `events.ndjson` for no failure that occurs today |
| (c) A kind's meaning never changes and a name is never reused; only new names are added | The stored `failure`, read under the one meaning of its kind | **Adopted** (decisions 3 to 6), with the reading that a case placed under a kind whose meaning covers it is no change (decision 5) — a strict "never change when a kind is raised" would have forbidden ADR-0060 and ADR-0079 after the fact. Its cost is in Consequences |
| (d) Leave (가) open | Undecided | Each later record that names or changes a kind would choose the means unrecorded, and a kind once written cannot be withdrawn from lines (ADR-0006 decision 5); the answer needs no new member, value or kind |
| (e) An index file like `ARCHITECTURE/EventKinds.md` for the `step` values | — | **Adopted** (decisions 7 and 8), as an index only, so that it holds no second definition. Its cost: a commit that forgets the row leaves it behind the records |
| (f) The code constants of `hilclaw/` as the list | — | Records fix values before code binds them, and a value of a reverted record stays used whether or not code ever bound it; the constants live in one module per contract (`check_identity.py`, `run_identity.py`) and are removed with their code, so they cannot hold `not used` or `reverted` values |
| (g) No list: a record searches the accepted records | — | Worked for four values (ADR-0040, ADR-0062), but `step` is a common word, the values lie in records of several contracts, and a reverted record is found only by reading it; "no accepted record and no tool has used" (ADR-0100 decision 3) would be checked by memory |

For (e) to (g): what a step ended with a `failure` and called again later meets is decided by part (가) alone; the list changes no call.

## Consequences

- **No code changes.** `hilclaw/journal.py` and `hilclaw/journaled_step.py` stay right: they look at no list of kinds and return the stored `failure` as it is (decision 1). `hilclaw/check_failure.py` stays right: a backend kind that a later record stops letting a backend raise would leave `BACKEND_KINDS` with that record's own code task and be answered as `operation_failure_invalid` thereafter, while lines holding it keep its meaning. No code task follows from this record.
- **`ARCHITECTURE/StepValues.md` is created** with exactly the four rows of today, each `defined`.
- **For a record that names or changes a failure kind**: it states whether the kind is a stored kind of which `step` values, checks the name against the kinds of its tool and the reserved kinds as ADR-0047 decision 4 did, and, for a stored kind, does not change its meaning (decision 5). This record does not make naming a kind ordinary: "A new value of `error.kind` is a data-plane change (CLAUDE.md A-13)" (cited: ADR-0083; ADR-0092), and a record that names one states its A-13 reading. **For a record that fixes a `step` value**: decision 8.
- **Not edited and now less exact**: ADR-0100 decision 4 ("the failure kinds" under "What is not a change" — still not a change of the result definition, now under decision 3 here) and its Boundaries (㊱); ADR-0100 decision 3 ("no accepted record and no tool has used" — now checked against the index); ADR-0010 decision 4 ("no list of kinds" — still no closed list, but a stored kind's meaning is fixed); ADR-0047 decision 5 ("A kind becomes a backend kind when a record of this contract names it" — and, once stored, it stays a stored kind with its meaning even when it is no longer a backend kind). No earlier ADR is edited.
- **The cost, stated**: stored kinds accumulate over the life of a contract and a caller must know every one that a line may hold, those no longer raised included; a step ended with a `failure` that a later source would not give stays ended and is not run again by the tool layer; `operation_failure_invalid` on an earlier line does not say which list of backend kinds its source had; whether the meaning of a kind covers a case placed under it is caught by review only. Lines written under the four values of today before the meanings as fixed here are not told apart by this rule; what a reader does with one is OD-11.
- **A-13 reading**: document mode; no code, dependency, settings file or schema file changes, and no member, value or kind of the data plane is added or changed. The new document indexes four values that ADR-0040 and ADR-0062 already fix.
- **Runtime acceptance items**: none.
- **Follow-up (document)**: a link to `ARCHITECTURE/StepValues.md` in the README document table, as for `ARCHITECTURE/EventKinds.md`.
