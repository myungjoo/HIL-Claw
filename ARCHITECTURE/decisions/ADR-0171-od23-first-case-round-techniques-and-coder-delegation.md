## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a source is named with it. Every other sentence is **this record's own**.

### 1. Scope

- **The choice of the technique ids of the spec of the first coder call of a round `r` ≥ 1** (`candidate` 1, `attempt` 1) of a run of ADR-0151 decision 1, the way the catalog's selection criteria reach it, the way the ids reach `spec.write`, the coder-driver's delegation for that call and the Round-Manager's steps from the read of the round's hypothesis record to the coder-driver's report.
- **Outside**: round 0, whose spec carries no technique (cited: ADR-0138 decision 2 (b)) and whose steps stay as they are; every step after the coder-driver's report in a round ≥ 1; everything under Boundaries.

### 2. (a) The holder of the choice: the technique-selector

- **The technique-selector chooses the ids**, as part of the selection work ADR-0132 decision 2 gives it. K1's first row names "기법 선택 (FR-7·8)" as its work (cited).
- **Why it**: choosing which techniques a call applies so that the call tests the round's hypotheses is judgement, which K2 condition 4 of ADR-0128 keeps from no sub-agent, and it is the agent that formed those hypotheses and read the earlier rounds and attempts (cited: ADR-0132 decisions 2 and 3). The Round-Manager would take the reading of earlier rounds into the manager's context, which ADR-0132 decision 2 rejected for the hypotheses (cited). The coder-driver judges nothing and has no read tool (cited: ADR-0126 decision 2; ADR-0141 decision 5); giving it the choice would make it a second selector. A fixed rule of a tool (for example "the next entry in id order") cannot relate a choice to the round's hypotheses, which FR-17 asks of a selection (cited: "가설 없는 기법 선택을 허용하지 않는다").
- **The criteria**, in this order of weight: the round's hypotheses — the ids are chosen so that applying them in the call of candidate 1 can show what the hypotheses whose `tested_by` holds candidate 1 expect; the selection criteria of the entries (decision 3); the standing and failures of earlier attempts and the records of earlier rounds, through the reads it already has (cited: ADR-0132 decision 3). Which entries, how many and in what order are its judgement; the order it gives is the spec's order and has no meaning beyond it (cited: ADR-0151 decision 2 (b)).
- **The hypotheses stay free of techniques** (cited: ADR-0059 decision 3): the record is written as ADR-0132 decision 4 says, and the choice is made after the record exists, so the record does not depend on the choice. What links a hypothesis to an id is the selector's reasoning, kept in no file; what a call was given is the spec's `guidance.techniques` (cited: ADR-0150 decision 3).
- **An empty list is allowed**: when `catalog.read` answers `catalog_unusable` — then only an empty list passes `spec.write` (cited: ADR-0151 decision 2 (b)), which covers a mock run (cited: ADR-0151 decision 1) —, when its result holds no entry, and when the selector judges that no entry serves the round's hypotheses, for example a hypothesis that the failure an earlier attempt showed is gone in the next call. ADR-0151 decision 2 (b) left this "not the writer's to judge" (cited); it is the selector's.

### 3. (b) How the selection criteria reach it: a registry read tool `catalog.read`

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Name (MCP name) | `catalog.read` (`mcp__hilclaw__catalog_read`); the name holds no `_` (ADR-0010 decision 4) |
| Request | Exactly `run_id` (ADR-0015 decision 4), required; any other member is refused |
| Result | `{"entries": [{"id": <id>, "when": <criteria>}, …]}`: one element per entry of the catalog directory of the run's accelerator, in ascending id order; `when` is the content of the entry's `## When` section with leading and trailing line feeds removed and nothing else changed |
| Checks, in order | ① the request (`invalid_request`); ② the data root (`no_data_root`); ③ the run's `instance.json` read and parsed (`run_file_missing`, `run_file_invalid`, `run_file_unsupported`, `run_file_unreadable`, as `instance.read` names them); ④ the directory of the `accelerator` of its `hw_profile` read by `hilclaw.catalog` — missing, refused by the catalog's rules or unreadable (`catalog_unusable`) |

- **Every entry is returned, unfiltered.** Every entry that passes the catalog's rules states the values of `APPLIES_TO` (observation), which every spec `spec.write` composes has (cited: the `hilclaw/spec_tool.py` docstring, "which no valid `instance.json` makes refuse today"). A later record that widens `Applies to` revisits this tool, so that an id it returns is not refused with `technique_not_applicable`.
- **The application guidance is not returned**: it is for the coder, which gets it in its spec (cited: FR-8; ADR-0150 decision 2), and the choice needs the criteria only.
- **Kinds.** Reused with their meanings: `invalid_request`, `no_data_root` (ADR-0015 decision 2); the four `run_file_*` kinds for `instance.json` (ADR-0019 decision 3) — **this record widens ADR-0019 decision 2 to this read**, and the property holds: such a call appended no line, selected no backend and wrote nothing; `catalog_unusable` of ADR-0151 decision 3. **No new kind.** It is not journaled and reads only `instance.json` and the catalog directory; nothing is created or kept. It joins `default_registry` in the source and in no other way (cited: #5 A-12 point 3).
- **The selector gets it**: the technique-selector's `tools` gain `mcp__hilclaw__catalog_read`, which ADR-0132 decision 3 foresaw ("Later reads are added by the records that create them", cited). The entry is an exact `mcp__hilclaw__<name>`, so the permission derivation pre-approves it with no change (cited: ADR-0139 decision 3), and the caller check lets it through for the selector alone (cited: ADR-0146 decisions 3 and 6).
- **Why a tool and not `Read` of `catalog/`.** The session directory holds `orchestration/` only (cited: ADR-0118 decision 5), and the delegation names no place (cited: ADR-0123 decision 2), so the selector has no path to `catalog/`; adding `catalog/` to `LAYOUT` would put content that is not the session's definition into it, which ADR-0150 decision 2 rejected for its place (cited). The tool knows the catalog as `spec.write` knows it, and the directory read is the one of the run's own accelerator, not one a model picks; a read with no judgement in it is a tool's, not an agent's choice of files (this record's reading, in the spirit of K2 condition 4 of ADR-0128, which keeps deterministic steps out of sub-agents).
- **Why not a `Skill`.** The session has no `Skill` tool (cited: ADR-0139 decision 4); a skill would need it in the built-in set and a skill file under `orchestration/skills/` that copies the `## When` sections, so two copies of each criterion could drift.
- **The FR-8 reading this rests on**, marked as this record's own and subject to the owner's objection: for these runs FR-8's third sentence holds — technique candidates start as documents in the repository, whose `## When` sections are the selection criteria (cited: ADR-0150 decisions 1 and 2) —; "선택 기준은 Orchestrator 세션에서 트리거에" is met when the criteria enter the turn session at the selection step, at the selecting agent's own request; the Skill form of the criteria is **not adopted for these runs and not rejected**: it stays open (Boundaries).

### 4. (c) How the ids reach the coder-driver and `spec.write`

- **The selector reports the ids in one fixed line** of its final message, `techniques: <id>, <id>, …` in its order, or `techniques: none` for an empty list. It gives the line only when the round's record exists — written by it, or answered `hypotheses_exist` (cited: ADR-0132 decision 4) — and `catalog.read` returned a result or `catalog_unusable`; otherwise no such line.
- **The Round-Manager copies the line into the coder-driver's delegation** (decision 6), and the coder-driver copies the ids into the `techniques` of its `spec.write` request (decision 5). Neither judges them.
- **K2 condition 3** ("subagent 반환값에 의존하면 subagent 사망 시 유실", cited by ADR-0128). The go-on of the round rests on the record read with `hypotheses.read`, not on the report (cited: ADR-0132 decision 6). The ids are a parameter of the next call, not an expensive result: when the report is lost the turn stops and the next turn makes the choice again (Walk-through). Once used, the ids are recorded by the spec that `spec.write` writes, a file checked by a tool (cited: ADR-0150 decision 3; ADR-0151 decision 2 (b)). A copy that changes an id into one that names no entry is refused (`technique_unknown`, `invalid_request`); **a copy that changes it into another valid id is not detected** (Consequences).
- **The data-plane stop condition is not met**: no file, member or value carries the ids beyond the spec's existing `guidance.techniques` (cited: ADR-0151 decision 2 (b)); `hypotheses.json` and `decision.md` are untouched.

### 5. (d) The coder-driver's round ≥ 1 delegation

- **The tuple**: `round` `r`, `candidate` 1, `attempt` 1 (cited: ADR-0151 decision 3).
- **The message names** the run id, the tuple, and **for a round ≥ 1 the ids of the selector's line in its order, or that there are none**, and no place (cited for the rest: ADR-0126 decision 6; ADR-0123 decisions 2 and 4).
- **Its `tools` do not change**: `mcp__hilclaw__spec_write` and `mcp__hilclaw__coder_run` (cited: ADR-0141 decision 5); it reads nothing.
- **Its fixed rule, extended**: for a round ≥ 1 its `spec.write` request carries `techniques`, the message's ids in their order, an empty array for none; for round 0 no `techniques` member (cited: ADR-0151 decision 3). If a round ≥ 1 delegation gives no list, it requests nothing and reports that. Otherwise rules 2 to 5 of ADR-0141 decision 5 hold in every round unchanged: one `coder.run` after a successful write; no call on any failure kind, `technique_unknown`, `technique_not_applicable`, `catalog_unusable` and `feedback_unreadable` among them; **on `spec_exists` no call — ADR-0141 decision 5's interim rule kept**, not an answer to OD-8 ㉖ (가) or OD-23 ① (가).

### 6. (e) The Round-Manager's round ≥ 1 steps after the read

- **A step 8 follows step 7, in a round ≥ 1 only, when step 7 returned the round's record**: if the selector's report holds exactly one `techniques:` line, the Round-Manager delegates to `coder-driver` with the `Agent` tool and waits for its report, its message as decision 5 says; otherwise it reports in one line that no choice was given and comes to the stop, delegating nothing.
- **Whatever the coder-driver reports** — a result with any `taken`, or any kind — the Round-Manager reports it in one line and **comes to the stop**: it reports in one line that the following steps of round `r` are not yet defined, writes nothing further, calls `run.commit` as "The end of a session" says and ends the session, the round not ended (cited: ADR-0143 decisions 2 and 7). In that turn it delegates no second time, issues no call itself and asks for no repair. This is the place ADR-0141 decision 6 gave round 0 before ADR-0142 (cited).
- **A failed read in step 7 still comes to the stop before any coder delegation** (cited: ADR-0132 decision 6).
- **No round ≥ 1 reaches `case.write`, `round.end`, an evaluator or an experiment-runner delegation** under these steps; the steps after the report are a later record's (Boundaries).

### Walk-through

| Situation | What follows |
|---|---|
| Round 1 of a `cpu_standin` run after a verified baseline | The selector writes the record, calls `catalog.read` (four entries), chooses `loop_order_ikj` and reports `techniques: loop_order_ikj`. | The selector dies after the record was written | No `techniques:` line; step 7 returns the record; step 8 reports that no choice was given; the stop. The next turn delegates the selection again, meets `hypotheses_exist`, chooses and reports; the round goes on |
| The Round-Manager miscopies an id into one that names no entry | `technique_unknown`; nothing written; the coder-driver reports it; the stop. The next turn chooses again |

| `hypotheses.read` fails | Reported; the stop; no coder delegation (ADR-0132 decision 6) |
| The Round-Manager or the coder-driver calls `catalog.read` | The caller check refuses it (ADR-0146 decision 6) |

On a machine without the image and the token of ADR-0137 decisions 4 and 5, every call ends in a kind and no round ≥ 1 kernel is produced (cited: the `default_registry` docstring).

- **The steps after the coder call in a round ≥ 1**: the checks, the measurement, the comparison (ADR-0140), the verdicts (ADR-0128), `case.write` and the closing steps (PLAN P2-1).
- **The lookup read tool** (ADR-0132 decision 5; PLAN P2-7); **the reads of earlier verdicts and measurements** a selection needs (ADR-0132 Boundaries).
- **N > 1** (OD-29), **repairs** (OD-19), **a second call or spec for one tuple** (OD-23 ① (가), OD-8 ㉖ (가)).
- **Hooks on the order of the steps** and a check of a request's `run_id` against the session's run (rest of OD-18; PLAN P2-4).
- **The catalog of other accelerators, kinds, dtypes, layouts and languages** (OD-22), and `catalog.read` once `Applies to` widens; **the Skill form of the selection criteria** (FR-8; ADR-0139 decision 4); `decision.md` and its writer; statistics (OD-7).
- **Owner-only**: the models of the orchestration's agents (#5 A-8); C-6, OD-25, OD-3.
- Runs outside ADR-0151 decision 1; any edit of an earlier record, of FR or NFR text, UsageCases, `catalog/`, CI, `.claude/` or any settings file; any new external dependency.
