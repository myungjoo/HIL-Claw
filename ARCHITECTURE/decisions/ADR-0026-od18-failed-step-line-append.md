## Context

- ADR-0019 gave a kind to what a call finds at the lookup (③) and at the selection (④) and left one gap by name: "Not in the table: a failure inside the append of the start line or of the end line (⑤, ⑦). The sentence of decision 2 cannot be said of it … This record gives it none of the four kinds and no other. Registered as sub-question ⑨; until it is answered such a failure reaches a caller as the code has it today" (decision 3).

A file system that is full at ⑦, after the step has run, therefore reaches a caller as a defect of the tool, and nothing in the answer says whether the step ran. The tests of these paths pin that the error passes through, not a kind (`tests/test_journaled_step.py`).
- **Fixed and not re-decided.** The form of a tool failure, the difference between a result that reports a failed experiment and a tool failure, no check in a front, no process that remembers an earlier answer (ADR-0010 decisions 3, 4 and 7). The two step lines, the identity, its three states, the repeated call, that an identity is never started twice, that no recovery is chosen for an open step, what an end line holds (ADR-0011 decisions 2 to 4, 6 and 7). The reserved kinds and the test for reserving one, the order ① to ⑦ of a call, that the lookup and the two lines are carried out in the tool function (ADR-0015 decisions 2 and 5, ADR-0016 decisions 2 and 3). The four run-file kinds, their sentence and the two moments they are raised at (ADR-0019). The line, append only, the torn tail and the durability statement (ADR-0006 decisions 2, 5, 6 and 10).

## Decision

A value below is **cited** when a source is named with it; every other value is **this record's own** choice — among them which failures are situations, the two kinds, their names and the sentence of each, that the step is not called after a failed start line, that no outcome is returned after a failed end line and that the answer has no place for one, the borders and the order of decision 5, the level of decision 6 and the rule for the message.

### 1. Scope and the situations

- This record is about a call of a journaled tool whose tool function carries out the two appends of a journaled step (cited: ADR-0015 decision 5), at the moments ⑤ and ⑦ of ADR-0016 decision 2. It defines no tool, and the terms of ADR-0011, ADR-0015, ADR-0016 and ADR-0019 are used unchanged.
- **A failed append** is an append of a step line that does not return because the file system, or what stands in the place of the log, refused it, while the process lives. The situations, as `hilclaw.events.append` has them on `main` ("Class today" is what is raised, to be mapped by the code task — decision 6):

| What fails at the append | Of the new line the log then holds (the code's, not the frame's) | Class today |
|---|---|---|
| The data root is no existing directory any more (it was one at ②) | nothing | `FileNotFoundError`, `NotADirectoryError` |
| What stands in the place of the log is no regular file | nothing | `events.CorruptEventsError` |
| The file system refuses the look at the place, the creation of the run directory or the opening of the file (no permission, read-only, full, an input / output error) | nothing | an `OSError` |
| It fails while a torn tail is looked for or removed | nothing; an earlier torn tail is removed or left | an `OSError` |
| It fails in the write of the line, in its flush or when the file is closed | nothing, a torn tail, or the complete line | an `OSError` |
| It fails in the request to make the line durable (`os.fsync`), after the line feed was handed to the operating system | the complete line, as a later read finds it | an `OSError` |

- **Not a situation** — each stays what it is today: a line the tool layer itself cannot form (`events.InvalidEventError` out of the append) and an outcome an end line cannot hold (`journaled_step.InvalidOutcomeError`: no end line is appended and the step stays open) — both are defects of the tool layer or of the tool, which is what `tool_crashed` means (ADR-0015 decision 2); an exception out of the step (⑥; cited: ADR-0019 decision 4); what the selection or `before_start` raises (④); the death of the process (cited: the three death points of ADR-0011 and of ADR-0015's Walk-through); a `BaseException` that is no `Exception`, which no kind answers (`hilclaw.tools.call`). A torn tail met at the append is removed and is no failure (cited: ADR-0006 decision 6). A run directory that is gone at the append is created again by the append and is no failure either (`hilclaw.events.append`; Limits).

### 2. What is established after a failed append

- **The log** (cited, with one reading). The line feed is the last byte written, and a complete line is never edited or removed (ADR-0006 decisions 5 and 6). ADR-0006 says of a death what it leaves; this record reads the same for a failure the process survives: the failed append edits and removes no complete line, and of the new line the log holds nothing, a torn tail — which is no line (ADR-0006 decision 6) — or the complete line. **Which of the three, and at which instruction, is the code's** (the table of decision 1): ADR-0006 decision 10 left the flush to the code task, and this record fixes nothing of how a line is appended.
- **After a failed start line.** *Frame property, this record's own*: **the step is not called.** A start line is "complete before the step begins to consume the resource" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 2), and of an append that did not return the tool layer does not know that. Where the log is as this call left it, the identity has **no line, or is open although nothing ran under it** — the state "may have run; outcome and amount unknown" that ADR-0011 decision 2 accepts for a death after the start line; the log cannot tell the two apart.
- **After a failed end line.** The step **has run to its end** — it returned an outcome or ended without one — and what it consumes is consumed (cited: the order of ADR-0016 decision 2; ADR-0011 decision 7, "a step that ended without an outcome has spent as well"). Where the log is as this call left it — the start line of this call still in it — the identity is **open, or ended**. Ended: the line holds the outcome and the amounts, and they are counted (cited: ADR-0011 decisions 3, 6 and 7). Open: the outcome and the amounts are in no line, no amount is counted for the step (cited: ADR-0011 decisions 6 and 7), and the outcome exists in the process of that call only.
- **What the answer does not establish**: which of its two states the identity is in; and **nothing of the identity at all when the log is not as this call left it** — the data root, the run directory or the file gone or replaced after the lookup or after the start line, which the first three rows of the table of decision 1 can be signs of. The failing call does not read the log again (Alternatives). The lookup of a later call establishes what then holds (decision 5).

### 3. The frame reserves two kinds

Reserved in the sense of ADR-0015 decision 2: one meaning in every tool, raised for no other situation. Only a tool function raises them, and only at the two appends.

| `kind` | Meaning, in every tool — the sentence a caller may rely on |
|---|---|
| `start_line_failed` | The append of the start line of this call failed. **This call did not call the step**: it called no operation, consumed nothing at any target and appended no end line. Where the log is as this call left it, the identity has no line or is open; the answer does not say which, and says nothing of the identity otherwise |
| `end_line_failed` | The append of the end line of this call failed. **This call ran the step to its end**, and the answer carries no outcome (decision 4). Where the log is as this call left it, the identity is open or ended; the answer does not say which, and says nothing of the identity otherwise |

- No reserved kind and no name in `hilclaw/` collides with the two (checked on 2026-10-09); each is a *name* of ADR-0001, and no tool's contract uses either: `hilclaw.tools.default_registry` is empty.
- **Why reserve at all** (the test of ADR-0015 decision 2). The situations belong to no tool: they arise in the part every journaled tool shares, and each sentence speaks of that part only, so a caller can rely on it without knowing the tool. The only kind they reach a caller with today says the tool is defective, and a full file system is no defect of it. And, as ADR-0019 decision 2 argues from `step_open`, a kind each tool named itself could also stand in the `failure` of an end line: a step that ended could then not be told from a call whose line failed.
- **Why two.** As ADR-0019 decision 2 did, this record splits where the fixed records already say something different, not by how a caller acts (Boundaries). Before a complete start line nothing is consumed (ADR-0011 decision 2); after the step, what was consumed is counted only when a complete end line holds it, and an open step's outcome and amount are unknown to whoever reads the log (ADR-0011 decisions 4, 6 and 7). With one kind, a caller would have to read a message that ADR-0010 gives no form in order to tell "this call consumed nothing" from "the step ran and its outcome may be in no line".
- **Why not more.** The cause is told by the message (decision 6). A split between "certainly no line" and "not known" would make the order of the instructions inside the append a property of the frame (decision 2).
- **What a kind lets a caller tell is all this record says of it.** What a caller, a role or a person does on either is not decided (Boundaries).
- **How this reads ADR-0015, ADR-0016 and ADR-0019.** The table of ADR-0015 decision 2 is "the part of every tool's contract that is common, not a closed list", and "a later record of the frame may add to the common part as long as no tool's contract uses the name, and none exists" (ADR-0016 decision 3). ADR-0019 decision 3 gave a failed append "none of the four kinds and no other" until sub-question ⑨ is answered; this record answers it. None of the three is edited.

### 4. The outcome of a step whose end line failed

- **A call whose append of the end line failed is the tool failure `end_line_failed` and returns no outcome**, and the answer has no place for the outcome or the amounts: no member is added to the error object (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4), and the message does not carry them (decision 6). The code returns none today (`hilclaw/journaled_step.py`); this record makes it a property of the frame.
- **Why** (from the fixed records). A first call "returns what the end line holds" (ADR-0015 and ADR-0016, Walk-through), and so does a repeated call (ADR-0011 decision 3). An outcome returned although no line holds it would be answered once and never again — the same request is `step_open` afterwards — so the outcome of a call would depend on something that is not in the data plane (against ADR-0011 decision 3 and ADR-0010 decision 7). Its amounts would be in no line: a caller would go on with a result while the log shows its step as open and counts nothing for it (ADR-0011 decisions 6 and 7). And the line of an expensive result "carries the result itself" so that the result file can be written from the line (ADR-0006 decision 7); without the line there is nothing to write it from.
- **How this reads ADR-0010 decision 4** ("A tool failure says that no outcome was established"). ADR-0011 decision 3 calls an open step a tool failure "since no outcome is established", although whether an outcome existed is not known (its decision 4). This record reads the word the same way, for the answer of a call: a call of a journaled tool establishes an outcome for its caller only when a complete end line holds it. Neither record is edited.
- **When the line is complete after all**, the identity is ended and a later call returns what the line holds (decision 5). The failing call is `end_line_failed` all the same: it does not know, and what ADR-0006 decision 10 guarantees is said of an append that has returned.
- **Not decided**: whether anything keeps an outcome that the process holds and no line does. This record chooses no second place for an outcome and rejects none beyond the answer itself (sub-question ⑪).

### 5. The same request again, and the borders

- **The same request again** (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3; ADR-0019 decision 3; for an open identity also the Walk-through of ADR-0015). The failed call left in the data plane what decision 2 lists and nothing in a process: its answer is kept nowhere (ADR-0010 decision 7). A later call with the same request, the same `execution` included, is answered by its lookup alone, after ② (`no_data_root` while the data root is gone): a situation of ③ when one then holds; **no line** — the call goes on to ④ and ⑤ and, when its start line is appended, the step runs; **open** — `step_open`, and nothing runs or is appended; **ended** — the stored `result` or `failure`. Nothing is added to that. In particular an identity that is open after `start_line_failed` is answered `step_open` like every open identity, and nothing here says that it may be run again (Boundaries).
- **A torn tail** changes no answer (cited: ADR-0006 decisions 5 and 6): the one a failed append left is no line, is skipped by the read and removed by the next append; an earlier one that the failed append removed took no line with it.
- **`step_open`** is the answer of the lookup only, for an identity found open: that call ran nothing and appended nothing (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3; ADR-0015, Walk-through). The two kinds are answered only by a call whose own lookup found no line. After either of them the identity can be open; the kinds differ in what the *call* did.
- **The four run-file kinds** stay the answers of a read at ③ or ④ (cited: ADR-0019 decision 2). The same cause met at an append — a data root that is gone, a place of the log that holds no regular file, a refusal of the file system — is answered with the kind of the append, since their sentence cannot be said of it. **`no_data_root`** stays the answer of ②: a repeated call asks the source again and is answered there while the directory is gone (as ADR-0019 decision 4).
- **`tool_crashed`** stays what ADR-0015 decision 2 made it, and stays the answer for an exception that is no tool failure and no situation of decision 1 (its "Not a situation" bullet, and any other exception out of an append — Limits). **`invalid_request`, `no_backend`, `not_offered`, `bad_result`** are untouched.
- **The order** in which one call can meet the kinds is that of ADR-0019 decision 4, continued: … `not_offered`; then `start_line_failed` (⑤); then, the step having run, `end_line_failed` (⑦); else what the end line holds.

### 6. Where the answer is given, and what the message carries

- **Below the fronts** (cited: ADR-0010 decision 3), **in the tool function, at the two appends**, where ADR-0015 decision 5 placed the two lines.
- **The property is fixed, not the code**: a call that meets a situation of decision 1 is a tool failure of the kind of its moment, alike through both fronts, and none of them reaches a caller as `tool_crashed`. Which classes are mapped, in which module, and whether `hilclaw.events.append` raises other classes, is the code task's; how a line is appended is unchanged.
- **Not in `hilclaw.tools.call`, by class** (the reason of ADR-0019 decision 5): that level does not know the moment. An `OSError` out of the step leaves the step open and is none of these situations, and one class is two kinds at the two appends.
- **For which tools**: every tool function that carries out the two appends. On `main` both builders, `hilclaw.journaled_tool.tool_function` and `hilclaw.hw_tool.tool_function`, go through `hilclaw.journaled_step.run_prepared`.
- **The message** follows ADR-0019 decision 6 with the log in the place of the frame file: it names `runs/<run_id>/events.ndjson` by its path relative to the data root and says what was found there or what the file system reported. It carries neither the outcome nor an amount (decision 4). The wording is the code's; a caller tells the cases apart by the kind and never by the message.

### Walk-through

One call of the journaled tool `example.tool` (a placeholder, as in ADR-0015 to ADR-0019) for an identity with no line. The first six rows are for a data root, a run directory and a log that stay as the call left them, and for a later call whose own appends do not fail; the last row is for the rest. The states of the log were produced once with a throw-away script on a temporary directory (not committed).

| The append that fails | The log afterwards | The answer | The same request afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⑤, before a byte of the line is written | as it was | `start_line_failed`; the step was not called | No line: the step runs for the first time |
| ⑤, part of the line is written | a torn tail | `start_line_failed`; the step was not called | No line: the tail is skipped, and the start line of that call removes it |
| ⑤, after the line feed | the start line | `start_line_failed`; the step was not called | `step_open`, although nothing ran |
| ⑦, before a byte of the line is written | the start line | `end_line_failed`; the step has run; no outcome is returned | `step_open`; the outcome and the amounts are in no line |
| ⑦, part of the line is written | the start line and a torn tail | the same | `step_open` |
| ⑦, after the line feed | the start line and the end line | `end_line_failed` | The stored `result` or `failure`; nothing runs, and the amounts are counted once |
| ⑤ or ⑦, and the data root, the run directory or the log is gone or replaced | not as the call left it | the kind of the moment | `no_data_root` at ② while the data root is gone; else what the lookup then finds: a situation of ③ when one holds (for example `run_file_missing` without `STATE.json`, `run_file_invalid` while the place of the log holds no regular file); for a log that is gone and no situation, no line — after ⑦ too, although the step ran; for a log replaced by another, whatever that one holds |

### 7. Limits

- **Nothing repairs or marks the run, and the answer is kept nowhere.** A caller that lost the answer — a turn that died — learns the state from a later call only, and for an open identity that call does not tell it whether the step ran (ADR-0011 decisions 2 and 4).
- **A failure after the line feed** leaves, after `start_line_failed`, an open identity under which nothing ran and which is not started again (ADR-0011 decision 3), and after `end_line_failed` an ended one.
- **A spend that is not counted** (cited: ADR-0011 decision 7): after `end_line_failed` with an open identity the total is a lower bound and the spend view lists the step. Nothing here counts or corrects the amount.
- **A failure that lasts.** A full or read-only file system fails every later journaled call of the run that reaches ⑤ in the same way, and nothing records it: no line can, and no file marks a run. The lookup writes nothing, so ended and open identities are still answered as far as the files can be read. When only ⑦ fails, each further identity consumes and stays open; the frame stops no caller (OD-19, OD-10). Whether a failure is lasting is not judged by a kind.
- **Two processes** (cited: ADR-0004 decision 10, ADR-0006 decision 10, ADR-0011 decision 9): the lookup and the appends are not atomic and no lock is taken; a second appender can remove the unfinished append of this call. This record's own, as ADR-0019 decision 7 says it of a read: two processes can differ in what the file system lets them write.
- **A log that does not stay as the call left it.** The sentences about the identity hold for a log that stays (decisions 2 and 3). When the data root, the run directory or the file is gone or replaced, a later call is answered by what its lookup then finds, and that can be "no line" for an identity whose step ran: the step is then run again, and the first spend is in no line. Nothing here prevents it; ADR-0011 decision 9 says the same of lines lost with a machine before the commit: "the total is lower than the truth and the step runs again".
- **A run directory that went after the lookup** and is gone at the append is created again by it (ADR-0004 decision 1: "A directory exists only when something was written into it"; `hilclaw.events.append`), so that append does not fail. At ⑦ the new log then holds an end line without its start line, which the fold counts and reports (ADR-0011 decision 6), and a later call is `run_file_missing` for the absent `STATE.json`.
- **Any other exception out of an append** — one that is no failure of the file system or of the place of the log — is still `tool_crashed`, which does not say whether the step ran (as for an exception out of the step, ADR-0019 decision 4).
- **A step or a tool that states one of the two kinds for something else** is not detected (as ADR-0015 decision 7 says of every reserved kind).

## Alternatives considered

| Point | Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | (a) No new kind: a failed append stays `tool_crashed` | Its reserved meaning is false for a file system that is full, and a caller cannot tell whether the step ran. |
| 3 | (b) One kind for both moments | The meaning would be "the step did not run, or it ran", told apart by a string without a form (decision 3). |
| 3 | (d) One of the four kinds of ADR-0019 | Their sentence — no line appended, the step not run — is false after ⑦, and after ⑤ whether a line was appended is not known; they are raised at a read (ADR-0019 decisions 2 and 5). |
| 3 | A kind per cause, or a kind for "certainly no line" | See decision 3: the message says the cause, and the second would fix the inside of the append. |
| 2 | After a failed append the call reads the log again and answers by the state it finds | A second read at the moment the file system has just failed, with failures of its own to answer; an identity found open would be answered `step_open`, which says that the call ran nothing (false after ⑦); and one found with no line could only be answered or appended again, which is a retry (Boundaries). |
| 2 | The step is called although the start line failed | Against ADR-0011 decision 2: a step would consume with no complete start line. |
| 4 | The outcome is returned in spite of the failed end line | See decision 4: answered once and never again, and its spend in no line. |
| 4 | The outcome, or the amounts, in the message or in a new member of the error object | A member changes the form of ADR-0010 decision 4; a message is free text nothing may be read from (ADR-0019 decision 6), and an amount taken from it would be an accounting beside the log (ADR-0011 decision 6). |
| 6 | The mapping in `tools.call`, by exception class | See decision 6: the moment is not known there. |

## Consequences

- **The code slice that can now be cut** (code mode, within the size cap): the two kinds beside the eleven of `hilclaw/tools.py` and in `RESERVED_KINDS`; the mapping of the situations at the two appends in `hilclaw.journaled_step.run_prepared`, by moment and with `InvalidEventError` left out; and the tests of `tests/test_journaled_step.py` that today expect the `OSError` itself (the tests around `fail_append`, `test_a_class_of_a_row_out_of_an_append_passes_through`, `test_a_failed_start_line_after_before_start_does_not_call_the_step`), which can then pin the kind, the log and the answer of the same request for each row of the Walk-through. Where and how is the code task's.
- **Docstring sentences that then change**: in `hilclaw/journaled_step.py`, "A failed append passes through. …" (the whole bullet), "Only the reads and the lookup are answered this way: the same classes out of `before_start`, out of the step or out of an append are not", and in `run`, "and what `hilclaw.events.append` and `step` raise, unchanged"; in `hilclaw/run_files.py`, "says nothing of a failed append (OD-18 sub-question 9)"; in `hilclaw/hw_tool.py`, "the same classes out of the selection, out of the step, out of an operation or out of an append are not"; in `hilclaw/tools.py`, the comment that names the records of the reserved kinds. `hilclaw/events.py` stays true as it is.
- **What the conformance tests of P1-1 gain**: the rows of the Walk-through through both fronts, with the kind pinned; "that every outcome is one the tool's contract names" (ADR-0016 Consequences) can then be checked at ⑤ and ⑦ too. A script of the mock cannot cause either failure — of the three death points a backend can cause one, and "the other two lie in the journaled path" (ADR-0016 decision 5 (d)) — so a test produces them at the file system or at the append; the mock is unchanged.
- **Not edited and now less exact** (follow-ups for the integrator): ADR-0019 — its Status ("what a call answers when the append of a step line fails"), decision 3 (last bullet), decision 4 ("a failed append reaches a caller this way today"), decision 7 ("then fail an append (sub-question ⑨)"), the row of its Alternatives on a failed append, and its Boundaries; the kind tables of ADR-0015 decision 2, ADR-0016 decision 3 and ADR-0019 decision 2, none of which holds every reserved kind. No sentence of UsageCases became false.
- **A-13 reading**: document mode; no code, dependency, settings file or schema file changes. The record continues the interface between layer 1 and layer 2 and names no member of a data-plane file: the lines, the envelope and the two event kinds keep ADR-0006 and ADR-0011 as they are, and the error object keeps the form of ADR-0010.

## Boundaries (not decided here)

- **What anybody does after the answer**: running an open step again, a further `execution` and who may ask for one, waiting, giving up, asking the user, a retry policy — a second try of the append inside the call included —, a health check, freeing space, repairing or truncating the log, marking a run as broken, notifying a person (OD-10 — it needs #5 B-2; the retry part and the sub-questions of OD-17; OD-19; OD-23; OD-27; PLAN P2-1, P2-4).
- **The spend of a step whose end line failed**: how an amount that is in no line is ever counted or corrected (OD-17 sub-question ②; OD-2, OD-19). Decision 7 of ADR-0011 is cited and no rule is added.
- **The append and the log**: a lock, a second writer and any atomicity of the lookup and the append (rest of OD-17; ADR-0004 decision 10); any change of how a line is appended, flushed or made durable (ADR-0006 stays as it is); a second file or place for an outcome.
- **Tools**: the contract of any tool — arguments, result members, its own kinds, `step` values, which tool is journaled; which HW tools `cpu_standin` offers (sub-question ⑦); the mock in a live session (⑧); sub-questions ①, ②, ③, ⑤, ⑥ and ⑩; a call about no run (OD-26).
- **Rest of OD-18 and beside it**: hooks; gates (OD-3 — owner-only); budget checks (OD-19); the roster (OD-23); the isolation means; access control.
- **Data plane and form**: any member, value list or validity rule of a data-plane file; any event kind beyond the two of ADR-0011; a member of the error object; any new external dependency.
