# ADR-0102: How a change in the unit or meaning of an axis of `spend` is told apart — the definition of an axis never changes and its name is never reused, so a changed axis is a new axis, the fold and `budget_spent` keep the two totals apart, and the axis names used are listed in one index, `ARCHITECTURE/SpendAxes.md` (part (나) of sub-question ㉟ of OD-8)

## Context

- **The open point.** "(나) How a change in the unit or meaning of an axis of `spend` is told apart, given that the fold of ADR-0011 decision 6 sums an axis across lines (with FR-3, OD-19, #5 A-8)" (cited: ADR-0097 Boundaries). "A new axis, or a change in the unit or meaning of an axis, is therefore **not a change in the `data` of `step_ended`** … It is not harmless: the fold of ADR-0011 decision 6 sums the amounts of an axis across lines, so a unit changed under the same axis name would be summed silently with the old one. How such a change is told apart (a new axis name, or otherwise) belongs to the axis definitions" (cited: ADR-0097 decision 4).
- **What the records hold.** "Fold: over a sequence of lines in their order, the sum per axis of every `spend`"; the stored view is "the totals, the **position** … and the open steps" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 6). "An amount is an exact integer in the unit of its axis … The unit is the axis definition's"; "From where the tool layer has an amount belongs to the tool's contract and to the definition of the axis" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 7). `totals` is an "object from an axis *name* to a **total**"; "An axis is in `totals` exactly when an end line of the folded prefix named it"; "`budget_spent` is closed" (cited: ADR-0012 decision 4).
- **No axis exists.** ADR-0011 "names **no axis, no unit and no party that measures**" (cited: decision 1). "No record names an axis that is accounted through the log"; "the `spend` of an end line of this tool is the empty object" (cited: [ADR-0040](ADR-0040-od18-correctness-check-call-identity.md) decision 6). `round_budget` and `wall_clock_budget_s` are names of members of `instance.json` "and nothing else", and their records name "no axis of the spend view" (cited: [ADR-0032](ADR-0032-od8-instance-record-round-budget.md) and [ADR-0033](ADR-0033-od8-instance-record-wall-clock-budget.md) decision 3).
- **Why now.** The log "is appended to for the whole life of a run and is never rewritten, while the tool layer may be replaced at a round boundary (NFR-7 ①)" (cited: ADR-0006 decision 8), and runs are resumed (FR-6). An amount written under one definition of an axis stays in the log after the definition has changed, and the fold adds it to whatever is later written under the same name. The rule has to exist before the first axis does: it cannot be applied afterwards to lines already written (ADR-0006 decision 5). The sibling questions were answered the same way: event `data` (ADR-0097), tool results (ADR-0100) and failure kinds ([ADR-0101](ADR-0101-od8-failure-kind-change-and-step-values.md)).

## Decision

### 1. What the code does today (observation, binds nothing)

- `hilclaw/journal.py`: `fold` adds each amount of an end line's `spend` to the total of the same name (`totals[axis] = totals.get(axis, 0) + amount`); `_check_end` checks `spend` as an object whose member names are *names* and whose amounts are *integers* from 0, and knows no list of axes; the module "names no step, no axis and no result member, compares no total with a limit" (cited: its module docstring).
- `hilclaw/run_state.py` checks `budget_spent.totals` as names to integers from 0 and knows no list of axes.
- `hilclaw/check_tool.py`, the one journaled tool function built in `hilclaw/`, ends every step with the empty `spend` (`return {}, result`; `StepFailed({}, …)`); `hilclaw/journaled_step.py` passes the `spend` a step returns into the end line.
- No module of `hilclaw/` names an axis. The names in tests (`example_axis`, `other_example_axis`, `a_axis` and the like) and in records (`example_axis`, ADR-0011 and ADR-0012 examples) are placeholders.

### 2. Terms

- **An axis of the log** is a budget axis whose amounts are stated in the `spend` of end lines (ADR-0011 decision 7). Whether a given quantity is one is not decided here (Boundaries).
- **The definition of an axis** is what fixes it under its *name*: at least its unit — what one unit of an amount is — and its meaning — what an amount counts, what it includes, and when it is taken as consumed —, and whatever else the defining source states of it, the party or means that measures included where it states them. It is stated by an accepted record; where a part of it is an owner decision (for the token element, should it be an axis of the log, #5 A-8), the record renders that decision, and the rule below binds the record.

### 3. The rule: a changed axis is a new axis, and a name is never reused

- **The definition of an axis is fixed when the record that defines it is accepted, and it never changes.** A later record that wants amounts of another unit or meaning **defines a new axis under a *name* of ADR-0001 that is not in the index of decision 6 and is no placeholder of a record or a test**, and states its definition in full.
- **A name is never reused.** An axis that no tool states amounts of any more keeps its name and its definition; no record gives the name another definition. This holds also for the name of an axis whose defining record is reverted: lines may hold it and are never removed (ADR-0006 decision 5).
- **How an amount is told apart**: by its axis name alone. Two amounts under one name have one definition, in any log and at any time. Nothing beside the amount — no unit or version member in `spend`, no member of `data`, of the envelope, of `STATE.json` or of `instance.json` — says which definition applies; the name is the only key.
- **A relation between the two axes** — for example that one unit of the new axis is a fixed part of one unit of the old — may be stated by the record that defines the new axis. It is information for a reader and is never applied by the fold or the stored view (decision 5).
- **What it does not fix**: the spelling of a new name (no suffix or pattern), which axes exist, their units and meanings, who measures (Boundaries).
- **Why the name** (this record's own). `spend` is a closed map from a name to an integer, and the fold already keys every total by that name: a new name keeps two definitions apart with no new member, value form or kind. It is the shape of ADR-0097 decision 2 for event kinds and of ADR-0101 decision 3 for failure kinds.

### 4. What counts as a change

- **Any change of the definition is a change**, with no distinction between kinds: a change of the unit, its scale included (for example a tenfold finer unit of the same quantity); a change of what an amount counts or includes, a narrowing or a widening included; a change of when an amount is taken as consumed or of how a measured quantity is brought to an integer, where the definition states it; a change of the measuring party or means, where the definition states it.
- **What is not a change**: a value the definition leaves open being used — a new tool or step that begins to state amounts of an existing axis, or a tool contract that obtains amounts from another source, as long as what it states is an amount of that definition (ADR-0011 decision 7 gives "from where" to the tool's contract and the axis definition); a change of code that keeps the definition; a new axis being defined; an axis whose amounts are no longer stated; the move of a quantity out of or into the log (ADR-0011 decision 7), which defines or stops using an axis and changes none.
- **Why no distinction** (this record's own). *A sum mixes whatever it is given*: the fold adds amounts under a name whatever wrote them (decision 1), so a scale or a scope changed under one name gives a total that is neither the old quantity nor the new, and a later "compatible" reading cannot separate the parts. *A change of meaning cannot be detected* in an integer. Unlike ADR-0101 decision 5, where a case placed under a kind is no change when the kind's meaning covers it, an amount has no case to place: it is a quantity in the unit, and only the definition says what it is.

### 5. What the fold and the stored view do with amounts on both sides

- **They keep them apart, by name.** The fold sums each name separately (ADR-0011 decision 6), so amounts under the earlier axis give one total and amounts under the later one another; no rule of the fold or of `budget_spent` converts, adds or compares the two. Each total sums only amounts written under its own definition, whichever source wrote the lines — also for amounts written under the earlier name, after the change, by a process on an earlier source (NFR-7 ①, FR-6).
- **`budget_spent` needs no new member or version.** The earlier axis stays in `totals` of every later view of the run, with its last total, since "An axis is in `totals` exactly when an end line of the folded prefix named it" (cited: ADR-0012 decision 4) and the folded prefix only grows; the later axis enters with its first end line.
- **What a budget is compared with** across such a change — whether a stated budget counts the later total alone, or both through a relation a record states — is enforcement and is not decided here (OD-19; Boundaries). The fold and the stored view give the parts; they combine nothing.

### 6. The axis names are listed in one index, `ARCHITECTURE/SpendAxes.md`

- **There is one list of axis names, the file `ARCHITECTURE/SpendAxes.md`.** It is an **index**: one row per defined axis, with three columns — the name, a link to the record that defines it (with its decision), and the state.
- **The states** are those of `ARCHITECTURE/StepValues.md` (ADR-0101 decision 7): `defined` — the defining record is accepted; `not used` — an accepted later record, linked in the row, says no tool of the current contracts states amounts of it any more (a process on an earlier source still may, decision 5) (for example when it is replaced under decision 3); `reverted` — the defining record has been reverted. A row is never removed and its name never changes.
- **What the index does not hold**: no unit, no meaning and no measuring party; the defining record is the only source and wins when the two disagree.
- **This record creates no file** and no axis exists, so the index has no row. It is created, with a link in the README document table, by a later document task, or, if none has done so, by the commit that lands the first record that defines an axis.

### 7. How a name enters the index

- **A record that defines an axis** chooses a *name* of ADR-0001 that is not in the index and is no placeholder of a record or a test (decision 1), checks that the string is no other name in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/` (as ADR-0101 decision 8 asks of a `step` value), and **adds its row** with the state `defined` **in the commit that lands the record**.
- A record that stops an axis being stated sets its row to `not used` and links itself; a record that lets a tool state amounts of a `not used` axis again sets it back to `defined` and links itself, the definition unchanged; a task that reverts a defining record sets the row to `reverted`.
- No row is added by code, by a test or by a run. **Nothing checks a line's axis names against the index**: `hilclaw/journal.py` checks the *name* form only (decision 1), and the guard is the records and their review, as for `ARCHITECTURE/StepValues.md`.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | What the fold does with amounts on both sides of a change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) A new axis under a name never used for every change of a definition | Two totals under two names | **Adopted** (decisions 3 to 5). No new member, value form or kind; the fold already keys by name. Its cost is in Consequences. |
| (b) A unit or a version carried with the amount (for example `{"axis": {"amount": 7, "unit": "…"}}`, or a `spend_version` member) | Sums by name and unit, or by name and version | A unit or version held with each amount changes the closed form of `spend` (ADR-0011 decision 7) and of `totals` (ADR-0012 decision 4), which `hilclaw/journal.py` and `hilclaw/run_state.py` reject (decision 1); a marker as a member of `data` is outside the closed member set of `step_ended` (ADR-0097 decision 1), which `hilclaw/journal.py` rejects; the first two are data-plane schema changes (CLAUDE.md A-13) for no axis that exists, and a marker as a member of `spend` itself would pass as an axis and be summed by the fold. A unit string needs a vocabulary of units and still says nothing of a change of meaning; a fold keyed by name and unit is a name in two parts. A rule that an absent marker means the first definition would be implicit, as ADR-0097 (b) found of "absent means 1". |
| (c) Axes never change and have no successor | One total that stays right | The name part is adopted. As a rule that a quantity once defined never gets another axis, rejected: FR-3's vocabulary and the decisions it waits on (#5 A-8 — owner; OD-2) can be revised, and a revised quantity would have nowhere to go but under the old name, which is the silent mixing this record prevents. |
| (d) Leave it to each axis definition | Undecided | The fold is common to every axis and sums by name whatever wrote the line, so one definition that changed in place would corrupt the totals of a run for every reader; the choice could not be made afterwards for lines already written (ADR-0006 decision 5). Some quantities of FR-3 wait on an owner decision (#5 A-8), and a rule left to each definition would be one more question put to it. |
| (e) A table of units per run in `instance.json` | One sum, read under the run's table | `instance.json` is written once (ADR-0006 decision 8, citing ADR-0004), so it cannot follow a tool layer replaced within the run (NFR-7 ①), and adding the member is a data-plane schema change. |
| (f) A new event kind or a raised `schema_version` per axis change | — | A change of an axis is not a change in the `data` of `step_ended` (ADR-0097 decision 4), so no new kind follows; `schema_version` is not raised by a change in the `data` of a kind (ADR-0006 decision 8) and was kept for the envelope by ADR-0097 decision 2 and (c). Not re-weighed. |
| (g) For the list: an index like `StepValues.md` | — | **Adopted** (decisions 6 and 7), as an index only. Its cost: a commit that forgets the row leaves it behind the records. |
| (h) For the list: the code constants of `hilclaw/`, or no list | — | Records define axes before code binds them, a name of a reverted record stays used whether or not code ever bound it, and "axis" and the likely quantity words are common in the records, so a search would check by memory (the arguments of ADR-0101 (f) and (g)). |

## Consequences

- **No code changes.** `hilclaw/journal.py` and `hilclaw/run_state.py` stay right: they key totals by name and know no list of axes, which is what decision 5 needs. `hilclaw/check_tool.py` keeps the empty `spend`. A later record that defines an axis brings its own code task. No code task follows from this record.
- **For a record that defines an axis**: it states the unit and the meaning (decision 2), chooses and checks the name (decision 7), adds the row, and states its A-13 reading. **For a record that changes one**: it defines a new axis, sets the earlier row to `not used` when no tool of the current contracts states amounts of it any more, may state a relation between the two (decision 3), and leaves to OD-19 what a budget is compared with.
- **Not edited and now less exact**: ADR-0097 decision 4 ("How such a change is told apart (a new axis name, or otherwise) belongs to the axis definitions") and its Boundaries (㉟ (나)); ADR-0100 and ADR-0101 Boundaries (㉟ (나)); ADR-0011 decision 7 ("The unit is the axis definition's" — and the definition of a name is now fixed). No earlier ADR is edited.
- **The cost, stated**: axis names accumulate over the life of the system; the totals of a run that crosses a change are split, and a reader that wants one quantity across the change must know both names and any stated relation; a definition changed in place by mistake is undetectable in the log, and only review of the records guards against it.
- **A-13 reading**: document mode; no code, dependency, settings file or schema file changes, and no member, value or kind of the data plane is added or changed.
- **Runtime acceptance items**: none.
- **Follow-up (document)**: create `ARCHITECTURE/SpendAxes.md` with no row and link it from the README document table (decision 6).
