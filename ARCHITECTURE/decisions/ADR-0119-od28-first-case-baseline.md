## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a source is named with it; it says what that source says. Every other sentence is **this record's own**.

### 1. Scope and terms

- **The runs** are those of ADR-0076 decision 1, the first case (the hw_profile names `cpu_standin` or the mock, and the op_signature is absent or one that ADR-0041 decision 5 covers), **that also meet two conditions**: (i) the run tools of ADR-0061 decision 1 cover them — `cpu_standin` in the local configuration of ADR-0038 decision 1, or the mock; (ii) they start from their own round 0, not resumed or branched from another run's state (S3; OD-8 sub-question ①). Other runs are outside, including the other runs of ADR-0076 decision 1 (for example `cpu_standin` placed on the LAN, #5 A-7 ③): nothing here is required or allowed of them.
- **To stand as the baseline of round 0** keeps the meaning of ADR-0113 decision 1: to be the measurement whose samples stand for the baseline of FR-1 in the comparisons of later rounds. **The baseline attempt** is the attempt whose measurement so stands. These phrases are this record's, not names of any file or member.

### 2. The baseline is the run's own round-0 bring-up code (a)

- **The baseline of such a run is a measurement of the run's own round-0 bring-up code.** That code is what a coder call of round 0 produces (cited: FR-1 "해당 HW에서 동작하는 코드 생성"; S0 step 4), and it is kept as an attempt of round `0000` in the run's tree, `rounds/0000/coder/candidates/<CC>/attempts/<AA>/`, with its build and run results at the same suffix below `measure/` and its evaluation below `eval/` (cited: ADR-0004 decisions 2 and 3). Nothing else is the baseline of such a run: no vendor-library kernel (decision 3), no best of an earlier run (decision 3) and no instance parameter that picks among them.
- **So a run of decision 1 whose round 0 gives a baseline has at least one attempt of round 0.** It is the attempt the hypotheses of round 1 may cite as `attempt` entries (ADR-0059 decisions 3 and 5). The `0000/01/01` of the Examples of ADR-0059, ADR-0073 and ADR-0075 is such an attempt where the first coder call of candidate 1 is the baseline attempt; a repair would give another `<AA>` (ADR-0004 decision 3), and which number it is is no part of this record.
- **The measurement is the baseline attempt's run at the board point**, by `board.run` under ADR-0061 decision 1 — a completed result of ADR-0063 decision 2. Reasons:
  - FR-1 makes the board point "최종측정" and the base of evaluate is the baseline (cited); ADR-0038 decision 1 calls the board point the "final verdict". This record reads that as making the board figure the one a later round's evaluate sets against the base; which figures later rounds actually compare, and which count for promotion or improvement, stays open (ADR-0061 decision 2; OD-15, OD-29, OD-13 sub-question ①). ADR-0007 decision 7 compares two measurements "of the same quantity", and a base taken at the board point needs no sim–board relation to be comparable with board figures.
  - C-4 makes the sim point the cheap early filter and the board the scarce resource (cited). The cost of this answer is one board run of one attempt in round 0. In the local configuration both points lie on the owner's machine (cited: ADR-0038 decision 1), so the cost there is that machine's time. For the mock, the run is scripted.
  - **The sim point is not chosen**, and "either" is not chosen. In the local configuration the two points "carry out the same local run; nothing but the execution point distinguishes the two operations" (**owner**, HQ-0005 (1), as ADR-0038 decision 2 quotes it), so there the choice changes no kind of measurement, though each operation times its own run. It matters where the points come apart, as in the configuration the owner foresaw for `cpu_standin` and did not decide (an emulated `sim.run` against a board of another ISA, OD-18 sub-question ⑫; ADR-0061 decision 1, "What could split them"): a sim-point base would then stand against board figures, which only a sim–board relation could make comparable (OD-15; #7 N7 asks the status of sim results). A base taken at the board point would not depend on how such a configuration relates the two points; whether this answer extends to it is for the record that adopts it.
- **Eligibility** is ADR-0113's (decisions 2 and 3) and is not restated: the mark "not converged" does not bar the measurement, and the bars it cites stay.

### 3. What is not the baseline of such a run, and what stays open (b, c)

- **(b) A vendor-library kernel.** OpenBLAS `cblas_sgemm`, the integrator's second candidate under #5 B-4, would be a new external dependency of the runtime, which is a BLOCKED reason (CLAUDE.md A-13). So this record **does not adopt it and does not exclude it**: it is not the baseline of a run of decision 1 under this record, and a later record that brings in the dependency through the owner may add it. **Whether a library figure is also recorded beside the baseline** — the proposal "두 값을 모두 기록" — is left open, together with the onboarding sub-question of UC-0 step 4. Neither is read into decision 2. nntrainer is not a baseline (**owner**, O1).
- **(c) An earlier run's best.** A best of another run, whether reached through its state or through a case (FR-18; ADR-0073), is not the baseline of a run of decision 1 (decision 2). A run resumed or branched from another run's state is outside this record (decision 1 (ii); ADR-0113 limit 5), and what its baseline is stays open.

### 4. The case answered: exactly one attempt can stand (d)

- **An attempt of round 0 can stand as the baseline** when its correctness checks passed at the sim point and at the board point (cited: FR-1, FR-9; #5 A-6, failed candidates "never enter `best`"), its board-point run gave a completed result, and no bar that ADR-0113 decision 3 cites applies.
- **This record answers only a round 0 in which exactly one attempt can stand and whose board point gave that attempt one completed result.** That measurement is the baseline. A round 0 with N = 1 meets it whenever its one candidate gets there, since a repair follows only a failed attempt (cited: FR-1 ⑴), unless a board-point failure that triage puts on the board (FR-1 ⑶) is followed by a further board run under OD-10 that leaves more than one completed result; P2-5 is N = 1.
- **Where exactly one round-0 attempt passes the sim point, this record requires that it is run at the board point**, since without that run there is no baseline (decision 2). It is a requirement on round 0 only, not a promotion rule (OD-15, OD-29) for any later round.
- **Left open, with the choice rule:** a round 0 that leaves more than one attempt that could stand (N > 1 in round 0, or more than one attempt passing the sim point), and an attempt with more than one completed board result. Which one stands is a choice among eligible measurements: the rest of OD-1 (#5 A-4, owner-only), with OD-18 sub-question ⑳ for several results of one attempt at one point, and OD-15 and OD-29 for which attempts reach the board point. ADR-0113 decision 3 leaves the counterpart about converged measurements — whether one is preferred where more than one could be the baseline — with the choice of baseline (OD-28) and OD-18 ⑳ (cited); this decision leaves that case open.

### 5. A round 0 with no attempt that can stand (e)

- **Not decided.** If bring-up fails up to the repair limit, or the one candidate fails at the board point (cited: FR-1 ⑵ "해당 후보 탈락"; ⑶ triage), or its board-point run gives only broken results (ADR-0063 decision 2), no attempt can stand. Whether the run stops, waits for the user, retries round 0 or goes on without a baseline is left open, with OD-19 (repair bound, termination) and OD-10 (failure and retry). This record does not say such a run has a baseline.

| Situation | What follows |
|---|---|
| P2-5: `cpu_standin`, N = 1. Round 0's first coder call passes sim and board; `board.run` returns `"converged"` | Attempt `0000/01/01` is the baseline attempt; its board measurement is the baseline (decisions 2, 4) |
| Same, but `board.run` returns `"not_converged"` | Still the baseline (ADR-0113 decision 2); the run is found imprecise (ADR-0113 decision 4) |

| Round 0 with N = 2, both candidates pass the sim point | Which reaches the board point and which stands is open (decision 4) |
| The one candidate fails at the board point | No attempt can stand; what follows is open (decision 5) |
| A run of the mock | The same rules; the figures are scripted (ADR-0061 decision 1) |
| A run branched from another run's round 4 | Outside this record (decision 1 (ii)) |
