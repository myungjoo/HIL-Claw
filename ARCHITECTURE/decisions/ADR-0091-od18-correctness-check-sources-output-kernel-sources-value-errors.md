## Context

- ADR-0090 left open every pre-start `ValueError` with no `parameter` — U1 to U6 of its N3 — because U2, U3 and U6, raised while an argument the operation forms is checked, and U4, which holds what `build` passes on from `subprocess`, share a class and no step of the operation's own tells them apart (cited: ADR-0090 decision 3). It named the way out, a `parameter` on U1 to U3 and U6, after which "what stays unnamed would be U5 and U4" (cited, same). Task T-0276 (PR #175, `1d61696`) made that change and placed nothing in a row.
- **Observations at `1d61696`** (no code is bound):
  - **K1. `build` names the checks of `sources` and `output`.** Its docstring: "Any `TypeError` or `ValueError` (including a subclass such as `UnicodeEncodeError`) from the checks of `timeout_s`, `flags`, `trailing_flags`, `sources`, `output`, `compiler`, `env` and `cwd`, including the one for no source and one that Python or the code of a handed object raises inside a check, such as for a value that cannot be iterated or a path object whose `__fspath__` fails, is raised with the attribute `parameter` set to the name of that parameter" (cited). The whole of `_checked_sources` — iterating the sources, testing each one's type, forming its absolute path, its path tests and the error for no source — runs inside `_parameter("sources")`, which sets `parameter` on a caught `TypeError` or `ValueError` and re-raises it unchanged. So U1 and U2 now carry `"sources"`. Inside `_parameter("output")` runs only the forming of the absolute path: "The check of `output` forms its absolute path; the tests of that path below are not part of it" (cited). So U3 carries `"output"` where the path is formed.
  - **K2. The tests of the artefact path** run after that step and outside any: `is_dir` of its directory, `exists` of it, and `is_dir` of the path, on a `pathlib.Path` that `build` made from the absolute path; no handed object's code runs in them, and an `OSError` of them carries `path_role` `"output"` and no `parameter` (cited for `path_role`: the docstring of `build`). The documentation of `pathlib` says that since Python 3.8 these methods return `False` instead of raising for a path holding characters the operating system cannot represent (cited; `pyproject.toml` requires 3.11 or later). On that statement no `ValueError` leaves these tests; this record did not establish it by running code.
  - **K3. `build_verification_executable`.** Its docstring: "any `TypeError` or `ValueError` (including a subclass) raised while iterating `kernel_sources`, carries the attribute `parameter` set to `"kernel_sources"` …; the `ValueError` for no kernel source carries none" (cited). So U6 carries `"kernel_sources"` and U5 none. It then calls `build` once with `PROGRAM_SOURCE`, a `Path` constant of its module, first among the sources, so U1 cannot arise through it (cited: ADR-0089 P4) and an error of the check of `sources` may concern the program source or a kernel source; "Whatever `hilclaw.clang_build.build` raises is passed on unchanged, with its own attributes" (cited).
  - **K4. What stays unnamed.** "A `TypeError` or `ValueError` from anywhere else, such as binding the call or creating the compiler process, carries no `parameter`" (cited: the docstring of `build`). Read from the code, the pre-start `ValueError`s that carry none are: of `build`, U4, one from after the check of `cwd` up to and including the start — the temporary name, the command, the clock, `subprocess.Popen` — whose classes under `subprocess` were not read; and of `build_verification_executable`, U5. Nothing runs in `build` before the first check. The tests of K2 would add a third only if the statement cited there did not hold.
  - **K5. `compiler_version`** still makes the checks of `timeout_s` and `compiler` outside any `_parameter`, and its docstring still says "except that they carry no `parameter`" (cited). No module of `hilclaw/` besides `clang_build` calls it, and no check of `correctness.check` does (cited for the latter: ADR-0089 P5).

## Decision

### 1. Scope and terms

- A call of `correctness.check`, the six kinds, S1 to S10 and the keeper, as ADR-0087 decision 1 has them. **A named `ValueError`** of this record is one whose `parameter` is `"sources"`, `"output"` or `"kernel_sources"` (K1, K3); **the unnamed rest** is U4 and U5 (K4). The operation reads the class (`isinstance`, so a subclass counts) and `getattr(error, "parameter", None)`, on the reading of ADR-0087 decision 2 that what it observes is what the function states of the error; ADR-0090 decision 1 read a `ValueError` the same way. **The sources** are the verification program and the compiled set the reader gave the operation, and **the artefact path** is the operation's own (cited: ADR-0087 decision 1; ADR-0060 decision 3; ADR-0056 decisions 3 and 4). **The mock** builds nothing and is unchanged.

### 2. A named `ValueError` of `"sources"`, `"output"` or `"kernel_sources"` is no row

- **The operation lets it pass: no row.** Each names an argument the operation forms, in which no setting of the backend's is held (decision 1). ADR-0087 decision 4 made the `TypeError` of `"sources"` and `"output"` no row and ADR-0089 decision 4 that of `"kernel_sources"`, after "a `TypeError` for an argument that is no setting … is a defect of the operation's own code and is no row" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4). ADR-0089 decision 3 read a `ValueError` for such an argument as no row as well, because the class follows how the value is wrong and not whose it is; **this record applies that reading to the three arguments**. An operation that lets the error pass is answered `operation_answer_invalid` (cited: ADR-0045 decisions 4 and 5); that is what the landed rules do with it, not an answer of this record.
- **Each case.** U1, no source: ADR-0060 decision 4 already lets "the `ValueError` for no kernel source or no source, which the reader's non-empty compiled set rules out" pass (cited); it now carries `"sources"`, which changes how the operation sees it and not where it goes, and it cannot arise through `build_verification_executable` (K3). U2, one raised inside the check of `sources` — while the sources are iterated, while a source's `__fspath__` runs, or while its absolute path is formed —, and U3, one raised while the absolute artefact path is formed, are refusals of a path value. U6, one raised while the kernel sources are made a tuple, is a refusal of the object the operation handed.
- **One raised inside a handed object's code** — an iterator of the sources, a source's `__fspath__` — **is no row as well.** It carries the same `parameter` as one the check raises itself (K1), so telling them apart would need where it was raised or its message, neither of which the functions state. The handed objects are the operation's own: the kernel sources come from the reader through the operation, and the program source is a constant of the harness (K3). A fault in their code is a defect of code; **this record reads it as no row**, as the cost of ADR-0087 decision 4 reads a defect of either side's code as a defect of code. This differs from ADR-0090 decision 2, where the handed object was a setting of the backend's and was read as S4 for that reason.
- **Not S5 and not S3.** S5 names "A file of the compiled set is no longer a regular file when the build is called, or the verification program is missing from the installation" and S3 "The build directory cannot be made or written" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4). A `ValueError` of these steps refuses a value before the file or directory of that value is tested; what a test finds of a source or of the artefact path is raised as an `OSError` with `path_role`, which ADR-0086 decisions 3 and 4 place in S5 and S3, and which this record does not revisit (K1, K2).
- **The cost, stated.** `"sources"` does not say whether the program source or a kernel source was refused (K3), and this record does not need it to, as both are no row. If the path of the installed program source could not be made absolute without a `ValueError` — this record did not read that it can — a fault of the installation, of the kind S5 names for a missing program source, would be answered as a defect of code. Likewise, if a platform let the operation make a build directory under a path whose absolute form raises a `ValueError`, a property of the machine would be answered as a defect of code; this record did not read that it cannot.

### 3. The unnamed rest stays open, narrowed to U4

- **What is unnamed**: U4 and U5 (K4). U5 the operation rules out by its own step, as it knows it handed a non-empty compiled set (cited for the step: ADR-0060 decision 4, "An operation places such an error by the step of its own at which it met it"; ADR-0090 decision 3).
- **What is left is U4 alone**: a `ValueError` from after the checks up to and including the start, which holds what `build` passes on from `subprocess`. That "was not read" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4) and stays open (cited: ADR-0083 decision 2); it may hold a setting `subprocess` refuses with a `ValueError`, which would be S4 by its meaning; whether one arises, for example for an `env` with no `items` method, which `build` leaves to `subprocess` (cited: its docstring), this record did not read. No step of the operation's own and no member of the error tells such a refusal from any other `ValueError` of U4.
- **So this record places no unnamed `ValueError`.** The two rows the class held under ADR-0090 decision 3 are no longer met together: the no-row cases now carry a `parameter` (decision 2). What stops a placement is no longer telling apart but reading: placing U4 in any row would read what `subprocess` raises into a row unread, the stop ADR-0087 decision 5 and ADR-0090 decision 3 kept. The entry now coincides with the `ValueError` part of the open entry "whatever `clang_build.build` still passes on from `subprocess`" (decision 7). Until it is placed, an operation that lets it pass is answered `operation_answer_invalid` (cited: ADR-0045 decisions 4 and 5); that is what the landed rules do, not an answer of this record. **The cost**: a setting that only `subprocess` refuses with a `ValueError`, if one arises, gets no S4.
- **What would let it be placed** (named, not decided): a reading of what `subprocess.Popen` raises for the arguments `build` hands it, in a later record; or a code change that makes `build` refuse such a setting in a named check before the start, as T-0267 did for `env` and `cwd`. Either is a later task's; this record binds none.

### 4. `compiler_version`

- **It stays a Boundary**: no check of `correctness.check` calls it (K5), so its unnamed errors reach no operation of a check.

### 5. No log, and the message

- **No log.** Every error of decision 2 is raised before a compiler process exists (K1, K3), so no compiler of that check ran and the operation has no log to hand over; the file of ADR-0078 is absent, which says "that no compiler of that check ran or that its log did not reach the tool function" (cited: ADR-0078 decision 2).
- **No message of `settings_refused`.** The operation raises no kind for these errors; the message is that of `operation_answer_invalid`, which carries neither the message nor the arguments of what was raised and may say of which kind the raised error was (cited: ADR-0045 decision 6). So the path or value a `ValueError` may show does not reach the log. No rule is added; the wording is the code's.

### 6. No data-plane change

- `parameter` is an attribute of an exception inside the tool function's process; it is no member of a file, a result, a request or a line. No row needs no kind. No value of `condition` or `error.kind` is added, no kind is reserved, and no `schema_version` or layout version changes. No case is left open for want of a new value: decision 3 is open because U4 was not read, and a new kind would not read it.

### 7. How far (마) is answered

- **Answered now, for `correctness.check` of the first case and of the mock**: a named `ValueError` with `parameter` `"sources"` or `"output"` of `build`, and with `"kernel_sources"` of `build_verification_executable` — no row, one raised inside a handed object's code included, with no log (decisions 2 and 5). With it U1 to U3 and U6 of ADR-0090 N3 are placed, and the `ValueError` of `build_verification_executable` for `kernel_sources`, which ADR-0089 decision 4 left out of its `TypeError` row, is placed by the same rule.
- **Narrowed**: the open entry "an unnamed pre-start `ValueError` of `build` or of `build_verification_executable`" (ADR-0089 decision 9), which ADR-0090 decision 7 narrowed to U1 to U6, now holds U4 and U5 only, of which the operation must place only U4, as it rules out U5 by its own step (decision 3). ADR-0090 decision 3's "What would let it be placed" is done, and its two-row reading of the class no longer holds for what reaches the operation.
- **Still open under (마)**, as ADR-0090 decision 7 lists them on `main`: that unnamed pre-start `ValueError`, now U4; an unnamed `TypeError` — of `build`, from binding the call or from `subprocess.Popen` or later, or from binding a call of `build_verification_executable`; whatever `clang_build.build` still passes on from `subprocess`, an `env` with no `items` method included; an unroled `OSError` of `build` before the start; the row of a `CompilerRunError` whose `timed_out` is false and whose `returncode` is `None`; and the `ValueError` or `TypeError` and the `OSError` for "a `cwd` that does not exist" of `local_run.run`, which wait for the record of `local_run.run`.
