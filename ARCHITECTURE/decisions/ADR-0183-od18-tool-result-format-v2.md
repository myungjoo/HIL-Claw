## Decision

### 1. Scope and terms

- **The calls**: every call of `correctness.check`, `sim.run` or `board.run` at every target the code serves today — a run whose hw_profile names `cpu_standin` in the local configuration ([ADR-0038](ADR-0038-od18-cpu-standin-execution-points.md) decision 1) or the mock — about a run whose stated `op_signature` is covered by an entry of `contract_table` admitted for that tool (ADR-0177 decisions 1 and 2): today the seven contracts for the check and fp32 `gemm` for the run tools, later every contract the table admits. **One format for every contract**, fp32 `gemm` included (**owner**, HQ-0078 ①).
- **Terms.** The *covering entry* of a call is the entry of `contract_table` whose `covers` answers `True` for the stated record (ADR-0177 decision 1). An *output operand* of a contract is an operand of its `task_contract` with the role `output` or `inout` (ADR-0002 decision 6). An operand is *judged by tolerance* when the verification record of its contract judges it by the element-wise predicate with `rtol` and `atol`, and *judged bit-exact* when that record compares it bit for bit; the table of Context gives the seven contracts' operands. A *v2 result* is a result under one of the four values of decision 2.
- **Form rules**, taken from ADR-0042 decision 1 unchanged (cited): a result is one JSON object in the strict sense, no member holds `null`, a member that does not apply is left out, and a string value is compared as it is. A *name* and an *integer* are those of ADR-0001.

- **One value per tool and point** (**owner**, HQ-0078 ①), and **the same value for every contract**: fp32 `gemm` and the six bf16 kinds share it, as one list serves both values of ADR-0040 (ADR-0100 decision 3, second bullet). The result definition of each value is decision 3 (the check) or decision 4 (the run tools), fixed when this record is accepted (ADR-0100 decision 3).
- **The spelling** (this record's own): each is the value its tool and point used until now, `correctness_check_sim`, `correctness_check_board`, `sim_run_timing`, `board_run_timing`, with `_v2` appended — *names* of ADR-0001 of 24, 26, 17 and 19 characters. The prefix keeps a value of one tool apart from every other tool's (ADR-0040 decision 3, ADR-0062 decision 3, cited for the manner); `v2` is the owner's name of the format, so a reader of a log sees which definition a line has. **The suffix is a part of a name, not a version**: nothing parses it, and no version member is added to a result (ADR-0042 decision 7, cited: "A result carries no version member"); a later definition takes another unused name, whatever its spelling (ADR-0100 decision 3). **Checked on 2026-10-29**: none of the four strings occurs in `hilclaw/`, `tests/`, `ARCHITECTURE/`, `orchestration/` or `catalog/`, and none is in StepValues or a placeholder of a record or a test (StepValues "Adding a row"). No two tools share a value (ADR-0015 decision 4, cited).
- **How a call chooses its value.** `correctness.check`: **from `point` alone** — `sim` gives `correctness_check_sim_v2`, `board` gives `correctness_check_board_v2` — the rule "the `step` of a call follows from the point its request states" (cited: ADR-0038 decision 4; ADR-0040 decision 3). `sim.run` / `board.run`: **from the tool alone** (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3). **No dtype, kind or read of `instance.json` chooses a value** (as ADR-0181 decision 4 already says for the run tools). The choice of ADR-0177 decision 3 — the bf16 value of the point when the read of ⓢ finds an `op_signature` whose `dtype` is `bf16` — and its rule that "a record covered in ④ whose `dtype` is not the one the `step` was taken for … is answered `op_signature_not_covered`" have no object under v2 and **are not applied**: in ④ a stated record is covered or not by `check_covers`, and `op_signature_not_stated` / `op_signature_not_covered` are answered as [ADR-0041](ADR-0041-od18-correctness-check-dimensions.md) decision 8 orders them (cited). Whether the code keeps the read of `instance.json` at ⓢ for anything else is the slice's.
- **The six values of today are retired** (**owner**, HQ-0078 ①): no contract calls a step under them once the slices of decision 9 have landed, and none is given to any tool again (ADR-0100 decision 3, cited). Their StepValues rows are set to `not used`, linking this record; the two bf16 values, which have no row, get theirs (fixed by ADR-0177 decision 3) with the same state.
- **Across the change** (cited: ADR-0100 decision 5): a call under a new value forms a new identity, never returns a result stored under a retired value and does not take an identity open under one as open. No stored run data exists (**owner**, HQ-0078 ①).

### 3. (b) The v2 result of `correctness.check`

- **A v2 result of `correctness.check` has the members of the table below and no other** (closed, in the sense of ADR-0042 decision 7, cited; only a record adds a member, and a record that does so takes a new `step` value, ADR-0100 decision 3).

| Member | Type and values | Present | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `outcome` | string: `"pass"`, `"fail"` or `"broken"` | always | ADR-0042 decision 2, unchanged; the pass rule over all operands below |
| `condition` | string: `"build_time_limit"`, `"build_exit_status"`, `"time_limit"`, `"exit_status"`, `"report_form"` or `"check_value"` | exactly when `outcome` is `"broken"` | ADR-0042 decision 3 as ADR-0060 decision 4 amends it, unchanged |
| `contract_key` | object with exactly `kind`, `dtype` and `layout`, each a string — the values of the covering entry | always | **owner** HQ-0078 ②; form below |
| `shape` | object with exactly the shape members of the kind of `contract_key`, each an integer ≥ 1 | always | **owner** HQ-0078 ③; form below |
| `draws` | integer ≥ 1 | always | ADR-0042 decision 5, unchanged |
| `tolerance` | object with exactly `rtol` and `atol`, each a JSON number, finite and not negative | always | ADR-0042 decision 5, unchanged; it gates the operands judged by tolerance |
| `counts` | object with exactly one member per output operand of the contract, named by the operand's name; each an array of `draws` entries in the order of the draws; an entry of an operand judged by tolerance is an object with exactly `failed` and `non_finite`, of one judged bit-exact an object with exactly `differs`, each an integer ≥ 0 | exactly when `outcome` is `"pass"` or `"fail"` | **owner** HQ-0078 ④; form below |

- **`contract_key`** (this record's own form of ②). An object, not a string or an array: the stated `op_signature` record names its `kind`, `dtype` and `layout` as members of those names (ADR-0001 decision 2, cited), so a reader compares the two member by member, and no separator or position is invented. The values are those of the covering entry, which are the stated record's own (`covers` matches them; ADR-0177 decision 1). **`language` is not a member**: ② names kind, dtype and layout, and the table holds at most one entry per kind, dtype and layout because a stated record never names the language (cited: ADR-0177 decision 1). *The name*: not `contract`, which in a spec is the whole contract (`task.contract`, ADR-0002 decision 6); not `key`, which is the hashed key of ADR-0001 (`op_signature.key`). `contract_key` is the table's key as ADR-0177 decision 1 uses the word ("keyed by"); it holds no digest. A result now says which contract it judged, which ADR-0177 decision 3 said it did not ("a result holds no `kind`, `dtype` or `layout`"; replaced by ②).
- **`shape`** (③). Its members are the shape members of the kind — the parameters of the contract's `task_contract`, which name the members of the stated record's `shape` (cited: ADR-0177 decision 1) — with the values of the stated record that the check used (ADR-0041 decision 2 as ADR-0177 decision 3 reads it per kind, cited). For fp32 and bf16 `gemm` it is exactly `m`, `k`, `n`, as ADR-0042 decision 5 had it. The order of the members in an object carries no meaning; the code writes them in `task_contract` order.
- **`counts`** (④). *Form* (this record's own): an object keyed by operand name, each member an array with one entry per draw. ④ says "`counts`는 계약의 출력·inout operand 이름별 항목", so the members of `counts` are the operand names; inside each, the per-draw array of ADR-0042 decision 4 is kept. *Which operands*: exactly the output operands of the contract (Context table: `c`; `y`; `c`; `out`; `out`; `cache_k`, `cache_v`, `out`), no input operand. *Which entry form*: by how the operand is judged — **by tolerance unless the verification record of the contract compares it bit for bit; today the bit-exact operands are exactly `cache_k` and `cache_v` of `sdpa_kv_cache`** (ADR-0165 decision 8). A later contract's verification record says how its operands are judged; an output operand no record judges cannot be in a v2 result, since its contract could not be `checked`.
  - **`failed`** is the number of elements of that operand in that draw that failed the predicate, **`non_finite`** how many of those were non-finite (ADR-0042 decision 4, cited, read per operand): `non_finite` ≤ `failed` ≤ the number of elements of the operand, the product of its `shape` in the contract for the stated dimensions (m·n or m·h·d in Context's table).
  - **`differs`** is the number of elements of that operand's allocation, as the verification of the contract compares it, that differ from what is expected after the call: for `cache_k` and `cache_v` of `sdpa_kv_cache`, the elements of the l + 2m rows of the allocation, guard rows included (ADR-0165 decisions 5 and 8), so `differs` ≤ (l + 2m)·h_kv·d. ADR-0165 decision 8's one count "over both allocations" is thereby split per allocation, as ④ asks of a per-operand entry; the sum of the two is that count. It holds no index, element or region within an allocation.
  - *Replaced*: HQ-0077's `cache_differs` (**owner**, HQ-0078 ④).
- **`outcome` of a completed run**: `"pass"` when, in every draw, every `failed` and every `differs` is 0; `"fail"` otherwise. It is ADR-0042 decision 4 and ADR-0177 decision 3 ("The outcome of such a check is judged over both counts") read over all output operands (cited for the verdict: ADR-0017 decision 7; ADR-0163 decision 8; ADR-0165 decision 8). So in a pass result every count is 0, and in a fail result at least one count is 1 or more.
- **Carried over unchanged** (**owner**, "그 밖의 멤버는 그대로 옮기고"): `outcome`, `condition`, `draws` and `tolerance`, with their names, values, presence rules and meanings (ADR-0042 decisions 2, 3 and 5; ADR-0060 decision 4); decision 6 of ADR-0042 (what a result never holds) and its Limits hold for every contract. **No member is renamed.**

- **Which measured values `unit` applies to** (this record's reading of ⑤). ⑤ puts "a unit member on the measured values" whose only value for now is `ns`. **It applies to `intervals` and `resolution`**, the two members ADR-0063 decision 4 calls "the measurement of a completed run", and to no other: `ns` is a unit of time, and the figure of `memory_bytes` is a number of bytes (ADR-0052 decisions 3 and 4, cited), which a unit whose only value is `ns` cannot describe. Whether the owner's later open list of units also reaches the memory figure is the owner's (Boundaries).
- **One `unit` for both.** The replay of the stopping rule runs over the intervals (ADR-0063 decision 4, cited), and the coarse-clock test of [ADR-0103](ADR-0103-od13-imprecise-timing-detection.md) decision 3 compares the resolution with the smallest interval (cited); both need the two in one unit, so one member states it for both.
- **Why the two members are renamed** (the only renames of this record). With the unit stated by `unit`, a name ending in `_ns` would state it a second time, and once the owner opens the list of units (⑤) the two statements could disagree — `intervals_ns` holding another unit. So `resolution_ns` becomes `resolution` and `intervals_ns` becomes `intervals`; their types, bounds, presence and meaning are ADR-0063 decision 4's. `memory_bytes` keeps its name: ⑤ does not reach it (above), and "그 밖의 멤버는 그대로 옮기고" (**owner**).
- **Presence of `unit`**: with the measurement it describes; a broken result holds no measurement (cited: ADR-0063 decision 3, after ADR-0009 decision 8) and so no `unit`. Its one value is the owner's; the value list is closed until the owner opens it (⑤; D23: a closed value list widened is a frame change).
- **Carried over unchanged** (**owner**): `outcome`, `condition`, `n_max`, `sample_form`, `memory_bytes`, `ceiling` and `bound` with their names, values, presence rules and meanings (ADR-0063 decisions 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7), and ADR-0063 decisions 8 (what a result never holds) and 11 (Limits) for every contract. A broken result holds no `unit`, `resolution`, `intervals` or `memory_bytes` (ADR-0063 decision 3, renamed members).

### 6. (c) The broken and tool-failure paths

- **Every v2 result has a known contract and shape.** A result is made only by a call that passed ④ — where the stated record is read and found covered — and reached the operation (check: ADR-0021 decision 5 and ADR-0041 decision 8; run tools: ADR-0061 decision 7 and ADR-0067 decision 5; cited). So `contract_key` and `shape` are present in every result, broken ones included, with the values ④ read; with them `draws` and `tolerance` (check) or `n_max`, `sample_form` and `ceiling`, and `bound` where a ceiling is stated (run tools), as ADR-0042 decision 5 and ADR-0063 decisions 5 and 7 have them for a broken result.
- **A broken result** holds `outcome` `"broken"`, `condition`, and the members of the bullet above; **no** `counts` (check) and **no** `unit`, `resolution`, `intervals` or `memory_bytes` (run tools). This holds for every condition, those found by the operation (`build_time_limit`, `build_exit_status`, `time_limit`, `exit_status`) and those found by the tool function.
- **A tool failure is no result** and gets no member (cited: ADR-0042 decision 1; ADR-0063 decision 1): its form is the `kind` and `message` of ADR-0010 decision 4, unchanged. Where no shape or contract is known — `op_signature_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_covered`, `tolerance_not_stated`, `draws_not_stated`, `objective_not_stated` and every other answer of ④ — there is therefore no result to hold one; these answers append no line (cited: ADR-0041 decision 8; ADR-0067 decision 5). A tool failure after the start line (for example `operation_answer_invalid`, `build_log_unwritable`, a tool failure the operation raised) is the `failure` of the end line under the new value, unchanged in form and kind.

### 7. (d) The build-log file names

- **The form stays** `runs/<run_id>/rounds/<RRRR>/measure/candidates/<CC>/attempts/<AA>/build-log-<step>-<EE>.txt` (cited: [ADR-0078](ADR-0078-od18-correctness-check-compiler-log-file.md) decision 3; [ADR-0108](ADR-0108-od18-run-tools-timing-build-compiler-log.md) decision 3), with `<step>` the call's `step` value, now one of the four of decision 2: for example `build-log-correctness_check_sim_v2-01.txt` and `build-log-sim_run_timing_v2-01.txt`.
- **"One identity, one name, and never the check's"** (cited: ADR-0108 decision 3) holds: the four values are distinct, and a name formed from a retired value is no longer written. The set of `<step>` values a name may hold (`hilclaw.layout.BUILD_LOG_STEPS` in code) is the four values of decision 2 and no other once the slices have landed; the names of the retired values are not admitted.

### 8. (e) The readers of check and run lines under v2

- **No reader keeps accepting the retired values.** Each reader reads the lines of the new value of its point or tool only. *Why*: no stored run data exists (**owner**, HQ-0078 ①), a line under a retired value is "a line of another step" to a call under the new value (cited: ADR-0100 decision 5), and reading two definitions under one point would need the two-value machinery of [ADR-0178](ADR-0178-od23-bf16-check-lines-read.md) for no line. A line under a retired value is neither read nor refused by any reader; a run whose log held checks under a retired value would have them passed over by the hard floor, and no such log exists (**owner**, HQ-0078 ①).

### A-13 reading

Read under the owner's decision D23 (2026-10-29; CLAUDE.md A-13, PROCESS/DRIVER.md §3 "승인된 열린 목록"): a data-plane schema change is a change of the frame; adding a value of an approved open list is not.

- **The frame change is the owner's** (HQ-0078 ① to ⑤): a v2 result for three tools, stored as the `result` of `step_ended` lines of `events.ndjson`, under four new `step` values with six retired; a contract-key member; `shape` with the kind's members; `counts` per output or inout operand with `failed` / `non_finite` or `differs`; a unit member on the measured values with the one value `ns`; the other members carried over; "멤버 이름·철자와 세부는 ADR이 정한다".
- **What this record adds to that frame, and why it is within "멤버 이름·철자와 세부"**:
  - *Spellings*: the four `step` values; the member names `contract_key` and `unit` (② and ⑤ give the members, not their names); `differs`, `failed` and `non_finite` are the owner's own words.
  - *Two renames* — `resolution_ns` → `resolution`, `intervals_ns` → `intervals` — names of members whose meaning, type and presence are unchanged; they follow from ⑤, which moves the unit into its own member (decision 4).
  - *Forms and details*: `contract_key` as an object of `kind`, `dtype`, `layout` (②); `counts` as an object keyed by operand name with a per-draw array (④); `unit` present with the measurement and applying to `intervals` and `resolution` (⑤); the bounds of `failed` and `differs` and the pass rule over all operands (④ with ADR-0042 decision 4 and ADR-0165 decision 8); the per-allocation split of the cache count (④'s per-operand entries).
  - **No member of a meaning ① to ⑤ do not name is added, no member is removed, and no closed value list is widened**: `outcome`, `condition`, `sample_form` and `ceiling` keep their values; `unit` has exactly the owner's one value. The step-value choice by point or tool alone is ①'s "도구·지점마다 새 `step` 값 하나".
- **Values of the approved open list "kernel contract table"** (`hilclaw.contract_table.TABLE`, ADR-0177; D23) enter the members whose definition is "what the contract fixes": the kind, dtype and layout of the covering entry into `contract_key`; the kind's shape member names into `shape`; the names of the contract's output and inout operands into the members of `counts`. D23 names exactly these three members. Which entry form an operand takes follows from how its contract's verification record judges it (ADR-0165 decision 8 for the caches), not from a new list.
- **The other files**: no member of `STATE.json`, `instance.json`, a spec or `artifacts.json`, no event kind, no member of the envelope or of the `data` of a line, and no layout version changes; the build-log names keep ADR-0078's form with other `<step>` values (decision 7). `ARCHITECTURE/StepValues.md` is an index (ADR-0101 decision 7).
- **No BLOCKED reason remains for this record**: the owner made the frame change; what is added is the owner's delegated detail and values of an approved open list.
