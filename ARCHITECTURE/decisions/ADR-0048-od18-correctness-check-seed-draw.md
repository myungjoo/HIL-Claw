## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a record and its decision, or a requirement, is named with it; it then says what that source says and adds nothing. Every other sentence is **this record's own** — among them that the draw lies before the start line, that a draw that yields no seed is answered before the start line, the kind `seed_not_drawn` and that it is the tool's own, that a call that reaches the draw asks the source once, that the two cases are answered alike, the rule for the message, and every weighing. No sentence is the owner's.

### 1. Scope and terms

- This record is about **a call of `correctness.check` about one attempt of a run that ADR-0021 and ADR-0038 decision 6 cover** — a run of the first case and a run whose hw_profile names the mock (cited for the runs: ADR-0021 decision 1; ADR-0038 decision 6) — **whose identity has no line and which was given none of the answers of ④** (the row of ④ in ADR-0046 decision 8).
- The terms of ADR-0010 (result, tool failure), ADR-0011 (identity, start line, end line, open, ended), ADR-0015 (reserved kind), ADR-0016 (backend, operation, the moments ① to ⑦), ADR-0021 (a check — "one call that reaches ⑥"), ADR-0040 (`execution`, `point`), ADR-0043 (the check parameters), ADR-0045 (a raised error, a raised request to end) and ADR-0047 (a kind outside the contract) are used unchanged.
- **The source** is the source of seeds of ADR-0021 decision 3. **A seed** is what that decision and ADR-0043 decision 2 make it: an integer of "the whole range of seeds the harness accepts" (cited: both). **The draw**: the tool function asks the source for the seed of the call.
- **A draw that yields no seed** has two cases. *The source fails*: asked, it raises an error and names nothing. *The source names no seed*: it returns something that is no seed. What a source raises that is no error but a request to end (the term of ADR-0045 decision 1) is neither case (decision 5).
- It fixes **four points and nothing else**: the place of the draw (decision 3); what is established and what the log holds when the draw yields no seed (decision 4); what the call answers (decision 5); and what the message carries, with the rule that the tool function writes nothing a source returned or raised to a file (decision 6). Decisions 7 and 8 say what follows, by citation and by these four, for a seed that was drawn and is not used and for the mock and the tests.

### 2. What is established at the draw, in either order

Each sentence is cited; nothing is added.

- **The draw comes after every answer of ④**: the seed is drawn "after ④ and after the answer of decision 4" (ADR-0021 decision 3), and that answer, `tolerance_not_stated`, is the last of ④ (ADR-0046 decision 8). For every answer of ④, "No line, nothing consumed, no seed drawn" (the row of ④ in ADR-0046 decision 8); of `tolerance_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_stated` and `draws_not_stated` their records say that the call "drew no seed" (ADR-0021 decision 4; ADR-0041 decision 4; ADR-0046 decision 5).
- **It comes before the operation is called** (ADR-0021 decision 3), and the operation is called at ⑥, after the start line of ⑤ (ADR-0040 decision 7).
- **A call answered by the lookup draws none**: of a situation of the lookup, an ended and an open identity, "For none of these is a backend selected, a tolerance read or a seed drawn" (ADR-0040 decision 7, the row of ③).
- **The start line holds no seed in either order**: its `data` "is exactly `step` and `execution`" (ADR-0021 decision 3; ADR-0011 decision 3).
- **A start line is "complete before the step begins to consume the resource"** (ADR-0011 decision 2), and an identity with a start line and no end line is open: "may have run; outcome and amount unknown" (same decision).

### 3. The place of the draw

- **The tool function draws the seed before it appends the start line: after the last answer of ④ and before ⑤.** A call reaches the draw only when its identity has no line and none of the answers of ④ was given.
- **A call that reaches the draw asks the source once.** What it then yields is the seed of the call or is no seed; a call whose draw yields no seed is answered (decisions 4 and 5), and **no value stands in for a seed**: no second answer of the source, no other source and no constant. That a seed is "not derived from the request, the identity, any file of the data plane or of the source, a clock, a counter or anything an earlier call left" is cited: ADR-0021 decision 3.
- **The two orders were weighed against five tests**: (i) which sentences of ADR-0021 decisions 3 and 4, ADR-0040 decision 7, ADR-0041 decision 4 and ADR-0046 decisions 5 and 8 stay exact; (ii) what a draw that yields no seed leaves in the log, and what the same request is answered afterwards; (iii) whether a further check then needs a rule nobody has; (iv) what a seed gives away that was drawn and is handed to no operation; (v) whether the order needs anything of what is open about recording a seed (OD-16 sub-question ⑤).

| Test | Before the start line (between ④ and ⑤) | After the start line (between ⑤ and ⑥) |
|---|---|---|
| (i) Sentences | "drew no seed" and, in the row of ④, "no seed drawn" stay exact, since the draw follows every answer of ④. **"A call that does not reach ⑥ draws no seed" is not exact for one call this contract answers by design**: its draw yielded a seed and its start line could not be appended (`start_line_failed` — decision 7). A defect of the tool function after the draw is `tool_crashed` in either order (Limits) | This record found no call answered by design for which one of those sentences is inexact: a call whose start line fails has asked no source |
| (ii) A draw that yields no seed | Nothing is appended by this call. Where the identity still has no line, the same request is carried out again as for an identity with no line and, where it reaches the draw, the source is asked again | A complete start line stands. The identity must be ended with a `failure` — the same tool failure again on a later call that the lookup answers — or stays open — `step_open` on such a call |
| (iii) A further check | Needs no rule: the same request can be repeated | Needs a higher `execution`, "which nobody may yet grant (retry part of OD-17)" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2), or a recovery rule for an open step (OD-10) |
| (iv) An unused seed | A seed can be drawn and handed to no operation (decision 7) | A call whose draw yielded a seed goes on to call its operation; this record found no call answered by design in which a drawn seed is left over |
| (v) Recording | Needs nothing of it: no seed is written or returned by the order | Needs nothing of it either |

- **The choice is before the start line.** It is the reasoning by which the two faults nearest to this one were placed. Of the selection: "inside the step it would end the identity with a `failure`, and the corrected call would need a further `execution`, which nobody may yet grant" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2). Of the tolerance: "The call knows whether it has a tolerance before anything is consumed at a target and before a start line could be appended" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 4). A source that yields no seed says nothing about the attempt and nothing about a target, and drawn after the start line it would use up an identity, or leave a step open, in which the tool function knows that no operation was called. Left open, the step would mean "may have run; outcome and amount unknown" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 2, of an open step), although the tool layer knows that it did not run.
- **What the choice costs.**
  - **One sentence of ADR-0021 decision 3 becomes less exact**, as test (i) says. It stays exact for every call that is answered before the draw and for a call whose draw yields no seed.
  - **A seed can be drawn and never used** (decision 7; Limits).
  - **A draw that yields no seed leaves nothing in the log, and the tool function writes nothing a source returned or raised to a file** (decisions 4 and 6). The tool layer keeps the answer of that call nowhere — "its answer is kept nowhere" (said by ADR-0026 decision 5 of its own case, from ADR-0010 decision 7) — so whoever reads the log later does not learn from it that such a call was made.
- **What would reopen it.** A record that answers OD-16 sub-question ⑤ and needs the draw to stand in another place for its answer. A recovery rule (OD-10, the retry part of OD-17) that needs a call without a seed to be visible in the log. A record of the frame that gives every HW tool one rule for what its tool function draws. A statement of the owner as named in Status. None of these is decided or excluded here.

### 4. A draw that yields no seed: what is established and what the log holds

- **Established, for both cases.** The identity had no line at the lookup of this call, and this call has appended none (decision 3). No operation was called, since the operation is called after the draw (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3). The call has no seed. Nothing is established about the attempt: no check was made of it — a check is "one call that reaches ⑥" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 1).
- **The call is answered there, before the start line, with a tool failure; it appends no line.** A tool failure "says that no outcome was established — the request was refused, or the tool could not do its work" (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4): here the tool could not do its work.
- **Four answers were weighed.** Two exist under the order of decision 3 and two only under the order not taken.

| Answer | The order it needs | The log after the call | The same request afterwards | Against the tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) A tool failure of a kind of this tool's own, before the start line | before | as it was | carried out again; where it reaches the draw, the source is asked again | Decision 5 |
| (b) The error passes out of the tool function, and the frame answers `tool_crashed` | before | as it was | as (a) | The reserved meaning is "The tool function raised something that is no tool failure" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2) — "the tool is defective" (same decision). A tool function that works as its contract says would have to let such a thing out on purpose, so "the frame's net for a defect would be used as a designed answer" (cited: ADR-0045 decision 3, of its candidate (a)). The kind is also the answer for an exception out of a step, "which leaves the step open" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 4), so it does not tell a caller that no line was appended |
| (c) The step is ended: an end line with a `failure` of a kind of this tool | after | the start line and an end line | the same tool failure again | Test (iii): the identity is used up by a fault that says nothing about the attempt |
| (d) No end line: the identity stays open | after | the start line | `step_open` | Test (iii); and the log would not tell the case from the death of a process during a check |

- **The choice is (a).** (c) and (d) do not arise under decision 3: no start line has been appended when the source is asked.

### 5. What the call answers: the kind `seed_not_drawn`

- **A call whose draw yields no seed is a tool failure of the kind `seed_not_drawn`.** For it, this call called no operation, consumed nothing at any target and appended no line; the same request can be repeated. These are the properties ADR-0021 decision 4 gave `tolerance_not_stated`, ADR-0041 decisions 4 and 5 gave their kinds and ADR-0046 decision 5 gave `draws_not_stated` (cited: each), but for one: of those kinds no seed was drawn ("drew no seed"; of `op_signature_not_covered`, "no seed drawn") because no source was asked, and here the source was asked and yielded none.
- **The two cases are answered alike.** What is established is the same for both (decision 4), and no landed record says anything different of the two — which is where ADR-0019 decision 2 split a kind: "it splits where the fixed records already say something different of each case" (cited). The frame did the same for its own source: "When the source fails, or what it names is not an existing directory, the call is a tool failure of the reserved kind `no_data_root`" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 3). The message says which of the two it was (decision 6).
- **The same request again.** It is answered by what its own call finds: nothing of the earlier call is kept (cited: ADR-0010 decision 7). Where the identity still has no line, the call is "carried out as any call whose identity has no line" (the words of ADR-0040 decision 3 for a new identity): ① to ④ again, with what the files then state, and, where none of their answers is given, the source is asked again. The call is answered `seed_not_drawn` again if that draw yields no seed; if it yields one, the call goes on to ⑤ and, when its start line is appended, is a check. This record does not say how often a call is repeated or by whom (decision 9).
- **It is this tool's own kind, raised only by the tool function and only at the draw**: before the start line, by this contract in no other situation and never as the `failure` of an end line. An operation that raises a tool failure of this kind raises "a kind of this contract that is no backend kind" (cited: ADR-0047 decision 1), and its step is ended with `operation_failure_invalid` (cited: ADR-0047 decision 4), so no end line of this tool holds `seed_not_drawn` as the `kind` of its `failure`. The message of that `failure` may name the raised kind (cited: ADR-0047 decision 4).
- **A raised request to end is not answered with this kind**: what a source raises that is no error but asks the process to end, or interrupts it, passes through the tool function, as ADR-0045 decision 5 has it for an operation (cited for the manner). No line was appended before it. Where the code draws the line between an error and a request to end is the code task's.
- **Why the tool's own and not reserved** — the test of ADR-0015 decision 2 as ADR-0021 decision 4 applied it: "the situation belongs to one tool, and the kinds the frame reserved were reserved because their situations belong to no one tool" (cited). A seed that the tool function draws is a part of this tool's contract (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3), and no other HW tool has a contract. A reserved kind would be a rule of the frame for contracts that do not exist.
  - **This reason is provisional in one respect.** A timing run has a seed parameter as well, and "Which seed a caller passes — fixed, fresh per run, recorded or not — is open" (cited: [ADR-0009](ADR-0009-od13-cpu-standin-timing-run.md) decision 5; OD-13 sub-question ⑦). Whether the tool function of another HW tool comes to draw anything is therefore not decided, and this record does not say that none will.
  - The argument ADR-0019 decision 2 took from `step_open` — a kind a tool names itself "could also stand in the `failure` of an end line, and a call that did not start the step could not be told from a step that ended" (cited) — guards kinds that are answered without a line, and this is one. It is met as ADR-0021 decision 4 met it for `tolerance_not_stated`: this contract never puts the kind on an end line (the bullet above).
  - The cost is that of ADR-0045 decision 4: "a later record of the frame may add to the common part as long as no tool's contract uses the name" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 3), so a record that reserves a kind for this situation in every HW tool takes another name or amends this record.
- **Why not `tool_crashed`**: decision 4, answer (b). ADR-0015 decision 2 and ADR-0019 decision 4 stand as written for every exception that does come out of the tool function; by this record the tool function of this tool does not let the error of a source come out.
- **The sentence a caller may rely on.** *In this call the source of seeds was asked and yielded no seed. This call called no operation, consumed nothing at any target and appended no line.* It says nothing about the attempt, about why the source yielded none, or about whether it will yield one at another call.
- **The name** is a *name* of ADR-0001 (`[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,31}`; 14 characters). Checked on 2026-10-13 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`: no reserved kind (ADR-0015 decision 2; ADR-0016 decision 3; ADR-0019 decision 2; ADR-0026 decision 3), no kind of another record of this contract (`tolerance_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_covered`, `draws_not_stated`, `operation_answer_invalid`, `operation_failure_invalid`), no other kind the code names, no `step` value, no value of `condition` and no member name equals it, and the string occurred in none of the three trees before this record.

### 6. What the message carries

- `error.message` is a string, and the error object holds a `kind` and a `message` (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4); no member is added to it. The answer therefore has no place for what a source raised beside those two.
- **What bounds it by citation.** "this record gives a result and a tool failure no content that holds the seed" (ADR-0021 decision 3, "Until it is answered"). A call whose draw yields no seed has no seed, so that sentence alone does not carry the next bullet.
- **This record's own: the message carries nothing a source returned and nothing of what a source raised.** No part and no rendering of a returned value; of an error, neither its message nor its arguments nor where it was raised. A source is to be one "whose output nobody can predict or influence before the draw" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3); a source that yields no seed is not working as that property says, and nothing lets the tool function establish that what it returned or raised tells nothing about what it names at another draw.
- **This record's own: the tool function writes nothing a source returned or raised to a file either**, and it does not hand the error of a source or its traceback on as its answer. Observation, binding nothing: the chained exception of `SeedSourceError` and the frames of a traceback hold what a source returned or raised, and `draw` does not filter them.
- **What the message says**, in the manner of ADR-0045 decision 6. That the draw yielded no seed; which of the two cases it was; and it may say of which kind the returned value or the raised error was, as the code names kinds of values (observation: in Python, the name of a type). That is all it may show of what the source did.
- The wording is the code's. "a caller tells the cases apart by the kind and never by the message" (cited: ADR-0026 decision 6), and nothing may depend on the wording.
- **This says nothing of a seed.** A value a source returned that is no seed is the seed of no check. Whether the seed of a check may be recorded, returned or kept stays as ADR-0021 decision 3 left it (decision 9).

### 7. A seed that was drawn and is not used

- **The case.** The draw yielded a seed, and the append of the start line failed. The call is `start_line_failed`: "This call did not call the step: it called no operation, consumed nothing at any target and appended no end line" (cited: ADR-0026 decision 3). Its seed is handed to no operation.
- **What holds for that seed, by citation.** The answer does not hold it: ADR-0021 decision 3 gives a tool failure "no content that holds the seed", and the message of `start_line_failed` names the log and "what was found there or what the file system reported" (ADR-0026 decision 6). "the tool function writes neither to any file" (ADR-0021 decision 3). Nothing is kept between calls (ADR-0010 decision 7).
- **The same request afterwards** is answered as ADR-0026 decision 5 says (cited): where the identity has no line, "the call goes on to ④ and ⑤" — under decision 3 it asks the source again where it reaches the draw, and the seed drawn in the earlier call plays no part in it; where the identity is open, the call is `step_open`, and no source is asked (cited: ADR-0040 decision 7, the row of ③).
- **`start_line_failed` is answered after the draw**, so for this tool one call can meet the kinds in this order: the answers of ④; then `seed_not_drawn`; then `start_line_failed`.

### 8. What follows for the mock and for the tests

- **By citation.** A script "is asked exactly once for each call of the operation and at no other moment. A call that does not reach ⑥ asks no script" (ADR-0044 decision 2). "the seed reaches the script as a part of its call, and a script reads no random source itself" (ADR-0021 decision 2).
- **With decisions 3 to 5**: a call answered `seed_not_drawn` asks no script, so the mock and its default script have no part in the draw, and **this record adds nothing to what a script or the default script does.**
- **A test and the source.** Whether a test binds a source of its own is left to the code (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3), and ADR-0021 reckons with a test that does (cited: its decision 6, "where a test binds the source of seeds"; its Consequences). Where the code lets a test bind one, a conformance test can bind a source that fails or that names no seed and so exercise decisions 4 to 6. In a registry that `default_registry` builds, "no request, option or variable of the front process sets it" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3). Observation: `hilclaw.harness.seed_source.draw` takes the source as a callable.

### 11. Limits

- **A source that draws badly without failing is not detected**: "A source that draws badly — predictable, or bound by a test and left in — is not detected by anything here" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 6). This record answers a source that yields no seed and no other defect of a source.
- **A seed can be drawn and never handed to an operation**: when the start line cannot be appended (decision 7); and, in either order, when the process dies after the draw and before the operation is called, or when a defect of the tool function's own code raises an exception there (`tool_crashed`). Such a seed was held by the process of the tool function and by nothing else this record names. That it tells nothing about a later draw rests on the property of the source (cited: ADR-0021 decision 3, "not derived from … anything an earlier call left") and holds as far as the source has that property; ADR-0021 says "Knowing the seed of a finished check tells nothing about the inputs of a later one" of a finished check only.
- **Nothing in the log shows that a call was answered `seed_not_drawn`**, how often, or for which identity (decision 3, the cost).
- **A caller cannot tell from the answer whether the fault is lasting.** A platform without a random source and a source that failed once are answered alike, and the same request can be answered `seed_not_drawn` at every call.
- **How hard a seed is to predict is what the source gives.** For the source of the code today that is the generator of the platform (observation: `hilclaw.harness.seed_source.system`), which is the platform's.
- **A defect of the tool function is not told from a fault of the source by the answer.** Where the code draws the line between a source that fails and a registry bound to something that is no source at all is the code task's; an exception that is no tool failure and comes out of the tool function is `tool_crashed` (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2).
- **A message that breaks decision 6 is not detected by anything here.**
- The limits of ADR-0011 decision 9, ADR-0016 decision 6, ADR-0021 decision 6, ADR-0026 decision 7 and ADR-0040 decision 8 stand unchanged.
