## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a record and its decision, or a requirement, is named with it; it then says what that source says and adds nothing. Every other sentence is **this record's own** — among them the three kinds of this contract, what "covered" means for these tools, the order of ④, the message rule and every weighing.

### 1. Scope and terms

- **A call of `sim.run` or `board.run` about one attempt of a run of ADR-0061 decision 1** — a run whose hw_profile names `cpu_standin` in the local configuration, or names the mock (cited). Both tools follow one contract (cited: ADR-0061 decision 1), and every point below is stated once for both.
- Of that call it fixes **five points and nothing else**: what a call answers for a valid file without `op_signature` (decision 2) and for a stated op_signature this contract does not cover (decision 3); what it answers for a valid file without `n_max` (decision 4); the order of every answer of ④ and its place before the draw (decision 5); and the properties and messages of the three answers (decision 6).
- **Decisions 2, 4 and 5 rest on nothing that only one backend has**: the read of the file and the check of what it states are the tool function's part, the same for every backend (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2 (a); ADR-0061 decision 3). So they hold alike at `cpu_standin` and at the mock.
- The terms of ADR-0016 (the moments ① to ⑦), ADR-0030 (ⓢ), ADR-0061 (the read of ④, the timing run) and ADR-0065 (the timing parameters) are used unchanged.

### 2. A run whose file states no op_signature: `op_signature_not_stated`

- **The source and the read are cited, not re-decided**: the dimensions are those of the op_signature the run states, taken in the read of ④, and no value stands in (ADR-0061 decision 3; ADR-0031 decision 5, "No path yields an op_signature the run did not state"). No further read is made (cited: ADR-0061 decision 6, "Why there and once").
- **A valid file of version 1 without the member `op_signature`: a tool failure of the kind `op_signature_not_stated`, of the run tools' contract, with the spelling and the meaning of the kind of `correctness.check`** (ADR-0041 decision 4): *the valid file of the run states no op_signature; this call called no operation, consumed nothing at any target and appended no line.*
- **Three answers weighed** (ADR-0061 Boundaries ⑲ (가) names them):
  - **(a) The kind of `correctness.check`, taken with its spelling and meaning by this contract.** Kinds the frame does not reserve stay "each tool's" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2), and "no record forbids two contracts from defining the same value with the same meaning" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 4, which took `seed_not_drawn` so). The situation and the sentence a caller relies on are the same; a caller that knows both contracts reads one situation under one name.
  - **(b) A kind of this contract with another spelling** (for example `timing_op_signature_not_stated`). Two names for one situation would have a caller learn which tool answered before reading the kind — the reason ADR-0064 decision 4 gave against `timing_seed_not_drawn` (cited for the reasoning).
  - **(c) A kind the frame reserves** (ADR-0015 decision 2). ADR-0041 decision 4 kept its kind unreserved because the situation "belongs to one tool **today**" and a reserved kind "would speak for tools whose contracts do not exist" (cited). **The first reason no longer carries**: the situation now belongs to three tools of two contracts. The second still does: `profiler.collect` has no contract, and whether it needs the op_signature is said by no record. A reserved kind would be a rule of the frame for that tool.
- **The choice is (a)**, and the "not reserved" part is **provisional**, as ADR-0041 decision 4 and ADR-0064 decision 4 marked theirs (cited). A record of the frame that reserves the situation takes another name or amends ADR-0041 and this record, since a later record of the frame "may add to the common part as long as no tool's contract uses the name" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 3). This is the revisit ADR-0041 decision 4 asked for: its kind stays the check's own and unreserved.
- **Why this class**, the reasons of ADR-0041 decision 4, each of which carries over: *not a result* — no outcome was established (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4), and a result needs `shape` (cited: ADR-0063 decision 9, "always"); *not `invalid_request`* — the request is not at fault, and a refusal reads nothing (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2); *not a kind of ADR-0019* — the file is valid (cited: ADR-0031 decision 5, "The member may be absent from a valid file"); *not a run on some other dimensions* — none stands in (above).
- **A member that is present and invalid is not this kind**: the file is invalid as a whole (cited: ADR-0031 decision 5) and is answered `run_file_invalid` in ⓢ (cited: ADR-0030 decision 4). A misspelt member name is passed over, and the file states none (cited: ADR-0031 decision 7).

### 3. A stated op_signature this contract does not cover: `op_signature_not_covered`

- **Covered, for these tools, is the predicate of ADR-0041 decision 5**: `kind` `gemm`, `dtype` `fp32`, `layout` `row_major`, and each of `shape.m`, `shape.k` and `shape.n` at least 1; an `ext` is not looked at. **It is the same predicate, for this contract's own reasons, which are not the check's:**
  - **The timing run is defined for one contract.** ADR-0009 takes the samples of "a C kernel of ADR-0008" (cited: its decision 1); its driver fills `float` arrays (cited: its decision 5) and restricts the dimensions "no further than ADR-0008 (each ≥ 1)" (cited: its decision 9). ADR-0008 "is bound to kind `gemm`, dtype `fp32` …, layout `row_major`", and "Another kind, dtype, layout or language has another contract" (cited: its decision 1); that contract "covers no shape that holds a 0" (cited: ADR-0037 decision 2).
  - **The forms this contract already has.** The timing parameters hold `m`, `k` and `n` "each of at least 1" (cited: ADR-0065 decision 2), and the member `shape` of every result holds the same (cited: ADR-0063 decision 9). A stated shape holding a 0 can be neither handed to an operation nor reported.
  - The check's reason — its oracle, which is the owner's for any other case (#5 B-7, cited by ADR-0041 decision 5) — **is not needed here and is not used**: a timing run "checks no output" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 6).
- **A valid file whose stated op_signature is not covered: a tool failure of the kind `op_signature_not_covered`, of the run tools' contract, with the spelling of the check's kind and the meaning** *the valid file states an op_signature that the contract of the called tool does not cover*. The three answers of decision 2 were weighed again and fall the same way. **The meaning is said relative to the called contract on purpose**: the two predicates are equal today, and if a later record widens one of them (say, a timing run of another dtype, without an oracle for it), each kind still says what it says of its own contract.
- **Why an answer.** Without it, a run that states an fp16 product or another layout would be timed by the driver of ADR-0009 under the contract of ADR-0008, and its result would report the stated `shape` as if that operation had been timed. **No timing run of another case is defined by this**: the answer says only that this contract has none. **Why two kinds**: they say different things of the run, and a caller can tell which without reading the message (cited: ADR-0041 decision 5).
- **Not seen**: the kernel language, which the file never states, and a kernel of another contract under the same op_signature (cited: ADR-0041 decision 5; ADR-0031 decision 7). What a call answers for them stays open, as for the check.

### 4. A run whose file states no `n_max`: `n_max_not_stated`

- **A valid file of version 1 without the member `n_max`: a tool failure of the run tools' own kind `n_max_not_stated`.** No bound is substituted: not 10, not `N_MAX_DEFAULT` and not the bound of another run (cited: ADR-0023 decision 4; ADR-0061 decision 3).
- **What was weighed against it** — each a reading that would let the call run:
  - **(a) Run under `N_MAX_DEFAULT` or 10.** Excluded by ADR-0023 decision 4 (cited above), which makes "default 10" "not a meaning of a file that is silent".
  - **(b) Run under 10 and record that bound in the result.** The member `n_max` of a result is "the bound of the repeats the tool function read in ④" (cited: ADR-0063 decision 5); a substituted value there would give the member a second meaning, a change of the closed list of ADR-0063 decision 9 stored in `events.ndjson` — a data-plane schema change, not made here.
  - **(c) Run with no bound.** The stopping rule needs one (cited: ADR-0007 decision 4, "reaches `n_max`"; ADR-0009 decision 9, "the caller, no default"), the timing parameters hold one (cited: ADR-0065 decision 2), and a result holds one always (cited: ADR-0063 decision 9).
  - **(d) A member of the request.** "A value from elsewhere is not what the run states and does not stand in for it: a member of a request" (cited: ADR-0023 decision 5).
  - **(e) A result of broken, or `invalid_request`, or a kind of ADR-0019.** No experiment was carried out; the request is not at fault; the file is valid (cited: ADR-0023 decision 4, "The member may be absent from a valid file").
- **The choice is a tool failure.** **Its kind is of this contract's own, and not reserved, provisionally**: the member states "the largest number of samples one measurement may hold" (cited: ADR-0023 decision 2), which only a timing tool needs; `correctness.check` has no use of it, so no kind of the check is taken, and whether `profiler.collect` needs it is said by no record — the reasoning of decision 2 (c) and of ADR-0061 decision 4 for the objective kinds (cited).
- **The name** is a *name* of ADR-0001 (16 characters), formed like `tolerance_not_stated` and `draws_not_stated` from the name of the member.
- **OD-8 sub-question ㉑, as far as a call goes**: for a call of these tools, a run whose valid file states no bound has no measurement taken — the call answers this kind. **Who writes the member, where 10 comes from, and where it is checked that an instance states a bound stay open** (sub-question ㉑; OD-23).
- **A member that is present and invalid** (an `n_max` below 5, for one) makes the file invalid (cited: ADR-0023 decision 4) and is `run_file_invalid` in ⓢ (cited: ADR-0030 decision 4).

### 5. The order of ④, and the draw

- **A content with several faults has one answer: the first, in the order below, whose condition holds**, as ADR-0046 decision 5 has it for the check (cited for the manner). The order is the rule; it is not left to the code: a caller could not rely on what a file that lacks two members is answered, and two sources could differ (the reason of ADR-0041 Alternatives, cited).

| Step | Answer | Its condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | one of the four `run_file_*` kinds | a situation of the read of ④ (cited: ADR-0019 decision 3) |
| 2 | `no_backend`, then `not_offered` | the selection (cited: ADR-0019 decision 4; ADR-0016 decision 3) |
| 3 | `op_signature_not_stated` | no op_signature stated (decision 2) |
| 4 | `op_signature_not_covered` | the stated one is not covered (decision 3) |
| 5 | `objective_not_stated` | no objective stated (cited: ADR-0061 decision 4) |
| 6 | `objective_not_covered` | the stated one is not covered (cited: ADR-0061 decision 4) |
| 7 | `n_max_not_stated` | no bound stated (decision 4) |
| — | the draw of the timing seed; `seed_not_drawn` | after the last answer of ④, before the start line (cited: ADR-0064 decision 3) |

- **The reason for each adjacent pair:**
  - **2 → 3** (the selection before the members): the order of ADR-0019 decision 4 stands, and ADR-0061 decision 4 places its kinds "after the selection has succeeded" (cited); a call whose backend cannot be selected reaches no run "whatever the file states" (the reason of ADR-0021 Alternatives, as ADR-0041 decision 4 cites it).
  - **3 → 4**: "covered" can be asked only of a stated record.
  - **4 → 5** (the op_signature before the objective): what the file shows of the case beyond the accelerator is in the op_signature (cited: ADR-0041 decision 4), and the sample form is that of a timing run of the stated operation; and **with the op_signature first, all three tools answer a file that states no op_signature, or one not covered, with the same kind whatever else it lacks** — the check asks for it first too (cited: ADR-0046 decision 8). A person who corrects such a run gets one first answer from every tool.
  - **5 → 6**: as 3 → 4, and the order of `hilclaw.run_objective` today (observation).
  - **6 → 7** (the objective before `n_max`): the objective says what is measured, `n_max` is a parameter of that measurement. With the coverage answers first, `n_max_not_stated` says that **the bound is the one thing missing** of a run this contract covers — the property ADR-0046 decision 5 kept for `tolerance_not_stated` (cited for the manner) — and a corrector learns that a run is not served at all before supplying a bound for it. No fixed input orders them by need. **The cost**: a file that states a covered op_signature and neither an objective nor a bound is answered `objective_not_stated`, and whoever corrects it learns of the bound from the next call.
  - **7 → the draw**: cited, ADR-0064 decision 3; "A call that reaches the draw" is one that none of steps 1 to 7 answered. So none of the three kinds draws a seed.
- **A call answered before ④ takes no member and gives none of these answers**: a refused request, `no_data_root`, a kind of ⓢ, a situation of the lookup, an ended or open identity (cited: ADR-0062 decision 6). **A repeated call under an ended identity** is answered with what the end line holds, whatever the file then states; **a further `execution`** reads the file again (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3; ADR-0041 decision 3 for the manner).

### 6. The three answers: properties, messages and names

- **For each of `op_signature_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_covered` and `n_max_not_stated`**: the call called no operation, built and ran nothing, consumed nothing at any target, drew no seed and appended no line; the same request can be repeated and is answered by what the file then states. It is answered in ④, before the start line, and this contract raises it in no other situation.
- **Never the `failure` of an end line.** ADR-0065 decision 8 has no end line of these tools hold `seed_not_drawn`, `objective_not_stated` or `objective_not_covered` (cited); **this record adds its three kinds to that list**, for the reason given there — "so that the kind keeps telling that no line was appended" (cited: ADR-0064 decision 4). The slice of ⑲ (나), which says what the tool function does with a tool failure an operation raises, is bound by this as by ADR-0065 decision 8.
- **The message** names the member it is about (`op_signature` or `n_max`), says that the file states none or that the stated one is not covered, and **shows nothing of the content of the file**: no value of the record, its key or its `label`, and no other member. *Why*: a caller tells the cases apart "by the kind and never by the message" (cited: ADR-0026 decision 6); the content is in the file; and a `label` is "Free text for humans" (cited: ADR-0001 decision 2), which a message would carry into the calling agent's context unchecked. The wording is the code's.
- **The names, checked on 2026-10-16 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`.** `op_signature_not_stated` (23 characters) and `op_signature_not_covered` (24) occur as the kinds of `correctness.check` (ADR-0041; `hilclaw/check_admission.py` and its tests) and in passages that cite them, and are taken with their spelling **on purpose** (decisions 2 and 3). `n_max_not_stated` occurs nowhere before this record. None of the three equals a reserved kind (ADR-0015 decision 2; ADR-0016 decision 3; ADR-0019 decision 2; ADR-0026 decision 3), another kind of the run tools' contract (`objective_not_stated`, `objective_not_covered`, `seed_not_drawn`, `operation_answer_invalid`), a `step` value (`sim_run_timing`, `board_run_timing`) or a member name; `n_max` is a member and `n_max_not_stated` is not.

### 7. Limits

- **One answer per call.** A file with two faults reports one; whoever corrects it learns of the next from the next call (decision 5).
- **Validity is not existence** (cited: ADR-0031 decision 7): a covered op_signature says the timing run is defined for the record, not that a kernel, a build or a target exists for it. The kernel language is not seen (decision 3).
- **Two sources, two answers.** A source that knows a `kind` this one does not takes a file that states it as valid and, by decision 3, answers `op_signature_not_covered` where this source answers `run_file_invalid` (cited: ADR-0041 decision 9, for the manner).
- **Whoever can write `instance.json` changes these answers between two calls** (cited: ADR-0031 decision 7; ADR-0023 decision 6).
- The limits of ADR-0061 decision 8 stand unchanged.
