## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a source is named with it. Every other sentence is **this record's own**.

### 1. Scope

- **The evaluation work of a turn of a run of ADR-0120 decision 1**: the correctness checks of an attempt at the sim and board points that the round needs (FR-1, FR-9; in round 0 those of ADR-0119 decision 4), the reading of an attempt's standing (ADR-0110), and in a round ≥ 1 the verdicts of that round's hypotheses (FR-17; ADR-0071). The files concerned are those of the round's `eval/` (ADR-0004 decision 4).
- **Outside**: measurement (`sim.run`, `board.run`, the `measure/` role) and its holder; selection, promotion and the rest of the roster; which checks a round asks for and when (P2-1); what follows a standing or a verdict; everything under Boundaries.

### 2. (a) The holder: the `evaluator` sub-agent, with the deterministic parts below it

- **The Round-Manager delegates the evaluation work to one sub-agent of its turn session, `evaluator`, and that sub-agent holds the evaluation role of ADR-0004 decision 4** for these runs. The Round-Manager evaluates nothing itself and calls no HW tool (K2 §5), and the turn loop evaluates nothing between turn sessions.
- **The judgement is the evaluator's**: which hypothesis a result bears on, which entries a verdict rests on, and the `cause` of each verdict — the mechanism FR-17 asks for, which ADR-0071 decision 3 leaves as text because no vocabulary exists.
- **What is deterministic is not its reasoning** (K2 condition 4): the outcome of a check is the result of `correctness.check`, recorded by the tool layer (K2 condition 3); the standing of an attempt is `check_standing.standing` over the log (ADR-0110 decision 2: "in the complete lines of the run's `events.ndjson` alone"); whether two measurements differ is the rule of ADR-0007 decision 7; whether a verdicts record is valid and agrees with `hypotheses.json` is `verdicts.validate` and `verdicts_hypotheses.check`. The evaluator reaches each through a typed tool of the registry (decision 3) and copies no computation into its own text.
- **So the reader of ADR-0110's rule for these runs** is the function, reached by the evaluator; the evaluator reports the standing (decision 5). What anyone does after it stays open (cited: ADR-0110 decision 3; P2-1; OD-10).
- **Why a sub-agent in round 0 too**, where no verdict is written: K2 gives `correctness.check` to the evaluator and moves HW tool calls off the manager (§5), and one holder of `eval/` keeps one tool list that has the check. This is not a sub-agent made for a deterministic step (K2 condition 4): the evaluator exists for the judgement of decision 2's second bullet, and a check it requests is a tool call of an existing agent. The cost is a fresh context for a round-0 delegation with little judgement in it.

### 3. (b) Its tools

- **From what `default_registry` registers today, the evaluator may use exactly `mcp__hilclaw__correctness_check`, its one HW tool, and the three read tools `mcp__hilclaw__state_read`, `mcp__hilclaw__instance_read` and `mcp__hilclaw__run_fresh`.** Its list also holds Claude Code's `Read`, `Grep` and `Glob`. Reading is not restricted (K4 premise 1). Later tools are added to its list by the records or slices that create them: `mcp__hilclaw__verdicts_write` (decision 6) and the tools of Consequences slice 3.
- **Not its tools**: `mcp__hilclaw__sim_run` and `mcp__hilclaw__board_run`, which belong to the experiment side of K2's roster and are not registered yet; `mcp__hilclaw__state_write` and `mcp__hilclaw__run_commit`, the Round-Manager's (W1; ADR-0127 decision 2); `mcp__hilclaw__blob_push` and `mcp__hilclaw__blob_fetch`, which nothing of its work needs.
- **No file-writing means and no shell**: its list holds no `Write`, `Edit`, `NotebookEdit` and no `Bash`, and no tool that delegates further. This is PLAN P2-3's "Write 권한 없음", and it removes from the agent that records scores the means of OD-16 threat A's "점수 레코드 조작 · 감사 로그 조작" and of patching the harness or the timer. Under K4 premise 2 "writes nothing" then rests on the tool list of the definition, not on a prompt; whether a session honours that list is item (xviii).
- **"하네스만 호출" (PLAN P2-3), as this record reads it**: of the HW tools the evaluator calls only the harness's check, `correctness.check`, and no measurement tool; every other tool it calls reads, computes or writes its own role's record through a check, and none runs a kernel.
- **The tools reach it by its `tools` list**, which names them by their MCP names (ADR-0010 decision 4) — the second of the three ways ADR-0118 decision 6 leaves open, chosen here for the evaluator only.

### 4. (c) What it is given

- **The Round-Manager's delegation message names the run id and the work, and no place**: for a check, the attempt tuple (`run_id`, `round`, `candidate`, `attempt`) and the point (`sim` or `board`, ADR-0040 decision 4); for verdicts, `run_id` and the `round`. The Round-Manager has the run id from its prompt (ADR-0123 decision 2) and the rest from its round; the run it names is the session's run (ADR-0123 decision 4), as ADR-0126 decision 6 has it for the coder-driver.
- **What a record of its role rests on, it gets through typed tools of the registry that take the `run_id`**: an outcome, a standing, a comparison, the round's hypotheses. So it needs no place, and ADR-0123 decision 2 stands unchanged. This is a rule about the grounds of its record, not about reading: what it reads besides, by `Read`, `Grep` or `Glob` wherever it finds a path, is not restricted (K4 premise 1). **It is not given paths and hashes** (#7 §2(8)): the session has no place to give, and no record of the data plane holds a hash of a run file outside the blob store. It is not given another agent's account of an attempt; what it rests on is the log and the files, read through tools (K2 condition 3).

### 5. (d) What it returns

- **One final message to the Round-Manager, as text**: one line per tool request it made, naming the tool, the tuple or round, and the result value — a check's `outcome`, the standing, or that the verdicts file was written — or the failure kind; for a verdicts delegation also one line per hypothesis `id` with its `verdict`. The exact wording is the definition's.
- **The message is not the record**: the check's lines in `events.ndjson` and the written `verdicts.json` are (K2 condition 3; ADR-0071 decision 1). The Round-Manager copies nothing of it into a file; what it does with it is P2-1's.

### 6. (e) What writes the evaluation role's files

- **`rounds/<RRRR>/eval/verdicts.json` is written by a new registry tool, `verdicts.write` (MCP name `mcp__hilclaw__verdicts_write`), which the evaluator calls**, acting for it as a tool acts for its caller. The tool function performs `hilclaw.verdicts_file.write` and so the steps of ADR-0071 decision 1 unchanged.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Request | Exactly `run_id` (ADR-0015 decision 4) and `record`: the whole content of the file as one JSON object; both required; any other member, or one missing, is refused |
| Result | The empty object |
| Checks, in order | the request (`invalid_request`, ADR-0015 decision 2); `record` valid by ADR-0071 decisions 2, 3, the file-local part of 4 and 6 at `schema_version` 1, and its `run_id` that of the request (`invalid_request`); the data root (`no_data_root`); the round's `decision/hypotheses.json` read and parsed (`hypotheses_missing`, `hypotheses_invalid`, `hypotheses_unsupported`, `hypotheses_unreadable`); the three rules of `verdicts_hypotheses.check` (`verdicts_mismatch`); no evidence entry of `kind` `comparison` (`comparison_not_given`, below); the write (`verdicts_exist` when the final name is taken, `verdicts_not_written` for any other failure of the file system) |

- **Every check before the write creates nothing**, so a refused record can be corrected and sent again, and FR-17's "evaluate에 이른 라운드는 판정 없이 끝나지 않는다" is not broken by a refusal. A record of round 0 is refused as invalid (ADR-0071 decision 6). **Reading `hypotheses.json` is the tool's step, not the write's**: `verdicts_file.write` still reads no other file (ADR-0071 decision 1).
- **The tool is not journaled**: it costs no limited resource and its outcome is the file, as for `state.write` (ADR-0124 decision 3). Its kinds are names of the tool layer, **not a change of the data plane**; none is reserved. The encoding of `record` into the bytes the write takes (ADR-0071 decision 2's text form) and the descriptions and input schema are the code slice's.
- **No `comparison` entry is written unless its `difference` was given by a function of the package** (decision 2; K2 condition 4). No such function is reachable yet, so **`verdicts.write` refuses every record that holds a `comparison` entry**, with `comparison_not_given`: the rule is held by the tool, not by a prompt (K4 premise 2). Until the comparison tool of Consequences slice 3 exists, the evaluator writes `check` entries only, and every verdict it writes is `inconclusive` (cited: ADR-0071 decision 4, no counted comparison). The record that adds the comparison tool changes this check of `verdicts.write` and says how the tool tells a given comparison. Whether the tool also holds a `check` entry's `outcome` against the log is not decided (Boundaries).
- **`eval.json` is not written by these runs while its fields are open** (OD-20): the outcome of each check is in the log, which is where ADR-0110 reads it. When a record gives its fields, it is written through a typed tool of the registry for the evaluator, never by an agent's own file tool; that record names the tool. So for these runs **no agent writes any file of `eval/` by its own means**.

### 7. (f) Its definition

- **The definition is `orchestration/agents/evaluator.md`**, copied into the session directory as `.claude/agents/evaluator.md` by `LAYOUT` (ADR-0118 decision 5), written by a later code-mode slice. Its frontmatter `tools` is the list of decision 3; it names no model (Boundaries).
- **It carries fixed rules**: every request carries the run id it was given, and only that one; request a check only for the tuple and point it was given, with `execution` 1 unless the delegation names another value (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3 as ADR-0015 decision 4 quotes it, the caller states 1 "unless a decision outside this record asked for a further execution"); write nothing by any means but `verdicts.write`; request no measurement; report as decision 5 says. Who may ask for a further execution stays open (ADR-0110 decision 6; OD-18 ⑭).

### Walk-through

| Situation | What follows |
|---|---|
| Round 0 of P2-5 needs the sim-point check of its bring-up attempt | The Round-Manager delegates the tuple `0000/01/01` and `sim`; the evaluator calls `correctness.check`; the tool journals the check; the evaluator reports `pass` and the standing `verified` (once the standing tool exists). No file of `eval/` is written |
| The board point of the same attempt, after a board run by the experiment side | As above with `board`; with checks passed at both points (cited: ADR-0119 decision 4; read here as the standing `verified` of ADR-0110), a completed board result and no bar of ADR-0113 decision 3, the attempt can stand as the baseline — which the round steps, not the evaluator, act on |
| Round 2 reaches evaluate | The Round-Manager delegates `run_id` and round 2; the evaluator reads the hypotheses and the results through tools, composes the record and calls `verdicts.write`; it reports the file written and one verdict per hypothesis |
| The record gives a hypothesis no verdict, or a value against its `expected` | `verdicts_mismatch`; nothing is written; the evaluator corrects and calls again |
| The verdicts of the round were already written | `verdicts_exist`; nothing is replaced (ADR-0071 decision 1); what a second write means stays open |
| The evaluator names another run's id | The tools serve that run; nothing stops it (cited: ADR-0123 decision 4; ADR-0015 decision 7). A later hook could see the `run_id` (ADR-0010 decision 6) |
| The evaluator dies during a check | The check's lines stand in the log (K2 condition 3); an open identity keeps the attempt from being verified (cited: ADR-0110 decision 3); the Round-Manager gets no report |
