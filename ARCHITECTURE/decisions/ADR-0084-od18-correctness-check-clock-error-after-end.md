## Context

- ADR-0083 P2 found one line of `build` that raised a plain `OSError` after the compiler had ended, `duration_s = time.monotonic() - started`, and its decision 3 named as one thing that would let a plain `OSError` be placed "A code change that raises no plain `OSError` after the compiler started". Task T-0255 (PR #161, `8782cf8`) made that change and placed nothing in a row.
- **Observations at `8782cf8`** (`hilclaw/clang_build.py`; no code is bound):
  - **C1. Where the clock read stands.** `duration_s = time.monotonic() - started` is the second statement of the `try` around `_communicate`, after `returncode, raw = _communicate(process, timeout_s, held)`, so an `OSError` from it is caught by that `try`'s `except OSError` and raised as `CompilerRunError` `from` it, with `timed_out=held.timed_out`, `log=held.decoded()` and the `removal_error` of a failed removal of the temporary file. The class docstring: "Raised by `build` from an `OSError` raised after creating the compiler process returned … before the process's return code was known, for example from reading its output or killing it, or, once the call had ended the process, from reading the clock for the build's duration."
  - **C2. The compiler had ended.** `_communicate` runs the process in `with process:` and returns from inside it; the exit of `Popen` used as a context manager closes the pipes and then calls `wait()`, and `communicate` itself waits for the process before it returns (CPython 3.11.9 and 3.14.4 `Lib/subprocess.py`). So when the clock is read, the compiler process has ended and was waited for, and the return code `_communicate` returned is bound in `build` — `None` after a kill for the time limit, otherwise `process.poll()`. **`CompilerRunError` does not carry it**: its members are `timed_out`, `log` and `removal_error`, and the `OSError` it was raised from.
  - **C3. `timed_out` and `log` of a failed clock read.**
    - **After a kill for the time limit**: `timed_out` is true (docstring: "or from reading the clock after such a kill"). `log` is what the call held (docstring: "A failed read of the clock leaves `log` as the steps before it left it"): not on Windows, `held.raw` is the `output` of the `TimeoutExpired` and the `wait()` after the kill reads nothing, so `log` is what that `output` held, and `None` where it held nothing — the docstring names "when the time limit passed before any output was read and nothing was read after the kill"; in CPython's POSIX `communicate`, a limit that passes in its final `wait`, after the pipe reached its end, raises a `TimeoutExpired` without `output` too (3.11.9 and 3.14.4), so `None` can also follow output that was read; on Windows, `held.raw` is what `communicate()` after the kill returned, so `log` is the output read after the kill. A `BuildResult` of the same build would hold `raw.decode(…)` of `held.raw or b""`: the same text, or an empty one where `log` is `None`.
    - **After a normal end** (exit 0 or a non-zero status): `timed_out` is false (docstring: "or from closing the process or reading the clock after a read that ended") and `log` is "all of it", on every platform. The docstring states the same two members for an error from closing the process after a read that ended.
    - **In both**, nothing is placed: the stat test and `os.replace` stand in the `else` of that `try` and do not run, and the `finally` removes the temporary file, the artefact a compiler that exited with 0 wrote in it included.
  - **C4. The sources of a plain `OSError` of `build`** — one that is none of its five classes — confirmed from the code: every statement inside the outer `try` is covered by an `except OSError` that raises one of the five classes or keeps the error for one (the construction of `Popen`, `_communicate` and the clock read, the stat test, `os.replace`), or stands in the `else` of such a `try` and raises none; the removal in the `finally` is caught and becomes a `removal_error`, a `TemporaryNotRemovedError` or a note; the lines after it form the classes and the `BuildResult` and raise or return them, and call nothing that raises an `OSError`. **So a plain `OSError` of `build` now comes only from the lines before the `try`, all of which run before `Popen` is called**: the path tests, forming the absolute paths, the temporary name, `started = time.monotonic()` and the search for the compiler — the sources (a) to (e) of ADR-0083 P3, whose lines `8782cf8` did not change (`git diff 0305d7b 8782cf8 -- hilclaw/clang_build.py` moves only the clock line and edits two docstrings). T-0255's Result: "A plain OSError of build now comes only from lines before subprocess.Popen (ADR-0083 P3 (a)-(e))".
- No owner comment on #5 speaks to ADR-0082, ADR-0083, T-0255 or ⑱ (마) (checked on 2026-10-17; every comment since 2026-10-16 carries the driver tail).

## Decision

### 1. Scope and terms

- A call of `correctness.check` as ADR-0081 decision 1 has it. The six kinds, S1 to S10 and the keeper are as ADR-0079 decision 1 has them; the time-limit branch and the other branch of `CompilerRunError` as ADR-0082 decision 1 has them. **A failed clock read** is an `OSError` from the clock read of C1. **A plain `OSError` of `build`** is as ADR-0083 decision 1 has it. **The mock** builds nothing and is unchanged.

### 2. A failed clock read after a kill for the time limit is S8, by ADR-0082 decisions 2 and 3

- **ADR-0082 decision 2 reaches it as it stands**: it is a `CompilerRunError` whose `timed_out` is true (C3), and the operation "can apply it from what `build` states alone: the class and its member `timed_out`" (cited: ADR-0082 decision 2). The operation cannot tell this source from the other steps of that branch, and needs not.
- **On which reading.** ADR-0082 decision 2 reads that "The error came afterwards, from the steps by which the call ends a build past its limit (L2)", and a clock read is not one of those steps. What carries the row is the next sentence: "the experiment established that the build did not end within its limit, and a later failure to kill, read or close the process says nothing about the attempt" (cited: ADR-0082 decision 2). **This record reads that sentence as reaching a failed clock read too**: the time limit had passed before it (C3), and reading the clock is no part of the attempt. The cost ADR-0082 decision 2 stated for a compiler whose end was not seen does not arise here: the compiler had ended and was waited for (C2).
- **What the answer loses.** Nothing of the result: an answer of form (B) "holds the condition and nothing else: no status, no time and no byte of the log" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4), so the duration the call failed to read is in no answer.
- **ADR-0082 decision 3 applies unchanged**: when `log` is not `None` the operation hands it to the keeper before it returns; when it is `None` it hands nothing over (cited: ADR-0082 decision 3). **The cost, stated**: not on Windows, where the call held no output at the limit, the same build without the failed clock read would leave an empty file of the log (C3; cited: ADR-0078 decision 2, "A log that is empty is kept as an empty file"), and this one leaves no file, which says that the log "did not reach the tool function" (cited: ADR-0078 decision 2). The text lost is the same in both: the call held none. That the compiler ended (C2) makes the sentence a caller may rely on after a failed write, "its compiler ended" (cited: ADR-0078 decision 7), exact for this source.
- **The `removal_error`** changes nothing, as ADR-0082 decision 2 has it.

### 3. A failed clock read after a normal end stays open, in the entry of ADR-0082 decision 4

- **It is a `CompilerRunError` whose `timed_out` is false** (C3), so it falls in the entry ADR-0082 decision 4 keeps open: "The row of a `CompilerRunError` whose `timed_out` is false stays open under (마)".
- **Not for the reason that entry gives.** ADR-0082 decision 4 keeps it open "for the reason of ADR-0081 decision 2: no wording of S3 to S10 names a compiler the call lost while it ran". This compiler was not lost: it ended and was waited for, and the call held its return code (C2). **This record reads "a compiler the call lost while it ran" as not covering it.**
- **Why it stays open all the same.** "What the operation observes decides the row" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4), and what the operation observes does not decide one:
  - **It is not told apart.** The class and its members state for it what they state for an error from closing the process after a read that ended: `timed_out` false and `log` all the output (C3). A row given to this source by what `build` states would be a row for that one too, which this record does not weigh.
  - **The row would depend on a status that is not stated.** For a non-zero status the build is S7, "The compiler ends with a non-zero status", which O1 (**the owner's**: "One each: `build_exit_status` for S7 and `build_time_limit` for S8.") makes a result. For status 0 it is no row by its wording: S9 is "Status 0 and no executable at the path, or it cannot be moved there" (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4), and here the artefact was neither tested nor moved but removed with the temporary file (C3); **this record reads S9 as not covering it** and reads no other row of S3 to S10 to do so. The error does not carry the return code (C2). Reading a status out of the log text would rest on what `build` does not state, as ADR-0081 decision 2 declined the exception chain.
- **Its log** is all the output, held by the error (C3); `build` no longer drops it, as ADR-0083 decision 3 had it for the plain `OSError` of that line. Whether an operation hands it over stays "part of the open row" (cited: ADR-0082 decision 4). ADR-0078 decision 6 lets an operation hand a log over before it raises, and decision 7 writes it "whatever the operation returned or raised"; **this record does not decide the hand-over apart from the row**, since by what `build` states it would be a rule for the closing source as well (above), and a record that places the row decides both. **The cost, stated**: until then the log of such a build, whose compiler ended by itself, is not kept, against the letter of ADR-0078 decision 2 ("The log of every build whose compiler process was started and has ended — by itself, or killed for its time limit — is kept"), and, where the status was non-zero, against O2 (**the owner's**: "Kept, not returned: in a file of the attempt, never in the result or in `events.ndjson`"). An operation that lets the error pass is answered `operation_answer_invalid` (cited: ADR-0045 decisions 4 and 5), which says what the landed rules do and is no answer of this record: a build that S7 would make a result is answered by a tool failure, against O1, and a build that exited with 0 and wrote its artefact by a tool failure as well. How often a machine's monotonic clock fails is not established here.
- **What would let it be placed** (named, not written here):
  - **A code change** of `clang_build.build` that states the return code it already holds (C2) for a failed clock read, and tells that source from the closing source, or that does not lose the build's result to a failed clock read. Then a record can weigh S7 for a non-zero status, with its log, by the value O1 gave.
  - **A record for status 0** with no artefact tested, which reads one row's wording to cover it or adds the situation to the list of ADR-0056 decision 5. The second may need a new value of `error.kind`, which this record does not add.
- **The other sources of that branch** — the first read, the kill after it, closing the process — and the row of the branch as a whole are not weighed here.

### 4. A plain `OSError` of `build` still has no row

- **What the operation now observes** (C4): the build failed before the compiler process was created, so no compiler ran and there is no log (cited: ADR-0078 decision 2, "S3, S5, S6 and S4 found before the build start no compiler or none that ran, so they have no log"). The reason "And not always before the start" of ADR-0083 decision 3 is gone, and with it the cost it stated for the line after the end; that line is now met by decisions 2 and 3.
- **The other reasons of ADR-0083 decision 3 still hold**, each checked against C4:
  - **"One class serves two rows."** The path tests still raise `FileNotFoundError` and `IsADirectoryError` for a source (S5) and for the output path (S3) (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4); their lines did not change (C4).
  - **"Sources no row names."** `os.getcwd`, `os.urandom`, `started = time.monotonic()` and the search for the compiler are still sources before the start (C4), and this record reads the wordings of S3, S5 and S6 as ADR-0083 decision 3 read them: as covering none of them.
  - **"'Before the start' is several rows."** All sources are now before the start, so the moment alone places nothing, as before.
- **So it stays without a row**, and until a record places it, an operation that lets it pass is answered `operation_answer_invalid` (cited: ADR-0045 decisions 4 and 5), with the cost ADR-0083 decision 3 stated for the sources before the start.
- **What would let it be placed**: the other three things ADR-0083 decision 3 names — a code change that tells the source tests from the output tests and both from the other lines before the start; a step of the operation's own, as ADR-0060 decision 4 allows; a record for the sources no row names. Its second item, "A code change that raises no plain `OSError` after the compiler started", is done (C1, C4).

### 5. How far (마) is answered

- **Answered now, for `correctness.check` of the first case and of the mock**: a failed clock read after a kill for the time limit, which is S8 with its log by ADR-0082 decisions 2 and 3 (decision 2); where a plain `OSError` of `build` comes from — only before the start (C4) — with its row left open for the reasons of decision 4.
- **Open under (마)**: the row of a `CompilerRunError` whose `timed_out` is false (ADR-0082 decision 4), a failed clock read after a normal end included (decision 3); a plain `OSError` of `build` (decision 4) — `FileNotFoundError` or `IsADirectoryError` for a source (S5) or for the output path (S3), the other classes of the path tests of ADR-0080 decision 4, and an `OSError` from `os.getcwd`, `os.urandom`, `time.monotonic` or the search for the compiler before the start; and the other entries ADR-0079 decision 5 names, as ADR-0083 decision 4 lists them — the `TypeError` of `clang_build.build`, whatever `clang_build.build` passes on from `subprocess` beyond the `OSError` its docstring names, and the `ValueError` or `TypeError` and the `OSError` for "a `cwd` that does not exist" of `local_run.run`.

## Alternatives considered

| Point | Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | **(a)** S8 by ADR-0082 decisions 2 and 3 (chosen) | The class and `timed_out` are what decision 2 reads, and its reason holds for a step after the end. The cost: no file of the log where nothing was read before the limit (decision 2). |
| 2 | **(b)** Take a failed clock read out of S8 | The operation cannot tell it from the other steps of the branch (C1, C3), and the time limit had passed before it either way. |
| 3 | **(c)** Read the closing source and the clock source as a compiler that ended, and place both now | Each would need a row by a status the error does not state (C2): S7 for a non-zero status, none for status 0 (decision 3). Placing the closing source is beyond this record. |

## Consequences

- **Code** (code mode, D12 rules; names and modules are the code task's; **this record binds no code**): the operation of `cpu_standin` for `correctness.check`, when it is written, applies ADR-0079 to ADR-0083 and this record; it answers every `CompilerRunError` whose `timed_out` is true, a failed clock read included, as ADR-0082 decisions 2 and 3 say, and leaves the other branch and a plain `OSError` to the landed rules. No change of `clang_build.build` is needed for this. A code task may make `build` state the return code for a failed clock read and tell it from the closing source (decision 3), and may tell the source tests from the output tests (decision 4).
- **Not edited and now less exact.**
  - ADR-0081 K1 (the `build` docstring as quoted at `065113b`: "Any other `OSError` raised while the process runs is raised as `CompilerRunError`"), now also for a failed clock read (C1).
  - ADR-0081 decision 2: "then the call failed to talk to it, and its end was not seen; no log and no return code reach the operation", for a failed clock read, whose compiler had ended (C2).
  - ADR-0082 L2 (the class docstring as quoted at `d3becf0`) and L4 ("In the other branch, `log` is not `None` only after an error from closing the process after a read that ended"), both now also for a failed clock read (C3); L5 ("Neither the members nor the docstrings say whether the compiler had ended when the error was raised"), since the docstring now says it for the clock read, though no member says it for an error.
  - ADR-0082 decision 2: "The error came afterwards, from the steps by which the call ends a build past its limit (L2)", for a failed clock read (decision 2).
  - ADR-0082 decision 4: "for the reason of ADR-0081 decision 2: no wording of S3 to S10 names a compiler the call lost while it ran", which does not fit a failed clock read after a normal end, and "It is not `None` after an error from closing the process after a read that ended", now also after a failed clock read (decision 3).
  - ADR-0083 title ("now from before the start but for one line") and Status ("where a plain `OSError` of `build` now comes from (P2 to P4)"), for that one line (C4).
  - ADR-0083 P2 ("One line is the exception … no `except OSError` covers it", "that output is dropped" and "or from that one line") and P4 ("or from `time.monotonic` after the compiler ended (P2). Its class does not tell the last apart from the others."), as statements about `build` after `8782cf8` (C1, C4).
  - ADR-0083 decision 3: "but the same class can come from after the compiler ended (P2)"; the reason "And not always before the start" and its cost, and its "S7, S8 or S9 may hold for it", which this record reads otherwise for status 0 with the artefact removed untested (decision 3); the second item of "What would let it be placed", now done; and "After the end, a build that S7 or S8 would make a result is answered by a tool failure, against O1", which no longer holds for a plain `OSError`; for a `CompilerRunError` after a normal end it holds for S7 (decision 3); and its alternatives (a), "and the line after the end may be S7, S8, S9 or a build that ended ok".
  - ADR-0083 decision 4: "and one from `time.monotonic` after the compiler ended" among the plain `OSError` entries; it is now a `CompilerRunError` (decision 5).
  - ADR-0083 walk-through row "The compiler exits with 0; reading the monotonic clock fails" ("a plain `OSError` from `time.monotonic`, no result").
  - ADR-0083 Consequences: "and may make `build` raise no plain `OSError` after the compiler started", which T-0255 did; the bullet on ADR-0081 K4 and decision 4 "both for the line after the end (P2, P4)", whose sentences hold again for it (C4); and the bullet on ADR-0078 decision 2 "for the line after the end, whose log `build` drops", now kept by the error and not handed over for a normal end (decision 3).
- **A-13 reading.** Nothing of the data plane is added or changed: no member, no value of `condition` or `error.kind`, no file, no version.
- **Runtime acceptance items**: none. Whether a real machine's monotonic clock fails during a build is the code tasks' observation.
