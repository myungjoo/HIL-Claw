## Context

- **O1** (**owner**, item 3 of https://github.com/myungjoo/HIL-Claw/issues/5#issuecomment-6182984913): "… Every report of such numbers to the user (at the start of the run, with each result or best shown, and in the end notification) carries a warning that the absolute values are not to be trusted and that improvements may be hard to establish. Recording in the data plane that a measurement or a run was made in such an environment is approved; its form is left to a record."
- **The integrator note on OD-13** (2026-10-15) reads that approval as the A-13 owner approval of the data-plane schema change needed to carry the mark, and of nothing else.
- **What ADR-0103 decided** (cited): a measurement is *found imprecise* when the measured ground (decision 3: `outcome` is `"not_converged"`, or 100 × `resolution_ns` > the smallest entry of `intervals_ns`) or the class ground (decision 4: the timing run executed in a VM guest; its input does not exist yet) holds; a broken run gives no finding. A run is found imprecise at a moment when by then one of its measurements has been found, or the class input shows an execution point of the run is a VM guest, and stays found (decision 5). The measured ground is "a function of one stored result and the constants" and can be computed "as soon as its result exists" (decision 6). "Where and in which form the finding is kept is part (나)", and "a mark on a measurement could be derived rather than stored" (Status; Consequences).
- **Where a result of the scope is kept today** (cited): `sim.run` and `board.run` are journaled tools, with the `step` values `sim_run_timing` and `board_run_timing` (ADR-0062 decisions 2 and 3). The `result` of a `step_ended` line "is the result object of the call itself" (ADR-0011 decision 3), appended by the tool layer "as soon as its outcome exists" (ADR-0011 decision 2) to `runs/<run_id>/events.ndjson`, which is append only (ADR-0006 decision 5). A result holds `outcome`, and, exactly when it is `"converged"` or `"not_converged"`, `resolution_ns` and `intervals_ns` (ADR-0063 decision 9). No member of `sim.json` or `board.json` is named and nothing writes them yet (ADR-0063 decision 10 and Limit 4).
- **No result of either tool exists yet**: no tool function of `sim.run` or `board.run` is built in `hilclaw/` (cited: ADR-0100 decision 1; at `b6dd95e` only `hilclaw/run_identity.py` and its test name the two values — observation).

## Decision

### 1. Scope and terms

- **Scope**: that of ADR-0103 decision 1 (cited), not narrower and not wider: a measurement taken by a call of `sim.run` or `board.run` about an attempt of a run of ADR-0061 decision 1, and such a run. Whether a run is in the scope is read from the hw_profile in its `instance.json`, not from the log, since a result does not show which backend answered (cited: ADR-0063 Limit 7). For a run outside the scope nothing here applies, even when its log holds lines under the same `step` values.
- **Terms.** *Found imprecise*, *the measured ground* and *the class ground* are ADR-0103's. **The run tools' end lines** of a run are the complete `step_ended` lines of its `events.ndjson` whose `step` is `sim_run_timing` or `board_run_timing` (ADR-0062 decision 3). **Derived** means computed, when a reader needs it, from what the data plane already keeps; **stored** means written into the data plane as a value of its own.

### 2. Both findings are derived; nothing is stored

- **The finding of a measurement and the finding of a run are derived** (decisions 3 and 4). No record, member, line or value is added to the data plane to hold either finding.
- **This record's reading of O1.** O1 approves recording "that a measurement or a run was made in such an environment" and leaves the form open. It does not ask for a member. A finding that is a fixed function of kept lines is recorded in the data plane in the sense that matters for O1: anybody who reads the run's files establishes it, now and later, without the process that computed it. ADR-0007 decision 3 treats the summary and the verdict the same way: they "are derived values", and what is kept is the samples. Half of the measured ground is a stored mark already, `outcome` `"not_converged"`, the mark of ADR-0007 decision 4 (cited: ADR-0063 decision 2). If the owner reads O1 as asking for an explicit member, the objection window is the place to say so (Alternatives (b), (c)).
- **Why derived** (this record's own):
  - *Every input is kept already* (decision 3), so a stored finding would be a second statement of the same lines, fixed at one source commit. ADR-0063 decision 4 left a summary out of a result for this reason.
  - *No new state "not computed"*: a mark stored apart from the end line (Alternatives (c), (d), (e)) has a writer that can die between the result and the mark (ADR-0011 decision 2: no form closes the window between an outcome and a line; ADR-0006 decision 10), and then a missing mark means nothing. A derived finding has no such gap (decision 5).
  - *It costs no new definition*: no new `step` values (ADR-0100 decision 3), no event kind (ADR-0098), no member of a file whose fields are open.
  - *One function for every machine and reproducible*: the derivation is one function of the kept lines on every machine kind (C-7), and the finding is reproduced from what is kept, as ADR-0007 decision 9 asks of a verdict and ADR-0103 decision 6 of the measured ground.
  - *"Stays found" follows from the log*: lines are never edited or removed (ADR-0006 decision 5), so a derivation over a longer prefix of the log keeps every earlier finding (with the constant of Limit 1).

### 3. The finding of a measurement: from its end line

- **The finding of a measurement is computed from the `result` of its run tools' end line**: the measured ground of ADR-0103 decision 3 applied to its `outcome`, `resolution_ns` and `intervals_ns`. A line whose `result` has `outcome` `"broken"`, and a line that holds `failure` and no `result`, give no finding (cited: ADR-0103 decision 3; ADR-0011 decision 3). An open step has no end line and no result, so no finding. A measurement is also found imprecise under the class ground (ADR-0103 decision 4); where that finding is kept, and how it follows, is left to the record that produces the class input, as for the run (decision 4).
- **Where each input of ADR-0103 decision 6 is kept today** (checked against the cited records):

| Input | Kept in | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `outcome` | `data.result.outcome` of the end line, in every result | ADR-0011 decision 3; ADR-0063 decisions 2 and 9 |
| `resolution_ns`, `intervals_ns` | `data.result` of the end line, exactly when `outcome` is `"converged"` or `"not_converged"` | ADR-0063 decisions 4 and 9 |
| `cv_max` | enters through the stored `outcome`; the replay over `intervals_ns` gives it again with the constants of the instance's source commit | ADR-0063 decision 4; ADR-0007 decision 8 |
| `resolution_share_max` | the harness source at the instance's source commit, named by `source_commit` of `runs/<run_id>/instance.json` | ADR-0103 decisions 6 and 7; ADR-0022; ADR-0007 decision 8 |
| which measurement | the envelope numbers with `step` and `execution` of the line; the point by `step` | ADR-0011 decision 3; ADR-0062 decision 3 |
| the moment | the position of the line in the file; `time` is the writer's clock reading | ADR-0006 decisions 3 and 4 |

- A repeated call appends nothing (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3), so a measurement normally has one end line and one finding. An end line whose identity is not open — a second end line, or one without a start line — is "counted and reported" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 6); which of two such lines gives the finding of the measurement is not decided here. Each is a run tools' end line for decision 4.
- **Lines under another `step` value are not read**: a later record that gives either tool a new `step` value for a changed result definition (ADR-0100 decision 3) states whether and how the results under it enter the finding.
- **A result file** (`sim.json`, `board.json`) that a later record makes hold the same members gives the same finding by the same function. This record names no member of it.

### 4. The finding of a run: a fold over its end lines, and room for the class ground

- **Ground (a) of ADR-0103 decision 5 is derived by a fold over the run's `events.ndjson` in line order**: the run is found imprecise at the position of the first run tools' end line whose measurement is found (decision 3), and at every later position. The end lines of both tools count (cited: ADR-0103 decision 5). The moment of a finding is that position; the moment a reader asks about is a prefix of the log.
- **Ground (b), the class ground, is left to the record that produces its input** (ADR-0103 decision 4). That record says where the input is kept and whether the run's finding under (b) is derived from it or stored, under the rule that governs what it adds. This record makes room for it and fixes nothing of it: the run's finding is (a), derived as above, or (b), from what that record keeps. Until that record exists, (b) never holds (cited: ADR-0103 decision 4 and Limit 1). How the class is established stays with that record and OD-14 sub-question ①.
- ADR-0103 decision 6 notes that the finding under (b) can be reproduced only if the input is kept where a later reader finds it. That stays a condition on the producing record. This record repeats it and does not add to it.

### 5. What an absent finding means

- **No mark is ever absent**, since none is stored. A reader with the run's log computes the finding of every measurement and of the run. "Not computed" is not a state of the data plane. Nothing of the scope was made "before this record" (Context: no result of either tool exists yet), and a result made at any time is read by the same function.
- **A negative answer means "not found imprecise under ADR-0103 as far as its inputs exist"**, not "the environment was precise". Until the class input exists it means "not found on the measured ground" (cited: ADR-0103 Limit 1). Drift and additive overhead are never seen by it (cited: ADR-0103 Limit 4). When the class input exists, its record says what a run that lacks the input (for example, lines written before that record) means.
- **A run whose `instance.json` states no `source_commit`** (allowed: ADR-0022; a reader then takes no other commit for it): the not-converged half of the measured ground is still derived from the line alone. The coarse-clock half needs `resolution_share_max`, which the run's files then do not identify, so it is computed with the value of whatever source the reader runs and is subject to Limit 1.
- **A measurement not in the log** (a torn tail, a death before the end line, a lost working tree — ADR-0006 decisions 6 and 10; ADR-0011 decision 2) is not a measurement of the run, and no finding is owed for it.
- **A result that breaks the presence rules** of ADR-0063 decision 9 (nothing checks a stored result — cited: ADR-0063 Limit 9) cannot be read under that table. What a reader does with it is not decided here.

### 6. What this gives O1

- O1 needs to know which numbers carry the warning (cited: ADR-0103 decision 1). For any number taken from a run tools' end line, a reader derives the finding of its measurement and the finding of the run at any prefix of the log: at that line, at the moment a report is made, or at the end of the run. A number named in a best or a decision log gets a finding only if that record lets a reader find the end line the number came from (decision 7, Limit 5). Which of these answers a report uses, where and how the warning is shown, and which component computes it are part (라) of sub-question ⑪ (OD-27) and the code task that applies ADR-0103.
- At the start of a run no end line exists, so a warning then can rest only on the class ground (cited: ADR-0103 decision 5). That is for the producing record.

### 7. Limits

1. **A derived finding is as stable as the constant it is computed with.** `resolution_share_max` is identified by the instance's source commit (the confirmed part of OD-11), which `instance.json` may not state (cited: ADR-0022; decision 5). A result does not name the commit that took it (cited: ADR-0063 Limit 8). If the constant changes during a run, or a reader uses another commit's value, a later derivation can differ from the finding behind a warning already shown, and a run could even read as no longer found, against ADR-0103 decision 5. A stored mark would freeze it (Alternatives (b), (c)). This is accepted because ADR-0103 decision 3 revises the constant with data from the first runs and no earlier change is foreseen, and a change is a source change that git keeps. The record that changes the constant revisits this one.
2. **Every reader recomputes**, and the finding of a run is a fold over the log from its first line. A stored view of the finding, like the stored spend view, is not excluded: a later record may add one if part (라) needs it. The log stays authoritative.
3. **Reported values are believed**: a kernel can shape its intervals (cited: ADR-0063 Limit 6; ADR-0103 Limit 5), so a forged clean result derives no finding.
4. **No runtime fact is added.** This record rests on no fact that only a runtime machine can establish. The facts on which ADR-0103 rests are its Limit 2 (cited), and they are unchanged.
5. **A number shown away from its end line** — in a best, a decision log or a later run — gets a finding only if the record that holds it lets a reader find the end line it came from. A `comparison` entry of a hypothesis verdict already names its measurement by an attempt *ref* and a `point` (cited: [ADR-0071](ADR-0071-od30-hypothesis-verdict-record.md) decision 3), which leads to the run tools' end lines of that attempt at that point; which `execution` it stands for is OD-18 sub-question ⑳. For the rest it is OD-20 (the best and its reference) and OD-30 (the records of the decision log; OD-23 keeps which role writes them). Using such a number in another run is part (다).

## Alternatives considered

| Point | Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | **(a) Nothing stored; both findings derived** | **Chosen** (decisions 2 to 4). |
| 2 | **(b) A new member of the `sim.run`/`board.run` result** | A changed result definition, so both tools take new `step` values, with rows in StepValues, and results under the old values stay unmarked (ADR-0100 decisions 3 and 5). Readers then need both value sets, and a missing member means "older value" as well as "not found". It holds the finding of one measurement only, not of the run, and the class ground cannot enter it before its input exists. A member put into the `data` of `step_ended` beside `result` would instead be a changed payload and a new kind (ADR-0097 decisions 2 and 3). It restates what the same result already holds. Its one gain is a finding frozen at the commit that took the measurement (Limit 1). |
| 2 | **(c) A new event kind in `events.ndjson`** (ADR-0098) | It would be appended after the end line, and nothing closes the window between two appends (ADR-0011 decision 2; ADR-0006 decision 10), so a death between them leaves a measurement found but unmarked, and absence means nothing. Which part of the tool layer appends it, and when, is the rest of OD-18 and OD-23. Like (b), it restates the end line. It would freeze the finding (Limit 1). |
| 2 | **(d) A member of `STATE.json`** | Its top level is open, so a member that may be absent would raise no version (ADR-0012 decision 2), and a later record may write it inside a round (ADR-0012 decision 6). But the file is replaced as a whole by its one writer, the Round-Manager (ADR-0004 decision 4; ADR-0012 decision 6), and whether a writer keeps a member it does not know is open (ADR-0013 decision 7, sub-question ⑫ of OD-8), so "stays found" would depend on each later writer. It would be a cached view of the log, and whether one is needed depends on where the warning is shown: part (라) of ⑪ (OD-27). The other members of the file are PLAN P1-8 and OD-23. Left open by Limit 2. |
| 2 | **(e) A member of `sim.json`/`board.json`** | No member of either file is named and nothing writes them (ADR-0063 decision 10 and Limit 4). Naming a mark there first would fix part of the files' form, which is OD-13 sub-question ⑩ and PLAN P1-8. They are written by the measurement role, which may die before it writes (ADR-0006 decision 7), and they are per attempt and point, with no place for the run's finding. |
| 2 | A member of `instance.json` | Written once at the start of the instance (ADR-0004 decision 1; [ADR-0025](ADR-0025-od8-instance-file-write.md)), so it cannot hold a finding made later. A start-time statement of the class would be the class input, the producing record's. |
| 4 | The run's finding under (b) decided here | Needs the input, its place and its moment, which are the producing record's and OD-14 sub-question ①. |

## Consequences

- **No code changes because of this record.** The code task that applies ADR-0103 computes the finding as a pure function of an end line's `result` and the constant, and a run's finding as a fold over the run tools' end lines. It writes nothing for either.
- **For the record that produces the class input**: it states where the input is kept and how the run's finding under (b) follows from it (decision 4). If it adds to the data plane, it follows the rule for that kind of addition.
- **For part (라)**: the place where a warning is shown reads the findings by decisions 3 and 4. If it needs a stored view, it adds one (Limit 2).
- **Statements in accepted records that are not edited and are now less exact:**
  - ADR-0103 Status, "Where and in which form the finding is kept is part (나)", and Boundaries, "Sub-question ⑪ (나)": (나) is now answered for its scope, except for the class ground.
  - ADR-0103 decision 6, "Which component computes it, and when, is left to the code task that applies this record once part (나) fixes where the mark goes", and Consequences, "once part (나) fixes where the mark goes": no mark goes anywhere; the code task computes it when it is needed.
  - ADR-0103 decision 5, "so a later reader computes the same finding for every later moment": under derivation this holds only while the reader uses the `resolution_share_max` of the commit that took the measurement (Limit 1).
  - ADR-0103 decision 6, "Under (b) the finding can be reproduced only if the class input is kept where a later reader finds it; that is for the record that produces it and for part (나)": (나) leaves it to the producing record (decision 4).
  - ADR-0061 Limit 3, "sub-question ⑪ of OD-13 [is] untouched: nothing here detects such an environment or marks a result of it", and ADR-0063 Limit 5, "A result taken in an imprecise timing environment carries no mark … ⑪ [is] untouched": a result still carries no mark, now by this record's decision. Parts (가) and (나) are answered for their scope.
  - ADR-0061 Boundaries, "Imprecise timing: its detection and marking", and ADR-0063 Boundaries, "The mark of an imprecise timing environment (…; its form is its own record's)": the form is now decided, and it adds nothing.
  - ADR-0063 decision 9, which lists "the mark of a result taken in an imprecise timing environment (OD-13 sub-question ⑪ (나))" among the questions whose answer could add a member: the answer adds none.
  - ADR-0007 decision 3, that the result files "must be able to hold … the mark of decision 4": unchanged in meaning; that mark is `outcome` and is one input of the derived finding.

## Boundaries (not decided here)

- **Sub-question ⑪ (가) outside the scope**: a simulator, a board (#5 B-2, B-3) or any other configuration; and with it (나) there.
- **Sub-question ⑪ (다)**: whether numbers of a measurement or run found imprecise may be reused across runs (FR-18) or judged against an absolute target (FR-2) or a constraint.
- **Sub-question ⑪ (라)**: where and how the warning is shown to the user, and whether a stored view of the finding is kept for it (OD-27).
- **The producing of the class input** (ADR-0103 decision 4), where it is kept and how the run's finding under (b) follows from it; and **OD-14 sub-question ①**.
- **OD-13 sub-questions ①** (paired re-measurement), **②** (what is done with a not-converged measurement), **⑥** (the spread measure) and **⑩** (the members of `sim.json` and `board.json`).
- **The fields of `STATE.json`, `instance.json`, `sim.json` and `board.json`** (PLAN P1-8); what a reader does with a result that breaks ADR-0063's presence rules; which component computes a finding, and when; whether a best or a decision log lets a reader find the end line of a number (OD-20, OD-18 sub-question ⑳, OD-30); which of two end lines of one identity gives the finding of its measurement (decision 3), which stays with the reporting of such a line (ADR-0011 decision 6) and the rest of OD-17 — the run's finding does not depend on it, since both lines count for decision 4.
- **Owner-only (DRIVER §9)**: the #5 A and B items, OD-3, OD-25, C-6.
- No change to ADR-0103's detection rule or its constant, to ADR-0007's improvement rule, to ADR-0004, ADR-0006, ADR-0011, ADR-0061, ADR-0063 or any other record; to NFR-3 or any FR; to `hilclaw/`, tests or CI; to EventKinds or StepValues. Nothing stops a run.
