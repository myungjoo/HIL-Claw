## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a record and its decision, or a requirement, is named with it; it then says what that source says and adds nothing. Every other sentence is **this record's own** — among them that the step is ended with a `failure` whatever kind was raised, which kind that `failure` holds, the kind `operation_failure_invalid` and that only the tool function raises it, that no backend kind is named, the rule for the message, and every weighing. No sentence is the owner's.

### 1. Scope and terms

- This record is about **a check** — "one call that reaches ⑥" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 1) — of `correctness.check` about one attempt of a run of the first case or of a run whose hw_profile names the mock (cited for the runs: ADR-0021 decision 1; ADR-0038 decision 6), **whose operation raised a tool failure**: "a tool failure an operation raises" (cited: ADR-0043 decisions 5 and 6).
- **Not in scope**, in the words of the landed records: an answer of neither form — a returned value "that is neither a byte string nor one of the two strings", or "something that is no tool failure" raised (cited: ADR-0043 decision 5; answered by ADR-0045); "the death of the process while the operation runs, after which the step is open" (cited: ADR-0043 decision 5); and a raised request to end (cited: ADR-0045 decision 5).
- **A backend kind**: a tool-failure kind that a record of this tool's contract names as one a backend may raise — one of "each tool-failure kind the complete contract lets a backend raise" (cited for the class: ADR-0021 decision 2). **A kind outside the contract**: every other kind a raised tool failure has — a reserved kind, a kind of this contract that is no backend kind, or any other *name*.
- It fixes **five points and nothing else**: what the log holds after the call (decision 3); what the call answers, under which kind and with which message (decision 4); which backend kinds the contract has (decision 5); what is done with a kind outside the contract (decisions 3 and 4); and what follows for the mock (decision 6).
- The terms of ADR-0010 (result, tool failure), ADR-0011 (identity, start line, end line, open, ended), ADR-0015 (reserved kind), ADR-0016 (backend, operation, script, the moments ① to ⑦), ADR-0021 (a check, the classes (i) to (iii)), ADR-0040 (`execution`, `point`), ADR-0043 (an answer of an operation) and ADR-0045 (a raised error, a raised request to end) are used unchanged.

### 2. What is established when the case arises

Each sentence is cited, but for the two marked as this record's reading.

- **The start line of the identity is complete**: a start line is "complete before the step begins to consume the resource" (ADR-0011 decision 2), and the operation is called at ⑥, after the start line of ⑤ (ADR-0040 decision 7).
- **The operation was called and its call is over**: an answer is "what a call of it returned or raised" (ADR-0043 decision 1).
- **No result exists.** A tool failure "says that no outcome was established" (ADR-0010 decision 4). *This record's reading*: that is what the backend says by raising one, and the tool function has nothing else from that call to make a result of.
- **The process lives**: its death is not meant (ADR-0043 decision 5, quoted in decision 1).
- **What a step consumed is consumed whether or not it has an outcome**: "A step that ended without an outcome has spent as well" (ADR-0011 decision 7). *This record's reading*: whether the experiment was carried out, in part or in whole, and whether anything was consumed at a target is not known from a raised tool failure.

### 3. What the log holds after the call

- **After an operation raised a tool failure, the tool function ends the step without an outcome: it appends the end line of the identity, and that line holds a `failure`. This holds whatever the kind of the raised tool failure is.** The identity is then ended. Which kind and message the `failure` holds is decision 4.
- **The member exists for this.** The `failure` of an end line "holds the `kind` and `message` of the tool failure when the step ended without an outcome", and an end line holds "exactly one of `result` and `failure`" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3); no result exists (decision 2).
- **The `spend` of that line** is what ADR-0040 decision 6 says of every end line of this tool: "the empty object — also on an end line with `failure`", as long as no record names an axis that is accounted through the log (cited). ADR-0045 decision 3 left it at that for its end line, and so does this record: it adds no axis, no amount and no rule.
- **Four candidates were weighed against five tests**: (i) whether it fits "no outcome was established" and the form of an end line (cited above); (ii) what a later call with the same identity is given, and whether the check can be asked for again without a rule nobody has yet; (iii) what the log shows of what happened; (iv) whether it needs a fact of a real target; (v) how it sits beside ADR-0045, under which an answer of neither form ends the step with a `failure`.

| Candidate | (i) Fit | (ii) The same request later | (iii) The log | (iv) A fact of a target | (v) Beside ADR-0045 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) An end line whose `failure` holds the kind and message that were raised, whatever they are | The form as ADR-0011 decision 3 words it | The raised tool failure again | The raised kind and message. For a kind outside the contract the line then holds a kind for a situation it does not mean, or one to which no record of this contract gives a meaning, and a message the backend's code chose (decision 4) | none | The same state; another kind |
| (b) An end line whose `failure` holds one kind of this tool's own, whatever was raised | A tool failure of the tool function, in that form | That tool failure again | That the operation raised a tool failure; not which. A caller could not tell two backend kinds apart "by the kind" (decision 4) | none | The same state; a kind beside `operation_answer_invalid` |
| (c) No end line: the raised tool failure passes through and the identity stays open | No end line; the identity is in the state "may have run; outcome and amount unknown" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 2), although the tool function knows that the call is over and that it has no outcome | `step_open` — the answer after the death of a process; the raised tool failure was answered once and is in no file | The start line only | none | An answer out of form would end the identity and a tool failure of the same backend would leave it open |
| (d) It is treated as an answer of neither form: an end line with `operation_answer_invalid` | The sentence of that kind says that what was raised was "no tool failure" (cited: ADR-0045 decision 4), and a tool failure an operation raises is "Not meant" by the case (cited: ADR-0043 decision 5) | That tool failure again | A kind whose sentence is false of the line | none | One kind for two cases the landed records keep apart |

- **Test (ii) does not tell the candidates apart in what it asks second.** Under each of them the start line is complete, so the step "is not run again under this identity" or is open (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4), and a further check of that attempt at that point is a new identity with a higher `execution`, of which "whether and when that is done is not this record's (OD-10, the retry part of OD-17)" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3).
- **The choice between the two states is an ended identity, as in ADR-0045 decision 3 and for its reason.** An open step says that it is not known "Whether the resource was consumed, how much, and whether an outcome existed" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4). After a raised tool failure the tool function knows that the call of the operation is over and that it has no outcome; the state ADR-0011 has for that is ended with a `failure` — "the step is over without an outcome" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4). The sentences quoted in Context presuppose the same. **Between (a) and (b) the choice goes by the kind that was raised** (decision 4): (a) for a backend kind, (b) for a kind outside the contract.
- **What the choice costs.** The identity is used up by a tool failure of a backend: no later line puts an outcome in its place under the landed records (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4; ADR-0006 decision 5, a complete line is never edited or removed), and "the corrected call would need a further `execution`, which nobody may yet grant" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2). An identity that was ended for a cause that would not occur again stays ended. What a backend consumed before it raised is on no line beyond the `spend` the tool function states, and an ended identity is not among the open steps the spend view lists (cited: ADR-0011 decision 7). *The other state* would cost the stop ADR-0040 decision 2 accepted for a death during a check ("an open step is a point the run does not pass by itself" — cited there from ADR-0011 Consequences) after every tool failure of a backend. **Neither cost is resolved here**; what follows either state "is left to the same places" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4).
- **What would reopen it.** A record that names a backend kind and finds that an ended identity is the wrong state for the situation of that kind; it then amends this decision for its kind. A recovery rule (OD-10, the retry part of OD-17) that needs such a step to be open. A record of the frame that gives all four HW tools one rule. A statement of the owner as named in Status. None of these is decided or excluded here.

### 4. What the call answers: the kind and the message

- **The call is the tool failure its end line holds.** A first call "returns what it holds" (cited: ADR-0040 decision 7, the row of ⑦), and a later call with the same identity that the lookup answers is given "the same tool failure again" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3); "no backend is selected or called for it" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2).
- **Where the raised kind is a backend kind, the `failure` holds that kind and the message that was raised**, the message within what the record that names the kind lets it hold. "What else a tool failure of a backend may hold belongs to the slice that names those kinds" (cited: ADR-0040 decision 2); this record names none (decision 5), so it says of such a message only what ADR-0021 decision 3 already says: no content "that holds the seed or that standard error" (cited). **Under the contract as it stands this bullet answers no call.** It is the rule a record that names a backend kind meets; such a record may decide otherwise for its kind (decision 3).
- **Where the raised kind is a kind outside the contract, the `failure` holds this tool's own kind `operation_failure_invalid`** — whichever it was: a reserved kind, a kind of this contract that is no backend kind, or a *name* no record of this contract names. **Under the contract as it stands this is every tool failure an operation raises.**
- **Why such a kind is not written to the line as it was raised.**
  - *A reserved kind.* "No tool's contract defines one of these values with another meaning, and no tool function or step raises one for another situation" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 2). A step that ended with a reserved kind a backend raised would be a step that raises it for another situation, and every later call would be given it from the log — for example `step_open` from an ended identity, the case ADR-0015 decision 2 reserved that kind against ("an ended step whose `failure` carried that kind could not be told from an open one", cited), or one of the four kinds whose sentence says that the call "appended no line, selected or called no backend, did not run the step and consumed nothing at any target" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 2), read from a complete end line.
  - *A kind of this contract that is no backend kind.* `tolerance_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_covered` and `draws_not_stated` are each never the `failure` of an end line (cited: ADR-0021 decision 4; ADR-0041 decisions 4 and 5; ADR-0046 decision 5), and of `operation_answer_invalid` "Only the tool function raises it" (cited: ADR-0045 decision 4).
  - *Any other name.* A backend "adds no result member and no failure kind of its own" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2 (b)). The line would hold, for good (cited: ADR-0006 decision 5), a kind to which no record of this contract gives a meaning — a name that no record names at all, or one that a record names for another tool, as [ADR-0039](ADR-0039-od18-blob-push-fetch-local.md) names `blob_unreadable` for the blob tools — and a record of this contract that later gives that name a meaning would find lines that hold it for another.
- **The sentence a caller may rely on for `operation_failure_invalid`.** *In the check of this identity the operation was called and its call is over; it raised a tool failure whose kind the contract, as the source of that call has it, does not let a backend raise. No result was made. The answer is what a complete end line of the identity holds: the identity is ended.* It says nothing about the attempt, about whether the experiment was carried out or anything was consumed at a target, or about why the backend raised.
- **It is this tool's own kind, raised only by the tool function and only as the `failure` of an end line** — never before the start line, and by this contract in no other situation. It is no backend kind: an operation that raises it raises a kind outside the contract and is answered by this same rule, with this kind. **When the append of that end line fails**, the call is `end_line_failed` and not this kind, and the identity is open or ended (cited: ADR-0026 decisions 3 and 4).
- **Why the tool's own and not reserved** — the test of ADR-0015 decision 2 as ADR-0045 decision 4 applied it. The situation is found by one tool's function against the list of one tool's contract; no other HW tool has a contract, and ADR-0040 decision 2 "says nothing of whether `sim.run`, `board.run` or `profiler.collect` is journaled" (cited). A reserved kind would be a rule of the frame for contracts that do not exist. The kind is answered from an end line only, so the argument ADR-0019 decision 2 took from `step_open` — which guards kinds that are answered without a line — does not ask for a reservation. The cost is that of ADR-0045 decision 4: "a later record of the frame may add to the common part as long as no tool's contract uses the name" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 3), so a record that reserves a kind for this situation takes another name or amends this record.
- **Why one kind and not three.** ADR-0016 decision 6 names "another kind" and "a reserved kind for another situation" side by side as one thing, a backend "that does not answer the contract" (cited), and no landed record says that a tool function answers the three classes differently; the message may say which it was.
- **What the tool function establishes is the kind, not the truth.** It must know the backend kinds of its contract to tell the two cases apart, as it must tell the two forms of an answer apart (cited for the form: ADR-0043 decision 5); this is no check of the frame. A backend kind raised for a situation it does not mean is taken as it is (Limits).
- **The name** is a *name* of ADR-0001 (`[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,31}`; 25 characters), formed after `operation_answer_invalid`. Checked on 2026-10-12 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`: no reserved kind (ADR-0015 decision 2; ADR-0016 decision 3; ADR-0019 decision 2; ADR-0026 decision 3), no kind of another record of this contract (`tolerance_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_stated`, `op_signature_not_covered`, `draws_not_stated`, `operation_answer_invalid`), no other kind the code names, no `step` value, no value of `condition` and no member name equals it, and the string occurred in none of the three trees before this record.
- **The message of `operation_failure_invalid`.**
  - `error.message` is a string, and the `failure` of an end line holds the `kind` and the `message` (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4; ADR-0011 decision 3); no member is added. The message is written to `events.ndjson` with the end line and returned by every later call the lookup answers from it.
  - **It carries nothing of the message that was raised, and it does not show a raised kind that is neither a reserved kind nor a kind of this contract.** Both are strings the backend's code chose, a backend "is handed the seed and captures what the process wrote" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 6), and nothing lets the tool function establish that such a string is free of the seed or of the standard error of a process (the bound is cited: ADR-0021 decision 3).
  - **What it says**: that the operation raised a tool failure of a kind the contract does not let a backend raise; it may say which of the three classes the kind was of; and where the kind is a reserved kind or a kind of this contract it may name it — the name is then one of a list the records fix.
  - The wording is the code's. "A caller tells the cases apart by the kind and never by the message" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 6), and nothing may depend on the wording.
  - **Not decided**: whether anything keeps the raised tool failure outside the log. Nothing here says that it is kept (OD-10).

### 5. Which kinds the contract lets a backend raise

- **The contract as it stands has no backend kind: no record names a tool failure a backend of this tool may raise, and this record names none.** That none was named before is cited: "is not listed" (ADR-0021 decision 5); "The tool-failure kinds a backend may raise are not named, here or elsewhere" (ADR-0044 decision 5).
- **The list is the records' and not a backend's.** A backend "adds no result member and no failure kind of its own; it may use fewer of the outcomes a contract names, never others" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 2 (b)). A kind becomes a backend kind when a record of this contract names it with the situation it means, and at no other moment; the tool function of a source commit has the list of that commit.
- **Why none for the mock.** Behind the mock "there is no hardware, no simulator, no other process and no network" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (a)), so no fault of a target arises behind it. What its scripts must be able to express is "each tool-failure kind the contract lets a backend raise" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (d)) — the kinds of the contract, of which there are none. Check parameters the default script cannot compute a report for stay as ADR-0044 decision 3 left them — "What the code does for them is the code task's" (cited) — and this record names no kind for them.
- **Why none for the local run of `cpu_standin`.** The operation's part there is "starting the process of the verification executable with the dimensions, `draws` and the seed, under its time limit, and capturing its report" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2). A process that was started and failed a condition is no tool failure: the operation returns one of the two conditions, and the call is a broken result (cited: ADR-0043 decision 4; ADR-0021 decision 5; ADR-0042 decision 3). What this record found beside that — an operation that cannot come to an executable or cannot start its process — goes, in this record's reading, with what an operation needs "to obtain and run the verification executable of the attempt at its target" (class (iii) — cited: ADR-0021 decision 2), and "what a call answers when no verification executable can be had" is not decided (cited: ADR-0021 decision 2; the third limit of Status). A kind is named with the situation it means, and neither those situations nor any other of that operation can be told before the operation of `cpu_standin`, and how it comes to the executable, are decided (OD-23; HQ-0010). **This record therefore names no kind for the local run and does not say that there will be one.**
- **Why not one general kind** for "the backend could not carry out the experiment". It would be the kind of every such situation, a fault of a board among them, and that is the third limit of Status.
- **What this means until a record names a backend kind**: every tool failure an operation of this tool raises has a kind outside the contract and is answered as decision 4 says of such a kind: with `operation_failure_invalid`, or with `end_line_failed` when the append of its end line fails.

### 6. What follows for the mock and for the tests

- **By citation.** The mock's operation "does not look at what the script answers", and "what the script raised passes through the operation unchanged" (ADR-0044 decision 2). "The default script never answers (B) and raises no tool failure" (ADR-0044 decision 3).
- **With decisions 3 to 5**: a tool failure that a script of a test raises is answered as decisions 3 and 4 say — under the contract as it stands by an end line with `operation_failure_invalid`, whatever kind the script raised, and by `end_line_failed` when the append of that line fails. **This record adds nothing to what the default script does**: it still raises no tool failure.
- **The list of ADR-0021 decision 2 is unchanged.** Its item "each tool-failure kind the complete contract lets a backend raise" (cited) has no member as long as no record names a backend kind. A tool failure of a kind outside the contract is nothing the contract lets a backend raise, so it is no further item of that list; a conformance test can exercise it all the same, because the mock passes on what a script raised.

### 7. What is not decided by this

- **Every fault of a board, of a simulator or of a target on another machine, and its kinds** (OD-10; #5 B-2, B-3); a reservation or a lock.
- **What a call answers when no verification executable can be had**: whether an operation of `cpu_standin` raises a tool failure then, and which kinds it may raise for it (the first clauses of sub-question ⑩; OD-23; HQ-0010). An operation that does raise one is answered as decisions 3 and 4 say.
- **What anybody does after the answer**: a retry, a further `execution` and who may ask for one, which ended check counts (OD-10; the retry part of OD-17; OD-18 sub-questions ⑪ and ⑭). Nothing in this record says what a caller does on `operation_failure_invalid` or on a backend kind.
- **Fault injection in a live session** (sub-question ⑧).
- **The other HW tools**: what a tool function of `sim.run`, `board.run` or `profiler.collect` does with a tool failure an operation raises (#5 A-4).
- **The spend** beyond ADR-0040 decision 6, and whether anything of the raised tool failure is kept outside the log.

### 8. What a call that reached ⑥ leaves and answers, side by side

Only the two rows marked **this record** are decided here; every other row is cited and holds as it stands in its source.

| What the operation did | The log after the call | The call answers | The same request afterwards | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Returned a report | The start line and an end line with a `result`; ended | A result: pass, fail, or broken with `"report_form"` or `"check_value"` | The stored result | ADR-0040 decision 7; ADR-0042 decision 3; ADR-0043 decision 4 |
| Returned one of the two conditions | The same | A result: broken with `"time_limit"` or `"exit_status"` | The stored result | ADR-0043 decision 4; ADR-0042 decision 3 |
| Returned a value of neither form, or raised an error that is no tool failure | The start line and an end line with the `failure` `operation_answer_invalid`; ended | Tool failure `operation_answer_invalid` | The same tool failure again | ADR-0045 decisions 3 to 5 |
| Raised a request to end that is no tool failure | The start line only; open | No tool failure by ADR-0045: what was raised passes through | `step_open` | ADR-0045 decision 5 |
| **Raised a tool failure of a kind outside the contract** — under the contract as it stands, every raised tool failure | The start line and an end line with the `failure` `operation_failure_invalid`, its message and the `spend` of ADR-0040 decision 6; ended | Tool failure `operation_failure_invalid` | The same tool failure again; no operation is called and nothing is appended | **this record**, decisions 3 and 4; the repeated call: ADR-0011 decision 3 |
| **Raised a tool failure of a backend kind** — no such kind exists | The start line and an end line whose `failure` holds the raised kind and, within what the record that names the kind lets it hold, the raised message, and the `spend` of ADR-0040 decision 6; ended | A tool failure of the raised kind | The same tool failure again; no operation is called and nothing is appended | **this record**, decisions 3 and 4; the repeated call: ADR-0011 decision 3 |

### Walk-through

The names of ADR-0040 to ADR-0046: the run `r1`, the attempt `round` 3, `candidate` 2, `attempt` 1, and "the request" `{"run_id": "r1", "round": 3, "candidate": 2, "attempt": 1, "execution": 1, "point": "sim", …}`. `runs/r1/instance.json` states a covered op_signature, a number of draws and a tolerance. In every row but the sixth the hw_profile of `r1` names the mock; in the first five a test built the registry and bound the mock to its own script. Each row starts from an identity with no line. **`example_kind` stands in for a kind that no record of this contract names; this record gives it no meaning. No value of `draws`, no seed and no tolerance value is shown.**

| The check | The tool function | The caller gets | The request again |
|---|---|---|---|
| The script raises a tool failure of the kind `example_kind` with a message | Finds that the kind is no backend kind; appends the end line with the `failure` `operation_failure_invalid` | Tool failure `operation_failure_invalid`; the message says that a kind outside the contract was raised and shows neither `example_kind` nor the raised message | The same tool failure, from the line; no script is asked |
| The script raises a tool failure of the kind `step_open` | The same | Tool failure `operation_failure_invalid`; the message may name `step_open` | The same tool failure `operation_failure_invalid` from an ended identity — not `step_open` |
| The script raises `tolerance_not_stated`, or `operation_answer_invalid`, or `operation_failure_invalid` | The same | Tool failure `operation_failure_invalid`; the message may name what was raised | The same |
| The script raises a `ValueError` (for contrast) | Ends the step with `operation_answer_invalid` (ADR-0045 decision 5) | Tool failure `operation_answer_invalid` | The same |
| The script raises `example_kind`, and the append of the end line fails | Appends no complete line, or a complete one | Tool failure `end_line_failed` (ADR-0026 decision 3) | `step_open`, or the stored `failure` (ADR-0026 decision 5) |

| The mock is bound to the default script | Is not met with a tool failure: the default script raises none (ADR-0044 decision 3). It judges the report the script returned | A result | The stored result |

### 9. Limits

- **The kind says that a kind outside the contract was raised, and nothing about the attempt or about what happened at a target.** A backend can have carried the kernel out, and consumed what that consumes, before it raised; the `spend` of the line does not show it (decision 3).
- **Until a record names a backend kind, `operation_failure_invalid` is the `failure` of the end line for every tool failure an operation raises** — also for one raised by an operation that had a cause no record has a kind for. The kind then says less than what happened, and the message does not make up for it (decision 4).
- **Whoever wants the check made again has no way under this record.** After an end line with a `failure` no result exists for the identity, and "only a pass counts" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 5); what follows is not decided (decision 7). Nothing in the answer tells a cause that would not occur again from a lasting one.
- **A backend kind raised for a situation it does not mean is taken as it is**, and so is its message: a backend that does not answer the contract in that way "is not detected by the frame" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 6), and not by this tool function either.
- **The list is that of the source that made the call.** A line written with `operation_failure_invalid` stays as it is (cited: ADR-0006 decision 5) when a later record names the raised kind as a backend kind, and two processes of two sources can answer one raised kind in two ways ("Two processes, two tables" — cited: ADR-0016 decision 6).
- **A defect of the tool function is not told from the backend's by the answer**, as in ADR-0045 decision 10: a tool failure the tool function's own code raises in its part of ⑥ is not what this record is about, and where the code draws that line is the code task's.
- **A message that breaks decision 4 is in the log for good** and is not detected by anything here. The choice among the names of the reserved kinds and of this contract's kinds is the backend's.
- The limits of ADR-0011 decision 9, ADR-0016 decision 6, ADR-0021 decision 6, ADR-0026 decision 7, ADR-0040 decision 8, ADR-0043 decision 8, ADR-0044 decision 7 and ADR-0045 decision 10 stand unchanged.

## Consequences

- **What the next slices of the second record must still add** — the list of ADR-0046 Consequences: every other member of the request, the input schema and the description; where the kernel of the attempt, the flags and the time limits come from, and with them what class (iii) adds to the form of ADR-0043; the tool-failure kinds a backend may raise — **struck off in part**: what the tool function does with a raised tool failure is decided, and which kinds are backend kinds is not (none so far); the spend beyond ADR-0040 decision 6. Of OD-18 sub-question ⑩ the first two clauses, the rest of the third, the kernel language and another accelerator.
- **Code** (observations; **this record binds no code**). `hilclaw.check_operation.ask` hands a `ToolFailure` an operation raises on as the same object; that fits this record, since the tool function is where the rule is rendered: it has the raised kind and ends its step. `hilclaw.journaled_step` leaves a step open on a plain `ToolFailure`; that convention stands, and under this record the step of this tool does not let a tool failure of an operation come out as one — it ends without an outcome (in that module: `StepFailed`). Neither module nor `hilclaw.tools` needs a change by this record; the kind is not reserved, and `RESERVED_KINDS` stays as it is. **Not yet possible**: the tool function and its registration, which wait on the rest of the second record.
- **Not edited and now less exact** (follow-ups for the integrator; the list holds what this record found): ADR-0021 decision 5 ("Tool failures of this contract so far" — one more; "is not listed" stays true); ADR-0040 decision 7 ("Which tool failures can stand as the `failure` of an end line of this tool — those a backend may raise — is a later slice's" — this record puts one more there that only the tool function raises) and the row of ⑦ in the tables of the order of a call (ADR-0040 decision 7; ADR-0041 decision 8; ADR-0046 decision 8), which does not name the tool failure the end line holds after a raised tool failure; ADR-0043 decision 6 ("belongs to the slice that names those kinds") and its Boundaries ("what the tool function does with one"); ADR-0044 Boundaries (the same words); ADR-0045, the second limit of its Status, decision 4 ("this one included, is not decided"), decision 8, the last row of its Walk-through, its Consequences and its Boundaries, where they put with the open slice what the tool function does with a raised tool failure. The item "the tool-failure kinds a backend may raise" in the Consequences and Boundaries of ADR-0040 to ADR-0046 stays true of which kinds are named; where ADR-0043 decision 6 had put the rule of the tool function with the same slice, that part is no longer open. In `hilclaw/check_operation.py` the docstring words that call the point "open" are less exact, since a record now has the rule; "That is no rule of a record" and "nothing here decides it" stay true of that module. No sentence of UsageCases became false.
- **A-13 reading**: document mode; no code, dependency, settings file or schema file changes. **This record gives one value of `error.kind` a meaning and a place: the `kind` of the `failure` of an end line of this tool** — as ADR-0045 did for `operation_answer_invalid`, and it is the second kind a record names for that place. That member exists: ADR-0011 decision 3 defined it for the tool failure of a step that ended without an outcome, it holds a *name*, and no record lists its values ("this record fixes the form and **no list of kinds**" — ADR-0010 decision 4); ADR-0040 decision 7 foresaw that a later slice says which tool failures stand there. The record also says that a backend kind, once a record names one, stands in that place; it names none. No member of `STATE.json`, `instance.json`, a spec, `artifacts.json`, a result file or a line of `events.ndjson` is added, removed or changed, and no rule by which a line is valid changes. **This is therefore read as no "데이터 plane 스키마 변경".** If the owner reads A-13 otherwise, that is an objection to this whole record.

## Boundaries (not decided here)

- **Faults of real targets**: every fault of a board, of a simulator or of a target on another machine and its kinds; a reservation or a lock (OD-10; #5 B-2, B-3).
- **Recovery and retry**: what anybody does after `operation_failure_invalid` or after a backend kind; a further `execution`, who may ask for one, and which ended check counts (OD-10; the retry part of OD-17; OD-18 sub-questions ⑪ and ⑭; OD-19).
- **Class (iii) and the operation of `cpu_standin`**: how an operation comes to the verification executable, what a call answers when none can be had beyond decisions 3 and 4 (whether an operation raises a tool failure then, and of which kind), flags, time limits and the environment (the first clauses of sub-question ⑩; OD-23; OD-14; the isolation means, HQ-0010); which backend kinds a later record names.
- **Fault injection in a live session** (sub-question ⑧).
- **The rest of the request**: every other request member, the input schema, the description. **The spend** beyond ADR-0040 decision 6. Whether a raised tool failure is kept outside the log.
- **The other HW tools**: the contracts of `sim.run`, `board.run` and `profiler.collect`, and any rule of the frame for a tool failure an operation raises (#5 A-4); any kind the frame reserves.
- **A target on another machine** (#5 B-2); **other cases than the first** (#5 B-7).
- **Owner-only (DRIVER §9)**: the #5 A and B items; OD-3; OD-25; C-6.
- Any change to ADR-0001 to ADR-0046, to UsageCases, to `hilclaw/`, to tests, to CI, to `pyproject.toml`, to any settings file, or to the `HIL-Claw-data` repository; any new external dependency; any new reserved `kind`.
