## Context

- **The open point.** "A change in the members of the `result` object is not a change in the `data` of `step_ended`"; "How a changed result is told apart is the tool contract's, and it is not decided here. A contract could do it by a new value of `step` (a new identity for the step), by a member of its result, or otherwise; the first changes what the repeated call finds across the change, which is OD-11's and OD-18's to weigh" (cited: ADR-0097 decision 4). This record takes the first; what the repeated call finds then (decision 5) follows from the identity of ADR-0011 decision 3 alone, and whether a turn calls again or keeps an earlier result stays with OD-11 (Boundaries) — no OD-18 rule beyond ADR-0015 decision 4 is needed.
- **What the contracts hold today.** Two closed lists: "Only a record adds a member, and a record that does so amends this table" (cited: ADR-0042 decision 7; ADR-0063 decision 9 "as ADR-0042 decision 7"). Four `step` values, fixed by the contracts (ADR-0040 decision 3, ADR-0062 decision 3). "`step` is not a member of a request: the tool's contract fixes it … Two tools of the registry do not share a `step` value" (cited: ADR-0015 decision 4).
- **The gap a closed list leaves.** "What a caller may rely on … **Not** that every result it is given was made under this record's table: a repeated call 'returns what the end line holds' … so a result that one source commit stored is returned, member for member, by a later one"; "A result carries no version member"; "How a reader treats a result of another list is the open question just cited … and is not decided here" (cited: ADR-0042 decision 7).
- **Why it matters.** The log "is appended to for the whole life of a run and is never rewritten, while the tool layer may be replaced at a round boundary (NFR-7 ①)" (cited: ADR-0006 decision 8); a resumed turn that finds a step ended may "Skip it. With a `result`, take it from the line" (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4); runs are resumed (FR-6). A result stored under one list can therefore reach a caller that relies on another.
- **A change of a landed list is no ordinary change.** "Once this table has landed, a change of it … is a change of a closed list stored in `events.ndjson`, and is read as ADR-0060 read it" (cited: ADR-0063 A-13 reading), that is, as a data-plane schema change put to the owner (ADR-0060 Context; HQ-0020). This record does not make such a change ordinary; it says how the results of one are told apart once it is made.

## Decision

### 1. What the code does today (observation, binds nothing)

- `hilclaw/journal.py`: the identity of a step line is formed from `step`, `execution` and the envelope numbers (`_checked`); `_ENDED` holds `step`, `execution`, `spend`, `result`, `failure`; "`result` is a `dict`; its members are not looked at" (cited: its module docstring).
- `hilclaw/journaled_step.py`: when the identity is ended, `run` returns `_answer(found.result, found.failure)` and calls no step — "A repeated call gives equal values from the first end line" (cited: its module docstring). The stored result is returned unchanged.
- `hilclaw/journaled_tool.py`: "``step`` is the contract's and is never read from a request" (cited: its module docstring). `journaled_tool.tool_function` and `hw_tool.tool_function` are given one `step` when the function is built; `journaled_tool.stepped_function` and `hw_tool.stepped_tool_function` are given the contract's `steps` and a `step_of` callable, so that the `step` of a call follows from the checked request. `correctness.check` is built by `hw_tool.stepped_tool_function` (`hilclaw/check_tool.py`), its `step` following from `point` (`check_identity.STEPS`, `check_identity.step_of`). No tool function of `sim.run` or `board.run` is built in `hilclaw/`, and no module in `hilclaw/` imports `hilclaw/run_identity.py`. The four values are bound once, in `hilclaw/check_identity.py` (`STEP_SIM`, `STEP_BOARD`) and `hilclaw/run_identity.py` (`STEP_SIM_RUN`, `STEP_BOARD_RUN`).
- Nothing in the code marks which list a stored result was made under.

### 2. Term

**The result definition of a `step` value** is what the accepted records of a tool contract state of a result of a step under that value: the member list, the presence rules and relations, and the type, form, values and meaning of each member. For the four values that exist today it is what the accepted records state at `ab71b86`, the amendments they made included (for example ADR-0060's amendment of ADR-0042's table). A **result** is the `result` of a `step_ended` line, read under the result definition of that line's `step`.

### 3. The rule: a changed result definition takes a new `step` value

- **The result definition of a `step` value is fixed and never changes.** It is fixed when the record that closes the member list of results under that value is accepted, or at `ab71b86` for the four values of today (decision 2). Before then, a record of the same contract that fills a part an earlier record of it left open (as ADR-0063 filled what ADR-0061 left with "No member of a result is named here") is not a change. A later record that wants results of another definition for a step gives that step **a new `step` value that no accepted record and no tool has used**, and states the result definition under it — in full, or as the earlier table amended under the new value.
- **Every `step` value whose result definition changes takes its own new value.** A list shared by several values changes all of them: a change of the list of `correctness.check` changes both values of ADR-0040, since one list serves both points (ADR-0042 decision 1, citing ADR-0038 decision 4; ADR-0042 decision 7).
- **A value is never reused.** A value that a contract stops using keeps its definition; no record gives it another, to the same or another tool, and a value whose record is reverted stays used, since lines under it may exist and are never removed (ADR-0006 decision 5). This extends "Two tools of the registry do not share a `step` value" (ADR-0015 decision 4) from tools to definitions; the new value is fixed by the contract and is not a member of a request, as that decision has it.
- **How a result is told apart**: by the `step` of its line alone. Two results made under one `step` value once its definition is fixed (decision 2) have one definition, in any log and at any time; results made before that are the case of Consequences ("The cost, stated"). Nothing inside `result`, and no member of `data`, of the envelope or of `instance.json`, says which definition applies.
- **What it does not fix**: the spelling of a new value (a *name* of ADR-0001 not used before; ADR-0040 and ADR-0062 say of theirs that they are "no rule for the `step` values of another tool"), and whether the contract keeps any relation to its earlier value beyond decision 5.

### 4. What counts as a change

- **Any change of the result definition**, with no distinction between kinds: a member added (required or optional), removed or renamed; a change in the type, form or meaning of a member; a narrowing or a widening of its values, a new value of an enumerated member such as `outcome` or `condition` included; a change in a presence rule or a relation. This mirrors ADR-0097 decision 3.
- **What is not a change**: a value that the definition leaves open being used; a change of code that keeps the definition; a change of anything outside the result — the request, the failure kinds, the axes of `spend`, what the step does (Boundaries).
- **Why no distinction** (this record's own). *A caller relies on a closed list member by member* (ADR-0042 decision 7): a caller that tests for a member, or for the absence of a value, misreads a result of another list even when today's journal passes the result through (decision 1). *A change of meaning cannot be detected* in a stored result: only a rule that does not judge compatibility catches it. *Compatibility is a reader's question* and belongs to OD-11. It is the same shape as ADR-0097 decision 3 and ADR-0006 decision 8.

### 5. What the repeated call finds across such a change

- **A call under the later definition forms its identity with the new value.** Every identity ended or open under the earlier value is another identity (ADR-0011 decision 3: identity = envelope numbers with `step` and `execution`). Before any call under the new value for its target, the identity presents **no line**: the step is run, and its start and end lines carry the new value.
- **It never returns a result stored under the earlier value**, and it does not take an identity open under the earlier value as open. A caller of the later definition is therefore given only results of that definition, which closes the gap ADR-0042 decision 7 named for every result made after the change.
- **The lines under the earlier value stay** (ADR-0006 decision 5). Their amounts are summed by the fold of ADR-0011 decision 6 like any other, and an identity open under the earlier value stays among the open steps of the spend view. A turn that wants to know what happened under the earlier value reads those lines as lines of another step.
- **How a caller states `execution` under the new value** is as ADR-0011 decision 3 states for any step; this record adds nothing.
- **NFR-7 ① and FR-6.** When the tool layer is replaced at a round boundary, or a run is resumed by a later source, a step ended under the earlier value is run again if it is called again; a step open under the earlier value may have consumed, and the new call runs anyway. Both are visible in the log and in the spend view; neither is hidden. Whether a turn should call again at all after a change, or keep an earlier result as the answer for its target, is not the tool layer's (OD-11; Boundaries).

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | What a step ended under the earlier list and called again under the later one meets | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) A new `step` value for every change of a result definition | No line: the step is run again; the earlier result is never returned | **Adopted** (decisions 3 to 5). It needs no new member, value form or kind; `step` is already in every step line, already the contract's (ADR-0015 decision 4), and already part of the identity the lookup matches (decision 1). The cost is a second execution, visible in the log and the spend view. |
| (b) A member inside the result that marks its list | The earlier result is returned; the caller must read the marker to know how to read the rest | Against ADR-0042 decision 7 ("A result carries no version member"), which is not reopened: nothing new argues for it. Adding the member to the lists of ADR-0042 and ADR-0063 is itself a change of a closed list stored in the log (ADR-0063 A-13 reading), and "absent means the first list" would be an implicit rule each reader must know. The repeated call would still return a result the caller cannot rely on. |
| (c) Rely on the P1 pin (`source_commit`, ADR-0010 decision 7, ADR-0022) and leave results unmarked | The earlier result is returned, unmarked | `source_commit` is the commit at the start of an instance (ADR-0022), in a file written once (cited: ADR-0006 decision 8, "`instance.json`, which is written once (ADR-0004)"); a tool layer replaced later in the run (NFR-7 ①), or another process on another source (ADR-0010 decision 7; ADR-0022, "Two processes, two sources"), writes results the member does not describe, and no line carries a commit. "… whether a tool checks its own commit against the one a run recorded is OD-11" (cited: ADR-0010 decision 7). This is the gap of ADR-0042 decision 7 left as it is. The P1 pin itself is the owner's (OD-11 confirmed part, #5 A-12 point 4) and is not changed; it is only not used to tell results apart. |
| (d) A new event kind per change | The repeated-call rule finds a step's state only from the two kinds it names (ADR-0011 decision 3), so a line of a new end kind is not found as an end until the journal and its reader change with the tool (ADR-0097 decision 4) | Rejected by ADR-0097 decision 4: "the event layer cannot know any tool's members" and every tool change would become a change of the journal and its reader (cited). Not re-weighed. |
| (e) Leave ㉟ (가) open | Undecided: each later record chooses | Every later change of a list would choose the means unrecorded, and it cannot be chosen afterwards for results already in a log (ADR-0006 decision 5). The rule needs no value, member or kind that does not exist and nothing of OD-11. |
| (f) A new value only for changes a reader cannot pass over (for example not for an added optional member) | The earlier result is returned for a "compatible" change | Decision 4: a caller relies on a closed list member by member, a change of meaning cannot be detected, and the line between compatible and incompatible is OD-11's. |

## Consequences

- **No code changes.** `hilclaw/journal.py` stays right: it does not look at `result`, and the identity already includes `step` (decision 1). `hilclaw/journaled_step.py` stays right: the lookup is by identity, so a new value finds no line of an earlier one. `hilclaw/journaled_tool.py` and `hilclaw/hw_tool.py` take `step` from the contract. A later record that changes a result definition brings its own code task, which changes the constant in `hilclaw/check_identity.py` or `hilclaw/run_identity.py` together with the list. No code task follows from this record.
- **For a record that changes a result definition**: it gives each affected step a new value, checks that the value is unused as ADR-0040 and ADR-0062 checked theirs, states the definition under it, leaves the earlier values with their definitions, and states its A-13 reading as ADR-0063 asks of a change of a landed list.
- **Not edited and now less exact**: ADR-0042 decision 7 ("Only a record adds a member, and a record that does so amends this table" — now under a new `step` value; "a log can come to hold results of two member lists" — now under two values; "How a reader treats a result of another list is the open question just cited (OD-8 sub-question ⑧)"); ADR-0063 decision 9 ("how a reader treats a result of another list is OD-8 sub-question ⑧ and OD-11"); ADR-0097 decision 4 ("How a changed result is told apart is the tool contract's, and it is not decided here") and its Boundaries (㉟ (가)). No earlier ADR is edited.
- **The cost, stated**: `step` values accumulate over the life of the system; a step called again after a change is executed again and spends again, a board measurement included; an identity open under the earlier value does not stop a call under the new one; a reader that wants every result of one tool across changes must know every value it has used. Results that a log may hold under the four values of today from before their definitions as fixed here (for `correctness.check`, before ADR-0060's amendment reached the code) are not told apart by this rule; what a reader does with one is OD-11. ADR-0060's amendment, the one change of a landed list so far, kept its `step` values; this rule would have asked new ones of it, and it does not reopen that amendment.
- **A-13 reading**: document mode; no code, dependency, settings file or schema file changes, and no member, value or kind of the data plane is added or changed.
- **Runtime acceptance items**: none.

## Boundaries (not decided here)

- **Sub-question ㉟ (나)**: how a change in the unit or meaning of an axis of `spend` is told apart (FR-3, OD-19, #5 A-8).
- **OD-11** beyond what the rule needs: read guarantees and migration; whether a run may pass from one source commit to another; whether a run may hold results of two definitions of one step and what a reader does with an older result — which counts for a target, comparisons across a change (S3, S5), incumbent re-measurement; a change of the request or of what a step does that keeps the result definition (the measurement-contract pin).
- **Recovery of an open step and retry** (OD-10, the rest of OD-17), an identity left open under an earlier value included.
- **Sub-question ㊱, registered under OD-8**, two parts. (가) Whether a change in the failure kinds a contract states for a journaled step — held as `failure` in an end line and raised again by the repeated call (ADR-0011 decision 3) — is told apart by the rule of decision 3, or otherwise. (나) Where the `step` values used so far are listed, if anywhere, so that a record can check that a value is unused (decision 3).
- The members of any tool's result; any new tool, `step` value or kind; `profiler.collect` (HQ-0025); the OD-18 sub-questions held by blocked tasks (T-0210, T-0239, T-0264).
