## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a source is named with it. Every other sentence is **this record's own**.

### 1. Scope

- **One spec**: that of the first coder call — `candidate` 1, `attempt` 1 — of a round `r` ≥ 1 of a run of ADR-0120 decision 1 whose `instance.json` states `candidates_per_round` 1 and an `op_signature` that ADR-0041 decision 5 covers — the run shape of ADR-0138 decision 1.
- **Outside**: round 0, which stays ADR-0138's unchanged; repair attempts (`attempt` > 1); other candidates and runs whose N is not 1; everything under Boundaries.
- **A run whose hw_profile names the mock** (ADR-0150 gives it no catalog): its spec is written like any other and **its technique list can only be empty** — an id is refused (decision 3, ⑦) because no catalog directory exists for it. Why not refuse the spec: `spec.write` checks no accelerator for round 0 (cited: ADR-0138 decision 3), and a mock run can still exercise a round ≥ 1 with no technique; an empty list says only that no technique is to be applied in the call (cited: ADR-0058 decision 1).

### 2. The source of each member

| Member | Value | Source, and the record that makes it right |
|---|---|---|

A file changed since the start gives what it states now (cited: ADR-0018 decision 6) |
| `id.round`, `id.candidate`, `id.attempt` | `r`, `1`, `1` | The request's tuple (decision 3) |
| `task.build`, `repair`, `ext` | absent | As in ADR-0138 decision 2: no member of `task.build` is named (OD-6 ②); `repair` is present "exactly when `id.attempt` > 1" (cited: ADR-0002 decision 2); no `ext` member is named |
| `guidance.techniques` | one `{id, text}` per id of the request, in its order | (b) and (c) below |
| `feedback.attempts` | the shown earlier attempts | (d) below |

- **(b) The technique list.** For each id of the request's `techniques`, in order, the entry is `hilclaw.catalog.technique(<accelerator>, <id>)`, `<accelerator>` being the `accelerator` of the `hw_profile` record of the `instance.json` read at ③, which becomes the spec's `hw_profile.record.accelerator` — the `{id, text}` of ADR-0150 decision 4, unchanged (cited). **The writer checks ADR-0150 decision 4's rule against the spec it composes**: the directory is that of the spec's accelerator (by the lookup), and the entry's `kind`, `dtype` and `layout` equal those of `task.op_signature.record` and its language equals `task.contract.language`. The entry's four values are those of `hilclaw.catalog.APPLIES_TO` (observation), so within decision 1 the second half cannot refuse today; it is kept, as ADR-0138 keeps its check ⑦, for a later catalog that widens one side. Then:
  - an id of the *name* form that names no entry of the directory: `technique_unknown`; an entry whose values differ from the spec's: `technique_not_applicable`; an id not of the *name* form, or one id twice in the request: `invalid_request` — **no entry is carried twice**, since a second copy of one guidance adds nothing;
  - a directory that is missing, does not pass `hilclaw.catalog.check` or cannot be read: `catalog_unusable`. **The catalog is read only for a non-empty list**, so an empty list never fails on it;
  - **the order is the request's**, unchanged; this record gives the order no meaning;
  - **the list may be empty and has no length bound** beyond one entry per id; whether a round ≥ 1 may carry none is not the writer's to judge (HQ-0048), and the writer refuses nothing for it.
- **(c) The source of the ids: a member `techniques` of the `spec.write` request**, an array of ids (decision 3). **This is the writer's end only**: which ids a round's spec carries, who chooses them and by what criteria, how the choice reaches the coder-driver, and what the round's hypothesis record or `decision.md` says of them are the selection side's (HQ-0048; cited: ADR-0150 decision 4 "Not decided here"). The form does not cross it: any record of the selection side can hand ids to the caller of the writer, and the writer reads no selection file. **No member of a run file names a round's techniques** — a hypothesis names none (cited: ADR-0059 decision 3) — so the request is the only place they can enter without a new data-plane member. **Until the Round-Manager's round ≥ 1 steps are defined (PLAN P2-1), no agent calls the writer for a round ≥ 1**: the Round-Manager stops after step 1 in rounds ≥ 1 (cited: ADR-0141 decision 6). So no placeholder id is needed or written (cited: ADR-0138 Alternatives (f)); a person, test or script that calls the command-line front with an empty list gets a spec that applies no technique.
- **(d) `feedback.attempts`.** **Shown**: every attempt of candidate 1 of each round 0 to `r` − 1 whose spec exists, found by listing `rounds/<QQQQ>/coder/candidates/01/attempts/` (one directory of known path, cited: ADR-0004 decision 3) and taking each `<AA>` that holds a `spec.json`, **and whose sim-point standing is settled**: `verified` or `not verified` (ADR-0110 decision 3, computed over the run's log as ADR-0131 decision 3 folds it). **Order**: ascending `round`, then `attempt`. Each entry, its `round`, `candidate` and `attempt` the attempt's tuple (cited: ADR-0002 decision 2), holds:
  - **`techniques`**: the `id` of each element of that attempt's own spec's `guidance.techniques`, in its order (cited: ADR-0002 decision 2, "the ids it applied" — **read here** as the ids its spec gave it, since whether the coder followed them is not known); round 0's are none (cited: ADR-0138 decision 2 (b)).
  - **`failure`**, present exactly when the attempt's standing is `not verified` at the sim point, or `verified` there and `not verified` at the board point (the first such point is *the failed point*). **`stage`**: `compile` when the first end line of the failed point, in line order, whose result is `"fail"` or `"broken"` (*the deciding result*) is broken with `condition` `build_exit_status` or `build_time_limit` — a compile or link failure and a build past its limit (cited: ADR-0060 decision 4) —, and otherwise the failed point's name, `sim` or `board` (the names of ADR-0002 decision 2). **`report`**: a fixed English sentence naming the stage, the deciding result's `outcome` and, for a broken one, its `condition` in words; for `compile`, followed by the compiler log of the deciding check's identity (ADR-0078 decision 3) when that file exists and is not empty. **It holds nothing else of the result** — no `counts`, `draws`, `tolerance` or `shape`: whether a tolerance is shown to the coder is open (cited: ADR-0002 Boundaries), and the path through `counts` is the one ADR-0042 decision 4 names. The log holds no seed (cited: ADR-0078 decision 5); **this record's reading** is that it holds no input or expected value of a check either, since they are drawn in the verification process from that seed and "do not exist as files" (cited: ADR-0002 Context), so the report keeps ADR-0002's "No field carries verification inputs". The sentences are left to the code slice and reviewed against this rule. A board failure is shown as the log has it and says nothing of FR-1 ⑶'s triage of its cause.
  - **`profile`: absent.** K3 says what the round's profile is on `cpu_standin`, but carrying it means giving `profile` defined members, which raises `schema_version` (cited: ADR-0002 decision 7; ADR-0050 decision 6) — a data-plane change (CLAUDE.md A-13) this record does not make. A later record that defines the members (with (B4)) and the version gives the measurements a place.
  - **Not shown**: an attempt whose sim-point standing is `undetermined` — a coder call that brought in nothing, a check never run, an open identity or a failure of class (ii) (cited: ADR-0110 decisions 2 and 3). ADR-0002 has no form for an outcome not known, and an entry without `failure` would say that the attempt built and passed where the log shows nothing. **An attempt `verified` at the sim point and `undetermined` at the board point is shown without `failure`**: the log shows its pass at the sim point and no failure at any point, which is what such an entry says; whether it would pass the board is not claimed. Hiding it would hide every attempt held at the board gate (OD-3).
  - **Dependencies.** An attempt of a run whose hw_profile names `cpu_standin` gets a settled standing only once a `cpu_standin` operation of `correctness.check` is in its backend table (Context; the stand-in backends are PLAN P1-1's, with HQ-0021, HQ-0026 and OD-14 ① — cited: ADR-0142 Boundaries), and the compiler log file exists only once ADR-0078's code lands (HQ-0026); until then `feedback.attempts` of such a run is empty, and a `compile` report without the log is the sentence alone. A mock run's attempts can have a settled standing today, since the table holds the mock; the mock builds nothing and leaves no log (cited: ADR-0078 decision 2), so its `compile` reports are the sentence alone. The designs read are on `main`; neither HQ is answered here.
- **(e) `feedback.base`: absent.** Naming the attempt a call starts from is choosing a line of descent (lineage, cited: ADR-0002 decision 4, OD-20), and no landed record says which attempt of a first-case run is the one to improve beyond the baseline's identity (cited: ADR-0119 decision 2); a `base` the coder cannot read would also be idle, and whether the coder gets earlier sources is OD-8, OD-14 and OD-23 (cited: ADR-0002 Consequences). The coder sees the earlier attempts' techniques and failures, not their sources beyond what a compiler log quotes (ADR-0078 decision 5).
- **No verification input** is in the spec: the run-side members are ADR-0138's, the entries hold ids and reports only, and (d) keeps the rule for each report.

### 3. What composes and writes it: `spec.write`, widened

- **The role stays the coder-driver's** (cited: ADR-0126 decision 4), and the means is ADR-0138's: the coder-driver calls `spec.write` through the MCP front before it requests the call (cited: ADR-0138 decision 3). **One writer for every spec of these runs**; nothing here defines the coder-driver's delegation of a round ≥ 1 or `coder.run` for it (Boundaries).

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Request | `run_id`, `round`, `candidate`, `attempt`, all required, as in ADR-0138 decision 3; and **`techniques`**, an array of *names* with no name twice, possibly empty, **required exactly when `round` ≥ 1**. `invalid_request` for: any other member; one missing; `techniques` with `round` 0, or absent with `round` ≥ 1; an element not a *name*, or one twice; `candidate` or `attempt` other than 1. **A request of `round` 0 is answered exactly as ADR-0138 decision 3 answers it** |
| Result | `{}` |
| Checks, in order | ① for the request above, ② to ⑥ of ADR-0138 decision 3 unchanged; for `round` ≥ 1: ⑦ the techniques of decision 2 (b), only for a non-empty list (`catalog_unusable`, `technique_unknown`, `technique_not_applicable`, the first id that fails deciding); ⑧ the earlier attempts' directories listed — a directory that is not there gives no attempt and is no failure — and each `spec.json` found read and accepted by `hilclaw.spec_file.parse` with the attempt's tuple as its `id` (`feedback_unreadable`); ⑨ when ⑧ found any, the run's `events.ndjson` read from its first line with the kinds, order and widening of ADR-0131 decision 3 (`run_file_missing`, `run_file_unreadable`, `run_file_invalid`, `run_file_unsupported`) — a log that is not there below a data root that is holds no line and is no failure (cited: ADR-0131 decision 3), so every attempt is then `undetermined` —, then, for the attempts found, a check result without an admitted `outcome` (`check_result_invalid`, ADR-0131 decision 3); ⑩ the compiler logs of decision 2 (d), a missing file being no failure (`feedback_unreadable` for any other failure of the read); ⑪ and ⑫ ADR-0138's ⑦ and ⑧ for the composed document (`spec_invalid`; `spec_exists`, `spec_not_written`) |

- **Every check before ⑫ creates nothing**, and reads only `instance.json`, the catalog directory, the earlier attempts' directories and specs, `events.ndjson` and the compiler logs, at this call, keeping nothing. `decision/hypotheses.json` and `decision.md` are not read.
- **Kinds.** Reused with their meanings: those of ADR-0138 decision 3; the four `run_file_*` kinds for the log — **this record widens ADR-0019 decision 2 to this read**, as ADR-0131 did, and the property holds: such a call appended no line, selected no backend, ran no step and consumed nothing; `check_result_invalid` (ADR-0131) for the same lines. **New**: `catalog_unusable`, `technique_unknown`, `technique_not_applicable` (24 characters), `feedback_unreadable`, each a *name* of ADR-0001 decision 1. Searched on 2026-10-23 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/`, `orchestration/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`: the four names do not occur. They are names of the tool layer, not values of the data plane; none is reserved.
- **`id.round` is not held against `STATE.json`**: `STATE.json` is the Round-Manager's (cited: ADR-0004 decision 4), the round reaches the coder-driver with its delegation (cited: ADR-0126 decision 6), and the order of a spec write against `STATE.json` is OD-8 ㉖ (다), which ADR-0138 left open; the delegation steps that would give the round are not defined (Boundaries). The cost: a wrong `round` from a caller writes a spec under that round, with the feedback as of that round.
- **Not journaled**, for ADR-0138's reasons: it costs no limited resource and its outcome is the file; reading the log appends no line.
- **Left to the code slice**: the module split, the input schema, the report sentences, the messages and the encoding (as ADR-0138 leaves its own).

| (c) The coder-driver composes `text`, or the whole spec, itself | A model copying guidance can drift from the entry (ADR-0138 Alternatives (b)); the coder-driver writes no file of the run with its own tools (ADR-0126 decision 2) | Rejected |
| (d) A separate tool for rounds ≥ 1 | Two writers of one file; ①–⑥ and ⑪–⑫ repeated; one more tool in the coder-driver's list | Rejected |
| (e) Composition folded into the coder-call function | Changes ADR-0126 decision 3, as ADR-0138 decision 4 weighs | Rejected |
| (f1) Every earlier attempt with a settled sim-point standing, in order | Keeps the baseline and every failure in view (K1, FR-1 ⑴); with N = 1 a round adds one entry | **Chosen**; the spec grows with the rounds |
| (f0) `profile` holding the run tools' results (K3) | Defined members of `profile` raise `schema_version` (ADR-0002 decision 7): a data-plane change | Not adopted here (decision 2 (d)) |
| (f2) The previous round only | Drops the baseline and earlier failures after round 1; K1 says "직전 라운드" but not "only" | Rejected |
| (f3) Contrastive top-k/bottom-k (#7 (6)) | Needs a ranking of attempts (OD-7), measurements in the spec (f0) and a k; with N = 1 and few rounds, every attempt fits any small k | Not adopted for this scope; open elsewhere |
| (f4) No feedback | Against FR-1 ⑴'s failure report back to code generation and K1 | Rejected |
| (f5) Undetermined attempts shown without `failure` | States a pass the log does not show | Rejected |
| (g) `feedback.base` naming the baseline or the latest verified attempt | Lineage (OD-20) and what is "best" are not settled; earlier sources may not reach the coder (ADR-0002 Consequences) | Rejected for now |

## Consequences

- **Code slices for the planner** (code mode; tests with fixture files only, no `claude`, D16), each within the D4 cap:
  1. A new module, for example `hilclaw/spec_feedback.py`, with `tests/test_spec_feedback.py`: the `feedback.attempts` of decision 2 (d) for a run and a round, over the attempt directories, `hilclaw.spec_file.parse`, the log through `hilclaw.check_standing`, and the compiler log path; no registry entry.
  2. `hilclaw/spec_tool.py` and `tests/test_spec_tool.py`: the request of decision 3, checks ⑦ to ⑩ with `hilclaw.catalog` and slice 1, and the new kinds; its docstring and that of `hilclaw/catalog.py` ("Nothing calls this module yet"; "the rule … is not checked") updated.
  The coder-driver's definition and the Round-Manager's round ≥ 1 steps wait on HQ-0048, HQ-0053 and PLAN P2-1.
- **Runtime acceptance items: none.** (lxvii) is not used: the tool keeps the front, caller and permission of ADR-0138, whose item (xxxiii) covers a session's call of it; a request member adds nothing a live session must show.
- **Less exact, not edited** (found by a search of `ARCHITECTURE/decisions/` and `hilclaw/` for "no record defines that spec", "later rounds", "round ≥ 1", "rest of OD-6", "how an entry reaches a spec" and "which and how many attempts"; not claimed complete). Each stays true outside decision 1:
  - ADR-0138 decision 1 ("later rounds" outside), Walk-through ("`round` 1 … `invalid_request`: no record defines that spec yet"), decision 3 (the request's tuple) and Boundaries ("Specs of later rounds"; "`feedback` selection, `feedback.base` and profiles").
  - ADR-0150 decision 4 ("Not decided here": how entries "reach `spec.write`", what checks the rule) and Boundaries ("Round ≥ 1 spec composition"); ADR-0141 decision 6 ("its spec is the rest of OD-6"; "The tuple is the only one `spec.write` accepts") and Alternatives (e) ("a later round's spec is open"); ADR-0126 Scope "Outside", decision 4 and Boundaries ("how it is composed and by what means it is written stay open"), as ADR-0138 lists them; ADR-0058 Boundaries ("what the orchestrator shows in `feedback`"); ADR-0002 decision 4 ("Not decided": which attempts and their order).
  - The docstrings of `hilclaw/spec_tool.py` ("a tuple other than 0, 1, 1") and `hilclaw/catalog.py` (above): true until slice 2 lands.
- **A-13 reading.** Document mode. No code, test, setting or dependency changes. **No data-plane schema change**: no member, file, kind, value or version of the data plane is added or changed and no validator changes.
- **UsageCases and StandardScenarios**: no sentence becomes false. S1 step 4 ("코더에 spec 위임") and S0 step 9 are supported in more detail for the first case: the spec now carries the round's techniques and the earlier failures. S0 step 8's "프로파일 결과·기존 코드" do not reach the coder through this spec — `profile` and `feedback.base` keep their places in ADR-0002 and stay open ((B4), OD-20) —, and the selection and ranking of S1 step 4 stay open (HQ-0048, OD-7). No verdict changes.
- **The cost, plainly.** The coder of a round ≥ 1 starts from scratch and sees no measurement: it gets the earlier attempts' technique ids and failures, not their sources (beyond what a compiler log quotes) or speed, so it cannot tell a fast attempt from a slow one until a later record gives `profile` members and a version. Attempts whose outcome is not known are hidden from it. Until the stand-in's check operation and ADR-0078's code land, the feedback of a `cpu_standin` run is empty or a compile failure comes without its log. The spec grows by one entry per round, and a compiler log has no size bound (ADR-0078 decision 4). A compiler log holds what the compiler echoes — its command, flags and search paths, and machine-local paths with any user name in them (ADR-0078 decision 5); through a `compile` report all of it now reaches a later coder process as well. Until the Round-Manager's round ≥ 1 steps are defined, no agent writes such a spec.
