## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a source is named with it. Every other sentence is **this record's own**.

### 1. Scope

- **The round-0 turns of the runs of ADR-0119 decision 1** — runs of ADR-0076 decision 1 that the run tools of ADR-0061 decision 1 cover and that start from their own round 0 — **that are also runs of ADR-0120 decision 1 and whose `instance.json` states `candidates_per_round` 1** (the runs whose round-0 spec ADR-0138 decision 1 gives; P2-5's N = 1). In such a round the one attempt of the call is `0000/01/01` (cited: ADR-0141 decision 6; ADR-0119 decision 2).
- **Outside**: every other run; rounds ≥ 1, which keep the stop after step 1; repair calls and their bound (OD-19); failure, triage and retry (OD-10); everything under Boundaries.
- **One prompt for every run.** The definition has one Round-Manager prompt for the runs of ADR-0120 decision 1 (cited: ADR-0118 decision 5; observation: `orchestration/round-manager.md`), and no step of it tells a run of this scope from another. So the steps below reach every round-0 turn that gets past their conditions. For a run outside this scope this record requires and allows nothing and gives no ground: what such a run's round 0 produces is not a baseline of ADR-0119 (its decision 1, cited). Where the tools do not cover such a run, their refusals stop it at the step that calls them — a run whose N is not 1 at `spec.write` (`candidates_per_round_not_stated`, `candidates_per_round_not_covered`; cited: ADR-0138 decision 3), a run no backend offers at `correctness.check` or `board.run` (`no_backend`, `not_offered`; cited: ADR-0019 decision 4) — and decisions 2 to 4 then stop as for any failure kind. **Whether the prompt must tell such runs apart before step 3, and what their round 0 gives, stay open** (Boundaries).
- **"The stop"** below is the present one, unchanged in form: the Round-Manager reports in one line what ended the step, and ends the session, writing nothing further (cited: `orchestration/round-manager.md`). It ends the turn before its round ends (cited: ADR-0118 decision 2 (ii)). **Whether such a turn's end commits is the owner's**: "such a turn's end commits what the run directory then holds, and after a death the next turn's entry commits what the dead turn left", the case file of the round in progress excepted (**owner**, answer to HQ-0043 (a), 2026-10-21, https://github.com/myungjoo/HIL-Claw/issues/5#issuecomment-6306369119). The record that carries that answer out — the widening of ADR-0127 decision 8 and of `run.commit` — is not yet made, and this record neither designs it nor forbids it: until it lands the stop commits nothing, as ADR-0127 decision 8 has it (cited), and when it lands the stop commits as it says. "Writing nothing further" below means no further write of the round's work.

### 2. (a) No attempt from the call: the stop, with round 0 not ended

- **The Round-Manager goes on to decision 3 only when the coder-driver reports a result of `coder.run` whose `taken` holds at least one name.** On every other report — any failure kind of `spec.write` or `coder.run` (`no_container_runtime`, `coder_not_ended` and `spec_exists` among them), a result with an empty `taken`, or no report it can read — it comes to the stop. It reads no further meaning into the kind or the names: whether `taken` holds a kernel is what the check finds (`no_kernel_source`, ADR-0110 decision 2 class (i)), not the Round-Manager's judgement. What a failure kind or an empty `taken` means for the round is "OD-19's and PLAN P2-1's" (cited: ADR-0141 decision 6); as P2-1's record, this one reads no more into a report than whether a check can follow it.
- **Round 0 is not ended**: no `round.end` in that turn. **This is what ADR-0119 decision 5 leaves**: a `round.end` here would start round 1 with no attempt that can stand — "goes on without a baseline", which that decision leaves open (cited) — and no other write of the round's work is made. What the turn loop does next is ADR-0122 decision 3 as it stands (cited): a turn that wrote the first `STATE.json` is progress and is followed by one more turn, which re-enters round 0 and repeats steps 1 and 2 (cited: ADR-0141 decision 6). Where `spec.write` succeeded in the earlier turn, that turn meets `spec_exists` and stops; where `spec.write` itself failed, it is called again and answers by what the run's files then hold. A turn that stops so leaves the run "as it found it", and the start ends and tells its starter why.
- **Not decided**: whether the run then stops for good, waits for the user, retries round 0 or goes on without a baseline (ADR-0119 decision 5; OD-19, OD-10); a repair call (OD-19); a second call for the tuple (OD-23 ① (가), OD-8 ㉖ (가)); what a later turn does in a round it re-enters beyond what the steps below give (OD-17). No retry, repair or termination rule is added.

### 3. (b) The checks of round 0: the sim point, then the board point, each delegated to the evaluator

- **Step 3** (round 0 only, after decision 2's condition holds): the Round-Manager delegates to `evaluator` with the `Agent` tool and waits for its report, the message naming its run id, the tuple `round` 0, `candidate` 1, `attempt` 1, the point `sim`, and no place (cited: ADR-0128 decision 4). **Step 4**: the same with the point `board`. No other execution, attempt or point is named, so the evaluator states `execution` 1 (cited: ADR-0128 decision 7).
- **Order and condition: step 4 follows step 3 only when the evaluator reports the standing `"verified"` at `sim`; step 5 (decision 4) follows step 4 only when it reports `"verified"` at `board`.** Any other report — `"not_verified"`, `"undetermined"`, a failure kind of `checks.read` or `correctness.check` without a standing, or no report it can read — comes to the stop, round 0 not ended (decision 2's reasons).
- **Why the standing and not the outcome.** ADR-0119 decision 4's "checks passed" is read as the standing `"verified"` of ADR-0110 at each point (cited: ADR-0128 Walk-through, "read here as the standing `verified` of ADR-0110"). The standing is the one word the evaluator reports from the package's computation over the log (cited: ADR-0131 decision 3; ADR-0128 decision 2); the `outcome` of one call does not say whether an open identity or a `failure` of class (ii) keeps the attempt from being verified (cited: ADR-0110 decision 3). **The Round-Manager takes the standing from the report** (cited: ADR-0131 decision 7, "the Round-Manager gets the standing from the evaluator's report"); it is not given `checks.read` (Alternatives).
- **Why the board check before the board measurement.** C-4 makes the board the scarce resource (cited), and a board-point check that does not verify leaves no attempt that can stand (cited: ADR-0119 decision 4), so a board run after it would give a measurement that cannot stand. ADR-0119 decision 4's "it is run at the board point" is **read here** as met by the board-point check where that check does not verify: the requirement exists so that a baseline can exist (cited: "since without that run there is no baseline"), and there it cannot. **This record reads FR-1** — "board(최종측정) + correctness" — **as fixing no order between the board measurement and the board check**. With this order, a sim pass followed by a board-check failure in round 0 leaves no board measurement, so the quarantine of FR-1 ⑶ ("해당 board 측정은 격리하고(기록은 남기되 …)") has nothing to isolate, and the triage (OD-10) has no board timing of that attempt to look at; that is a cost (Consequences), and the triage itself stays OD-10.
- **No profile step.** On `cpu_standin` "the round's profile is what `sim.run` / `board.run` already return", and `profiler.collect` answers `not_offered` (**owner**, O2); for the mock no record gives a profile step. Round 0 calls no `profiler.collect`.

### 4. (c) The holder of the measurement: a new sub-agent `experiment-runner` with exactly `board.run`

- **Step 5**: the Round-Manager delegates to `experiment-runner` with the `Agent` tool and waits for its report, the message naming its run id and the tuple `round` 0, `candidate` 1, `attempt` 1, and no place. **The experiment-runner calls `mcp__hilclaw__board_run` once**, with exactly `run_id`, `round`, `candidate`, `attempt` as given and `execution` 1 (cited: ADR-0106 decision 2; ADR-0062 decision 3), and reports the result's `outcome`, with its `condition` when `outcome` is `"broken"`, or the failure kind. It never calls it twice in one delegation, never retries, never states another `execution` (who may is OD-10 and the retry part of OD-17, cited: ADR-0062 decision 3), copies no interval or figure into its report and writes nothing by any means.
- **Its definition** is `orchestration/agents/experiment-runner.md`, with `name` `experiment-runner` and **`tools` exactly `mcp__hilclaw__board_run`**: no read tool, no file tool, no `Bash`, no `Agent`, and no `background` member, for the reason ADR-0141 decision 5 gives for the coder-driver (cited: `background: true` keeps a subagent in the background), since step 5 relies on a foreground delegation as decision 3 does. Like the coder-driver's, its inputs are the message and what its tool returns, and it judges nothing; the list is the least-privilege one (NFR-4; K2's per-agent allowlist). **`mcp__hilclaw__sim_run` is not on the list**, since round 0 calls no `sim.run` (decision 5); a record that gives a round ≥ 1 a sim measurement adds it. Whether the sim and board measurements later have one holder or two (K2 §6) is not decided.
- **Why a sub-agent and not the Round-Manager inline.** K2's agreed revision moves the calls of the HW experiment tools from the manager to an experiment-runner sub-agent (cited), and an accepted record already states "The Round-Manager evaluates nothing itself and calls no HW tool (K2 §5)" (cited: ADR-0128 decision 2), which an inline `board.run` would contradict. It also keeps the measurement off the agent that holds `state.write`, `round.end` and `run.commit` (cited: ADR-0124, ADR-0135, ADR-0127). **Against it**, K2 condition 4 ("판단·소화가 불필요한 결정론적 단계는 subagent화하지 않음", cited): the step has no judgement, and ADR-0133 decision 2 kept `case.write` inline on that condition (cited). **Weighed**: §5 is the agreement's specific rule for exactly these tools and condition 4 its general one; the specific rule is followed, and the cost — a fresh context for one tool call per round-0 turn that gets that far — is stated (Consequences).
- **Why not the evaluator.** Its tools exclude `mcp__hilclaw__sim_run` and `mcp__hilclaw__board_run`, "which belong to the experiment side of K2's roster", and its fixed rule is "request no measurement" (cited: ADR-0128 decisions 3 and 7); its giving them was rejected because "it would perform the measurements it judges" (cited: ADR-0128 Alternatives (b); #7 §2(8)).
- **Why not the coder-driver.** Its `tools` are exactly `spec.write` and `coder.run` and its rule is the call (cited: ADR-0141 decision 5); a measurement would mix the `coder/` and `measure/` roles of ADR-0004 decision 4 in one agent.
- **Why not the turn loop or a package function between sessions.** A round's work would span two sessions, against "turn ≡ Round" (cited: ADR-0118 decision 2; ADR-0128 Alternatives (a)).
- **The `measure/` role, as far as this goes.** The experiment-runner is the caller of the board measurement for these runs. It writes no file below `measure/`: the result is the end line of the journaled call (cited: ADR-0062 decision 2; ADR-0063 decision 1). The path table gives `sim.json`, `board.json` and `build.json` to the measurement role (cited: ADR-0004 path table); which agent or tool of that role writes them, and whether round 0 of these runs writes any, stays OD-23 (cited: ADR-0004 decision 4); their content is OD-13 and `build.json`'s fields are the code tasks' and OD-23's (cited: path table), and the members of `sim.json` and `board.json` that carry the figure and the verdict are PLAN P1-8's (cited: ADR-0063 Limits 4, "nothing yet writes `sim.json` or `board.json`"). **K2's roster row, as read here**: its holder and its tools (`board.run`) are taken; its outputs "sim.json, board.json" are not answered by this record, whose experiment-runner writes no file.
- **The permission lists of ADR-0139, checked against it.** The new file adds `Agent(experiment-runner)` and `mcp__hilclaw__board_run` to the pre-approved list by derivation, with no change of ADR-0139 or of the derivation's code (cited: ADR-0139 decision 3); it names no built-in tool, so the built-in set of decision 4 is unchanged (`Agent` is already there); the Round-Manager's fixed list gains nothing. **The limit of ADR-0139 decision 5 applies**: the pool is shared, so the Round-Manager *can* call `mcp__hilclaw__board_run`; that it does not rests on its prompt ("Do not call `mcp__hilclaw__board_run` yourself") until a hook of PLAN P2-4 checks the calling agent.

### 5. (d) No `sim.run` timing in round 0

- **Round 0 asks for no `sim.run`.** The baseline is the board-point measurement and "The sim point is not chosen" (cited: ADR-0119 decision 2). The early filter at the sim point, which C-4 asks of the sim (cited), is the sim-point check of step 3, which decides whether the board is used at all. With N = 1 and no repair, no choice among attempts needs a sim figure (cited: ADR-0119 decision 4), and no record of round 0 reads one.
- A later record may add one — for example to collect sim–board pairs for OD-15 — and says then what reads it.

### 6. (e) After a baseline exists: the closing steps, with no case and no verdicts

- **On `outcome` `"converged"` or `"not_converged"`** the attempt `0000/01/01` is the baseline attempt and its measurement the baseline (cited: ADR-0119 decisions 2 and 4; ADR-0113 decision 2, the mark does not bar it), and the Round-Manager goes to "The closing steps of a round" as they stand: **`case.write` is skipped in round 0** (cited: ADR-0133 decision 1), then `round.end` with `round` 0, then `run.commit`, then the session ends (cited: ADR-0135 decisions 2 and 5; ADR-0127 decisions 3 and 5).
- **The bars of ADR-0113 decision 3**: a broken result has no measurement (above); the checks are verified at both points (decision 3); a memory-ceiling verdict *over* cannot arise, since no valid `instance.json` states `memory_ceiling` (cited: ADR-0053 decision 4 as ADR-0061 decision 6 cites it). The record that admits that constraint says what round 0 does with *over*.
- **On `"broken"`, a failure kind of `board.run`, or no report it can read**: the stop, round 0 not ended. That is the case "its board-point run gives only broken results (ADR-0063 decision 2)" of ADR-0119 decision 5 (cited); what follows is open there.
- **No verdicts work in round 0.** Round 0 "applies no technique" and has no hypothesis record (cited: ADR-0059 decision 5; ADR-0138 decision 2 (b)), and a verdicts record of round 0 is invalid (cited: ADR-0071 decision 6). No verdicts delegation is made.
- **How the baseline is referenced** stays open (cited: ADR-0119 Boundaries; OD-20, OD-8): the end line of the call holds the result (cited: ADR-0062 decision 2), and nothing else is written for it.

### 7. (f) Until `board.run` is registered: the stop moves to after step 4

- **While `board.run` is not in `default_registry`, the round-0 steps end with step 4**: on the standing `"verified"` at `board`, the Round-Manager reports in one line that the board measurement of round 0 waits on `board.run`, and ends the session — the stop, moved from after step 2, as ADR-0141 decision 6 moved it from after step 1. **Step 5 and the experiment-runner's definition land only together with the registration** (Consequences, slice 2), so no agent file names a tool the server lacks (the reason ADR-0141 Alternatives (b) gave against an unregistered tool, cited).
- **What a turn session meets meanwhile, with the package's registry**: in a mock run, `coder.run` fails with `no_container_runtime` (cited: ADR-0141 decision 3), and decision 2 stops; in a `cpu_standin` run the same, and a check would answer `no_backend` or `not_offered`, the backend table holding the mock alone (cited: the `default_registry` docstring; ADR-0019 decision 4), a failure kind that leaves no line, so the standing the evaluator reports from `checks.read` is `"undetermined"` (cited: ADR-0110 decision 3, "no line"; `orchestration/agents/evaluator.md`, "A check"), and decision 3 stops. **Steps 3 and 4 are reached only by tests**, with a registry bound to stubs (D16).
- **This depends on nothing unlanded**: not on ADR-0068 or HQ-0021 (the contract used is ADR-0061 to ADR-0063, ADR-0067 and ADR-0106), not on HQ-0026 or OD-14 ① (no backend is presumed), not on HQ-0053 (no coder start).
