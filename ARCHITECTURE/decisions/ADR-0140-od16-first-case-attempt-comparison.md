## Decision

A sentence is **cited** when a source is named with it. Every other sentence is **this record's own**.

### 1. Scope

- **The comparison entries of `verdicts.json`** written by the evaluator of a turn of a run of ADR-0120 decision 1: the means by which the evaluator gets a `difference`, which journaled results of `sim.run` and `board.run` enter it, the rule applied, what `verdicts.write` holds of such an entry, and what the evaluator's definition becomes.
- **Outside**: which attempt `against` must be and which comparisons a verdict must include; promotion, improvement and the best; every item under Boundaries.

### 2. (a) The means: a typed read tool, recomputed by `verdicts.write`

- **The evaluator gets the `difference` of a pair by calling one tool of the registry, `timings.compare`, through the package's MCP front**, naming the run by `run_id`, the metric, the point and the two attempts. It copies the returned `difference` into the `comparison` entry with the same `metric`, `point`, `measured` and `against`.
- **`verdicts.write` recomputes every `comparison` entry of a record from the log by the same function and refuses the record when the entry's `difference` is not the one the function gives** (decision 5). **This is how a given comparison is told from an invented one** (ADR-0128 decision 6): not by a mark in the entry, which would be a new member, but by recomputation. The rule is a function of the two ordered sample sets alone, with a fixed seed (cited: ADR-0007 decision 9), so the tool and the write give the same `difference` over the same lines; if the log gains a timing line of either attempt between the two calls, the write sees the log as it then is.
- **Not journaled**: the call costs no limited resource and can be repeated (the reasoning of ADR-0124 decision 3, as ADR-0131 decision 3 applies it). It appends no line, selects no backend, creates nothing and keeps nothing between calls. It is a tool of the registry beside the FR-10 tools, not an FR-10 tool, added to `default_registry` in the source (cited: #5 A-12 point 3).

### 3. (b) The tool `timings.compare`, and which results enter

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Name (MCP name) | `timings.compare` (`mcp__hilclaw__timings_compare`); the name holds no `_`, so the mapping of ADR-0010 decision 4 stays reversible |
| Request | Exactly `run_id`, `metric`, `point`, `measured` and `against`, all required; any other member, or one missing, is refused. `run_id` has the form of ADR-0015 decision 4; `metric` is exactly `"latency_minimisation"`, the one admitted metric (cited: ADR-0059 decision 3); `point` is `"sim"` or `"board"`, compared as it is, and names the `step` read, `sim_run_timing` or `board_run_timing` (cited: ADR-0062 decision 3); `measured` and `against` are each an object of exactly `round`, `candidate` and `attempt` in the forms of ADR-0015 decision 4, `round` 0 admitted, and the two are not equal |
| Result | `{"difference": d, "measured": {"execution": n, "outcome": o}, "against": {"execution": n, "outcome": o}}`: `d` is `"lower"`, `"higher"` or `"not_different"` (decision 4); for each side, the `execution` of the end line whose measurement entered and that result's `outcome`, `"converged"` or `"not_converged"` |
| Checks, in order | the request (`invalid_request`, ADR-0015 decision 2); the data root (`no_data_root`); the read of the run's `events.ndjson` from its first line, with the kinds, their order and the widening of ADR-0131 decision 3 (`run_file_missing` for a data root gone since ②, `run_file_unreadable`, `run_file_invalid`, `run_file_unsupported`); the step lines in line order, the first that fails deciding: a step line that breaks ADR-0011 (`run_file_invalid`) or an end line of `measured` or `against` at the `step` whose `result` is not an admitted timing result (`timing_result_invalid`, below); then `measured` and after it `against` by the rule below (`measurement_several`, `measurement_unknown`, `measurement_missing`, in this order); the two results' `shape` (`shapes_differ`); the domain of the package's functions of ADR-0007 decision 7 (`samples_out_of_domain`) |

- **The measurement of one `ref` at one point.** Let M be the end lines of that attempt at the point's `step`, every `execution` together, whose `result` has `outcome` `"converged"` or `"not_converged"`, and let U be its end lines that hold a `failure` together with its identities with a start line and no end line (the "open" of ADR-0011 decision 1). **The attempt has a measurement at the point exactly when M holds one line and U is empty**; its samples are that result's `intervals_ns`, in order. Otherwise the tool refuses, naming the side in the message: **`measurement_several`** when M holds more than one line — two executions, or two end lines of one identity (two turns at once, cited: ADR-0110 decision 2); else **`measurement_unknown`** when U is not empty; else **`measurement_missing`** — no line at all, or only end lines with a `"broken"` result.
- **Why one and only one.** Samples of two measurements are never pooled (cited: ADR-0007 decision 4), so a side is one measurement, and choosing one of several — the first, the last, the better — is the choice ⑳ asks about, which this record does not make: **for a comparison, no execution among several is chosen; the comparison is refused**. **An open identity or a `failure` end line may stand for a measurement that is not known**: whether an open step had an outcome is not known (cited: ADR-0011 decision 4), and a `failure` can end a call after the operation ran — `operation_answer_invalid` ends the step once "the operation was called and its call is over" (ADR-0065 decision 7). Comparing the one known measurement beside such a line would choose among possibly two, so the comparison is refused, as an open check and a `failure` of class (ii) keep an attempt from being verified (cited: ADR-0110 decisions 2, 3). **Every `failure` refuses, whatever its kind**: which kinds of the run tools lie before any timed run, the counterpart of ADR-0110's class (i), is not sorted by any landed record (⑲ (나), HQ-0021), and this record does not sort them.
- **Why a broken result neither enters nor refuses.** A broken run yields no measurement, "however many intervals it reported" (cited: ADR-0063 decision 3, after ADR-0009 decision 8): it is a result that states that no measurement exists, so beside one measurement there is nothing to choose among; and no comparison entry can name a broken run (cited: ADR-0071 decision 4). What a broken run says about the candidate is the check's and the round steps' (ADR-0110; OD-10), not the comparison's.
- **"Not converged" enters like any other** (cited: ADR-0007 decision 4; ADR-0114 decision 3), on either side: nothing above refuses or sets aside a result for its `outcome` `"not_converged"`, and decision 5 leaves such a comparison counted exactly when ADR-0071 decision 4 counts it (ADR-0128 Consequences slice 3).
- **An admitted timing result** (for `timing_result_invalid`): its `outcome` is `"converged"`, `"not_converged"` or `"broken"` (cited: ADR-0063 decision 2), and for the first two it holds `intervals_ns`, an array of at least `n_min` = 5 integers of at least 1, and `shape`, an object of exactly `m`, `k`, `n`, each an integer of at least 1 (cited: ADR-0063 decisions 4, 5). Its other members are not looked at. Only end lines of the two named attempts at the `step` are held to it, as `check_result_invalid` holds only the request's scope (ADR-0131 decision 3). The kind is a name of the tool layer, **not a change of the data plane**, and not reserved.
- **The same quantity.** Decision 7 compares "two given measurements of the same quantity" (cited: ADR-0007 decision 7), and "a timing of one shape says nothing about another" (cited: ADR-0063 decision 5). **`shapes_differ`** when the two results' `shape` differ. The point is one by the request. `sample_form` and `n_max` are not compared: for `latency_minimisation` the samples are the interval lengths (cited: ADR-0071 decision 3), which `intervals_ns` holds whatever the form, and `n_max` bounds the repeats, not the quantity.
- **`samples_out_of_domain`** when the package's functions of decision 7 refuse the two sample sets (today `InvalidSamplesError` for sums or ratios beyond the float range; observation). **It never compares a set the rule's domain excludes**: a set of fewer than `n_min` samples is no measurement (cited: ADR-0007 decisions 4, 5), and it is refused before, as `timing_result_invalid` or `measurement_missing`.
- **A missing log is no failure** (cited: ADR-0006 decision 2; ADR-0019 decision 1): it holds no line, so the answer is `measurement_missing` for `measured`. The tool reads no `STATE.json` and no instance record, so a `run_id` that names no run is answered the same way, and **today every call is answered `measurement_missing`**: no log holds a timing line while `sim.run` and `board.run` are unregistered (Context).
- **The reserved kinds of ADR-0019 are widened once more**, to the read of the log by `timings.compare`, with the meanings and the property ADR-0131 decision 3 gives them for `checks.read`. The six new kinds of the table are names of the tool layer, not reserved.
- **It does not check that the run is one of ADR-0120 decision 1**, that the two attempts' rounds stand in any order, or that either attempt passed a check: the scope is the caller's (as for `checks.read`), the file's rules are `verdicts_file.validate`'s, and what counts is decision 5's.
- **Left to the code slice**: the module, the description and input schema, how the read shares code with `checks_tool` and `verdicts.write`, the messages, and what a number beyond the float range in a stored result does (for `checks.read` it is `bad_result`).

### 4. (c) The rule applied

- **The ordered pair is A = `against` and B = `measured`** (cited: ADR-0071 decision 3), with x the samples of `against` and y those of `measured`. The interval is that of ADR-0007 decision 7 for the ordered pair (A, B), the ratio T(y\*)/T(x\*) over `resamples` = 2000 replicates with the fixed seed of decision 9 (today `ratio_interval(x, y)`; observation).
- **`difference`** is `"lower"` when the whole interval lies below 1 / (1 + `delta`), `"higher"` when it lies wholly above 1 + `delta`, and `"not_different"` otherwise (cited: ADR-0071 decision 3; ADR-0007 decision 7). `is_different` gives no direction (observation), so the code compares the interval's ends; the strict comparisons are those of `is_different`.
- **No parameter is added**: not a margin beyond `delta`, not a magnitude, not an equivalence rule that would let `"not_different"` refute (OD-13, OD-1; ADR-0071 decision 4). The order is never exchanged: the pair is the entry's (cited: ADR-0007 decision 7, "the code task must not assume more").

### 5. (d) `verdicts.write` holds `comparison` entries

- **`comparison_not_given` is removed.** The checks of `verdicts.write` become, in order: the request; the record's validity and `run_id` (both `invalid_request`); the data root (`no_data_root`); the round's `hypotheses.json` (the four `hypotheses_*` kinds); the rules of `verdicts_hypotheses.check` (`verdicts_mismatch`); the read of the run's `events.ndjson` from its first line, for every record (`run_file_missing`, `run_file_unreadable`, `run_file_invalid`, `run_file_unsupported`); the step lines in line order, the first that fails deciding (`run_file_invalid`; `check_result_invalid` for an end line of an attempt and point a `check` entry names; **`timing_result_invalid` for an end line of an attempt that a `comparison` entry names as `measured` or `against`, at the `step` of its `point`**); every `check` entry founded (`check_unfounded`); **every `comparison` entry founded (`comparison_unfounded`)**; **every counted comparison verified (`comparison_unverified`)**; the write (`verdicts_exist`, `verdicts_not_written`). Within each of the last three entry checks, the first failing entry in record order decides.
- **`comparison_unfounded`**: decisions 3 and 4, applied to the entry's `metric`, `point`, `measured` and `against` over the lines read, give no `difference` (any of the kinds `measurement_several`, `measurement_unknown`, `measurement_missing`, `shapes_differ`, `samples_out_of_domain`) or give another one than the entry's.
- **`comparison_unverified`** — the answer to ADR-0131 decision 4's open point, **a counted comparison rests on the standing `"verified"`**: for a comparison entry that ADR-0071 decision 4 counts (`point` `board`, and the same verdict holds `check` entries at `board` with `outcome` `"pass"` for its `measured` and its `against`), each of the two attempts has the standing verified at `board` (cited: ADR-0110 decision 3, computed today by `check_standing.standing` over the same lines; observation). *Why*: ADR-0110 decision 3 makes verified "the only standing that does" pass the floor and the one on which FR-9 lets an attempt be compared (cited), and ADR-0131 decision 4 admits a `"pass"` entry beside an open check or a `failure` of class (ii) (cited); without this check a comparison would count for an attempt that does not pass the floor. *Why on the writer and not on the `check` entry*: ADR-0131 rejected tying the `"pass"` entry to the standing because it "hides a true pass beside an open identity" (cited: its Alternatives); here the pass entry stays, and the evaluator may record the comparison without the two `"pass"` entries, where it counts for nothing.
- **A `comparison` entry needs no `check` entry of its own.** Requiring founded `check` entries for both `ref`s of every comparison was weighed and not taken: ADR-0071 decision 4 lets a comparison "be recorded but count for nothing" (a sim comparison, or one whose side has no `"pass"` entry), and the requirement would forbid what that decision admits. **Whether a comparison counts stays a rule of the value** (ADR-0071 decision 4, held by `verdicts_mismatch`), with the standing of the line above added for those that count.
- **These are names of the tool layer, not a change of the data plane** (as ADR-0128 decision 6 says of its kinds): no member, value or version of `verdicts.json` changes, `verdicts_file.validate` is unchanged, and a file that breaks decision 5 is still a valid file by ADR-0071. **Every check before the write still creates nothing**, so a refused record can be corrected and sent again. `verdicts.write` reads no file besides `hypotheses.json` and the log.
- **What it does not hold**: which attempt `against` is, that a verdict includes every comparison the evaluator requested or could have, and anything of a comparison's `metric` beyond the one admitted value.

### 6. (e) The evaluator

- **What `orchestration/agents/evaluator.md` becomes in the code slice** (Consequences slice 3). Its `tools` list adds `mcp__hilclaw__timings_compare` (ADR-0128 decision 3: later tools are added "by the records or slices that create them"). It is a read; the evaluator still holds no file-writing means and no shell.
- **"Verdicts"**: after `hypotheses.read` and `checks.read`, for each hypothesis whose `expected` names `latency_minimisation`, the evaluator chooses the pairs it judges — `measured` an attempt of a candidate in `tested_by` of the file's round, `against` another attempt of the run — and calls `timings.compare` for each with the `run_id`, the metric, the point and the two `ref`s. On a result it writes a `comparison` entry copying `difference`; on a failure kind it writes no entry for that pair and its `cause` says which kind. For a comparison at `board` to count, it adds `"pass"` entries at `board` for both sides only when `checks.read` gave both the standing `"verified"` there (decision 5). It composes each verdict's value by ADR-0071 decision 4, sends the record with `verdicts.write`, and corrects and sends again on a refusal. The sentences "`verdict` `inconclusive`", "no `comparison` entry" and that the `cause` says no comparison could be given go. **Which attempt `against` is stays its judgement**: no record fixes the base (Boundaries), and the definition names none.
- **The pre-approval follows.** The pre-approved list of a turn session gains every entry of the `tools` list of each agent file copied into the session (cited: ADR-0139 decision 3, source 2), so `mcp__hilclaw__timings_compare` is pre-approved at the next turn after the slice lands, with no change to the turn-loop code. The test of the evaluator file's `tools` set changes in the same slice.
- **The Round-Manager is not given the tool**: the evaluator's report carries what it needs (ADR-0128 decision 5), and the Round-Manager's list of ADR-0139 decision 3, source 1 is unchanged.

## Alternatives considered

| Point | Alternative | Weighing | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | **A typed read tool; `verdicts.write` recomputes** | ADR-0128 decisions 2, 4 and 6 met; the model copies a value it did not compute; the copy is held by the writer (K4 premise 2) | **Adopted** |

| (b) | A `"not_converged"` side refused or uncounted | Against ADR-0114 and ADR-0128 Consequences slice 3 | Rejected |

## Consequences

- **Slices it enables** (code mode, later tasks; each with fixture logs only and a stub `claude` or none — D16; each within the D4 cap):
  1. **Now**: the tool `timings.compare` of decisions 3 and 4, in a module of `hilclaw/` with its test file (each kind in its order, the walk-through rows, a `"not_converged"` side, a broken line beside one measurement, a `failure` line beside one, two end lines of one identity, a missing log), registered in `default_registry` of `hilclaw/tools.py` with its docstring; the docstring of `hilclaw/run_identity.py` ("which of several executions counts").
  2. **With or after slice 1**: decision 5 in `hilclaw/verdicts_tool.py` — `comparison_not_given` removed, `timing_result_invalid` in the step pass, `comparison_unfounded`, `comparison_unverified` — with the docstring, the description and the tests of `verdicts.write`.
  3. **After slices 1 and 2**: `orchestration/agents/evaluator.md` as decision 6 says, and that file's test of the `tools` set.
- **What it does not enable**: a `supported` or `refuted` verdict in a real run before `sim.run` and `board.run` are registered (T-0210, HQ-0021); until then every call is `measurement_missing` and every verdict stays `inconclusive`.
- **Less exact, not edited** (found by a search of `ARCHITECTURE/decisions/`, `hilclaw/` and `orchestration/` for "comparison tool", "comparison record", "until the comparison", "comparison_not_given", "until a tool gives a comparison" and "⑳"; not claimed complete):
  - ADR-0128 decision 3 ("the tools of Consequences slice 3"), decision 6 ("refuses every record that holds a `comparison` entry"; "Until the comparison tool … exists"; its list of checks), Consequences slice 3 and "The cost, plainly" ("Until the comparison tool exists"), and Boundaries ("the contracts of the read, standing and comparison tools"; "OD-18 ⑳ for several executions").
  - ADR-0131 decision 4 ("Whether a counted comparison must rest on the standing `"verified"` is the comparison record's"), decision 5 (its list of checks, "no `comparison` entry"; "anything of a comparison"), decision 6 ("every verdict … `inconclusive` until the comparison tool exists"), Consequences slice 4 and "The cost, plainly", and Boundaries ("The comparison").
  - ADR-0129 decision 5 ("every verdict `inconclusive` until the comparison exists").
  - ADR-0071 decision 3 ("which execution of … a timing run an entry stands for … (OD-18 sub-question ⑳)"), decision 4 "Open, and not filled here" (the base stays open; the means and the executions do not) and Boundaries ("which execution of a check or a timing run counts (OD-18 sub-question ⑳)"); ADR-0114 Boundaries and ADR-0110 Boundaries (⑳), for comparisons of these runs.
  - ADR-0063 decision 4 ("Which component … compares measurements … is not decided here"), for these runs; ADR-0019 decision 2 ("only at the lookup or the selection"), widened once more.
  - `hilclaw/verdicts_tool.py` (the docstring's "until a tool gives a comparison", the comment of `KIND_COMPARISON_NOT_GIVEN` and the description's "refused for now"), `hilclaw/run_identity.py` and `hilclaw/tools.py` docstrings, and `orchestration/agents/evaluator.md` "Verdicts": slices 1 to 3.
- **UsageCases and StandardScenarios**: no sentence becomes false; the note of S0 step 6 loses the comparison tool of these runs, and keeps the base of the comparison.
- **A-13 reading.** Document mode. No code, test, setting or dependency changes. **No data-plane change**: no member, file, kind of event, value or version of `events.ndjson`, `verdicts.json` or any other data-plane file is added or changed, and no validator of a data-plane file changes; the `comparison` entry of ADR-0071 is used as it stands; the eight new kinds are names of the tool layer; the `run_file_*` kinds keep their meanings.
- **Runtime acceptance items** (D16; nothing was tried, nothing is required of the development machine): **none added**. ADR-0128 item (xviii) is used, not changed, for the new `tools` entry; the result of `timings.compare` is a few members, so no counterpart of ADR-0131 item (xxii) arises; and a computation over two sets of at most `n_max` samples comes nowhere near the idle window of an MCP call that ADR-0128 item (xx) is about.
- **The cost, plainly.** One more tool and three more checks of `verdicts.write` to keep. A second timing run of an attempt at a point — asked by anyone, for any reason — makes that attempt uncomparable there for the rest of the run, since the log is never edited (cited: ADR-0006 decision 5); so does an open identity left by a death, and so does any `failure` end line, even one whose run never started. A broken run followed by a completed one compares the completed one, so a caller can repeat a broken timing run until it completes. With the base open, the evaluator chooses `against` and which comparisons a verdict holds, so it can choose the pair that agrees with the expectation (ADR-0114 decision 4, "Whether a verdict may leave out a comparison", open). Every call reads the whole log.
