## Decision

Whose a sentence is: **the owner's** where a sentence of O1 to O5 is quoted; **cited** where a requirement, a record with its decision number or a file of the code is named with it — it then says what that source says and adds nothing; **this record's own** otherwise — among them the side of every setting, the place of the two new values in the order, the class of S3 to S6, S9 and S10, the six kinds and their messages, and every weighing. The record's own design is what the objection window is for.

### 1. Scope and terms

- A call of `correctness.check` about one attempt of a run of the first case or of a run whose hw_profile names the mock (cited: ADR-0021 decision 1; ADR-0038 decision 6), at the moments at which ADR-0056 decision 5 finds S3 to S10: in ⑥, inside the operation, after the reader of ADR-0055 found a compiled set.
- **Not this record**: the roster and who calls the tool (OD-23); the run tools — their timing build is theirs, and O3 reaches it only as a principle (decision 7); any setting **value** and the timeout hierarchy (OD-14); part (다) of ⑰; what anybody does after an answer.
- The terms of ADR-0016, ADR-0021, ADR-0043, ADR-0047, ADR-0055 and ADR-0056 (the output path, the build directory, S1 to S10) are used unchanged. **A setting**: one of the rows of ADR-0017 decision 9 that ADR-0056 decision 4 left open.

### 2. Limits (they hold for every statement of decisions 3 to 6)

- **Nothing was built or run.** What a real compiler, linker or container does in each situation was not tried.
- **The settings have no values**, so this record cannot say which flags make a build fail, how long a build may take, or whether a setting a backend holds is refused.
- **A backend that holds or reads its settings otherwise is not detected**: "A backend that does not answer the contract … is not detected by the frame" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 6).
- **The kernel's process is as unprotected as before**: "the process is not sandboxed" (cited: ADR-0017 decision 10). O4 decides that builds and runs use a container; until OD-14 sub-question ① designs it and a record replaces the host process, ADR-0056 decision 2 holds as written, and this record's list of situations is read from the host code.
- **The cause is not established.** As for the run, "A defect of the harness against a defect of the kernel: not told apart by either class" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 5): a broken result of decision 4 can follow from a fault of the machine or of the backend's settings, and a tool failure of decision 4 can follow from what the attempt's source made.

### 3. Part (나): through which side an operation has each setting

- **Every setting is the backend's own.** The operation of `cpu_standin` has its flags, trailing flags, compiler, time limit of the build, time limit of the process and environment from a source this record does not fix; **the tool function hands none of them over**, and an operation stays called with the four things of ADR-0056 decision 3. The source and the values are open: of the two time limits with OD-14 and its hierarchy; of the compiler with the isolated environment of O4, whose design is OD-14 sub-question ①; of the flags, the trailing flags and the environment (thread settings among them) with sub-question ⑱ (라) (and OD-6 sub-question ②, OD-16 sub-question ⑥). **Under this record an operation reads no setting from a file of the run or of the attempt, from the hw_profile it is handed, from the request or the check parameters, or from any other file of the data plane** (⑱ (가)). The defaults of the code (`compiler=None` for `clang` on `PATH`, `env=None` inheriting the calling process's environment, empty flags) are no values of this record, and "no default" of the time limit of the process (cited: ADR-0017 decision 9) stands.
- **Three answers were weighed.**
  - **(a) Each is the backend's own.** The compiler is part of the toolchain of the isolated environment the build runs in (O4, **the owner's**: "The toolchain, the build of a kernel, its correctness run and its timing run use an isolated environment"), whose changes need the user's approval (O4, item 2) — a property of the machine, not of a candidate. The two time limits belong to a hierarchy OD-14 registers for the whole run. Nothing crosses the cut that is not decided, and the mock is unchanged.
  - **(b) Each is handed by the tool function from a named file or member.** The file a candidate's settings are prescribed in is the spec: "Settings the orchestrator **prescribes** for a candidate are in the spec, in `task.build`; version 1 names no member" (cited: ADR-0002 decision 6 (b)). Handing anything from it needs a member name of `task.build`, and handing anything from `instance.json` a new member there; either is a schema change of the data plane this record may not make (CLAUDE.md A-13).
  - **(c) A split by setting** — for example the time limits the backend's own while flags a candidate is prescribed come from `task.build`. The part from `task.build` meets the stop of (b).
- **The choice is (a).** Its cost, stated: **a setting a later record lets the orchestrator prescribe in `task.build` does not reach the verification build under this record.** Today none can be prescribed with a meaning: no record names a member of `task.build` (version 1 "names no member" — cited: ADR-0002 decision 6 (b)). Whether and through which side such a member reaches an operation is registered (sub-question ⑱ (가)); that record revisits this choice for it. **This choice is provisional in that respect.**
- **The same holds for a setting the coder chooses**: "Whether the coder may choose settings itself … are not decided" (cited: ADR-0002 decision 6 (b)), and under this record a setting the coder wrote down reaches no build. **The cost is concrete**: "OpenMP and SIMD intrinsics are allowed" (cited: #5 A-7 ② as ADR-0008 Context has it), so a kernel within its contract that needs a flag the backend does not give fails to build, and that is stored as a broken result `build_exit_status` from which a caller reads a property of the attempt. If the owner reads that as wrong, it is an objection to this decision. Registered (⑱ (가)).
- **Two further costs, stated and not resolved.** (i) The two time limits belong to a hierarchy OD-14 may state for a run, and an operation is handed the output path, not the data root (cited: ADR-0056 decision 3), so a time limit stated in a file of the run has no way to an operation under this record; OD-14 has stated none. (ii) The hw_profile an operation is handed names in `toolchain` the "Version of every software component between the kernel source and its execution on the accelerator: compiler, …" (cited: ADR-0001 decision 1). Whether the compiler the backend holds must agree with it, and what a call answers when it does not, is not weighed here. Both are registered (sub-question ⑱ (가)).
- **The mock.** Its operation and its scripts are given no setting: the default script "stands for no kernel language, no source, no build and no flag" (cited: ADR-0044 decision 3). A script of a test is handed nothing more than ADR-0056 decision 3 says; it may return either new string of form (B) (decision 4), as a script must be able to express "a result that reports a failed experiment" (cited: ADR-0016 decision 5 (d)), and raise any of the six kinds.
- **The settings table of ADR-0056 decision 4, as amended** (ADR-0056 is not edited; the changed row is in bold):

| Setting (ADR-0017 decision 9) | Through which side | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Path of the executable; working directory | The operation's own (cited: ADR-0056 decision 4) | the code task's (ADR-0056 decision 4) |
| **Flags and trailing flags** | **The backend's own; the tool function hands none** | open (⑱ (라); OD-16 ⑥; a prescribed one: ⑱ (가)) |
| **Compiler** | **The backend's own** | clang, "as the single toolchain" (cited: #5 A-7 ② as ADR-0008 Context has it); which installation and version: open (OD-14 ①, the container of O4) |
| **Time limit of the build; time limit of the process** | **The backend's own** | open (OD-14) |
| **Environment** (thread settings travel here — cited: ADR-0017 decision 9) | **The backend's own** | open (⑱ (라)) |

- **What it means for OD-16 sub-question ⑥ (a consequence, not a decision).** The two checks of an attempt (O5, **the owner's**) are two builds by one operation from settings its backend holds, so nothing in the contract keeps them apart; whether they are the same depends on the backend's source at the two calls, which nothing fixes or records (⑰ (다)). Whether the verification build and the timing build use the same settings is open as before: the timing build is the run tools', and only a record of theirs can say whether their settings are the same backend's own.

### 4. The class of every situation S3 to S10

- **O1 applied.** S7 — "The compiler ends with a non-zero status: a compile or a link failure" (cited: ADR-0056 decision 5) — is a result with `outcome` `"broken"` and `condition` `"build_exit_status"`; S8 — "The build passes its time limit" (cited, same) — a result with `outcome` `"broken"` and `condition` `"build_time_limit"` (**the owner's**: "One each: `build_exit_status` for S7 and `build_time_limit` for S8."). The result holds `shape`, `draws` and `tolerance` and no `counts`, as every broken result does (cited: ADR-0042 decision 7); no member is added.
- **The operation finds both and returns them in the form (B).** The form (B) of ADR-0043 decision 4 becomes **one of four strings**: `"time_limit"`, `"exit_status"`, `"build_time_limit"`, `"build_exit_status"`; the tool function makes the broken result with the returned value as its `condition`, as it does for the first two ("the tool function puts the value an operation returned into `condition` as it is" — cited: ADR-0043 decision 4, which also ties the two spellings). An answer of form (B) still holds the condition and nothing else: no status, no time and no byte of the log.
- **The table of ADR-0042 decision 3, as amended** (ADR-0042 is not edited; the added rows are in bold). `condition` is a string with exactly six values, in this order:

| Value | The condition that failed first | Found by |
|---|---|---|
| **`"build_time_limit"`** | **S8: the build of the verification executable did not end within its time limit** (O1) | **the operation** |
| **`"build_exit_status"`** | **S7: the compiler ended with a non-zero status — a compile or a link failure** (O1) | **the operation** |
| `"time_limit"` | as ADR-0042 decision 3 | the operation |
| `"exit_status"` | as ADR-0042 decision 3 | the operation |
| `"report_form"` | as ADR-0042 decision 3 | the tool function |
| `"check_value"` | as ADR-0042 decision 3 | the tool function |

- **Why there.** The order is "the first that fails in the order in which ADR-0017 decision 7 lists the conditions of a completed run" (cited: ADR-0021 decision 5), and ADR-0042 decision 3 did not re-decide it. A build is no condition of a completed run, so **putting the two new values before that order extends it, and the extension is this record's own**. A build comes before the process: when it ends not ok no process is started, and none of the four run conditions is tested. Within the build, the time limit comes first as it does for the process ("a process that was ended for its time limit is reported as the time limit" — cited: ADR-0021 decision 2); `clang_build` reports a build that timed out with `returncode` `None` (cited), so the two never hold together. The member list of ADR-0042 decision 7 changes in its `condition` row only: six values, present exactly when `outcome` is `"broken"`.
- **S3 to S6, S9 and S10 are tool failures of backend kinds**, each named here with the situation it means (cited for the rule: ADR-0047 decision 5). The test is that of ADR-0021 decision 5 — whether the experiment was carried out and what it established about the attempt. In S3, S5 and S6 no build ended with a status at all, and in S4 none did or a setting of the process was refused after a build, and under decision 3 each follows from the machine, the installation or the backend's own settings: a compiler that is not found is a fault of the machine "if the backend holds them" (cited: ADR-0056 decision 6), and the backend now does. In S9 the compiler reported success and in S10 the build did, so the failure the owner classed as a result (O1) did not occur, and no process of the attempt was started, so no experiment was carried out. A tool failure "says that no outcome was established" (cited: ADR-0010 decision 4).
- **The cost, stated.** S5 can follow from an `output/` that changed after the reader saw it (cited: ADR-0055 L1, L5), and S9 and S10 may follow from what the attempt's source made — this record has not read that they cannot. Then a caller reads a property of the attempt out of a tool failure, the reading ADR-0021 decision 5 warned against. A result for them would need a value of `condition` the owner has not approved. **If the owner reads one of them as a result, that is an objection to this decision.**

| | Situation (ADR-0056 decision 5; in S4 the empty compiler prefix and the wrong type are this record's reading of the code) | Answer | Kind or `condition` | Moment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S3 | The build directory cannot be made or written | tool failure | `build_directory_unusable` | ⑥, before the build |
| S4 | A setting is one the functions refuse (a flag that sets the output, a time limit that is no positive finite number, an environment that cannot be handed over; an empty compiler prefix or a setting of the wrong type; the process time limit the platform cannot wait for) | tool failure | `settings_refused` | ⑥, before the process the refused setting belongs to is started; for the last case after the process was started and killed |
| S5 | A file of the compiled set is no longer a regular file when the build is called, or the verification program is missing from the installation | tool failure | `build_input_missing` | ⑥, before the compiler is started |
| S6 | The compiler is not found, or its process cannot be started | tool failure | `compiler_unavailable` | ⑥, at the start of the build |
| S7 | The compiler ends with a non-zero status | **result**, `outcome` `"broken"` | `build_exit_status` (O1) | ⑥, the build ended |
| S8 | The build passes its time limit | **result**, `outcome` `"broken"` | `build_time_limit` (O1) | ⑥, the build ended |
| S9 | Status 0 and no executable at the path, or it cannot be moved there | tool failure | `build_output_missing` | ⑥, the build ended |
| S10 | The executable cannot be started: not found, not executable, or refused by the system | tool failure | `executable_unstartable` | ⑥, at the start of the process |

- **Every row is found by the operation, after the start line**, and none by the tool function (cited: ADR-0056 decision 5). A result is answered from the end line with a `result`; a tool failure from the end line with a `failure` holding the raised kind and message (cited: ADR-0047 decisions 3 and 4), its `spend` that of ADR-0040 decision 6, and `end_line_failed` when that append fails (cited: ADR-0026 decision 3). Any backend may raise the six kinds, a script of the mock included; the tool function raises none of them itself.
- **Why six kinds.** Split "where the fixed records already say something different of each case" (cited: ADR-0019 decision 2): ADR-0056 decision 5 keeps the six apart by what failed and when. Within a row the cases are one kind, as R1 to R4 are one kind in ADR-0056 decision 6; the message may say which. One kind for all six was weighed: it is akin to the "one general kind" ADR-0047 decision 5 declined, and a caller could not tell the six apart by the kind.
- **The names** are *names* of ADR-0001. Checked on 2026-10-15 in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`: none of the eight strings occurs there; none is a reserved kind, a kind of another record of this contract, a `step` value or a member name. `build_exit_status` and `build_time_limit` are the owner's spelling.
- **What the functions on `main` report, against the rows** (observations of Context; how an operation places what it observes is the code task's). A `BuildResult` with `ok` false tells three rows apart by itself: `timed_out` (S8), a non-zero `returncode` (S7), `returncode` 0 (S9). **That a compiler ended by a signal (a negative `returncode` on POSIX) is S7 is this record's reading** of "a non-zero status" (ADR-0056 decision 5), as `exit_status` holds "a non-zero status or a signal" (cited: ADR-0042 decision 3); the limit "The cause is not established" (decision 2) covers a compiler that a fault of the machine ended. O1 answers "a compile or link failure"; if the owner reads a compiler ended by a signal as outside it, that is an objection to this reading. The errors do not: one class can arise in two rows — an `OSError` from starting the compiler (S6) or from moving the artefact (S9); a `FileNotFoundError` or `IsADirectoryError` for a source (S5) or for the output path (S3); an `OSError` from starting the compiler for a missing working directory (S3) or a compiler that cannot be started (S6); a `ValueError` or `TypeError` of `local_run.run` for an `env` (S4) or for an argument the operation's own code formed (below); an `OSError` of `clang_build.build` from removing its temporary file (below), which comes up in the same class from the same call as S6 and S9. An operation places such an error by the step of its own at which it met it (for example a test it makes before it calls the build), and **a code task that finds an error it cannot place in one row registers it**: the list is closed "for what this record read and for nothing else" (cited: ADR-0056 decision 5). The rest: a `ValueError` or `TypeError` for a setting — a time limit, a flag, the compiler — and from `local_run.run` an `OverflowError` for its time limit → S4, whether the setting is refused for its value or for its type, as every setting is the backend's own (decision 3) and S4 follows from the backend's own settings (above); a `TypeError` for an argument that is no setting (a path, an argument of the command) is a defect of the operation's own code and is no row (below; that code formed the argument from what it is handed, and no setting of the backend's is in it), and a `TypeError` of `clang_build.build`, whose docstring does not say which argument, is an error of two rows (Consequences); what `clang_build.build` passes on from `subprocess` beyond the `OSError` its docstring names (an `env` that cannot be handed over, a time limit too long to wait for) was not read, and falls under the rule just cited; a `NotADirectoryError` that `clang_build.build` raises before it starts anything, which it does for the output path only (for a source it raises `FileNotFoundError` or `IsADirectoryError`) → S3, while one raised once it has started anything is an `OSError` of the two-row entries above; `CompilerNotFoundError` → S6; `ProgramNotFoundError` or an `OSError` from starting the process → S10, but for an `OSError` for "a `cwd` that does not exist" (cited: `hilclaw/local_run.py`), which means S3 and is an error of two rows as well. An error the operation lets pass that is no tool failure — one from removing the temporary file, a `TypeError` or `ValueError` of `local_run.run` for an argument its own code formed or an empty command, a `TypeError` of the build for an argument of the wrong type, or the `ValueError` for no kernel source or no source, which the reader's non-empty compiled set rules out — is answered `operation_answer_invalid` (cited: ADR-0045 decisions 4 and 5). That says what the landed rules do with it; it is no answer of this record.
- **What the operation observes decides the row.** A build directory that cannot be written and shows only as a non-zero status of the compiler is S7 to the operation, a broken result `build_exit_status`; the limit "The cause is not established" (decision 2) covers it. The row S3 holds for what the operation finds itself before the build.

### 5. The messages and the compiler's log

- **The message of each of the six kinds**, in the manner of ADR-0045 decision 6 and ADR-0056 decision 6: it may say which case of its row it was (for S4 which setting; for S5 a kernel file or the verification program; for S10 not found, not executable or refused) and of which kind the error was, as the code names kinds of values. **It shows no path, no name of an entry, no byte of a file, no text of an error, no value of a setting and no part of the compiler's log.** The message is written to `events.ndjson` for good (cited: ADR-0006 decision 5). The wording is the code's; a caller tells the cases apart by the kind. A message that breaks this is not detected.
- **The compiler's log is kept and not returned** (O2, **the owner's**): "in a file of the attempt, never in the result or in `events.ndjson`". A result of S7 or S8 holds `condition` and nothing of the log, and no message holds any of it. **Its path and form, and the side that writes it, are not decided here** ("left to a record (with `build.json`, ADR-0004 decision 3)" — **the owner's**; beyond the two step lines, "For every other file the fixed inputs name a role and no holder" — cited: ADR-0016 decision 2 (d)). Either side needs a change of a landed sentence: under ADR-0056 decision 3 an operation "writes nothing below the data root", and form (B) carries nothing of the log to the tool function (decision 4); the record that gives the writer amends the one it needs. O2 answers a question asked about S7 and S8; this record applies it to the log of those builds and does not say whether the log of a build that ended ok, or of S9, is kept as well. Both are sub-question ⑱ (나).

## Consequences

- **Code slices** (code mode, D12 rules; names and modules are the code task's; **this record binds no code**). (1) The two values in `hilclaw/check_result.py` (`CONDITIONS`, in the order of decision 4) and the two strings of form (B) where the tool function tests for it (`hilclaw/check_answer.py`, which takes `CONDITIONS[:2]`), with their tests. Two places pair the values by position today — that one and `hilclaw/check_judging.py`, which zips `verification.Broken` with `CONDITIONS` — so new values put first, as decision 4 orders them, break both unless the code task changes them (an observation, binding nothing). (2) The six kinds in `hilclaw.check_failure.BACKEND_KINDS`, with their tests. **Not yet possible: the operation of `cpu_standin` for this tool**, and so the tool's registration. It lacks every setting value — flags and environment (⑱ (라)), the two time limits (OD-14) — the container O4 decides on, with its compiler (OD-14 ①), and the path and form of the file that keeps the compiler's log (⑱ (나)). That is said plainly: those stops stand.
- **Telling the rows apart is a condition of the operation's code.** The errors decision 4 names as arising in one class in two rows, from one call of `clang_build.build` or `local_run.run`, cannot be placed by an operation that sees the class alone. The code task that writes the operation makes them distinguishable — for example by a change of those functions, binding nothing here — or registers what it cannot tell apart; this record gives no kind to an error of two rows (⑱ (마)).
- **Not edited and now less exact.** ADR-0042 decision 3 ("exactly four values"; "which one of the four conditions"; "the order is that decision's own and is not re-decided"), decision 6 ("For the two conditions an operation finds") and the `condition` row of decision 7; ADR-0021 decision 2 ("which of two conditions") and decision 5 ("one class for all four conditions"; "which one of the four conditions"); ADR-0043 decision 4 ("one of two strings"; "There are exactly two"), the (B) row of decision 7 and the passages that say "neither a report nor one of the two conditions"; ADR-0044 decisions 2 and 3 where they speak of the two conditions, and decision 5 ("the two cases without a report are the two strings of decision 4"); ADR-0045 decision 1 ("one of the two strings of decision 4"), decision 4 ("neither a report nor one of the two conditions of ADR-0043 decision 4") and Alternatives ("`condition` has 'exactly four values'"); ADR-0047 decision 5 ("the operation returns one of the two conditions"; "This record therefore names no kind for the local run") and the row "Returned one of the two conditions" of decision 8; ADR-0056 decision 3 ("writes nothing below the data root"; the (B) row of its table), decision 4 (the settings table; "Nothing of the build reaches a file"), decision 6 ("S3 to S10 are given no answer here"; "Until ⑰ is answered") and decision 8; ADR-0017 decision 9 ("the caller" for the build settings — now the backend's own caller, the operation).
