## Why
PLAN P1-5 is open and stays open after this task (`task.objective` waits on #5 A-4). Its op side was closed by ADR-0008 (T-0030, 2026-10-07) and carried into the package by `hilclaw/gemm_contract.py` (T-0110, 2026-10-09). T-0110 met **one point no record states** and filed it "for a later record" (its `## Result`, "Provisional choices" and "For a later record"):

- **ADR-0001 decision 1 (line 34)**: "An *integer* is in the range 0 to 2^53 - 1"; decision 2 (line 43): `shape` is a "map: parameter → integer". So a `gemm` op_signature whose shape holds a 0 is a valid record with a key, and `hilclaw.keys.op_signature_key` (`_integer`, lines 182-185) and `hilclaw.spec.validate` (`_size`, lines 208-211) accept it.
- **ADR-0008 decision 6 (line 84)**: the caller guarantees that "`m`, `k`, `n` are each at least 1". ADR-0017 decision 3 (line 44) repeats it for a verification run ("restricted no further than ADR-0008 (each ≥ 1)"), and the harness refuses a smaller value in four places (`hilclaw/harness/timing.py`, `verification.py`, `verification_arrays.py`, and the two C programs, header lines 14-15).
- **ADR-0008 decision 9 (line 115)** gives the rule of the op_signature records "for each of the four B-4 shapes and each M ∈ {1, 32, 512}" and says nothing of another shape of the contract.
- **The code took a side provisionally**: `hilclaw.gemm_contract` refuses a dimension below 1 ("this module's choice, not a rule of a record", docstring lines 66-71; `_shape`, lines 119-127), and `tests/test_gemm_contract.py::test_zero_is_accepted_by_keys_and_refused_here` (line 328) pins the difference from `keys`.

So today two programs that follow the records can differ: one makes the op_signature record and the `task.contract` of a shape such as m = 0, k = 2048, n = 8192 (a valid key, a valid spec), the other refuses it; and the first has a kernel no caller of the contract may ever call. Whoever binds the values next (the writer of specs, OD-23; the start of an instance, ADR-0031) would have to choose again.

DRIVER §6: a task that needs a design-level decision carries the record that closes it. **This task writes the record.** It ticks **no PLAN checkbox**.

### The rule as landed, and what it is a statement about (alternative (a) of `## Why`)
- **Scope**: the one contract of ADR-0008 (`gemm`, `fp32`, `row_major`, `c`), in the one case that the shape of an op_signature holds a 0 in `m`, `k` or `n`. Nothing is said of a shape whose dimensions are each at least 1, of an upper bound, or of another kind, dtype, layout or language.
- **Rule**: the contract of ADR-0008 covers no shape that holds a 0. For an op_signature record whose `kind` is `gemm`, `dtype` is `fp32`, `layout` is `row_major` and whose `shape` holds a 0, **ADR-0008 defines no `task.contract` and no call**.
- **It is a statement about the contract - about what ADR-0008 defines - and about nothing else.** It is not a statement about the validity of a record, a spec or a file, and not about what a program does.
- **The four cases**: (1) such a record given to `hilclaw.keys.op_signature_key` is a valid record with a key (ADR-0001, unchanged); the record says only that the contract does not cover it. (2) The same record inside a spec: the spec is valid or invalid by ADR-0002 alone (an operand shape value of 0 is then one of the op_signature shape values); whatever its `task.contract` holds, that object is not a `task.contract` of the contract of ADR-0008; nothing is said of a kernel written to that spec or of a call of it. (3) The same record in `instance.json`: valid or invalid by the records of that file (ADR-0031 decision 3), which states no contract in any case; ADR-0008 defines no `task.contract` and no call for the shape the member states. (4) A request for the `task.contract` of such a shape under this contract: there is none, and **nothing is substituted** (not the three entries of decision 5 with a 0 in them, not those of another shape).
- **What is cited and what is the record's own**: that a shape value may be 0 is ADR-0001 decision 1; that no call has a dimension below 1 is ADR-0008 decision 6; that the runs take no smaller value is ADR-0009 decision 9 and ADR-0017 decisions 3 and 9. The record's own part is the reading of ADR-0008 decisions 1, 5 and 6 together: the operand entries of a shape describe the arrays of a call, so where ADR-0008 has no call it has no entries either, and the contract covers no shape for which it has no call (stated in the negative direction only). Decision 9 is read as written (twelve records, nothing of another shape); decision 4's "whatever the shape" is read with "of this contract".
- **The sentences of ADR-0008 that speak against the reading are named in the record**: decision 1 ("Another kind, dtype, layout or language has another contract, defined by its own record") and decision 8 ("The contract of (`gemm`, `fp32`, `row_major`, `c`) is the one of this record") name no shape and can be read the other way. The record reads them as saying which contract the four values have, not that the contract has something for every shape. **The reading is chosen, not found**, and the record says so.
- **Why (a)**: "an op_signature of the contract without a call" would need a rule for the verification and the measurement of a kernel never called at its shape (a rule added to ADR-0009 and ADR-0017) or an answer to OD-16 sub-question ①; "no rule" leaves two followers differing; "a call with a 0 is allowed" is a change of decision 6, which only a superseding record makes (decision 8). The rule does not rest on the operation being undefined (for k = 0 the product is a matrix of zeros); ADR-0008 gives no reason for "at least 1" and the record supplies none in its name.
- **Walk-through keys** (computed with `hilclaw.keys.op_signature_key`): (1, 2048, 8192) `gemm-485b9bc494d477eb`; (0, 2048, 8192) `gemm-d765fb955295628a`; (1, 0, 8192) `gemm-ae3e9e269ea6e812`; (0, 0, 0) `gemm-f5ed2636b2d123a9`.
