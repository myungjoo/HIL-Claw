### The builder
One public function that returns the tool function. Recommended form, **not binding**: `tool_function(*, source, table, seeds, covers)`.

- `source`: the data-root source, as `hw_tool.stepped_tool_function` takes it.
- `table`: the `BackendTable` the tool is bound to.
- `seeds`: the source of seeds, a callable without arguments (`hilclaw.harness.seed_source`). No default: the module binds no source and names no default one.
- `covers`: the predicate of ADR-0041 decision 5, handed to `check_admission.admit` as it is. No default.

It is built over `hw_tool.stepped_tool_function` with `tool="correctness.check"`, `steps=check_identity.STEPS`, `step_of=check_identity.step_of`, `numbers=check_identity.NUMBERS`, `check=check_identity.check`, the reader `hilclaw.instance.read`, and the hw_profile of what that read returned. The function it returns has these properties for a request `r` (the moments are those of ADR-0048 decision 10):

1. **Moments 1 to 3 are the builder's of `hw_tool`, unchanged**: the frame members and then `point` checked, `invalid_request` before anything is asked; the `step` from `point`; `no_data_root`; the four `run_file_*` kinds at the read of the stamp; an ended identity answered from its end line and an open one with `step_open`, with nothing read, selected, drawn or appended.
2. **Moment 4, one read.** `instance.json` is read once, by `hilclaw.instance.read`; the hw_profile handed to the selection, and the op_signature, the number of draws and the tolerance, are of that one read. The answers in this order and no other: a situation of the file (the `run_file_*` kinds); `no_backend`; `not_offered`; then the four of `check_admission.KINDS` in their order. For each: no line, no operation called, **the seed source not asked**, and the same request can be repeated.
3. **The draw, between 4 and the start line.** After `check_admission.admit` returned, `check_seed.draw(seeds)` is called once. A draw that yields no seed is `seed_not_drawn`, as `draw` raises it: no line, no operation called, repeatable; at the repeated call the source is asked again. A `BaseException` that is no `Exception` out of the source passes through with no line.
4. **The start line** holds exactly `step` and `execution`; no seed is in it, in the end line, in a result or in a message (ADR-0021 decision 3; ADR-0048 decision 7). When its append fails the call is `start_line_failed` as `journaled_step` answers it, and the drawn seed reaches no operation.
5. **The step** calls `check_seed.check(operation, r, hw_profile, admitted, seed)` once, with the request as the caller gave it, the hw_profile record of the one read, the `Admitted` and the seed of 3 - **only a seed `draw` returned** - and:
   - a returned result ends the step with that result and the `spend` `{}` (ADR-0040 decision 6);
   - `check_ending.EndedWithoutOutcome` ends the step with a `failure` of its `kind` and `message` and the `spend` `{}` (`journaled_step.StepFailed`); the call is answered with that tool failure, and a repeated call with the same one from the end line;
   - everything else `check` raises passes through unchanged and is not turned into an end of the step (a `BaseException` that is no `Exception`, ADR-0045 decision 5; a `ToolFailure` the operation did not raise; an error of this side): the identity stays open, by the convention of `hilclaw.journaled_step` and `hilclaw.check_ending`.
6. **Two requests that differ only in `point` are two identities** with two lines each and their own `data.step`; a further `execution` is carried out in full, the read, the admission and a fresh draw included (ADR-0040 decision 3; ADR-0048 decision 5).
7. The function keeps nothing between calls: no seed, no instance, no result. It does not modify the request. What reaches the step from the place before the start line (the `Admitted` and the seed) lives for that call only.
8. **Building**: `TypeError` / `ValueError` in the manner of the existing builders for a bad `table`, and `TypeError` for a `source`, `seeds` or `covers` that is not callable; state the order in the docstring. Building asks no source, reads no file, asks the table nothing and calls `covers` not at all.
