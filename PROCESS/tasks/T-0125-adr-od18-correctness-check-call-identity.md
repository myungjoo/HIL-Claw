## Why
PLAN P1-1 (the FR-10 tool contracts, the mock backend, the conformance tests) is open. `blob.push` / `blob.fetch` are registered (ADR-0039, T-0124). Of the four HW tools none has a complete contract. `correctness.check` is the furthest: ADR-0021 fixed four points of its contract (the split, the seed, the tolerance, the classes of outcome) and ADR-0038 (T-0088, with the repository owner's answer to HQ-0005) fixed which tools `cpu_standin` offers, that the two checks of an attempt are two calls each stating its execution point in a required request member, and that a run at the mock is answered by the same contract.

> whether the tool is journaled, its `step` values and the meaning of `execution`; the name and values of the member of decision 4 and every other member of the request, the input schema and the description; where `draws`, the dimensions, the kernel of the attempt, the flags and the time limits come from; the members of the result and the tool-failure kinds a backend may raise; the spend; the operation and the default script of the mock.
