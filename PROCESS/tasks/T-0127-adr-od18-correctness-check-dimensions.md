## Why
PLAN P1-1 (the FR-10 tool contracts, the mock backend, the conformance tests) is open: none of the four HW tools has a complete contract. `correctness.check` is the furthest. ADR-0021 fixed the split, the seed, the tolerance and the classes of outcome; ADR-0038 the execution points; ADR-0040 (T-0125, landed as `eb3f13f`) the identity of a call: journaled, the `step` values `correctness_check_sim` / `correctness_check_board`, the request member `point`, and the six required members `run_id`, `round`, `candidate`, `attempt`, `execution`, `point`.

ADR-0040 Consequences lists what the next slices of the second record must still add:

> every other member of the request, the input schema and the description; where `draws`, the dimensions, the kernel of the attempt, the flags and the time limits come from; the members of the result and the tool-failure kinds a backend may raise; the spend beyond decision 6; the operation and the default script of the mock; and, from ADR-0038 decision 4, whether an operation is handed the point.

**This task is the second slice: the dimensions of a check.** Both sides of the cut need `m`, `k`, `n` (ADR-0021 decision 2, the table), and no record says where a call has them from (ADR-0021 Boundaries: "the dimensions and shapes outside the `op_signature`"; ADR-0031 decision 2: "what a call of `correctness.check` does with this member is not said here"). Since ADR-0021 was written, ADR-0031 gave a run a place to state its op_signature (`instance.json`, member `op_signature`, which may be absent), and `hilclaw.instance.read` returns it from the same read that gives the hw_profile and the tolerance. The slice is chosen because it is tied to none of the open owner questions and because the later slices hang on it: the form of a complete report follows from `m`, `n` and `draws`, the mock's default script must produce a product of the right shape, and whether the request carries a member for the dimensions decides a part of the input schema.

The task ticks **no PLAN checkbox**: P1-1 stays open, and the tool still cannot be registered after this record.
