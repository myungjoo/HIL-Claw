## Why
PLAN P1-1 ("FR-10 도구 계약 ... 인터페이스 + mock 백엔드 + 백엔드 적합성 테스트") is open. The frame every tool contract shares is fixed by ADR-0010, ADR-0015, ADR-0016 and ADR-0019, and the path of a journaled tool is on `main` (`hilclaw/journaled_step.py`, `hilclaw/journaled_tool.py`, `hilclaw/hw_tool.py`). The order of a call is ① the request, ② the data root, ③ the lookup, ④ the selection, ⑤ the start line, ⑥ the step, ⑦ the end line (ADR-0016 decision 2). ADR-0019 gave a kind to what is found at ③ and ④ and left one gap by name:

> **Not in the table: a failure inside the append of the start line or of the end line** (⑤, ⑦). The sentence of decision 2 cannot be said of it: after a failed start line the identity has no line or is open, and after a failed end line the step has run and the identity is open or ended (`hilclaw/journaled_step.py`, `hilclaw/events.py`). This record gives it none of the four kinds and no other. Registered as sub-question ⑨; until it is answered such a failure reaches a caller as the code has it today. - ADR-0019 decision 3

- **OD-18 sub-question ⑨** (OpenDecisionProposals, line 223): what a call answers when the append of its start line or of its end line fails - the identity then has no line, is open or is ended, and after a failed end line the step has run - and whether the frame reserves a kind for it (registered "with the recovery of an open step: OD-10, the retry part of OD-17").
- **What the code does today**: `hilclaw.events.append` raises (`OSError`, `CorruptEventsError` for a path that is no regular file, an error for a data root that is gone, `InvalidEventError`); `hilclaw.journaled_step.run_prepared` lets it pass ("A failed append passes through"); `hilclaw.tools.call` turns every exception that is no `ToolFailure` into the reserved kind `tool_crashed`, whose meaning (ADR-0015 decision 2) says the tool function is defective. A full disk at ⑦, after a board measurement was paid for, therefore reaches the caller as a defect of the tool, and nothing tells it whether the step ran.
- **Why it is needed for P1-1**: the conformance tests are to check "that every outcome is one the tool's contract names" (ADR-0016 Consequences) and the mock's script must express every death point of the journaled path (ADR-0016 decision 5 (d)). ⑤ and ⑦ are the two moments of that path whose failure has no named answer.

What the ADR **decides**. The content of each decision is the executor's to work out and justify from the fixed inputs; the reviewer's Approve confirms it. For every point give the choice, the alternatives considered and one or two lines of reason; mark each statement as **cited** (with the record and the decision) or as **this record's own**:
1. **Scope and the situations.** A call of a journaled tool whose append of the start line (⑤) or of the end line (⑦) fails. List the situations from what the fixed records and today's code show, at least: the file system refuses the write or the flush (full, read-only, permission, input / output error); the place of the log is no regular file at the append although the lookup read it; the data root or the run directory is gone between the lookup and the append; a failure after the line feed was written (the flush). **Say which are not situations of this record**: a line the tool layer itself cannot form (`InvalidEventError`, `InvalidOutcomeError` - an outcome an end line cannot hold, no end line appended), an exception out of the step (⑥), the death of the process (ADR-0015 Walk-through), what `before_start` raises.
2. **What is established about the identity after each failure, and what the answer may therefore say.** After a failed start line: the step was not called by this call; the identity has no line, or is open although nothing ran (when the failure came after the line feed). After a failed end line: the step has run and consumed; the identity is open, or ended (when the failure came after the line feed); the outcome exists in the process and is in no line unless the line is complete. State these as today's code has them (cite the module) and say which of them the record makes a property of the frame and which stay the code's. **State the sentence a caller may rely on for each answer** - as ADR-0019 decision 2 has one sentence for its four kinds - and say plainly what the answer does **not** establish.
3. **Whether the frame reserves a kind, and how many.** Apply the test of ADR-0015 decision 2 (one meaning in every tool; a caller acts on it without knowing the tool). The names are the record's; a name must be a *name* (ADR-0001) no tool's contract uses (`hilclaw.tools.default_registry` is empty - confirm).
4. **The outcome of a step whose end line failed.** Today the outcome is not returned in the place of the error. Decide whether that is a property of the frame, with the reason from the fixed records (ADR-0011 decision 3: a repeated call answers from the first end line, and "equal values" could not hold for an outcome that is in no line; ADR-0006 decision 7), or say exactly why the inputs do not carry it and where it is decided. **Do not decide a second place where an outcome is kept.**
5. **The same request again, and the borders.** What a later call with the same request is answered - by the lookup alone (no line: the step is started; open: `step_open`; ended: the stored answer), cited from ADR-0011 decision 3 and ADR-0019 decision 3, with nothing added. Whether a torn tail the failed append removed, or left, changes anything (ADR-0006 decision 6).
6. **Where the answer is given and what the message carries**: below the fronts, in the tool function, at the two appends - the property fixed, not the code (ADR-0019 decision 5 as the model; **not by class in `hilclaw.tools.call`**, for the reason of its line 88). The message: by reference to ADR-0019 decision 6, or the record's own rule if that one does not fit.
7. **Limits**: nothing repairs or marks the run; the answer of a failed append is itself kept nowhere (ADR-0010 decision 7), so a caller that lost it learns the state only from a repeated call; the failure after the line feed; two processes; a spend that is not counted for an open step (cited: ADR-0011) - and that a full disk can make every later journaled call of the run fail the same way without any record of it.
