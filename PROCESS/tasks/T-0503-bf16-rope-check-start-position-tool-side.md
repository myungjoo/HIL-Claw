## Why

**State.** PLAN P2-14 (Llama 3.2 1B end-to-end, the D18 goal) needs every operation of P2-11 to come from an instance, and the instance start admits a contract only when it is both `checked` and `timed` (ADR-0177 decision 2). After T-0498 to T-0502 (`main` `80d08368`), `correctness.check` serves bf16 `gemm`, `rmsnorm`, `elementwise_add`, `swiglu` and `sdpa_kv_cache` on the mock and `cpu_standin`, and those five entries are `checked`. ADR-0177 decision 5 item 3 is next: "`correctness.check` for `rope`, after the record that gives the start position its source (decision 4)". T-0502's Result named it as the alternative next candidate. Item 4 (the bf16 timing run) is not planned here: the results of `sim.run`/`board.run` have a closed `shape` of exactly `m`, `k`, `n` (ADR-0063 decision 5), so its record will very likely meet the same data-plane question HQ-0077 answered for the check, and its tasks are left to a later planner run.

**Why an ADR.** ADR-0164 decisions 4 and 12 make the start position P "a parameter of the run, given by the caller with no default"; for the op_signature shapes the caller passes P = 0 for m ∈ {128, 2048} and P = 127 or P = 2047 for m = 1, "one per run", and "whether a check of an m = 1 op_signature runs both is the caller's policy, with the number of draws (OD-16 sub-question ⑤)". The stated record does not hold P (ADR-0161 decision 3: the position is neither a member nor a parameter of the contract). So a check of `rope` needs a record of where its P comes from — part of **OD-16 sub-question ⑤**, a design-level OD the driver closes under DRIVER §9, written with its first code slice in one task (DRIVER §6 "task 단위" ①, D20). The same record also settles, so that the next task (the `cpu_standin` operation for `rope` and its `checked` flag) needs no record of its own (D20 ②): the `cpu_standin` time limits of the `rope` verification build and process (part of **OD-14**; ADR-0179 covers the other five kinds only), and how the rows the `rope` program reads on standard input (ADR-0164 decision 3) reach a process in the ADR-0144 container (today `hilclaw/standin_container.py` hands the client no standard input).

**Size and cut.** Slice 3 touches the ADR, three registers and about seven modules — above the 10-file cap — so this task takes the ADR, the tool side and the mock. The `cpu_standin` operation for `rope` (standard input through the container client, the limit) and the `checked` flag of the `rope` entry follow in a task the next planner run creates once this ADR has landed (DRIVER §6 "task 단위" ④: a task whose content depends on an unlanded decision is not created ahead).

**PLAN order and D18.** P2-11 and P2-14 are PLAN checklist items; D18 does not apply. P2-10 waits on HQ-0076 (T-0493). No RuntimeAcceptance `실패` item exists as of `main` `80d08368`, so none is fixed here.

**D16.** Everything is verified with tests in this repository; nothing starts `claude`, needs a board, a real container runtime or real Llama weights. Facts only a real container on a C-7 machine can establish (whether the client passes the rows unchanged, whether the limits fit) are runtime acceptance items for `PROCESS/RuntimeAcceptance.md`, not tasks.
