## Why

T-0319 landed ADR-0113 as `7dca399` after 7 review rounds. The round-7 Approve left two non-blocking nits. They are recorded in T-0319's `## Result`, in the bullet "Review". They were not applied, because applying them would have needed an eighth round, which is past the 7-round cap:

1. ADR-0113 decision 2, the bullet "**Same answer for both, and why.**" (line 42), says "a best is the base of the comparisons that follow it". Whether later candidates are compared against the best is part of the choice rule for a best (the rest of OD-1, owner-only, #5 A-4). Decision 3 of the same record leaves that rule open. Stated as a plain fact, the sentence reads as if it assumed part of that rule. The reviewer proposed a conditional form, for example "where a best is the base of later comparisons".
2. ADR-0113 decision 2, the first "Why" bullet (line 37), says "That is the effect NFR-3 asks for, an interval and not a cut-off." NFR-3 asks that variance be handled "반복 측정/신뢰구간 등으로" (Requirements line 46). It does not say "not a cut-off". The sentence mixes a citation with this record's own reading. ADR-0113 Status (line 6, "Three kinds of content") requires the two to be kept apart. The Alternatives row (a) (line 72), "Noise is handled by the interval of ADR-0007 decision 7, which NFR-3 asks for", has a similar shape and should be checked with it.

The record's decisions do not change. Only these sentences are made exact. This is the same kind of follow-up as T-0312 and T-0316. It is small and in document mode, and it needs no owner decision, no runtime and no HQ. The §9 owner window of ADR-0113 is not affected, because nothing decided changes.
