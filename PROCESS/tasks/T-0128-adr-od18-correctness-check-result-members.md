## Why

ADR-0021 fixed the split, the seed, the tolerance and the classes of outcome; ADR-0038 the execution points; ADR-0040 (T-0125) the identity of a call; ADR-0041 (T-0127, landed as `d5c0528`) the dimensions of a check and the two answers `op_signature_not_stated` / `op_signature_not_covered`.

ADR-0041 Consequences lists what the next slices of the second record must still add:

> every other member of the request, the input schema and the description; where `draws` comes from; ~~the dimensions~~ (this record); where the kernel of the attempt, the flags and the time limits come from; the members of the result and the tool-failure kinds a backend may raise; the spend beyond ADR-0040 decision 6; the operation and the default script of the mock; and whether an operation is handed the point.

**This task is the third slice: the members of a result.** ADR-0021 decision 5 fixed what a result "must at least let a caller tell apart" (which of pass, fail, broken - exactly one - and, for broken, which one of the four conditions, the first that fails in a stated order) and what it "must never contain", and left the rest: "Whether it holds more (the per-draw counts of ADR-0017 decision 7, the dimensions, `draws`, the tolerance used) and every name and type are the second record's." ADR-0041 decision 7 handed on one more point: "Whether the dimensions used are a member of a result is the result slice's", with the limit that neither line of a step names the shape, "so the log does not show it unless a result does" (its decision 9). - Scope: a result of a call of `correctness.check` that reached moment 6 - a check - about one attempt of a run that ADR-0021 and ADR-0038 decision 6 cover and whose stated op_signature is covered (ADR-0041 decision 5); the same form at both points and for a run at the mock (cited: ADR-0038 decisions 4 to 6). Tool failures are not results and get no member here.
- The record decides, as its own design, **these points and no other**:
  1. **How a result reports which of pass, fail and broken it is**: the name of the member, its type and its three values (exactly one per result - cited: ADR-0021 decision 5). Weigh at least one member with three values against two members in the manner of `Judgement` (`verdict` / `broken`, an observation of the code, which binds nothing) and against a boolean. Check each name the record fixes for a collision in `hilclaw/`, `tests/` and `ARCHITECTURE/`, and say so with the date.
  2. **How a broken result names its condition**: the name of the member, its four values - one for each condition of ADR-0021 decision 5, in that decision's order (the time limit, the exit status, the form of the report, a check value), the value being the first condition that fails (cited, not re-decided) - and whether the member is absent, or present with which value, in a result that reports pass or fail (compare ADR-0039 decision 4, "left out otherwise, never `null`"; the choice is the record's).
  3. **Whether a result holds the per-draw counts** that the Python side of the harness returns for a completed run - per draw the number of elements that failed the predicate and how many of those were non-finite (cited: ADR-0017 decision 7) - and if so under which member, in which form, and what a broken result holds there. Weigh: NFR-2 and the use of a fail by whoever acts on it; that the counts hold no element of the inputs or of the reference (check that claim against ADR-0017 decision 7 and say what a count can and cannot reveal); that the number of entries shows the `draws` of the check; that a broken run has no counts "also when the draws it did report contain an element outside the tolerance" (cited); and that what of a verification is ever shown to a coder is not decided (ADR-0017 decision 7; ADR-0002 Boundaries; OD-6) - the record says what a result holds, not which role reads it. Indices and the largest deviation are **not** added (cited as undecided; listed under Boundaries).
  4. **Whether a result names what the check was made with**, each weighed on its own: the dimensions (ADR-0041 decision 7 and decision 9: the log shows the shape of a check only if a result does; two checks of one attempt can be about two shapes); the tolerance used (ADR-0021 decision 6: they can have two tolerances); the number of draws used (if named, **only as what this check used** - no value, no source and no default of `draws` is fixed, and the record says which later slice supplies it); the execution point (already in the `step` of the identity - ADR-0040 decision 3; say whether a result repeats it). For each: in or out, the member name, type and form where it is in, and the reason. If two answers cannot be told apart by the fixed inputs, take one as provisional and say so.
  5. **What a result never holds**: cited from ADR-0021 decision 5 and decision 3 (no element of the inputs or of the reference; and, until OD-16 sub-question 5 is answered, not the seed, the command line or the standard error of the kernel's process), not re-decided. Then, as the record's own and only as far as it follows from points 1 to 4: whether anything else of what the runner captured for a broken run (the exit status, the signal, the time that passed) is a member. **The planner expects "none under this record"**, since "What is kept of a broken run and what follows it is OD-10 and OD-14" (ADR-0017 decision 7); the record may not close that question in either direction - it says what a result holds today and what a later record may add.
  6. **Whether the member list of a result is closed by this record** ("these members and no other", as ADR-0039 decision 4 has it) or stays open for the later slices, and what a caller may rely on in either case. Say whether a result carries a version member, with ADR-0010 decision 7 and ADR-0006 decision 8 weighed, and what follows for a result that was stored by one source commit and is returned by a later one (cited: ADR-0011 decision 3, a repeated call returns what the end line holds; OD-11 for reading across versions - not decided here).
- The record also states, **without deciding them**:
  - **OD-16 sub-question 5 is not closed.** Say which sentence of this record an answer "the seed may be recorded" would reopen.
  - **What anybody does after a fail or a broken result** (FR-1, OD-10, OD-14, OD-19) and **who writes the result file of an attempt** from an end line (ADR-0011 decision 4, last bullet; PLAN P1-8; OD-23): a result of this tool is not that file, and no member of that file is named.
  - **The `spend` of the end line** stays as ADR-0040 decision 6 left it.
  - **How the operation reports to the tool function** (the form between the two sides of the cut; what the operation returns for the two conditions it finds) is the slice of the backends', except where a sentence of ADR-0021 decision 2 is cited.

- A walk-through in the local configuration with the names of ADR-0040 and ADR-0041 (the run `r1`, `round` 3, `candidate` 2, `attempt` 1): a check that passes; one that fails; one broken by each of two conditions, one found by the operation and one by the tool function; the same request again after an ended check (the stored result, member for member); a further `execution` after the file changed its op_signature or tolerance (what the two stored results then show, by point 4); a run at the mock.
