## Why

T-0069 (PR #60, `21ce62f`) landed the first half of the code slice its Consequences cut: the four constants in `hilclaw/tools.py`, the module `hilclaw/run_files.py`, and the rows of moment 3 in `hilclaw.journaled_step._state`.

**This task is the second half: the rows of moment 4.** Today on `main` (`1a72026`) `hilclaw.hw_tool.tool_function` calls the reader of the hw_profile bare (`hilclaw/hw_tool.py` line 112), so a missing, unreadable, invalid or other-version `runs/<run_id>/instance.json` leaves the tool function as `instance.MissingInstanceError`, an `OSError`, `instance.InvalidInstanceError` or `instance.UnsupportedVersionError`, and `hilclaw.tools.call` reports each as `tool_crashed` - the outcome ADR-0019 decision 5 excludes ("none of them reaches a caller as `tool_crashed`"). ### The answer at the selection (`hilclaw/hw_tool.py`)
For an identity with no line, what the call of `hw_profile_of(data_root, run_id)` raises is answered as follows, before `backends.select` is called and before the start line is appended:

| What the call of the reader raises | Answer |
|---|---|
| `instance.MissingInstanceError` | `ToolFailure` of kind `run_file_missing` |
| `instance.InvalidInstanceError` (an empty or partial file, a `hw_profile` that breaks ADR-0018 decision 3, a path that is no regular file) | `run_file_invalid` |
| `instance.UnsupportedVersionError` | `run_file_unsupported` |
| `FileNotFoundError` or `NotADirectoryError` (the data root gone since moment 2; the file gone between the check and the read) | `run_file_missing` |
| any other `OSError` | `run_file_unreadable` |
| anything else, a `ToolFailure` and a `BaseException` that is no `Exception` included | passes through unchanged, the same object |

- Each `ToolFailure` is chained to the error it answers for (`__cause__`), as `run_files.reading` does it. The expected form is one `with run_files.reading(data_root, run_id, "instance.json", <the three classes and their kinds>):` whose block holds the call of the reader and nothing else.
- **Only the call of the reader is mapped.** `backends.select` is outside the block: `keys.InvalidRecordError` for what is no valid record still passes through (`tool_crashed` through `tools.call`), and `no_backend` / `not_offered` are unchanged. The step, both appends, the request checks, the source and the lookup are untouched by this task; `hilclaw/journaled_step.py`, `hilclaw/journaled_tool.py`, `hilclaw/run_files.py` and `hilclaw/tools.py` are **not edited** (if `run_files` turns out to lack something, say so in `## Result` and propose it as a follow-up rather than widening the task - unless it is a defect that makes a row unanswerable, which is BLOCKED with the question).
- **Message** (decision 6): for the three classes the reader's message is used as it is (it starts with `runs/<run_id>/instance.json`); for an `OSError` it is what `run_files.read_failure` composes. A reader other than `hilclaw.instance.hw_profile_of` that raises one of the three classes with another message gets that message unchanged: state it as a limit in the docstring, do not check it.
- The reader is still asked once per call that reaches the selection, and never for an ended or an open identity (ADR-0019 decision 1 "Two moments"): `instance.json` in any state does not change the answer of such an identity.
- The signature of `tool_function`, its eight parameters, what building raises and the order of a call are unchanged.
